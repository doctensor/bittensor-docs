What it is

Almanac, formerly Sportstensor, blends two contests into one validator weight vector. Market miners trade Polymarket through the team's app and earn on profit and volume. Forecasting miners submit agents scored against the market price. The team says this feeds its forecasting agent, Alma.

How it works

Miners

Market miners link a Polymarket wallet to an Almanac account and their coldkey, then trade in the app or by API. Forecasting miners fund portal credits and submit an agent with the miner CLI.

Validators

Validators pull trade history and agent assignments from the team's servers, run agents in Docker sandboxes with no network, score both tracks and set one weight vector.

Incentive

The chain splits emission 50% miners, 40% validators, 10% owner. In the code, miners' share goes 95% to market traders and 5% to forecasting. Market score is decayed volume share (power 0.65) times decayed positive-profit share (0.35). Forecasting is 70% accuracy against the market price and 30% edge.

Roles and requirements

Miner (market) Medium barrier

Trades Polymarket markets in Almanac's app or by API; pay follows decayed profit and volume.

  • Needs 3+ epochs of trading and 5+ trades; skipping the on-chain address step leaves a miner at zero (team, 2026-09-30).

Miner (forecasting) Low barrier

Submits an agent that validators run on live events and score against the market price.

  • Pool is 25% allowance (3% cap per miner) and 75% skill (50+ predictions); no duplicate-agent check (team, 2026-10-21).

Validator High barrier

Syncs trades, runs agents in sandboxes and sets one blended weight vector.

  • 10 hotkeys hold permits and 8 set weights; one validator earns 72.7% of dividends (2026-11-14).

Getting started

  1. Pick a track, then register: btcli subnets register --netuid 41 (testnet 172).
  2. Market: make an Almanac account and Polymarket deposit wallet, link your coldkey and run scripts/run_market_miner.py once.
  3. Forecasting: portal account and gateway key, build from src/agent/examples, submit with miner/cli.py; keep credits funded.
  4. Validator: build the Docker images and run scripts/run_validator.py under PM2; a permit needs a 1,000 TAO stake.

Caveats

  • Scores, trade history and the model gateway are team services, so outsiders cannot recompute scores; outside validator access is untested.
  • The almnc.ai leaderboard covers forecasting only; taostats blends both tracks, so top uids can differ.
  • Forecasting score resets (57 miners listed, none pending) are not in the public repo; their rules are undocumented.
  • Uids 53 and 9 (different coldkeys) sit among six miner uids within 0.04 points; both were cleared in a 20-unstake sweep on 11-09; one operator is likely, unproven.
