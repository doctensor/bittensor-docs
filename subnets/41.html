What it is

Almanac, formerly Sportstensor, blends two contests into one validator weight vector. Market miners trade Polymarket through the team's app and earn on profit and volume. Forecasting miners submit agents that validators score against the market price. The team says this feeds its commercial forecasting agent, Alma.

How it works

Miners

Market miners link a Polymarket wallet to an Almanac account and their coldkey, then trade in the app or by API; each buy pays a 1% fee into the reward pool. Forecasting miners fund portal credits and submit an agent with the miner CLI.

Validators

Validators pull trade history and agent assignments from the team's servers, run agents in Docker sandboxes with no network, score both tracks and set one weight vector. Model calls go through the team's gateway, so no GPU is needed.

Incentive

The chain splits emission 50% miners, 40% validators, 10% owner. In the code, miners' share goes 95% to market traders and 5% to forecasting. Market score is decayed volume share (power 0.65) times decayed positive-profit share (0.35). Forecasting is 70% accuracy against the market price and 30% edge.

Roles and requirements

Miner (market) Medium barrier

Trades Polymarket markets in Almanac's app or by API; pay follows decayed profit and volume.

  • Needs 3+ epochs of trading and 5+ trades; skipping the on-chain address step leaves a miner at zero (team, 2026-09-30).

Miner (forecasting) Low barrier

Submits an agent that validators run on live events and score against the market price.

  • Pool is 25% allowance (3% cap per miner) and 75% skill (50+ predictions); no duplicate-agent check (team, 2026-10-21).

Validator High barrier

Syncs trades, runs agents in sandboxes and sets one blended weight vector.

  • 10 hotkeys hold permits and 8 set weights; one validator earns 73.0% of dividends (2026-11-07).
  • Trade history and assignments come from team APIs; outside validator access is untested.

Getting started

  1. Pick a track, then register: btcli subnets register --netuid 41 (testnet 172). Market needs Polymarket funds; forecasting needs portal credits.
  2. Market: make an Almanac Market account and Polymarket deposit wallet, link your coldkey, and run scripts/run_market_miner.py once, or you show no emissions.
  3. Forecasting: portal account and gateway key, build from src/agent/examples, submit with miner/cli.py; keep credits funded.
  4. Validator: build the Docker images and run scripts/run_validator.py under PM2; a permit needs a 1,000 TAO stake.

Caveats

  • Scores, trade history and the model gateway are team services and validators post one vector, so outsiders cannot recompute scores.
  • The almnc.ai leaderboard covers forecasting only; taostats blends both tracks, so top uids can differ.
  • Forecasting score resets (54 miners listed) are not in the public repo, and their rules are undocumented.
