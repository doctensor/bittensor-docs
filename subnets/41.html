What it is

Almanac, formerly the sports-prediction subnet Sportstensor, runs two incentive mechanisms and blends them into one set of validator weights. IM1, Almanac Market, pays miners who trade Polymarket prediction markets through the team's terminal, based on profit, volume and edge against the competition. IM2, Almanac Forecasting, pays miners who submit forecasting agents that validators run on real market events and score against the market price. The team says the output feeds Alma, its commercial forecasting agent, and that it plans to fine-tune a model on the reasoning traces miners' agents produce. The team's own stats show the market side is mostly sports: about 89% of roughly $22.4 million of tracked volume up to 2026-10-22.

How it works

Miners

Market miners register a UID, create an account on the team's trading terminal, deploy a Polymarket proxy wallet, link the Bittensor coldkey to the account and publish on chain a short commitment of their trading address (its first five characters) so validators can map the UID to the account. They then trade in the web app or through an API script; no miner process has to keep running afterward. Every buy carries a 1% fee that funds the reward pool. Forecasting miners instead create a portal account, fund credits that pay for the model calls their agent makes through the team's gateway, build an agent from the repository's examples, and submit it with the miner CLI signed by their hotkey. The agent must return a probability, its reasoning and a belief path. Both tracks can share a UID, but setup differs. The team said on 2026-10-21 that it has no duplicate check for forecasting agents and that testing different variants of one agent on several UIDs is fine, while each submission costs money.

Validators

Validators sync trade history for market miners and, for forecasting, run each submitted agent in a locked-down Docker sandbox on events assigned through the team's orchestrator API, then post predictions and reasoning traces back to the team's portal. Per the team, assignments are limited to markets that resolve in one to seven days with outcome prices between 0.25 and 0.75, and each miner gets up to 50 event predictions a day. Each validator scores both mechanisms and sets one blended weight vector. No GPU is needed because model inference goes through the team's central gateway; the README asks for a Linux amd64 host with Docker. On chain at block 9343967 (2026-10-23) nine UIDs held validator permits and seven had set weights; all seven posted the same vector (159 targets, differences under 0.1%), which fits every validator running the same code against the same central services rather than judging independently. One validator held about 91% of validator alpha and 81% of dividends.

Incentive

Per the code on main (scoring files last changed 2026-10-19), 95% of validator weight goes to the market slice and 5% to the forecasting slice (10% before 2026-10-19). On chain at block 9343967 the subnet emission split was 10% owner, 40% validators and 50% miners with nothing burned or recycled at that level, matching the team's 2026-10-19 post, and there is a single on-chain mechanism, so the market and forecasting blend exists only in the validators' weight vectors. Market scoring multiplies a trader's share of decayed volume (to the power 0.65) by their share of decayed positive profit (to the power 0.35), decays history with a half-life of roughly nine and a half days, caps any one trader at 6% of the pool, returns a fee floor of 0.57 times fees to active traders with a positive trailing return, and reserves 2% for dormant but historically profitable miners. The market pool's budget comes from the fees it generated and payouts are boosted by up to 75%, with anything unspent sent to a burn UID. The team's stats show rewards at about 1.7 times fees over the last 30 days ($163.9k against $98.6k), so the boost is in use. Forecasting scoring weighs accuracy at 70% (Brier skill against the market price stored when the prediction was made) and edge at 30%, with gates for invalid answers, too few predictions, worse-than-coin-flip accuracy and inactivity. Its slice splits 25% to an allowance pool (3% cap per miner) and 75% to a skill pool that pays only miners who beat the market over at least 50 recency-weighted predictions; the rest burns. On 2026-10-23 the team's leaderboard feed showed 92 forecasting miners sharing 66.8 of the 180 alpha a day budgeted to that slice (the top five took about 32% of it) and 63% of the slice going to the burn UID.

Getting started

  1. Decide which track you want. Market mining needs real capital on Polymarket and pays on edge, volume and winning; forecasting needs portal credits and an agent. The team says market setup is the more involved one.
  2. Register a UID on netuid 41 with Bittensor SDK v11 (btcli subnets register --netuid 41); testnet is netuid 172. Clone the repository and install its requirements.
  3. Market track: read the team's market docs for the fee pool, scoring and cap rules, create an account on the Almanac Market app, deploy the safe or proxy wallet, sign the approvals, fund it, and link your coldkey either in the app settings or with scripts/run_api_trading.py.
  4. Market track: run scripts/run_market_miner.py once to publish your trading address on chain. The team says many miners skip this step and then show no emissions on chain; then trade in the app, or by API after reading the Polymarket v2 migration notes (Polymarket's Data API v1 retires on 2026-10-24).
  5. Forecasting track: create a portal account and gateway API key, put the key in a repo-root .env, build an agent from src/agent/examples (the README recommends v1_agent2_basic.py), test it with miner/cli.py test-agent, then submit it with miner/cli.py submit-agent using your wallet and hotkey names.
  6. Keep portal credits funded (the team suggests staying above about 2 dollars) and keep the agent returning valid predictions; scoring fades after 24 hours without a prediction and goes to zero after 72.

Caveats

  • Per-trader market scores, the trade-history source and the forecasting scores come from team-run services (the central model gateway, the orchestrator API, the portal and market resolution). The 2026-10-15 incident shows validator weights depending on that API, and on 2026-10-23 all seven weight-setting validators posted the same vector, so outsiders cannot recompute scores or see independent evaluation from the public code. One validator holds about 91% of validator alpha.
  • The burn target is a hard-coded UID. Code sends unspent weight to UID 46 (UID 210, the owner's hotkey, before 2026-09-30). On 2026-10-23 UID 46 was not the owner's hotkey or coldkey, had never set weights and held 3.15% of incentive, which equals the unspent forecasting slice (5% times the 63% burn share). The chain read does not show who holds it or that anything paid to it is destroyed, and no explanation of the change was found in the channel.
  • Payout versus fees is not stated consistently. The team's stats feed shows rewards at about 1.66 times fees over 30 days and 1.67 times over 7 days, matching the code's boost of up to 75%; the team described fees as roughly equal to rewards on 2026-10-20, and its docs describe each day's budget as that day's fee pool without the boost. The rewards figures are the team's own accounting in dollars, not a per-trader chain read.
  • The 2026-09-29 report of trading miners receiving nothing was acknowledged by the team but its resolution is not stated. The team's stats show rewards credited every day from 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-05, so no zero day appears there. Two validators were also reported on 2026-10-13 not passing market prices to agents; identical weight vectors now say nothing about that.
  • The almnc.ai leaderboard covers the forecasting mechanism only, while taostats blends both mechanisms, so a top UID on one may not appear on the other. Market trader standings are on the Almanac Market site.
  • The team told one miner that consistently beating a 0.25 Brier score is the general bar in forecasting but that it depends on the rest of the field; profitability in either track is not promised and market miners risk their own trading capital plus the 1% buy fee.
  • Questions about revenue, buybacks and the alpha token are redirected by the team to its Telegram, so none of that is covered here. The forecasting score-reset feature announced on 2026-10-07 is not in the public repository, but the team's leaderboard feed carries reset records for 43 of 123 listed miners (resets about 30 days apart in the sample); its rules are not documented in the repository.
