What it is

Almanac, formerly Sportstensor, blends two contests into one validator weight vector. Market miners trade Polymarket through the team's app and earn on profit and volume. Forecasting miners submit agents.

How it works

Miners

Market miners link a Polymarket wallet to an Almanac account and their coldkey, then trade in the app or by API. Forecasting miners fund portal credits and submit an agent with the miner CLI.

Validators

Validators pull trade history and agent assignments from the team's servers, run agents in Docker sandboxes with no network, score both tracks and set one weight vector.

Incentive

The chain splits emission 50% miners, 40% validators, 10% owner. In the code, miners' share goes 95% to market traders and 5% to forecasting. Market score is decayed volume share (power 0.65) times decayed positive-profit share (0.35); one trader's cap is 20% of the pool (6% before 11-17). Forecasting is 70% accuracy against the market price and 30% edge; near-identical agents share one skill claim.

Roles and requirements

Miner (market) Medium barrier

Trades Polymarket markets in Almanac's app or by API; pay follows decayed profit and volume.

  • Needs 1+ epoch of trading (3+ before 2026-11-17, code) and 5+ trades; skipping the on-chain address step leaves a miner at zero (team, 2026-09-30).

Miner (forecasting) Low barrier

Submits an agent that validators run on live events and score against the market price.

  • Pool is 25% allowance (3% cap per miner) and 75% skill (50+ predictions) (team, 2026-10-21); one agent per hotkey (README, 2026-11-16).

Validator High barrier

Syncs trades, runs agents in sandboxes and sets one blended weight vector.

  • 10 hotkeys hold permits and 8 set weights; one validator earns 71.8% of dividends (2026-11-23).

Getting started

  1. Pick a track, then register: btcli subnets register --netuid 41 (testnet 172).
  2. Market: make an Almanac account and Polymarket deposit wallet, link your coldkey and run scripts/run_market_miner.py once.
  3. Forecasting: portal account and gateway key, build from src/agent/examples, submit with miner/cli.py; keep credits funded.
  4. Validator: build the Docker images and run scripts/run_validator.py under PM2; a permit needs a 1,000 TAO stake.

Caveats

  • Scores, trade history and the model gateway are team services: outsiders cannot recompute scores, and outside validator access is untested. The almnc.ai leaderboard shows forecasting only.
  • Forecasting score resets (55 miners listed, 15 pending) are not in the public repo; their rules are undocumented.
  • Uids 104, 53 and 9 (different coldkeys) ranked 3rd to 7th on 11-17, fell to 0.7% on 11-21, now 1.7%; their hotkey alpha moves together (58, 24, 7, 26, 45, 18). One operator is likely, unproven.
  • Validators on older code can still set weights (chain key 1; the code asks for 10); who runs the 11-17 code is unread.
  • No source says why rewards fell on 11-16 to 11-19, or why burn uid 46 swung from 29% to 97% (11-18, soon after PR #70, logging only), 3% (11-21), 21% (11-22) and 15% now.
