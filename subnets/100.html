What it is

Cortex calls itself an autonomous research network: the team runs the challenges and a gateway that seals each epoch's scores, and validators submit them. Bounty rewards bug reports; HyperTrain is a research-stage attempt at verifiable shared training. Formerly Platform, then BASE.

How it works

Miners

Bounty miners file reproducible bug reports; HyperTrain miners train in rounds while other nodes replay samples to audit them.

Validators

Validators download the gateway's sealed vector, verify it against the chain, recompute the weights and submit them.

Incentive

Bounty pays one point per report the team marks valid, cumulatively. HyperTrain rewards would vest over about ten rounds.

Why nothing is paid

  • Chain split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners (2026-10-31). All miner-side incentive lands on uid 0, the owner's own hotkey, and is burned; that uid holds 74.5% of the emission paid to uids. Until about 2026-10-19 roughly 70% sat there and the rest reached 26 to 85 other uids.
  • A challenge that fails, is not on the team's container list or has no open topic gets no score and its share burns to uid 0; on 2026-10-31 all three answered empty.
  • Bounty must read its whole report feed within 120 s: 21,000 reports took 37 s on 2026-10-16, so today's 131,030 would take nearly 4 minutes. That fits the 10-day 'refresh in progress' status but is arithmetic, not a team statement.

Getting started

  1. Bounty: install the cortex CLI (Python 3.12+, uv, libsodium), register a hotkey, and ask the team to authorise pairing with a dedicated Cortex Chat account.
  2. HyperTrain: only with an RTX 5090; run the self-check and join, then send the signed request to the operator.
  3. Validators: run scripts/run-validator.sh with --verify-only first, then with a registered wallet.

Caveats

  • The burn's cause is unconfirmed; explanations here come from public code and arithmetic, and the team's deployed setup is not public.
  • Bounty counts are the team's own, reviewed by operators with an AI agent; no report published since 2026-10-21 was marked duplicate or invalid, which this page cannot explain.
  • Validators are told to read their profile from /v1/trust; both public hosts answer 404 (2026-10-31).
  • Not researched: product and partnership claims; no bounty report was independently verified.
