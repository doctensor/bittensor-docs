What it is

Bitcast pays creators for posts that meet brand briefs: mechanism 1 covers X (98% of emission) and mechanism 0 YouTube (2%). Miner platforms such as Stitch3 bring creators; validators score the posts. On 2026-11-27 one uid holds all X incentive and the burn uid all YouTube incentive.

How it works

Miners

On X, a miner platform records creators' tweets, commits them on chain and pays the creators. On YouTube, a miner serves OAuth access to the channels it operates.

Validators

On X, validators check each tweet against its campaign and an LLM judges brief fit; the score follows author influence and engagement. On YouTube, they read channel analytics and score videos in USD.

Incentive

Each campaign's USD pool is spread over seven days by tweet score. X weights pay each miner 95% of what it owes creators and give the largest the rest; if emission is short, all are paid pro rata. On YouTube, weights follow earned USD and the burn uid takes the unclaimed rest.

Getting started

  1. Creators on X: join a miner platform such as Stitch3 and link your wallet.
  2. X miner: follow the bitcast-x README with a hotkey that meets the 15,000-alpha rule.
  3. Builders: start from x-miner-template; you handle creator login and payouts.
  4. YouTube miners and validators: follow the bitcast README; X validators use the bitcast-x runbook.

Caveats

  • Who runs uids 157 and 158 is not stated. Uid 157 holds 0 alpha on its own hotkey (uid 158: 19,700); neither has set weights; a lock to the owner hotkey was not read.

  • Validator versions are unobservable (version key 0), so the newest code rules cannot be confirmed on validators.
  • Dashboard figures are team-reported; the team wallet and buyback lock were not checked on chain.
