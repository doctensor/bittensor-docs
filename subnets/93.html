What it is

Bitcast pays creators for posts that meet brand briefs: mechanism 1 covers X (98% of emission) and mechanism 0 YouTube (2%). Miner platforms such as Stitch3 bring creators; validators score the posts. One uid now holds 98% of X incentive; the YouTube side burns 99% of its incentive.

How it works

Miners

On X, a miner platform records creators' tweets, commits them on chain and pays the creators. On YouTube, a miner serves OAuth access to the channels it operates.

Validators

On X, validators check each tweet against its campaign and an LLM judges brief fit; the score follows author influence and engagement. On YouTube, they read channel analytics and score videos in USD.

Incentive

Each campaign's USD pool is spread over seven days by tweet score. Weights first cover what each miner owes creators, then split any surplus by score. On YouTube, weights follow earned USD and the burn uid takes the unclaimed rest. The platform, not the subnet, pays creators.

Getting started

  1. Creators on X: join a miner platform such as Stitch3 and link your wallet.
  2. X miner: follow the bitcast-x README with a hotkey that meets the 15,000-alpha rule.
  3. Builders: start from the x-miner-template repo; you handle creator login and payouts.
  4. YouTube miners and validators: follow the bitcast README; X validators use the bitcast-x runbook.
