What it is

Bitcast pays creators for posts that meet brand briefs. Mechanism 1 covers X and mechanism 0 YouTube, about 98% and 2% of emission by the READMEs and the team. Miner platforms such as Stitch3 bring creators; validators score the posts. On chain, one miner uid holds about 90% of X incentive.

How it works

Miners

On X, a miner platform records creators' tweets, commits them on chain and pays the creators. It must hold 15,000 alpha, locked to the owner hotkey or staked to its own. On YouTube, a miner serves OAuth access to the channels it operates.

Validators

On X, validators check each tweet against its campaign (window, terms, originality, author influence) and an LLM judges brief fit; the score follows the author's influence and engagement from influential accounts. On YouTube, they read channel analytics and score videos in USD.

Incentive

Each campaign's USD pool is spread over seven days by tweet score. Weights first cover what each miner owes creators, then split any surplus by score. On YouTube, weights follow earned USD and the rest goes to the burn uid. The subnet only recommends a payout; the platform pays creators.

Getting started

  1. Creators on X: join a miner platform such as Stitch3 and link your wallet.
  2. X miner: follow the bitcast-x README with a hotkey that meets the 15,000-alpha rule.
  3. Builders: start from the x-miner-template repo; you handle creator login and payouts.
  4. YouTube miners and validators: follow the bitcast README; X validators use the bitcast-x runbook.

Caveats

  • Who runs uid 157 is not on chain; that it is Stitch3 rests on team statements (2026-09-02, 2026-09-15).
  • The 98/2 split between X and YouTube comes from the READMEs and the team; it was not read from chain.
  • Which validator version runs is unobservable: the weights version key is 0 and the latest tag (v2.2.0) predates main's 3.0.0.
  • Dashboard figures are team-reported; the team wallet and the lock on bought-back alpha were not checked on chain.
