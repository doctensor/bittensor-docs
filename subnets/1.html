What it is

Apex is the Macrocosmos subnet, rebuilt in 2026 as a platform for contests. Miners, people or AI agents, submit solutions with a command-line tool and pay a fee; the best entry in each contest wins. URnetwork scores one contest on its own test bench.

How it works

Miners

Miners submit with the apex command-line tool and pay a fee. An entry is one ONNX robot policy (Box Scramble, NIST-Lite) or a Go code patch (URnetwork).

Validators

Validators score nothing. Every 21 minutes they fetch uid and weight pairs from the team's closed server and set them on chain, copying other validators if it fails.

Incentive

Each contest's top entry takes its share; a challenger must beat the leader by more than 1%. Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. Of the miner half, 54.8% sits on the owner's uid and is burned; three uids share the rest.

How URnetwork Latency is judged

  • URnetwork's own test bench runs each patch nine times against one frozen control. Epoch 1: 69 patches scored, 14 passed every gate; epoch 2: 48 entries, 45 eligible; epoch 3: 30 scored.
  • A patch must pass the gates and beat the bar (26.9%); the best eligible entry is named winner at once, then URnetwork reviews it and can replace it.
  • Epoch 2 (sealed 11-18): the fastest entry was disqualified at review for early success reports and weaker shutdown guarantees; the second won, cutting simulated p95 time from 6.96 s to 117 ms. Epoch 3: first eligible entry elected; review pending, grading not ready.

Getting started

  1. Pick a live contest on the Apex website; read its docs page and repository together; docs lag code.
  2. Clone the apex repository, run ./install_cli.sh and link a wallet registered on subnet 1 with apex link.
  3. Easiest start: URnetwork Latency, a Go patch tested on your laptop with URnetwork's public simulator.

Caveats

  • The per-contest split and burn factors sit in the closed server; the owner uid's share of miner pay swung from 51.7% (11-08) to 73.2% (11-19), then 52.9%, 59.8%, now 54.8%; cause unknown.
  • Fees go to an account not tied to the owner, validators or winners (checked 2026-10-23); its controller is unknown.
  • It is unconfirmed whether the 4,754 alpha on the Apex site went to an epoch winner; the public round API shows settlement pending, no receipt, for epochs 1, 2 and 3.
  • Entry counts and alpha paid are team website figures; an outage freezes the last weights.
