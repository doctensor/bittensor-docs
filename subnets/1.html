What it is

Apex is the Macrocosmos subnet, rebuilt in 2026 as a platform for contests. Miners, people or AI agents, submit solutions with a command-line tool and pay a fee; the best entry in each contest wins. A partner, URnetwork, now scores one contest on its own test bench.

How it works

Miners

Miners submit with the apex command-line tool and pay a fee. An entry is one ONNX robot policy (Box Scramble, NIST-Lite) or a Go code patch (URnetwork).

Validators

Validators score nothing. Every 21 minutes they fetch uid and weight pairs from the team's closed server and set them on chain, copying other validators if it fails.

Incentive

Each contest's top entry takes its share; a challenger must beat the leader by more than 1%. Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. Of the miner half, 66.6% sits on the owner's uid and is burned; three uids share the rest. A finished contest's last winner keeps a fading payout for about ten days (team, 2026-07-08).

How URnetwork Latency is judged

  • URnetwork's own test bench runs each patch nine times against one frozen control. Results stay sealed until published; epoch 1 closed 2026-11-05 13:06 UTC and was still grading on 2026-11-07.
  • A patch must pass six gates and beat the bar (26.9% in epoch 1). The best entry is named winner at once; URnetwork then reviews it by hand and can revoke the win.
  • An entry is one patch file, not a commit, that may change any of ten repositories (docs, 2026-11-05).

Getting started

  1. Pick a live contest on the Apex website; read its docs page and repository together; docs lag code.
  2. Clone the apex repository, run ./install_cli.sh and link a wallet registered on subnet 1 with apex link.
  3. Easiest start: URnetwork Latency, a Go patch tested on your laptop with URnetwork's public simulator.

Caveats

  • The per-contest split and burn factors sit in the closed server; the burn share rose from 52.7% (2026-10-23) to 71.4% (2026-11-07), cause unknown.
  • Fees go to an account not tied to the owner, validators or winners (checked 2026-10-23); its controller is unknown.
  • URnetwork's epoch 1 result was not public on 2026-11-07 05:30 UTC; a winner can be revoked in review after promotion.
  • Entry counts and alpha paid are the team's website figures; a server outage freezes the last weights.
