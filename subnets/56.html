What it is

Gradients (repo G.O.D) runs weekly tournaments in which miners submit open-source training code for text and image models. Validators train it on their own GPUs, score the models and set weights from the standings. The team uses it to power gradients.io, a fine-tuning service.

How it works

Miners

Run an endpoint (port 7999, registered with Fiber) that returns a GitHub repo and an exact 40-character commit for each tournament type. The repo needs the required Dockerfiles, a licence and no bundled data or models. The entry fee is burned; one entry counts per coldkey, IP and GitHub account, and duplicates are screened in three tiers.

Validators

Validators build each repo, train it on managed GPUs and evaluate it. Text: a group round, pairwise knockouts, then a six-task boss round against the champion, which the challenger must win on five of six tasks. Image: three small tasks, a knockout, then six tasks at a 1% margin.

Incentive

Text is guaranteed 0.50 of weight and image 0.30; the other 0.20 follows entrant counts of earlier tournaments (now 0.65 text, 0.35 image). A champion winning by over 0.10 enlarges its type's pool by twice the excess (none now). Six uids hold all of it: text ranks 1-3 and image ranks 1-3 of the 2026-11-02 tournaments. The champion enters under the owner hotkey and the code maps it back, so beaten text champion uid 148 is paid at rank 2 and image champion uid 159 at rank 1. Entrants of an open tournament drew about 0.002% each on 2026-11-09 and earn nothing now.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Submits a public training repo and commit for each tournament; validators train it on their GPUs.

  • Entry opens Monday; at least four validated miners are needed.
  • The 2026-11-02 tournaments (17 text, 12 image entrants) ended 2026-11-06. Those of Monday 2026-11-09 were still running on 2026-11-13: 10 text and 8 image entrants.
  • Code: round 2 also reviews your pip packages; one it cannot fetch (an unpinned git dependency) holds your entry. Since 2026-11-09 round 2 also compares you with the champion's repo.
  • Docs: since 2026-11-12 training images must use CUDA 12.8 or lower.

Validator High barrier

Creates tasks, trains miners' repos on trainer GPUs, evaluates the models and sets weights.

  • The docs do not say whether outside validators run their own tournaments.
  • Code: since 2026-11-09 multi-GPU jobs run only on SXM or NVLink trainers.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/miner.md in the repo.
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 56, register with Fiber and post your miner IP an hour before Monday's start (text 11:00 UTC, image 13:00 UTC).
  3. Build a training repo with the required Dockerfiles and licence and pin a full 40-character commit.
  4. Hold TAO for the entry fee (text 0.7, image 0.4); it is burned.
  5. Follow #gradients for overhaul news.
