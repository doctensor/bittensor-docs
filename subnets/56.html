What it is

Gradients (repo G.O.D) runs weekly tournaments in which miners submit open-source training code for text and image models. Validators train it on their own GPUs, score the models and set weights from the standings. The team uses it to power gradients.io, a fine-tuning service.

How it works

Miners

Run an endpoint (port 7999, registered with Fiber) that returns a GitHub repo and an exact 40-character commit for each tournament type, text or image. The repo needs the required Dockerfiles, a licence and no bundled data or models. The entry fee is burned; one entry counts per coldkey, IP and GitHub account, and duplicates are screened in three tiers. Winning repos are republished, so submit only code you will open-source.

Validators

Validators build each repo, train it on managed GPUs and evaluate it. Text: a group round, pairwise knockouts, then a six-task boss round against the champion. On most tasks the challenger must win 55% of decided samples and lead by 0.01 nats or 1% of the champion's loss, and it needs five of six tasks. Image: three small tasks, a knockout, then six tasks at a 1% margin. Tournaments should end by Friday 14:00 UTC, after the team reviews the winner's code.

Incentive

Text is guaranteed 0.50 of weight and image 0.30; the other 0.20 follows recent entrant counts. Inside a type the top three ranks get about 76, 19 and 5%. A champion winning by over 0.10 earns a boost (the code marks its interaction with the minimums undecided). Nothing is burned by design. On chain (2026-10-30) 6 uids earn and 13 validators weight uid 148 first.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/miner.md in the repo.
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 56, register with Fiber and post your miner IP an hour before Monday's start (text 11:00 UTC, image 13:00 UTC).
  3. Build a training repo with the required Dockerfiles and licence and pin a full 40-character commit.
  4. Hold TAO for the entry fee (text 0.7, image 0.4); it is burned.
  5. Follow #gradients: the text overhaul may change tasks, fees and margins.

Caveats

  • The text overhaul was announced for about four weeks after 2026-10-08 and has no date since; rules and fees may change.
  • Mechanism numbers come from the public repo (2026-10-28) and docs; what validators run shows only as consensus on chain.
  • Uids are not mapped to winners; the drop from 9 to 6 earners on 2026-10-24 fits the end of environment payouts but is not proven.
  • Usage and revenue are not published; the fees endpoint on api.gradients.io returned HTTP 500 on 2026-10-30.
