What it is

Zils sells small decision models through an API (open sign-up since 2026-11-09). Miners are paid when their published models improve a nightly 'soup' in one of four pools (lite, mini, flash, z3). Until 2026-11-03 the same owner ran Astrid, a trading contest subnet.

How it works

Miners

A miner fine-tunes a pinned Qwen model (0.8B for lite up to 27B for z3), publishes it on Hugging Face and commits its hash on chain; one hotkey per pool.

Validators

Each day after a pool's deadline a validator downloads the committed bodies, scores them on a private exam bank and on Zils Outcomes, and averages up to five that improve the pool. Seats and crowns use the last three rounds.

Incentive

Lite and mini get 25% of emissions each, flash and z3 15% each, customer-job miners 20% (burns until customers pay). Per pool: five seats of 16% and a 20% crown; unearned shares burn on the owner's uid. On 11-28 the paid seats are mini 4%, lite 1% (a scout seat), z3 2.4% and two flash seats of 2.4%; crowns paid on 11-23 to 11-26 have ended.

Getting started

  1. Check your GPU (BF16 NVIDIA, 12 GiB free); install the miner with the zils.ai installer (Linux or WSL 2, Git, uv).
  2. Register a hotkey on subnet 127 (burn about 0.0005 TAO on 2026-11-28); use one hotkey per pool.
  3. Soup: train or merge a body, publish it on Hugging Face, commit it before the deadline. Customer jobs: qualify the host, run the worker.

Caveats

  • Why the Astrid owner moved to Zils, and what happens to the unpaid Astrid prizes, is unknown.
  • Seats change hands every day or two and no reason is given; the 0.5 floor is about 1.5 standard errors, so pay can come and go.
  • The exam is private and no hotkey serves traffic yet, so pay rests on the team's own scoring.
  • The docs lag the repo; this page follows the changelog, the code and the chain. Chat after 2026-11-02 was unreadable.
