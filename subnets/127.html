What it is

Zils sells small decision models through an API (early access). Miners are paid when their published models improve one of four daily 'soup' pools; two pools are open. Until 2026-11-03 the same owner ran Astrid, a trading contest subnet.

How it works

Miners

A miner fine-tunes or merges one of four pinned Qwen-based models, publishes it on Hugging Face with a file naming their hotkey and commits its hash on chain: one commitment per hotkey, a separate hotkey per pool.

Validators

Each day after a pool's deadline a validator downloads the committed bodies, scores them on a private exam bank and on Zils Outcomes (real-outcome labels), and averages up to five that improve the pool.

Incentive

Four pools (0.8B, 4.5B, 9B, 27B) get 25% each; only the first two are open. Per pool: five seats of 16% and a 20% crown (4% and 5% of the miner side); unearned shares burn on the owner's uid; a scout (a public model re-uploaded) earns a quarter seat.

Getting started

  1. Check your GPU: BF16 NVIDIA with about 12 GiB free for the mini pool.
  2. Install the miner with the installer on zils.ai (Linux or WSL 2, Git, uv).
  3. Register a hotkey on subnet 127 (burn 0.0005 TAO on 2026-11-07); use one hotkey per pool.
  4. Train or merge a body, publish it on Hugging Face with the ownership file and commit it before the round deadline.

Caveats

  • Why the Astrid owner moved to Zils, and what happens to the unpaid Astrid prizes, is unknown.
  • Why pay fell from three miners to one in a day is not stated; the only paid uid (193) shares a coldkey with two more.
  • The exam is private and no traffic reaches miners yet, so pay rests on the team's own scoring.
  • The docs lag the repo; this page follows the changelog and the chain. Chat after 2026-11-02 was unreadable (archive down).
