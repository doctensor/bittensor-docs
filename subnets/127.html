What it is

Zils sells small decision models through an API (open sign-up since 2026-11-09). Miners are paid when their published models improve a daily 'soup' pool (two of four are open). Until 2026-11-03 the same owner ran Astrid, a trading contest subnet.

How it works

Miners

A miner fine-tunes or merges a pinned Qwen-based model, publishes it on Hugging Face and commits its hash on chain; one hotkey per pool.

Validators

Each day after a pool's deadline a validator downloads the committed bodies, scores them on a private exam bank and on Zils Outcomes, and averages up to five that improve the pool.

Incentive

Lite and mini get 25% of emissions each, flash and z3 15% each (closed: they burn), customer-job miners 20% (burns until customers pay). Per pool: five seats of 16% and a 20% crown (a seat is 4% of the miner side, a crown 5%); unearned shares burn on the owner's uid. A seat needs 0.8 points; since 11-13 the code credits a fine-tune with what it adds over its base (docs still show a 1.2-point gate).

Getting started

  1. Check your GPU (BF16 NVIDIA, 12 GiB free); install the miner with the zils.ai installer (Linux or WSL 2, Git, uv).
  2. Register a hotkey on subnet 127 (burn about 0.0005 TAO on 2026-11-13); use one hotkey per pool.
  3. Soup: train or merge a body, publish it on Hugging Face, commit it before the deadline. Customer jobs: qualify the host, run the worker.

Caveats

  • Why the Astrid owner moved to Zils, and what happens to the unpaid Astrid prizes, is unknown.
  • Pay moved three times in three days; the site marks the seats 'unpaid' without a reason. PR 242 landed after uid 205 lost pay, so it does not explain that.
  • The exam is private and no traffic reaches miners yet, so pay rests on the team's own scoring. Uid 205's coldkey holds 38 uids.
  • The docs lag the repo; this page follows the changelog, the code and the chain. Chat after 2026-11-02 was unreadable.
