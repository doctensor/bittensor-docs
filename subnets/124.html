What it is

Swarm is an open benchmark for drone-flying AI, run by a team building a physical drone lab. Miners train flight models in simulation and submit them privately; validators fly them through generated worlds.

How it works

Miners

A miner submits a flight policy with the swarm command-line tool: its hash goes on chain, the model to the team's private vault. One hotkey gets one model, in one mission, for good.

Validators

Validators fly seeds from the team's backend in Docker and send scores back; the backend decides the champion list and each validator turns it into weights. Only whitelisted coldkeys get evaluation work (runbook).

Incentive

Each mission's slice (Sentinel 95%, Test Field 5%) pays its latest champions (ten in Sentinel, three in Test Field), at 0.7 to the power of rank plus up to 30% for closing the gap to a perfect score. Chain split since 2026-11-10: 18% owner, 49% validators, 33% miners.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner guide and the Sentinel guide.
  2. Install the repository on Ubuntu (Python 3.11); copy your mission's starter agent.
  3. Test locally with swarm model test; epoch seeds are secret.
  4. Register a fresh hotkey on subnet 124 (0.5 TAO burn, the floor).
  5. Run swarm model submit. The slot locks after evaluation; expect a queue.
  6. Validators: ask the team for backend access and approval of your coldkey.

Caveats

  • The chain shows what is paid, not that scoring is fair: the champion list comes from a closed backend.
  • About 42% of emission goes to the owner side (18% cut plus its validator's dividends; our arithmetic). The split moved toward validators on 2026-11-10; who set it is unread.
  • Queue speed and compute figures are the team's words (read 2026-10-31); the feed no longer shows upload or score times.
  • UID 0 is the burn address in code but the owner's own slot on chain; burned weight is untested.
  • Revenue, funding and client claims are the team's own. The Q&A bot is not team policy.
