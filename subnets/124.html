What it is

Swarm is an open benchmark for the AI that flies drones, run by a team that is also building a physical drone lab. Miners train a flight model in simulation for one mission and submit it privately; validators fly it through generated worlds. Each mission has its own champions and a fixed emission share.

How it works

Miners

A miner builds a flight policy for one mission and submits it with the swarm command-line tool, which commits its hash on chain and uploads it to the team's private vault. One hotkey gets one model in one mission for good. A new model runs the full benchmark (1,000 seeds; 250 in Sentinel). Winners are published to an open champions repository.

Validators

Validators run each model in Docker, take seeds from the team's backend and send scores back, then recompute weights from the backend's champion list with the open formula. The runbook says only whitelisted coldkeys get evaluation work; the others mirror weights.

Incentive

Emissions split by mission: Office Interceptor 30%, Swarm Autopilot 20, Swarm SAR 20, Autopilot 15, SAR 15. Inside a mission the last five champions are paid, each at 0.7 to the power of its rank, plus a bonus of up to 30% for how much of the gap to a perfect score it closed. A mission with no payable champion burns its share. On chain: 18% owner, 41 validators, 41 miners.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Trains and submits a drone flight model for one mission.

  • A GPU helps for training; the repo says it is not required.
  • One hotkey, one mission, one model, ever; a retry needs a new hotkey and burn.
  • Sentinel containers get 6 GB RAM and 2 CPUs (branch docs).

Validator High barrier

Runs the team's evaluation containers and sets weights; in practice open only to team-approved operators.

  • The repo compute file lists less (3 cores, 16 GB); the guide says 12 and 48.
  • The owner's validator earns 74.5% of validator dividends; one other permit holder has set no weights for about 32 days.

Swarm Sentinel (launch due 2026-10-29)

From its branch docs (not merged on 2026-10-28; may change).

  • A drone leaves its dock, patrols a simulated solar park day and night, reports every person inside the fence and lands back.
  • Seed score: 70% detection, 20% coverage, 10% flight; each miss or false alarm zeroes 10 of a model's best 250 seeds.
  • Pays the last ten champions from 65% of emissions; Swarm SAR (20%) and SAR (15%) stay.
  • Until it has a first champion its 65% burns, if deployed as written.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner guide and the guide for your mission (docs/families); Sentinel's is on its branch until launch.
  2. Install the repository on Ubuntu (Python 3.11) and copy the starter agent for your mission.
  3. Test locally with swarm model test and the mission's family id; local scores match validators but epoch seeds are secret.
  4. Register a fresh hotkey on subnet 124 (burn 0.5 to 0.6 TAO on 2026-10-28).
  5. Run swarm model submit. The slot locks after evaluation, so treat it as final.
  6. Validators: ask the team on Discord for backend access; a whitelisted coldkey is also required.

Caveats

  • The chain shows what is paid, not that scoring is fair: the champion list comes from the team's closed backend.
  • About 49% of emission goes to the owner side (18% owner cut plus its validator's dividends; our arithmetic).
  • Sentinel is a design until launch: its branch was not merged on 2026-10-28.
  • Office Interceptor was called solved on 2026-10-07 yet still pays; it closes 2026-10-29.
  • UID 0 is the burn address in code but the owner's own slot on chain; burned weight is untested.
  • Revenue, funding and 'little owner alpha sold' are team claims. The Q&A bot is not team policy.
