What it is

Swarm is an open benchmark for drone-flying AI, run by a team building a physical drone lab. Miners train a flight model in simulation and submit it privately; validators fly it through generated worlds. Each mission has its own champions and emission share; since 2026-10-29 the main one is Sentinel.

How it works

Miners

A miner builds a flight policy for one mission and submits it with the swarm command-line tool, which commits its hash on chain and uploads the model to the team's private vault. One hotkey gets one model, in one mission, for good. Models wait in a queue, then run the full benchmark (2,000 seeds in Sentinel, 1,000 elsewhere).

Validators

Validators claim seeds for one model at a time from a shared pool on the team's backend, fly them in Docker and send scores back. The backend decides the champion list; each validator recomputes weights from it with the open formula. Only whitelisted coldkeys get evaluation work (runbook); the others mirror weights.

Incentive

Each mission's share pays its last five champions (ten in Sentinel), at 0.7 to the power of rank plus up to 30% for how much of the gap to a perfect score they closed. A mission with no champion burns its share. Chain split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Trains and submits a drone flight model for one mission.

  • One hotkey, one mission, one model, ever; a retry needs a new hotkey and burn.
  • Sentinel container: 6 GB RAM, 2 CPUs (guide).
  • Models wait in a queue: the newest champion took 65 h from upload to score (API).

Validator High barrier

Runs the team's evaluation containers and sets weights; in practice open only to approved operators.

  • 12 permits, 10 with weights (2026-11-07); the owner's validator earns 62.0% of dividends; two holders set none for over 5 tempos.

Swarm Sentinel (live since 2026-10-29)

  • A drone patrols a simulated solar park day and night, reports every intruder and lands back in its dock.
  • Seed score: 70% detection, 20% coverage, 10% flight; each miss or false alarm zeroes 5 of a model's best seeds.
  • Eight champions are crowned (best score 0.923); their models stay private. A screening phase exists in code but is off.
  • Champions 235 and 49 share a coldkey with six more UIDs; UID 111's holds two fresh ones: likely one participant per coldkey, several models (our inference).

Getting started

  1. Read the miner guide and the Sentinel guide.
  2. Install the repository on Ubuntu (Python 3.11); copy your mission's starter agent.
  3. Test locally with swarm model test; epoch seeds are secret.
  4. Register a fresh hotkey on subnet 124 (burn 0.5 TAO on 2026-11-07).
  5. Run swarm model submit. The slot locks after evaluation; expect a queue.
  6. Validators: ask the team for backend access; an approved coldkey is also required.

Caveats

  • The chain shows what is paid, not that scoring is fair: the champion list comes from a closed backend.
  • About 43% of emission goes to the owner side (18% owner cut plus its validator's dividends; our arithmetic).
  • Queue speed and compute figures are the team's words (last read 2026-10-31); queue order is unverified.
  • UID 0 is the burn address in code but the owner's own slot on chain; burned weight is untested.
  • Revenue, funding and client claims are the team's own. The Q&A bot is not team policy.
