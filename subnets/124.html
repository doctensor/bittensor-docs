What it is

Swarm is an open benchmark for drone-flying AI, run by a team building a physical drone lab. Miners train flight models in simulation and submit them privately; validators fly them through generated worlds.

How it works

Miners

A miner submits a flight policy with the swarm command-line tool: its hash goes on chain, the model to the team's private vault. One hotkey gets one model, in one mission, for good.

Validators

Validators fly the team backend's seeds in Docker and report scores; the backend picks champions and validators turn the list into weights.

Incentive

Each mission's slice (docs: Sentinel 95%, Test Field 5%) pays its latest champions, weighted by rank and score gain. Chain split since 11-10: 18% owner, 49% validators, 33% miners.

Swarm Sentinel (live since 2026-10-29)

  • A drone patrols a simulated solar park, reports every intruder and lands in its dock.
  • Seed score: 70% detection, 20% coverage, 10% flight; each miss or false alarm zeroes 5 best seeds.
  • Seventeen champions crowned, ten paid. UIDs 187, 182, 99 and 198, registered 11-23, began earning on 11-24 and 11-25; UID 169 (score 0.989) fell from 30.9% to 7.5%.
  • Release 6.1.0 (11-22) made thieves harder and restarted benchmark labels at 1.0.0 without re-evaluation. Newcomers score 0.44 to 0.86, older champions 0.92 to 0.99.

Swarm Test Field (added 2026-11-17)

  • A drone flies the company's real test field in Andorra and reports where each person stands. Models are published.
  • Three crowns on 11-18 (UIDs 1, 164, 188); the team dropped UID 188's crown by hand and kept UID 164 as solver.
  • UID 164 earned 5% until late 11-21; the share then sat on the owner slot (UID 0) until 11-23. No UID earns it since 11-24. Docs: a solved mission stops paying after a victory window.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner guide and the Sentinel guide.
  2. Install the repository on Ubuntu (Python 3.11); copy your mission's starter agent.
  3. Test locally with swarm model test; epoch seeds are secret.
  4. Register a fresh hotkey on subnet 124 (0.5 TAO burn, the floor).
  5. Run swarm model submit. The slot locks after evaluation; expect a queue.
  6. Validators: ask the team for backend access and approval of your coldkey.

Caveats

  • The chain shows what is paid, not that scoring is fair: the champion list comes from a closed backend.
  • About 43% of emission goes to the owner side (18% cut plus its validator's dividends; our arithmetic).
  • Queue speed and compute figures are the team's words (read 2026-10-31); the feed no longer shows upload or score times.
  • Where the Test Field 5% went is untraced.
  • Revenue, funding and client claims are the team's own. The Q&A bot is not team policy.
