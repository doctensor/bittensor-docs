What it is

Endure (SN30) is a risk-intelligence subnet. Miners forecast four risk figures (worst drop, volatility, average price, pool liquidity) for 15 subnets' alpha tokens over 5 and 30 days; validators score them against real outcomes. The owner currently burns 98% of miner reward, so the other 53 paid miners share about 2%.

How it works

Miners

Each day a miner commits a hash of its forecast bundle, then reveals the bundle and a nonce later in the day (commit 11:00 to 19:30 UTC, reveal 20:30 to 00:00). The bundle is 15 subnets x 4 outputs x 2 horizons = 120 forecasts. Forecasts identical to another hotkey's share one hotkey's weight.

Validators

A validator freezes the subnet list, takes commits and reveals, and resolves the real values from archive data once each horizon ends (about 6 or 31 days). A forecast scores 1 inside a grace band and falls linearly to 0 at a cutoff; understating risk reaches 0 three times sooner than overstating it. Skipped forecasts score 0. Scores feed a 5-round half-life average.

Incentive

Weight is the blended score cubed. Each validator gives the owner's uid the owner's burn share (9800 of 10,000 on the team validator's status page, 2026-10-30) and splits the rest by earned weight. On chain the owner's uid holds 98.06% of incentive, so non-owner miners share about 1.9%. The team dashboard showed no scored 30-day cell on 2026-10-30.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Sends daily forecasts for 15 subnets and is scored on real outcomes.

  • Pay is small by design: about 0.0036 TAO a day per paid miner, gross (2026-10-30).
  • A non-archive endpoint makes the neuron refuse to start (runbook).

Validator Medium barrier

Resolves outcomes from archive data, scores forecasts, sets weights and serves a signed feed.

  • 11 hotkeys hold permits; all rank the owner's uid first (2026-10-30).
  • Docs list two independent validators as an open requirement.

Product and consumers

  • Forge, a TAO lending market, is the first intended consumer: testnet now, mainnet planned soon (team, 2026-10-27).
  • Endure itself does not lend, hold collateral or liquidate (README).
  • Team dashboard (2026-10-30): 27 scored 5-day rounds average 0.936 out of 1; no scored 30-day cells yet.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/running_on_mainnet.md and docs/mining.md or validating.md; the miner guide still says testnet.
  2. Get your own mainnet archive RPC: the public default is rate-limited and a non-archive endpoint is refused.
  3. Register a hotkey on netuid 30 (burn 0.999999999 TAO, 64 of 96 slots used on 2026-10-30).
  4. Run the prod image (ghcr.io/endure-network/endure-subnet-miner or -validator) with --endure.serving_stage mainnet.
  5. Miners: build your own forecasts and keep the state folder; check the validator's /health first.

Caveats

  • The 98% burn rate is the team validator's own report, matching the chain's 98.06%; the on-chain commitment was not read.
  • Accuracy, usage and Forge plans come from the team's dashboard, API and X posts; none is visible on chain.
  • Only the team's validator is confirmed on v0.1.3; the other 10 permit holders are unidentified.
  • Docs call the economics experimental and the README still describes a testnet soak; scored 30-day results are untested.
  • A Synthdata (SN50) partnership was announced 2026-06-12 as in development; no code confirms it.
