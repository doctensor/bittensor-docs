What it is

Endure (SN30) is a risk-intelligence subnet. Miners forecast four risk figures for 15 subnets' alpha tokens over 5 and 30 days; validators score them against real outcomes. The owner currently burns 98% of miner reward, so the other 53 paid miners share about 2%.

How it works

Miners

Each day a miner commits a hash of its forecast bundle (11:00 to 19:30 UTC), then reveals it with a nonce (20:30 to 00:00). The bundle is 15 subnets x 4 outputs x 2 horizons = 120 forecasts. Identical forecasts share one hotkey's weight.

Validators

A validator freezes the subnet list, takes commits and reveals, and resolves the real values from archive data after each horizon (about 6 or 31 days). A forecast scores 1 inside a grace band and falls linearly to 0 at a cutoff, three times sooner for understated risk. Skipped forecasts score 0; scores feed a 5-round half-life average.

Incentive

Weight is the blended score cubed (a plain mean of all forecast averages, 30-day ones included; v0.1.4 will weight them by maturity). Each validator gives the owner's uid the owner's burn share (9800 of 10,000 on the team validator, 2026-11-14) and splits the rest by earned weight. On chain the owner's uid holds 98.09% of incentive; non-owner miners share about 1.9%.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Sends daily forecasts for 15 subnets and is scored on real outcomes.

  • Pay is small: about 0.0035 TAO a day per paid miner, gross (2026-11-14).
  • From v0.1.4 (not live yet) a hotkey without a 30-day record earns about half of an equally accurate miner until about day 30 to 60.

Validator Medium barrier

Resolves outcomes from archive data, scores forecasts, sets weights and serves a signed feed.

  • 11 hotkeys hold permits; all set weights within the last tempo (2026-11-14).
  • Docs list two independent validators as an open requirement.

Product and consumers

  • Forge, a TAO lending market on testnet, is the first intended consumer; its risk limits start manual, with Endure's feed the long-term aim.
  • Alpha buybacks: Endure's site says 60% of Forge revenue, Forge's docs say over 50% is intended; the audit is 'planned' (team, 2026-11-02).
  • Docs roadmap: six undated phases; Endure is in Phase 2 (hardening).

Getting started

  1. Read docs/running_on_mainnet.md and docs/mining.md or validating.md; the miner guide still says testnet.
  2. Get your own mainnet archive RPC: the public default is rate-limited and a non-archive endpoint is refused.
  3. Register a hotkey on netuid 30 (burn 0.999999999 TAO, 64 of 96 slots used on 2026-11-14).
  4. Run the prod image (ghcr.io/endure-network/endure-subnet-miner or -validator) with --endure.serving_stage mainnet; it is v0.1.3 until the key 2045 cutover.
  5. Miners: build your own forecasts; check /health first.

Caveats

  • The 98% burn rate is the team validator's own report, matching the chain's 98.09%; the on-chain commitment was not read.
  • Accuracy, usage and Forge plans come from team sources; none is visible on chain.
