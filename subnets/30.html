What it is

Endure (SN30) is a risk-intelligence subnet. Miners forecast four risk figures for 15 subnets' alpha tokens over 5 and 30 days; validators score them against real outcomes. The owner burns 98% of miner reward.

How it works

Miners

Each day a miner commits a hash of its forecast bundle (11:00 to 19:30 UTC), then reveals it with a nonce (20:30 to 00:00). The bundle is 15 subnets x 4 outputs x 2 horizons = 120 forecasts. Identical forecasts share one hotkey's weight.

Validators

A validator freezes the subnet list and resolves the real values from archive data after each horizon (about 6 or 31 days). A forecast scores 1 inside a grace band and falls linearly to 0 at a cutoff, three times sooner for understated risk. Skipped forecasts score 0; scores feed a 5-round half-life average.

Incentive

Weight is the blended score cubed. Since v0.1.4, a forecast's 5-day and 30-day averages are added and divided by 1 plus the 30-day record's maturity; up to 20% of the 5-day score is held back until that record matures. Each validator gives the owner's uid its burn share (9800 of 10,000 on the team validator) and splits the rest by earned weight. On chain the owner's uid holds 98.06% of incentive.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Sends daily forecasts for 15 subnets and is scored on real outcomes.

  • Pay is small: about 0.004 TAO a day per paid miner, gross (2026-11-27).
  • v0.1.4: a hotkey with no 30-day record earns about half of an equally accurate miner until about day 30 to 60; key-2044 peers are rejected.

Validator Medium barrier

Scores forecasts against archive data, sets weights, serves a signed feed.

  • 11 hotkeys hold permits, all setting weights; uid 27 (not owner-linked) takes 40.9% of dividends, the owner's uid 48 35% (2026-11-27).
  • Docs list two independent validators as an open requirement; use your own keyed archive, the public one throttles.

Product and consumers

  • Forge, a TAO lending market on testnet, is the first intended consumer; its risk limits start manual, with Endure's feed the long-term aim.
  • Alpha buybacks: Endure's site says 60% of Forge revenue, Forge's docs over 50%; audit 'planned' (team, 2026-11-02).

Getting started

  1. Read docs/running_on_mainnet.md and docs/mining.md or validating.md; the miner guide still says testnet (netuid 504), mainnet is 30.
  2. Get your own mainnet archive RPC: the public default is rate-limited and a non-archive endpoint is refused.
  3. Register a hotkey on netuid 30 (burn 0.999999999 TAO, 64 of 96 slots used on 2026-11-27).
  4. Run the prod image (ghcr.io/endure-network/endure-subnet-miner or -validator, v0.1.4, key 2045) with --endure.serving_stage mainnet.
  5. Miners: build your own forecasts; a validator's /health shows its key.
