What it is

Ditto pays miners for open-source memory for AI agents. Agent miners upload a Docker harness that DittoBench scores; generator miners upload programs that write its test cases. The team sells a Ditto assistant.

How it works

Miners

Agents upload a Docker memory-and-tools harness; generators upload a Go program that makes test packs. Each upload costs 0.075 TAO. A leader's source goes public after 48 hours; building on it is allowed, an unchanged copy is not.

Validators

Screening is build and safety checks only (source review ended 2026-11-07). Validators run agents offline with one locked model (gpt-oss-20b), grade without an LLM judge and set weights from the platform's ledger.

Incentive

Pay follows DittoBench v18 (v19 is staged; the docs still say v15). Nothing is burned; all 12 validators with weights put uid 245 first.

Roles and requirements

Miner (agent) Low barrier

Builds a memory-and-tools harness and competes on DittoBench.

  • Practice needs Rust 1.85+, Go 1.23+ and Ollama; uploading needs Python 3.12+ and uv.
  • Scored runs: 8 cases at once, no network; v18 stops a run at $1.00 per 350 cases.
  • The five paid agent uids registered between 2026-09-13 and 2026-11-12; the crown holder, uid 245, on 2026-11-12.

Miner (generator) Low barrier

Writes a Go program that generates benchmark test packs.

  • Output must be deterministic; 50% or more overlap with another owner's output is rejected.
  • 31 generators scored in 7 days; the five paid (0.68 to 0.23) sit on five coldkeys; the ledger pays one entry per owner.

Validator Medium barrier

Runs agents and generator rounds; sets weights from the ledger.

  • Smaller hosts: 80 GB disk and 16 GB RAM for 4 slots; a 64 GiB host runs 7 of 8.

How the contests score

  • Agents: the crown holder keeps the 50% slot unless a challenger wins three confirming duels; the top five split 50/20/13/10/7% of 70%. Uid 245 holds the crown at 0.754; uids 149 (0.782) and 137 (0.772) have 0 of 3 confirmations. Uids 243 (0.843) and 246 (0.783) score higher but are not eligible.
  • Bench v18 is live, v19 staged. A score expires after two seasons (about 9.6 hours) until re-tested. Readmission, a fee to re-run an agent, starts with v19 and a 7-day notice; none is announced.
  • Generators: a round every four epochs (about 4.8 hours); rounds 26146 to 26150 scored, 26151 running. The chain pays the board's top five.

Getting started

  1. Agents: work in miners/dittobench-starter-kit, rehearse with scripts/local-rehearsal.py, then ditto verify, upload and status.
  2. Generators: start from miners/dittobench-generator-starter-kit and pass the offline qualification.
  3. Validators: follow docs/VALIDATOR.md with a hotkey that holds a validator permit.

Caveats

  • Rules change often (14 releases since 2026-11-16, two new features inactive); the docs lag.
  • Open: whether uid 149 or 137 takes the crown, why uids 243 and 246 are not eligible, and why board and chain order agent seats 4 and 5 differently.
  • Members allege hard-coded benchmark phrases in top agents and a team-linked validating miner (the team denies it; unverified).
  • Unknown: usage, revenue, and what 7,000 alpha of manual transfers fund.
