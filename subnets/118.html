What it is

Ditto pays miners for open-source memory for AI agents. Agent miners upload a Docker harness that DittoBench scores; generator miners upload programs that write its test cases. The team also sells a Ditto assistant (Free, $19, $97 plans).

How it works

Miners

Agents upload a Docker memory-and-tools harness; generators upload a Go program that makes test packs. Each upload costs 0.1 TAO (one-hour cooldown). A leader's source goes public after 48 hours; building on it is allowed, an unchanged copy is not.

Validators

A team-run screener reviews each agent; an inconclusive review waits for a person. Validators run agents offline with one locked model (gpt-oss-20b), grade without an LLM judge, run generator rounds and set weights from the platform's ledger.

Incentive

Pay follows DittoBench v16; v17 takes over once its five priority agents hold full v17 scores. Nothing is burned; all 11 validators with weights follow the platform ledger.

Roles and requirements

Miner (agent) Low barrier

Builds a memory-and-tools harness and competes on DittoBench.

  • Practice needs Rust 1.85+, Go 1.23+ and Ollama; uploading needs Python 3.12+ and uv.
  • Scored runs: 8 cases at once, no network.

Miner (generator) Low barrier

Writes a Go program that generates benchmark test packs.

  • Output must be deterministic; 50% or more overlap with another owner's output is rejected.
  • 15 generators scored in 7 days; the top five scored 0.34 to 0.45. Paid uid 213 shares a coldkey with 11 other uids.

Validator Medium barrier

Runs agents and generator rounds; sets weights from the ledger.

  • Smaller hosts: 80 GB disk and 16 GB RAM for 4 slots.

How the contests score

  • Agents: the crown holder keeps the 50% slot unless a challenger leads by the required margin over three shared seeds; the top five split 50/20/13/10/7%. The board lists 2 agents and only uid 149 is eligible, so it takes the whole pool.
  • Bench v16 (title duels, priced inference cost) sets pay; v17 takes over when all 5 priority agents hold v17 scores (3 now).
  • Generators: a round every four epochs (about 4.8 hours) against the top five agents; rounds 26081 to 26084 were void (panel of 1, minimum 3). The chain still pays the board's top five 40/25/15/12/8%.

Getting started

  1. Agents: work in miners/dittobench-starter-kit, rehearse with scripts/local-rehearsal.py, then ditto verify, upload and status.
  2. Generators: start from miners/dittobench-generator-starter-kit and pass the offline qualification.
  3. Validators: follow docs/VALIDATOR.md with a hotkey that holds a validator permit.

Caveats

  • The owner sets the burn and split and changed them often; the docs lag the live values.
  • The team runs screening, scoring and the ledger; rulings were reversed often; 23 agents are under review.
  • Unexplained: why the agent board fell from 6 entries to 2 and the generator panel to 1.
  • Members allege hard-coded benchmark phrases in top agents and a team-linked validating miner (the team denies it; unverified).
  • Unknown: usage, revenue, and what the service wallet and 7,000 alpha of manual transfers fund.
