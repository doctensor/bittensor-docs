What it is

Numinous is a graph forecasting subnet. Each day the team publishes 50 questions about companies in a knowledge graph built from the news. Miners send statements about relationships the news will report, such as one company acquiring another; a statement earns credit when the team's judge finds a later news event showing it happened.

How it works

Miners

Miners use the numi tool: read the open round, check what the graph already holds, and send one sealed forecast per question before midnight UTC; sending again before then replaces it. A forecast is a bundle of statements (company, relation, company). Repeating what the graph held counts as wrong; probabilities are not scored.

Validators

Validators run no miner code. They pull closed rounds and opened forecasts with the team's verdicts, check hashes and signatures, compute credits daily at 15:00 UTC and set weights from 16:00 UTC. The team's backend judges; validators check the maths, not the verdicts.

Incentive

A forecast earns its statements judged correct in the last 30 days divided by the square root of their number; one news event pays a miner once. The top 25 miners share 98% of weight by credits squared; 2% is spread by rank over everyone with credits, so ranks 26 and below hold about 0.6%.

Rounds and judging

  • Judged so far (2026-11-07): 591 statements correct, 7,759 not. In every round with a correct statement one company holds at least half: 126 of the 143 in 10-30 are one Marvell question, 35 of the 44 in 10-31 one Salesforce question. Forecasts stay open 30 days (first close 2026-11-22).
  • Why 'not correct': 40% repeat what the graph held, 33% name a standing state, 14% leave a party unnamed, 9% were already public. Only 15 say no news event followed.

Who earns

  • Four miners tie on 4.00 credits at ranks 25 to 28: rank 25 holds 2.07% of weight, ranks 26 to 28 about 0.013% each.
  • The 25 top seats sit on 20 coldkeys (chain): one holds ranks 1, 9 and 25 (14.1% of weight), another four seats (8.8%) with identical results. 61 of the 151 earners share a result with another uid; in 11 of those 17 groups all sit on one coldkey.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner setup guide in the repo (Python 3.11 or newer) and install the numi tool with pip install -e .
  2. Register a hotkey on subnet 6 (0.2 TAO burn on 2026-11-07). Testnet is netuid 155 (--env test).
  3. Set your wallet and hotkey in environment variables, then run numi round to see the open round and deadline.
  4. Use numi entities and numi graph to see what the graph holds, write the forecast JSON, check it with numi validate, send it with numi submit --question <id>.
  5. Follow verdicts with numi forecasts and the public leaderboard.

Caveats

  • Rewards rest on the team's judge; its model and prompts are not published; 15 verdicts were hand-fixed.
  • Only the rounds of 2026-10-28 to 11-02 have paid since 10-27; none of 11-03 to 11-06. A true forecast pays only if the team's pipeline finds the event.
  • The geopolitics graph announced for 2026-10-23 is still 'coming soon'; all questions seen are about large companies; the graph upgrade due the week of 11-02 had not shown by 11-07.
  • Chat about agents is outdated; the miner guide's exit-code table still says the first forecast counts. Chat after 2026-10-31 was unreadable.
