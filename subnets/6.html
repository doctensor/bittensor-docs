What it is

Numinous is a graph forecasting subnet. Each day the team publishes 50 questions about companies in a knowledge graph built from the news. Miners send statements about relationships the news will report, such as one company acquiring another. A statement earns credit when the team's judge finds a news event after the deadline that shows it happened.

How it works

Miners

Miners use the numi tool: read the open round, check what the graph already holds, and send one forecast per question before midnight UTC. A forecast is a bundle of statements (company, relation, company; probability and rationale optional). It is sealed until the deadline, and sending again before then replaces it. Repeating what the graph already held counts as wrong. Probabilities are not scored.

Validators

Validators run no miner code. They pull closed rounds and the opened forecasts with the team's verdicts, check each against its hash and the miner's signature, compute credits every day at 15:00 UTC and set weights from 16:00 UTC. The team's backend does the judging; validators check the maths, not the verdicts.

Incentive

A forecast earns its statements judged correct in the last 30 days divided by the square root of their number; one news event pays a miner once. 98% of weight goes to the top 25 miners by credits squared and 2% by rank to the rest. With no credits anywhere, all weight goes to the owner's uid.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner setup guide in the repo (Python 3.11 or newer) and install the numi tool with pip install -e .
  2. Register a hotkey on subnet 6 (0.2 TAO burn on 2026-10-30). Testnet is netuid 155, used with --env test.
  3. Set your wallet and hotkey in environment variables, then run numi round to see the open round and its deadline.
  4. Use numi entities and numi graph to see what the graph already holds, then write the forecast JSON, check it with numi validate and send it with numi submit --question <id>.
  5. Follow verdicts with numi forecasts and the public leaderboard.

Caveats

  • Rewards rest on the team's judge; its model and prompts are not published. About 5% of 3,383 judged statements are correct and 15 verdicts needed hand fixes.
  • Rounds from 2026-10-26 show no correct statements yet; forecasts stay open 30 days, so later credit is possible.
  • The geopolitics graph announced for 2026-10-23 is still 'coming soon'; all questions seen are about companies.
  • Older chat about agents, sandboxes and the reasoning pool no longer applies. The miner guide's exit-code table still says the first forecast counts.
