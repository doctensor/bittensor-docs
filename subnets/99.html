What it is

Netuid 99 changed hands on 2026-11-06: the earlier project (Thirty Spokes) was removed from the chain and a new owner registered the number five minutes later. The owner started the subnet on 2026-11-11 at 13:22 UTC, after 5.0 idle days, and has set no name, repo, site or docs. One uid, the owner's own, takes all incentive; the miner share burns.

How it works

Miners

Not described: no task, repo or docs exist (2026-11-13). Of 219 slots only uid 0, the owner's own, earns incentive.

Validators

Not described. Uid 0 (the owner's) and uid 1 hold the two validator permits and put all weight on uid 0; uid 1's coldkey is not the owner's and was seen validating on at least seven other subnets.

Incentive

Incentive is 100% on uid 0, the owner's own hotkey; the chain's MinerBurned setting withholds it (41% of subnet emission), so no outside miner is paid. Uid 0 got 83.4% of last-epoch emission (about 4,912 of 5,888 alpha a day), uid 1 16.6%. Split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; unspent incentive is set to Burn.
