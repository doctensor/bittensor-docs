What it is

Thirty Spokes was a model-routing subnet. Miners trained a 'conductor', a fine-tune of Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, that hands agentic tasks to OpenRouter models, and one owner-run validator duelled each challenger against the king. Public results stopped at 2026-10-01. On 2026-11-06 the chain removed its slot; netuid 99 now belongs to a different owner.

How it works

Miners

A miner submits full weights on the pinned architecture (about 70 GB, bf16, no adapters). The 'conductor' picks an OpenRouter model for each agentic task and never writes the answer; worker calls run on the miner's own key.

Validators

One owner-run validator replays the king and each challenger on the same fresh slice of HLE and LiveCodeBench tasks, scoring quality minus a measured price. A challenger needs a paired gain above 0.05 plus three robustness checks. Each window publishes a reveal.

Incentive

Documented: king 85%, five ex-kings 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1%, unfilled slots burn to uid 0. On chain (2026-11-05): 85.0% on uid 25, 15.0% on uid 18, the former king. The owner hotkey earns 86.6% of dividends; owner cut 18%.

Miner High barrier

  • Pinned base Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, bf16; the hotkey must be ed25519; one submission per hotkey, ever.
  • Queue depth is 6; three submissions have waited in the public queue since 2026-09-24.

Validator High barrier

The owner runs the only validator and sets weights.

  • Design: one validator run by the owner (whitepaper D1); outsiders can only check the published reveals.
  • Chain: 4 permits, 3 set weights (owner and two outside hotkeys), all 85% on uid 25.
