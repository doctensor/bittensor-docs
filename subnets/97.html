What it is

Ingot is a contest to compress one open model. Miners submit a 1.75-bit copy of Qwen3.8-27B; the copy whose answers stay closest to the full-precision original is crowned king. The Dendrite team runs all scoring.

How it works

Miners

Miners shrink the reference model into a ternary file (each weight is -1, 0 or +1 with a shared scale). Genesis, the starting file, is Prism ML's Ternary Bonsai 2; an entry is genesis with your own weights, no larger, uploaded privately and published only if it wins.

Validators

The team's validator draws 800 samples per eval (agent runs, reasoning puzzles, science, math), has the reference model answer, then has king and challenger read it and measures each one's drift: KL divergence of next-word probabilities. Identical files are rejected.

Incentive

A challenger becomes king at once if its KL is 3% lower than the king's. The newest five kings, across all rounds, get 20% of miner weights each for five days; a share nobody fills goes to uid 0 and is burned.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Builds a ternary GGUF closer to the original than the king and uploads it for one scored duel.

  • One .gguf, no larger than genesis (7.2 GB); its metadata and 851 tensors stay, ternary codes only.
  • One scored duel per hotkey; a failed validation returns an attempt (3 maximum); a duplicate file spends the hotkey.
  • Many hotkeys mean more tries: the king coldkeys hold 66, 40, 15 and 8 uids, none owner-linked (our reading).
  • Kit (2026-11-06): miner/ternary loads a king in PyTorch, trains it across a node's GPUs and packs a valid file; the team says its loop does not yet improve a king.

Validator High barrier

In practice team-run: it evaluates every entry on its own GPU box and sets weights.

  • Three hotkeys hold permits (owner uid 0 and two outside coldkeys) and set identical five-uid weights; who runs the evaluator is unknown.

Does closer mean better?

Winning means closeness to the original, not better answers. Team feed (2026-11-05, unverified): original, start file, kings XVII to XXVII:

  • Terminal-Bench 2.1: 71.9; 50.6; 46.1 to 58.4. SWE-bench Multilingual: 71.3; 58.0; 55.3 to 63.7. GPQA Diamond: 85.4; 77.8; 74.2 to 78.3.
  • tau3 Banking: 41.2; 28.9; 22.7 to 38.1. MATH-500: 98.0; 96.4; 96.8 to 98.0.
  • XXVII tops eleven kings on Terminal-Bench (58.4) and SWE-bench (63.7), 7.8 and 5.7 over the start file; the rest stay within 7 points of it.

Getting started

  1. Clone the repo; in the miner env template set your keys and netuid 97.
  2. Register with 'ingot register' (burn about 0.05 TAO on 2026-11-07).
  3. Quantize or retrain from the q2 genesis file, keeping its header and tensor layout (miner/ternary can load and pack it).
  4. Run 'ingot check-model --path model.gguf' until every check passes.
  5. Run 'ingot submit-private --path model.gguf'; one scored duel per hotkey (about 45 minutes; queue empty).

Caveats

  • The contest changed four times since 2026-10-16; the team promised notice before q2 ends but gave no date (chat after 2026-11-02 unread).
  • The team scores everything on its own box; outside validators have no guide and three permit holders send identical weights.
  • KL scores of different kings are not comparable: each challenger gets its own 800-sample draw.
  • Benchmarks are the team's own, in two settings since 2026-10-31. No licence file.
