What it is

Ingot is a contest to compress one open model. Miners submit a 1.75-bit copy of Qwen3.8-27B; the copy whose answers stay closest to the full-precision original is crowned king and published on Hugging Face. The Dendrite team runs all scoring.

How it works

Miners

Miners shrink the reference model into a ternary file (each weight is -1, 0 or +1 with a shared scale). Genesis, the starting file, is Prism ML's Ternary Bonsai 2. A valid entry is genesis with your own weight values: one GGUF, no larger than genesis, uploaded privately and published only if it wins.

Validators

The team's validator draws 800 samples per eval (terminal and software-engineering agent runs, tau3, science, math), has the reference model answer, then has the king and challenger read that answer. It measures each one's drift: KL divergence of next-word probabilities, with hidden-state distance as a guard. Identical files are rejected.

Incentive

A challenger becomes king at once if its KL is 1.5% lower than the king's and its distance is no worse. The newest five kings, across all rounds, get 20% of miner weights each for five days; an unfilled share goes to uid 0 and is burned (one share from 2026-10-31 23:49Z, when XIII's days end, until a newer king arrives).

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Builds a ternary GGUF closer to the original than the king and uploads it for one scored duel.

  • One .gguf, at most 8 GB and no larger than genesis (7.2 GB); genesis metadata and 851 tensors stay, ternary codes only.
  • 'ingot check-model' runs every check locally.
  • One scored submission per hotkey; a failed validation returns an attempt (3 maximum); an identical-file duplicate spends the hotkey.

Validator High barrier

In practice team-run: it evaluates every entry on its own GPU box and sets weights.

  • Four hotkeys held validator permits on 2026-10-31; three set the same five-uid weights, one being the owner's uid 0. Whether the others run the evaluator cannot be told.

Does closer mean better?

Winning means closeness to the original, not better answers. The team's own benchmark feed (2026-10-31, unverified):

  • GPQA Diamond: original 81.3; q2 genesis 69.7 and 75.8 in two runs; q2 kings 70.2 to 77.3 (XVII 77.8, new setting).
  • Terminal-Bench 2.1: original 57.3 and 64.0; genesis 46.1 and 52.8; kings 41.6 to 48.3 (XVII 56.2, Bonsai 2 50.6, new setting).
  • Repeat runs of one file differ by about 4.8 Terminal-Bench points, as much as most king-to-genesis gaps.

Getting started

  1. Clone the repo; in the miner env template set your keys and netuid 97.
  2. Register with 'ingot register' (burn about 0.098 TAO on 2026-10-31).
  3. Quantize or retrain from the q2 genesis file, keeping its header and tensor layout.
  4. Run 'ingot check-model --path model.gguf' until every check passes.
  5. Run 'ingot submit-private --path model.gguf'; you get one scored duel per hotkey.

Caveats

  • The contest changed three times in 15 days; the team promises notice before q2 ends but gives no date.
  • The team scores everything on its own box; outside validators have no guide and three permit holders send identical weights.
  • Sample sets changed on 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-30: KL values of earlier and later kings are not comparable.
  • Benchmarks use two settings since 2026-10-31; only the newest king and baselines were re-run under the new one. The repo has no licence file.
