What it is

Ingot is a contest to compress one open model. Miners submit a 1.75-bit copy of Qwen3.8-27B; the copy whose answers stay closest to the full-precision original is crowned king. The Dendrite team runs all scoring.

How it works

Miners

Miners shrink the reference model into a ternary file. An entry is the genesis file (Prism ML's Ternary Bonsai 2) with your own weights, no larger, uploaded privately and published only if it wins.

Validators

The team's validator draws 800 samples per eval, has the reference model answer, then measures how far king and challenger drift from it: KL divergence of next-word probabilities, every answer token weighing the same within its domain (since 11-09). Identical files are rejected.

Incentive

A challenger becomes king at once if its KL is 2% lower than the king's. The newest five kings, across all rounds, get 20% of miner weights each for five days; a share nobody fills goes to uid 0 and is burned.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Builds a ternary GGUF closer to the original than the king and uploads it for one scored duel.

  • One .gguf, no larger than genesis (7.2 GB); its metadata and 851 tensors stay, ternary codes only.
  • One scored duel per hotkey; a failed validation returns an attempt (3 maximum); a duplicate file spends the hotkey.
  • Many hotkeys mean more tries: the five paid kings' three coldkeys hold 63, 16 and 6 uids, none owner-linked (our reading).

Validator High barrier

In practice team-run: it evaluates every entry on its own GPU box and sets weights.

  • Three hotkeys hold permits (uid 0 and two outside coldkeys). All three set the same five-king row; who runs the evaluator is unknown.

Does closer mean better?

Winning means closeness to the original, not better answers. Team feed (2026-11-14, unverified): original, start file, kings XVII to XXXVII:

  • Terminal-Bench 2.1: 71.9; 50.6; 46.1 to 59.6. SWE-bench Multilingual: 71.3; 58.0; 55.3 to 63.7. GPQA Diamond: 85.4; 77.8; 74.2 to 78.8.
  • tau3 Banking: 41.2; 28.9; 22.7 to 38.1. MATH-500: 98.0; 96.4; 96.8 to 98.6 (XXVIII and XXIX are above the original).
  • XXIX tops Terminal-Bench (59.6), XXVII SWE-bench (63.7). Repeat runs of one file differ by 4.8 points on Terminal-Bench, 2.4 on GPQA and 2.0 on tau3 (other sampling setting): small gaps between kings are noise. XXXIII, XXXVI and XXXVII have runs still open.

Getting started

  1. Clone the repo; in the miner env template set your keys and netuid 97.
  2. Register with 'ingot register' (burn 0.05-0.07 TAO on 2026-11-14).
  3. Quantize or retrain from the q2 genesis file, keeping its header and tensor layout.
  4. Run 'ingot check-model --path model.gguf' until every check passes.
  5. Run 'ingot submit-private --path model.gguf': one scored duel per hotkey, about 45 minutes.

Caveats

  • The contest changed five times since 2026-10-16; the team promised notice before q2 ends but gave no date (chat after 2026-11-02 unread).
  • The team scores everything on its own box.
  • KL scores of different kings are not comparable: each challenger gets its own 800-sample draw, and scores before 11-09 weighted samples differently.
  • Benchmarks are the team's own. No licence file.
