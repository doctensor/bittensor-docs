What it is

blockmachine sells JSON-RPC access to blockchains, including archive nodes. A gateway routes each customer request to a competing miner's node and bills only for successful answers. The team behind Taostats runs it and is its largest named customer (its own claim).

How it works

Miners

A miner runs an archive (full history) or full (recent blocks) node for a chain behind the team's gateway, registered with the bm tool at a fixed bid of $0.50 per million compute units (CU, request work). Traffic follows a routing score, pay follows CU served. Checks cover chain, client family, tip distance and archive depth; wrong answers exclude the UID.

Validators

Validators read each epoch's gateway logs and CU files, cross-check answers and set weights from each miner's CU. The weight code is public but is only the last step: ranking and routing come from the team's closed gateway.

Incentive

Emission is 41% miners, 41% validators and 18% owner. Since 2026-10-29 the miner pool is split equally across the 11 chains; on each, 90% goes to the five archive nodes with the most CU and 10% to the ten top full nodes, pro rata (confirmed by the payout dashboard). Others earn nothing there. Monthly buybacks of alpha (the subnet token) are burned.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Runs an archive or full node per chain behind the team's gateway.

  • Only the top 5 archive and top 10 full nodes per chain are paid (dashboard, 2026-11-07).
  • Rough pay per chain: top-5 archive about 0.40 TAO a day each, top-10 full about 0.02 (dashboard rule, 2026-11-07 price).
  • TAO nodes must run with --rpc-methods=unsafe or are ineligible; open the RPC port only to the gateway (README, 2026-11-05).
  • One hotkey per coldkey; at most one archive and one full node per chain; all 256 UIDs are taken; client families only, no versions.
  • Registration is per region: default eu; --region us-east is a separate registration that does not serve or earn yet (README, 2026-11-06).

Validator Medium barrier

Reads the gateway's logs and CU files and sets weights; blockmachine runs one validator itself.

  • 13 of 64 possible permits are held; 9 set weights within a tempo, 4 have not for 5+ tempos (chain, 2026-11-07).

Scoring since 2026-10-29

  • Routing score multiplies success, reliability, latency, price and slow-response factors. New by 2026-11-06: archive nodes also get a throughput factor (0.1 to 1; 0.1 until first scored).
  • Latency: archive nodes keep full marks up to 20 ms p95 (0.78 at 26 ms); full nodes lose marks from 1 ms.
  • Reliability maxes out after 3 good days (95% successful requests each). Roadmap: US and Asia regions; a US East registration exists, not live.

Getting started

  1. Pick a chain and sync an accepted client family; archive nodes need full history.
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 19 (burn about 1 TAO, 2026-11-07); a newcomer takes the lowest-ranked of 256 slots after about 3 days.
  3. Install the CLI (pip install blockmachine, Python 3.10+), run the README install script on the node's server, then bm miner login, add and test.
  4. Watch the public dashboard; latency decides traffic, so load-test your hardware.

Caveats

  • Scoring comes from the team's closed gateway, registry and simulator, and the rules keep changing: new-miner earnings are not predictable.
  • Pay follows traffic served, not routing score: 28 of the 55 best archive nodes and 80 of the 107 best full nodes were paid (dashboard, 2026-11-02, not re-read).
  • Usage and revenue are team figures: buybacks are on chain, but request counts are not independently shown.
  • A 2026-10-25 chat claim that the team sells more alpha than it burns is untested.
  • No team chat after 2026-10-31 could be read (archive unreachable on 2026-11-07).
