What it is

blockmachine sells JSON-RPC access to blockchains, including archive nodes. A gateway routes each customer request to a competing miner's node and bills only for successful answers. The team behind Taostats runs it and is its largest named customer (its own claim).

How it works

Miners

A miner runs an archive (full history) or full (recent blocks) node per chain behind the team's gateway, registered with the bm tool at a fixed bid of $0.50 per million compute units (CU, request work). Traffic follows a routing score, pay follows CU served. Wrong answers suspend the miner on that chain (none suspended now).

Validators

Validators read each epoch's gateway logs and CU files, cross-check answers and set weights from each miner's CU; ranking and routing stay in the team's closed gateway.

Incentive

Emission is 41% miners, 41% validators and 18% owner. The miner pool is split by region (docs: Europe 100%, US East 0%; the 11-12 record: half each), then across the 11 chains by fixed shares, from 17.5% for BNB Chain and Polygon to 2.5% for Scroll; per region 90% goes to the five archive nodes with the most CU and 10% to the ten top full nodes. Buybacks of alpha (the subnet token) are burned.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Runs an archive or full node per chain behind the team's gateway.

  • Only the top 5 archive and top 10 full nodes per chain and region are paid (dashboard).
  • Rough pay at the 2026-11-13 price in Europe (half the pot): a top-5 archive node about 0.38 TAO a day on BNB Chain or Polygon, 0.22 on Base or Ethereum, 0.05 on Scroll; a top-10 full node 0.021 to 0.003. US East had one archive node on 10 of 11 chains.
  • TAO nodes must run with --rpc-methods=unsafe or are ineligible; open the RPC port only to the gateway (README, 2026-11-05).
  • One hotkey per coldkey; at most one archive and one full node per chain; all 256 UIDs are taken.
  • Registration is per region: default eu; --region us-east is separate (README, 2026-11-06). The 11-12 record paid 13 US East uids.

Validator Medium barrier

Reads the gateway's logs and CU files and sets weights; blockmachine runs one validator itself.

  • 14 of 64 possible permits are held; 9 set weights within a tempo, 5 not for 5+ tempos (chain, 2026-11-13).

Scoring since 2026-10-29

  • Routing score multiplies success, reliability, latency, price and slow-response factors; archive nodes also get a throughput factor since 2026-11-06.
  • Latency: archive nodes keep full marks up to 20 ms p95; full nodes lose marks from 1 ms. Reliability maxes out after 3 good days.

Getting started

  1. Pick a chain and sync an accepted client family; archive nodes need full history.
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 19 (burn about 1 TAO, 2026-11-13); a newcomer takes the lowest-ranked of 256 slots after about 3 days.
  3. Install the CLI (pip install blockmachine, Python 3.10+), run the README install script on the server, then bm miner login, add and test.
  4. Watch the dashboard; latency decides traffic, so load-test your hardware.

Caveats

  • Scoring comes from the team's closed gateway, registry and simulator, and the rules keep changing: new-miner earnings are not predictable.
  • Pay follows traffic served, not routing score: on 2026-11-02 only 28 of the 55 best archive nodes were paid (not re-read).
  • No team chat after 2026-10-31 could be read (archive unreachable 2026-11-13): the 11-09 and 11-10 record gap and the 50/50 region split are unexplained.
