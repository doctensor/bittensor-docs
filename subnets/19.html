What it is

blockmachine sells JSON-RPC access to blockchains, including archive nodes. A gateway routes each customer request to a competing miner's node and bills only for successful answers. Validators turn each miner's served work into weights. The team behind Taostats runs it and is its largest named customer (its own claim).

How it works

Miners

A miner runs an archive (full history) or full (recent blocks) node for a chain behind the team's gateway, registered with the bm tool at a fixed bid of $0.50 per million compute units (CU, a measure of request work). Automatic checks cover chain, client family, tip distance and archive depth; wrong answers exclude the UID from payouts.

Validators

Validators read each epoch's gateway logs and CU files, cross-check answers and set weights from each miner's CU. The weight code is public but is only the last step: ranking and routing come from the team's closed gateway. On chain, 9 of 10 validators with weights share the same top miner.

Incentive

Emission is 41% miners, 41% validators and 18% owner. Since 2026-10-29 the miner pool is split equally across the 11 chains; per chain, 90% goes to the five archive nodes that served the most CU and 10% to the ten top full nodes, pro rata; others earn nothing there. An epoch is 7,200 blocks (about 24 hours). Monthly buybacks of alpha (the subnet token) are burned.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Runs an archive or full node per chain behind the team's gateway.

  • Only the top 5 archive and top 10 full nodes per chain are paid; being eligible is not enough (site, 2026-10-30).
  • One hotkey per coldkey, at most one archive and one full node per chain. All 256 UIDs are taken, so a newcomer replaces the lowest-ranked after about 3 days of immunity.
  • Client families only, no versions: reth, geth, op-reth, bor, nitro or subtensor depending on chain (README, team 2026-10-29).

Validator Medium barrier

Reads the gateway's logs and CU files and sets weights; blockmachine runs one validator itself.

  • 11 of 64 possible permits are held; 9 set weights in the last day and 2 have not for weeks (chain, 2026-10-30).
  • The owner's hotkey earns 49% of validator dividends.

Scoring since 2026-10-29

  • Team-run simulated traffic replaced capacity tests and is raised until responses slow, so faster nodes score higher.
  • Latency: rolling-hour p95, full marks at 20 ms (the site says 1 ms for full nodes), zero at 1,000 ms; slow tails also count.
  • Reliability maxes out after 3 good days (was 7). Announced, not live: US and Asia gateways and region-split rewards.

  • Live on 11 chains; six monthly on-chain burns (9,797 alpha).

Bear case

  • Net outflow of 763 TAO in 30 days; earners fell from 43 to 28.
  • Revenue proof is about 4% of miner alpha; the main customer is the owner's team.

What would change this view: Sustained inflow or independent revenue proof would make it bullish; more outflow or another rule reversal, bearish.

Getting started

  1. Pick a chain and sync an accepted client family (the miner README has the table); archive nodes need full history.
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 19: burn about 1 TAO (2026-10-30); a newcomer takes the lowest-ranked of 256 slots after about 3 days.
  3. Install the CLI (pip install blockmachine, Python 3.10+), run the README install script on the node's server, then bm miner login, add and test.
  4. Watch the public dashboard (Payouts, Live routing score, Eligibility); latency now decides traffic, so load-test your own hardware.

Caveats

  • Scoring comes from the team's closed gateway, registry and traffic simulator; only the final weight step is public code.
  • Rules changed twice in two weeks and regional gateways are planned, so new-miner earnings are not predictable.
  • A miner said on 2026-10-29 that full TAO nodes were unpaid unless the hotkey also ran an archive node; the site says top-10 full nodes are paid; no team answer yet.
  • Usage and revenue are team figures: buybacks are on chain, but request counts are not independently shown.
