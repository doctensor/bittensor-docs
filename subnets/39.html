What it is

Netuid 39 was Basilica, a GPU-rental project run by Covenant AI, which said in April 2026 it was leaving Bittensor. Cathedral, a community team, kept the slot running for four months and moved to SN94 in late September.

How it works

Miners

The former team's miner docs (undated) say to register on SN39, run a CPU server plus A100-class or better GPU nodes, and earn compute units plus rental revenue. On chain five unlinked UIDs share all incentive; no work is shown.

Validators

Fifteen hotkeys hold validator permits; eleven set weights in the last tempo. Eleven of 12 weight rows give the same split, 28 / 22 / 22 / 18 / 9% over UIDs 128, 205, 113, 13 and 20, none of which has set weights. UID 30 (last weights 2026-10-17) alone weights UID 68, unstaked and unpermitted.

Incentive

Five UIDs hold all incentive: 128 (28.4%), 205 (22.3%), 113 (21.9%), 13 (18.5%) and 20 (8.9%), with 14.2 / 11.1 / 11.0 / 9.2 / 4.5% of last-epoch emission (together roughly 20 TAO a day). None is owner-linked. UIDs 205, 113 and 128 hold 3,630, 10,730 and 1,742 alpha. UID 13 moved 1,000 alpha to another hotkey on 11-16 and 11-17 and 600 on 11-18 (about 18 TAO; not sales; target unknown) and now holds 318. Split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners.
