What it is

Netuid 39 was Basilica, run by Covenant AI, which said in April 2026 it was leaving Bittensor. The community team Cathedral then kept the slot running for four months without owning it, and moved to SN94 in late September. The owner key changed twice in 2026. Today the slot has no program: its on-chain name reads 'deprecated' and its chat channel is 'pending'.

How it works

Miners

No miner program or documentation was found. Miners would get 41% of subnet emission, but the part landing on owner-owned keys is set to be burned, and all incentive sits on one such UID. Burn events were not read, so this says 'set to be burned', not 'burned'.

Validators

Thirteen hotkeys hold validator permits (64 slots exist). Twelve set weights within the last tempo (360 blocks, about 72 minutes); one has not for about 13 days. Twelve put all weight on UID 136, one on UID 68. Who runs them is not documented.

Incentive

One of 256 UIDs, 136, has incentive (100%). It uses the owner's current keys, is an active validator, and got 62.8% of last-epoch emission (about 3,700 of 5,900 alpha, the subnet's token, per day; a floor). The split is 0 burn / 0 recycle / 18 owner / 41 validator / 41 miner (the chain default).

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

No miner program exists; all incentive sits on the owner's UID and the miner share is set to burn.

  • Cathedral's miner software now targets SN94; its cutover note says re-pointing an old SN39 install is unsupported.

Validator Medium barrier

Sets weights; 12 of 13 permit holders point at the owner's UID, so there is little to score.

  • 12 of the 13 holders are not the owner's; the owner's UID 136 weights itself 100%.

Ownership and alpha holders

Chain, 2026-10-30, unless noted.

  • The owner keys changed on 2026-04-09 and on 2026-08-14 (coldkey and hotkey in one block). The chain shows a key change, not a sale or its price.
  • Chat says the second was a takeover through the chain's conviction mechanism (holders lock alpha behind a key) and cost close to 4,000 TAO (Cathedral, 2026-08-09 and 2026-09-01).
  • The owner's coldkey holds 0.05% of the alpha read, so UID 136's validator dividends mostly go to other stakers of its hotkey (20.2% of the alpha read; its take rate was not read). The top 5 coldkeys hold 52.8%.

Caveats

  • The on-chain name reads 'deprecated' (set 2026-04-10 under the previous owner key); the owner's plans are unknown.
  • Chain weights are validator opinions, not payouts. Burn events and the owner UID's take rate were not read.
  • The chain dates two owner-key changes but not a sale or price; the conviction mechanism, the cost of about 4,000 TAO and community voting come from chat.
  • Who runs the other 12 validators, and whether any miners exist, is unknown. The tracker's GPU claims were not confirmed.
  • Stake shares cover about 79% of staked alpha; one holder can use many coldkeys.
