What it is

Netuid 39 was Basilica, a GPU-rental project that said in April 2026 it was leaving Bittensor, then a community team's slot. It is now Kokoah's: a non-custodial Hyperliquid trading app whose subnet pays traders for perpetuals they trade through it.

How it works

Miners

Link one Hyperliquid account to a registered hotkey with a signed on-chain commitment, then trade perpetuals through Kokoah so each order carries its builder code. No miner software runs. An account is paid on days its average value is at least $5,000 (from 2026-11-24, was $10,000), after three qualifying trades worth $1,000.

Validators

Each validator reads linked accounts' public fills, the exchange's daily builder-fee file and the chain, scores each UTC day after it ends and sets weights. The code is deterministic, so all 11 weight rows are identical.

Incentive

Rules: 80% of the miner pool by builder fees paid, 20% to accounts whose 30-day record passes a statistical test. Chain: 1 of 8 linked accounts earns (UID 13); four did until 2026-11-24. The miner pool is about 2,944 alpha (20 TAO) a day; emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Trade Hyperliquid perpetuals through Kokoah from a linked account; no miner software. Pay follows the Kokoah fees you pay.

  • Counts: perpetual fills carrying Kokoah's builder code. Never counts: other front ends, spot, trades with other miners or your own linked accounts.
  • Skill reward needs 30+ daily returns after the link, 10 round trips, 7 active days and $500 average value in 30 days.
  • Kokoah's leaderboard (day 2026-11-23): 1 of 8 linked accounts eligible; 3 never qualified, 4 under the $10,000 average.

Validator High barrier

Reads linked accounts' public fills and the chain, scores each UTC day, sets weights. Needs a validator permit (stake).

  • 15 hotkeys hold permits; 11 set weights within a tempo, 4 have not for over 5 tempos (2026-11-24).

Getting started

  1. Miner: register a hotkey (burn 0.0005 TAO), link a Hyperliquid account worth $5,000 or more (from 2026-11-24), trade perpetuals through Kokoah, then check the leaderboard (docs/miners.md).
  2. Validator: run Kokoah's validator image with a permitted hotkey; --watch scores without setting weights (docs/validators.md).

Caveats

  • Pay, fee, volume and skill figures come from Kokoah's own API; the chain split matches its weights, but fees and volume were not checked against Hyperliquid.
  • Hotkeys were matched to UIDs by registration block; the chain tool masks keys.
  • Buyback, burn and the owner-share lock are Kokoah's statements, not checked on chain.
  • Rules changed on 11-23 and 11-24, and no scored day uses the $5,000 floor yet; commits and PRs come from bot accounts.
