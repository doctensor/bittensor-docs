What it is

Netuid 39 was Basilica, run by Covenant AI, which announced in April 2026 that it was leaving Bittensor. A community team, Cathedral, then kept the slot running for about four months without holding the owner key, building a verified-compute sandbox product with confidential-hardware evidence, plus distillation and voice work. In late September Cathedral said it was moving to its own slot, SN94, and its code now targets SN94. SN39 itself has no program of its own today: its on-chain identity reads deprecated, and the channel name is the placeholder pending.

How it works

Miners

No current miner program or documentation for this slot was found. Cathedral's miner software (Cathedral Sandbox) now targets SN94, and its validator repo states that re-pointing an old SN39 installation to another netuid is not a supported migration.

Validators

Eleven hotkeys hold validator permits and all of them set weights on 2026-10-21. Ten of the eleven put their weight on one UID and one validator puts it on a different single UID. Who runs these validators and what they check is not documented in the sources found.

Incentive

On 2026-10-21 exactly one of 256 UIDs had non-zero incentive: 100 percent of it. That UID uses the same coldkey and hotkey as the subnet owner, holds a validator permit, and received about 62 percent of last-epoch emission. The chain does not show intent: this could be a deliberate holding pattern or a leftover of validators copying one target.

Recent activity

  • 2026-10-21 (Chain reads for netuid 39 at blocks 9334318 to 9334328) Chain read: 256 of 256 UIDs registered, 11 validator permits, incentive non-zero on one UID that uses the owner's keys, and 210 distinct coldkeys among the UIDs. Channel messages in the last 30 days were greetings only.
  • 2026-09-28 (GitHub cathedralai/cathedral-validator) The Cathedral validator repository merged a change moving the validator to subnet 94 and added a cutover document telling operators to stop their SN39 writers.
  • 2026-09-27 (#pending and #cathedral (SN94)) Cathedral announced SN94 as its next home and was welcomed there. A message the next day asked the chain's admins to confirm Cathedral is no longer building on 39 so that the SN94 name can be changed; no answer was found in the data read.
  • 2026-09-23 (#pending) The slot's owner said their attention was on another project. No later owner statement was found.
  • 2026-09-09 (#pending) Cathedral described three tracks (sandbox, distillation, voice), said AMD SEV-SNP hardware had completed a live validation cycle and received validator weight, and gave no public launch date.
  • 2026-09-01 (#pending) The Cathedral team said it had run the slot for months without owning it, was looking to buy its own slot, and might restart mining on SN39 with no owner emissions in the meantime. Several members objected to using a slot someone else owned; the team said it did not dispute who the owner was.
  • 2026-04-13 (#pending (pinned)) A pinned notice said the subnet's original owner had announced its departure from Bittensor entirely and that control of the owner key had not changed.

Getting started

  1. There is no documented way to mine or validate on SN39 today.
  2. If you came for Cathedral's work, use its SN94 materials instead; see the SN94 page on this site and the cathedralai repositories.

Caveats

  • This slot's identity on chain still reads deprecated, and the chat channel name is pending. The slot owner's plans are unknown.
  • Chain weights are validator opinions, not payouts. The chain shows where incentive points, not why. Whether the owner UID's emission is kept, burned or redistributed was not checked.
  • Who runs the eleven validators, and whether any miners exist, is unknown. Cathedral's code left the slot, so those validators are likely not running Cathedral's validator.
  • The Cathedral material here is its own description of its work; the repository and chat describe the past SN39 phase and were not independently tested.
  • A moderator note said the slot was in an unusual situation after the original owner left; all ownership statements in chat come from members, not from chain data about people.
