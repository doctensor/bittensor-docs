What it is

OpenRoboto pays miners who improve AI models that turn camera images and a text instruction into robot-arm movements, and who collect first-person video of real work. Reward weight is 15% for the weekly simulation contest and 85% for data collection, scored by a private team backend.

How it works

Miners

Simulation miners fine-tune the season's open π0.5 base model, upload a full checkpoint to Hugging Face, pay the entry fee and announce it on chain; the champion's model is published about 24 hours after the crown. Data miners record first-person video with team OR-S1 devices.

Validators

A thin client copies settled weights from the team's backend (read key published in a config file) onto the chain; it cannot recompute scores. On 2026-10-31, 10 of 11 weight-setters ranked the same uid first; one sent everything to the owner uid.

Incentive

Simulation is king of the hill: a challenger must beat the champion by more than 0.01 mean, and the top three share 70 / 20 / 10 of the simulation weight. Data pay follows accepted minutes x rate x quality x novelty (unpublished).

Getting started

  1. Check open seasons and fees at api.openroboto.ai/api/v1/competitions; docs lag the live rules.
  2. Register a hotkey on subnet 80 (0.0005 TAO), pip install -U openroboto, then run openroboto init and doctor.
  3. Fine-tune the season's base model, export a full merged checkpoint and test it free with openroboto check.
  4. openroboto submit pays the 0.2 TAO fee and announces the commit; confirm with openroboto status.
  5. Data collection: shift.openroboto.ai. Validators: a permit needs 1,000+ TAO stake, then openroboto validator run.

Caveats

  • Scoring, ranking, held-out data and data-track payouts run on a private team backend.
  • One Hugging Face account holds the champion in v2.0 and v2.2 to v2.6; its coldkey holds 11 uids, the runner-up's 55 of 256. The team says it is not theirs.
  • 85% of weight sits on seven data uids, each under its own coldkey and none owner-linked; holders, rates and review are not public.
  • Real-robot Season 2 has no announced result; Season 1 has read 'paying' since 2026-09-29.
