What it is

OpenRoboto pays miners who improve AI models that turn camera images and a text instruction into robot-arm movements, and who collect first-person video of real work. No simulation season is open; all reward weight sits on data collection (15% before).

How it works

Miners

Simulation miners fine-tune the season's open π0.5 base model, upload a full checkpoint to Hugging Face, pay the entry fee and commit it. Data miners record first-person video with team OR-S1 devices.

Validators

A thin client copies the team backend's weights; it cannot recompute scores.

Incentive

Simulation is king of the hill: a challenger must beat the champion by more than 0.01 mean, and the top three share 70 / 20 / 10 of the simulation weight. Data pay follows accepted minutes x rate x quality x novelty (unpublished).

Getting started

  1. Check open seasons and fees at api.openroboto.ai/api/v1/competitions (none on 2026-11-14); docs lag.
  2. Register a hotkey on subnet 80 (0.0005 TAO), pip install -U openroboto, then run openroboto init and doctor.
  3. Fine-tune the season's base model, export a full merged checkpoint and test it free with openroboto check.
  4. openroboto submit pays the season's fee (0.2 TAO burn so far; Gamma for task seasons) and commits; confirm with openroboto status.
  5. Data collection: shift.openroboto.ai. Validators: a permit needs 1,000+ TAO stake, then openroboto validator run.
