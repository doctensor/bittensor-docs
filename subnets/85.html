What it is

VidAIO (SN85) is a video-processing subnet from the team behind vidaio.io. Miners enter competitions with private code that the team runs on hidden clips; the best results share miner emissions for two weeks.

How it works

Miners

A miner submits code, not outputs: a private GitHub repo with a Dockerfile providing /app/run.sh. The team builds it and runs it in a sealed sandbox on 16 hidden clips committed by hash. Entries sharing a coldkey, IP, GitHub account or outputs count once.

Validators

A team-run authority runs every entry and publishes one signed log per epoch (about 72 minutes), anchoring its hash on chain. Other validators check the log and submit its weights unchanged.

Incentive

Scores are per-clip means; removal, interpolation and upscaling credit only gains over a free baseline, and the last two give 30% to speed. A first place above the crown bar pays the top five 90/4/3/2/1; otherwise entries above the podium bar share 50/24/13/8/5. Between results half goes to a licensing treasury, half to the owner hotkey (a burn, per the team).

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Writes a video-processing program and enrolls it in a competition; the team runs it on its own GPU.

  • Sandbox for 004 and 005: one L40S, 32 CPU, 64 GiB, no network, 4 clips per 1-hour batch, image up to 20 GB.
  • Own code under Apache-2.0 or MIT; downloads must be hash-pinned. A failed run, timeout or oversize output zeroes the 4-clip batch.

Validator High barrier

Outside validators run a thin image that copies the team's signed weights; the team does the scoring.

  • It checks the signed log against its on-chain anchor and does not recompute scores; the README says this is not an independent audit.

Licence and training-data rules

  • Code, weights and training data must allow commercial use (own footage, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA are fine; LTX-2.x outputs are not). Training data cannot be verified.
  • The 004 winner's released archive (77 files, 194 MB): MIT code, four own fine-tuned weight files, training scripts and clip lists, and a LICENSES.md for third-party parts; no training data.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/MINING.md and your competition's manifest on vidaio.io/competitions (stake floor, bars, dates).
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 85 and hold 500 alpha at enrollment (checked once, nothing locked); stay registered through the payout window or the share burns.
  3. Build a private repo from an examples/competition_contenders template with LICENSE and LICENSES.md, hash-pinned downloads and the team's read-only GitHub account invited.
  4. Run scripts/competition_precheck.py with --commercial, score the practice pack with score_practice.py, then enroll once with scripts/competition_enroll.py (no edit or cancel).
  5. Validators: use the thin-validator image and ask the team for S3 credentials, in the channel, not DMs; beware fake team accounts.

Caveats

  • The team runs scoring, sandboxes, enrollment and the anchor; the code is mirrored, but what runs and the hidden clips cannot be checked.
  • No revenue, customer or buyback figure exists; the payouts page still says 'Licensing payments: none yet'.
  • Competition 003's hidden clips were promised after the result; none is linked 18 days later.
  • Payouts are narrow: window 004 pays 5 of 30 ranked entries, the winner 89.55% for a 0.0007 lead; gaps between windows burn half of miner emission.
