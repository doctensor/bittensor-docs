What it is

VidAIO (SN85) is a video-processing subnet from the team behind vidaio.io. Miners enter private code that the team runs on hidden clips.

How it works

Miners

A miner submits code, not outputs: a private GitHub repo with a Dockerfile providing /app/run.sh. The team builds it and runs it in a sealed sandbox on 16 to 20 hidden clips committed by hash. Entries sharing a coldkey, IP, GitHub account or outputs count once.

Validators

A team-run authority runs every entry and publishes one signed log per epoch (about 72 minutes), anchoring its hash on chain. Other validators check the log and submit its weights unchanged.

Incentive

Scores are per-clip means; removal, interpolation and upscaling credit only gains over a free baseline, the last two giving 30% to speed. A first place above the crown bar pays the top five 90/4/3/2/1; otherwise the podium bar gives 50/24/13/8/5. Between results half goes to a licensing treasury, half to the owner hotkey (a burn, per the team).

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Writes a video-processing program and enrolls it in a competition; the team runs it.

  • Sandbox for 006: one A100-80GB, 32 CPU, 64 GiB, no network, 4 clips per 1-hour batch, image up to 40 GB.
  • Code and models under Apache-2.0, MIT or BSD in 006; downloads must be hash-pinned. A failed run, timeout or oversize output zeroes the 4-clip batch.

Validator High barrier

Outside validators run a thin image that copies the team's signed weights; the team does the scoring.

  • The README says the log check is not an independent audit.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/MINING.md and your competition's manifest on subnet.vidaio.io (stake floor, bars, dates).
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 85 and hold 500 alpha at enrollment and, from 006, until finalization (nothing locked); stay registered through the payout window or the share burns.
  3. Build a private repo from an examples/competition_contenders template with LICENSE and LICENSES.md, hash-pinned downloads and the team's read-only GitHub account invited.
  4. Run scripts/competition_precheck.py with --commercial, score the practice pack with score_practice.py, then enroll with scripts/competition_enroll.py (--replace or withdraw until the deadline).
  5. Validators: use the thin-validator image and ask the team for S3 credentials (beware fake team accounts).

Caveats

  • The team runs scoring, sandboxes, enrollment and the anchor; the code is mirrored, but what runs and the hidden clips cannot be checked.
  • Hidden clips of 003 to 005 are promised after each result; none is linked 17 to 30 days later.
  • Payouts are narrow: window 005 pays 5 of 34 ranked entries, the winner 89.55% for a 0.032 lead; gaps between windows burn half of miner emission.
