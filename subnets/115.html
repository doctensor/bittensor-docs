What it is

MoirAI says it is building a creative harness (CPH): software that takes a plain-language brief through planning, model choice, drafts, refinement and delivery. Miners are meant to propose upgrades to it and validators to test them. None of that is live: no code, rules or evaluator are published.

How it works

Miners

First phase: miners improve a public harness (workflows, skills, prompts), lately framed as rule packages that turn messy briefs into project plans. Later: selling spare compute as paid inference. The submission format is unpublished.

Validators

Meant to run candidate versions under shared conditions: objective checks, multimodal-model judging and human spot checks, with a promised public rubric and held-out tasks. No evaluator exists yet.

Incentive

Unpublished. The team lists brief fit, output quality, gain over baseline, consistency, reliability, and time and cost as candidates (2026-09-14). Today the miner share is aimed at the owner's own uid and burned.
