What it is

MANTIS pays miners for signals that help predict market moves. Miners upload encrypted predictions about once a minute; validators open them after a delay and score what each adds. Newer FLOW and LOOP challenges pay Bitcoin trades and AI research.

How it works

Miners

Each miner encrypts prediction vectors for every challenge and uploads one file per hotkey to its own Cloudflare R2 bucket, committed on chain, about every 60 seconds. For FLOW it also submits Bitcoin trades, each backed by a collateral bet posted first (EVM contract).

Validators

Validators download the files, decrypt them after a delay (code default one week), score each challenge with walk-forward regressions, merge near-copy miners into one signal and set weights every 360 blocks.

Incentive

Challenge scores are combined by fixed weights (table below). The code puts at least 52% of weight on the owner's uid 0 (burn). FLOW pays on path-adjusted, size-weighted trade returns, minus a 0.10% fee from 2026-11-23.

LOOP, the new research challenge

Added 2026-11-20. Dashboard, 2026-11-24: 13 audited pipelines from 9 miners; 9 with a x1 verdict.

  • Miners publish an AI research pipeline, paid with their own SayGM key. Daily, the owner's validator (the only one) screens, runs and replays each; an AI judge checks that nothing was smuggled in.
  • Beating the best audited score earns credit that halves every 14 days; with none, the slice burns.

Getting started

  1. Register a hotkey on subnet 123 (burn 0.15 TAO on 2026-11-24).
  2. Install MINER_GUIDE.md's dependencies (the pinned time-lock wrapper, not the PyPI package).
  3. Create a Cloudflare R2 bucket, commit its public URL on chain; the object key must equal your hotkey.
  4. Build predictions for every active challenge, encrypt with generate_and_encrypt.py, upload about once a minute.
  5. For FLOW, follow flow_post.py: fund a collateral position, then post a bet before each trade opens.
  6. For LOOP, read LOOP.md: fund a SayGM key, adapt autoresearch/pipelines/reference.py, test it locally, add it to your payload.
  7. To validate, read install_reqs.sh and autoupdate.sh; there is no validator guide.

Caveats

  • The team's own validator pays: uid 0 holds 53.6% of incentive, 52% by code plus an unquantified FLOW and LOOP share.
  • The price feed is team-run and froze on 2026-09-01, 2026-10-12 and 2026-11-11; every challenge depends on it.
  • Scoring detail goes out by direct message only; the public archive lagged until 2026-10-30.
  • The team is trusted: its owner key reads every payload on arrival (docs: 'for trading'); only it settles FLOW, outside the repo; the live contract was not compared with the source.
  • LOOP is young: only the owner's validator judges it, and live trades are not yet checked against the pipeline's model.
