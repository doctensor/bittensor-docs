What it is

Hippius sells cloud services: encrypted file storage (Drive), S3-compatible storage, an AI model registry (Hub), a CDN and confidential compute. Files are split into 30 erasure-coded pieces held by storage miners; a separate Hippius blockchain tracks them and pays miners.

How it works

Miners

Storage miners run a Hippius chain node and the Arion miner, offering 2 TB+ and a static IP; they hold encrypted file pieces and answer storage audits.

Validators

Validators score nodes on the Hippius chain (storage, bandwidth, uptime, failures). The team runs the Arion validator and gateway. On Bittensor, 12 validators hold permits and a script sets weights from that ranking.

Incentive

A node scores 70% bandwidth and 30% storage, times uptime, minus penalties. On Bittensor the code gives miners 1% of the weight and uid 238 the other 99%; the chain agrees. A Hippius-chain bank pays families by weight.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Runs a Hippius node and the Arion miner, stores encrypted file pieces and answers storage audits.

  • Nodes sit in families under one account; the first node is free, a family holds up to 35.
  • Pay arrives as hAlpha on the Hippius chain, not as Bittensor incentive (team).

Validator High barrier

Scores nodes on the Hippius chain; sets Bittensor weights from the ranking.

  • 12 of 64 validator slots hold permits; all 12 set weights within one tempo, top weight on uid 238.
  • Not read: who holds the 12 permits and who writes the on-chain quality reports.

How the money flows

  1. Bittensor sets aside 41% of emission for miners (about 54 TAO a day); the code gives miners 1% and uid 238, the Hippius code's emission capture account, 99%.
  2. The team says (2026-08-04) this emission is routed to the Hippius chain. On chain, three bridge guardians sign a deposit of 147 or 293 alpha about every two hours, and a bank pays families by weight (3,500 alpha a day cap).
  3. Not traced: the hop from uid 238 to the bridge. The daily amounts match within 0.03%.

Getting started

  1. Ask in the channel whether a family slot is free before buying hardware or locking funds (600 of 600 used on 2026-10-30).
  2. Run and sync a Hippius chain node (docs: Running Hippius Blockchain Node).
  3. Register your family and node from the provider dashboard or the hippius CLI; this needs Alpha.
  4. Run the Arion miner (v0.1.38) with the team's validator and warden node IDs and your family ID, with the UDP ports open; without the warden ID audits fail.

Caveats

  • Miner pay is not visible on Bittensor (uid 238 holds 99.7% of incentive); individual payouts were not read.
  • Customers (Ditto and ZapMe are named), revenue and storage totals come from the team's posts; on-chain customer deposits were about 86 alpha in 7 days.
  • The code was read at its 2026-10-22 state, matching live runtime 92022; 92023 (miner payment paths) is not live. Whether burned hAlpha withdrawals released Alpha was not checked.
