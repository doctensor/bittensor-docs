What it is

Hippius sells cloud services: encrypted file storage (Drive), S3-compatible storage, an AI model registry (Hub), a CDN and confidential compute. Files are split into 30 erasure-coded pieces held by storage miners; a separate Hippius blockchain tracks them and pays miners.

How it works

Miners

Storage miners run a Hippius chain node and the Arion miner on a server with 2 TB+ and a static IP.

Validators

Validators score nodes on the Hippius chain (storage, bandwidth, uptime, failures); the team runs the Arion validator and gateway. On Bittensor a script sets weights from that ranking.

Incentive

A node scores 70% bandwidth and 30% storage, times uptime, minus penalties. On Bittensor the code gives miners 1% of the weight and uid 238 the other 99%, and the chain agrees; a Hippius-chain bank then pays families by weight.

  • Permit hotkeys hold 47% of all alpha (owner's hotkeys 29.1%). Not read: who writes the on-chain quality reports.

How the money flows

  1. Bittensor sets aside 41% of emission for miners (about 52 TAO a day); the code gives miners 1% and uid 238, the Hippius code's emission capture account, 99%.
  2. The team says uid 238 is its bridge entry and all miner emission crosses to the Hippius chain as hAlpha. On chain its hotkey holds only one or two epochs (about 147 or 295 alpha), cleared about every 2 hours; three guardians sign a matching deposit; a bank pays families by weight (3,500 alpha a day cap).
  3. Not traced: the transfer itself on the Bittensor side. Daily amounts match within 0.1%.

Getting started

  1. Ask in the channel whether a family slot is free before buying hardware or locking funds.
  2. Run and sync a Hippius chain node (docs: Running Hippius Blockchain Node).
  3. Register your family and node from the provider dashboard or the hippius CLI; this needs Alpha.
  4. Run the Arion miner (v0.1.38) with the team's validator and warden node IDs and your family ID, with the UDP ports open; without the warden ID audits fail.
