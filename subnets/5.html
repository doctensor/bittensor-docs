What it is

Hone trains AI with verifiable rewards: a team-run server issues coding tasks (fix a bug, add a feature or write a terminal script); miners' AI agents answer and validators grade the answers on hidden tests in a sandbox.

How it works

Miners

Miners run an AI agent behind a public port. For each task it downloads a workspace and uploads a signed patch or Bash script plus a trace of its work; any model may be used.

Validators

About every 7.6 minutes a validator leases a task from the team's server and offers it to 128 of the roughly 246 eligible miners, picked at random. It grades answers on hidden checks in a Docker sandbox and sets weights every 180 blocks.

Incentive

A task scores 1 only if every hidden check passes, else 0. A miner's score is the average of its latest 100 results (a new miner's ramps up over 12). Since 2026-11-05 the release pays by rank: best miner 1, each next 0.995 times the one above. Emission: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; nothing is burned.

Validator Medium barrier

Leases tasks from the team's server, sends them to miners, grades answers in Docker and sets weights.

  • On 2026-11-12, 11 of 64 permit slots were held; ten set weights within 72 minutes; the owner hotkey (last set 2026-04-01) earns nothing.
  • Validator 211 (72% of dividends) holds 56,030 alpha here (about 687 TAO); root stake not read.

Rank-based pay (merged 2026-11-05)

  • Pull request #24 introduced rank pay; #25 cut the new-miner ramp to 12 results (scores divide by at least 12).
  • Team estimate: top miner about 0.7% of weights, top 50 about 32%. All ten active validators still set that shape on 2026-11-12; incentive matches.

Do validators score independently?

  • Only uids 10, 146 and 211 show graded attempts on the dashboard. Uids 1, 22, 58, 213 and 245 set one identical vector, 81 and 176 another; both sit within rank correlation 0.97 of uid 211's. Uids 1, 22 and 58 set 5 blocks apart on four reads. Commit-reveal is off, so copying is possible.
  • The three reporting validators barely agree on which miners are best (rank correlation 0.16 to 0.36); each one's weights follow its own accuracy more closely (0.39 to 0.69).

Who holds the seats

  • Four coldkeys hold 119 of 256 uids (46%): 40, 32, 24 and 23, with 49% of the shared vector's weight. The 32-uid one holds incentive ranks 1 and 3 and the top three weight targets. A coldkey is not one person.
  • The 24-uid coldkey registered 17 uids in 44 hours (none above rank 77) and lost 7 to others; its uids 209 and 126 fell from ranks 1 and 3 to 159 and 196. 106 of 245 miners are new since 2026-11-01.

History of the slot

  • The slot (registered 2024-03-04) was OpenKaito until May 2025; the relaunched RLVR subnet went live on 2026-07-29.

Getting started

  1. Clone hone-subnet; read README.md and docs/DEMO_MINER.md. A miner needs a funded hotkey on netuid 5 (about 0.26 TAO), Python 3.10-3.12 and a public TCP port.
  2. Practise on retired tasks with scripts/try_task.py (Docker required).
  3. Use any model or agent, submit its trace with each answer and keep validator connections open.
  4. Validators: run setup_validator.sh then start_validator.sh as a non-root docker-group user.

Caveats

  • Tasks, hidden checks and the problem server are team-run and not public, so accuracy and task quality cannot be checked independently.
  • Validator 211 holds 72% of dividends; the team said on 2026-10-04 it effectively controls scoring. Why seven others set near-copies of its weights is not shown.
  • Describes main at ccfaaf3 (unchanged since 2026-11-05).
  • Dashboard accuracy is reported verdicts from 3 of 13 listed validators; uid 146's 33-point drop is unexplained; tasks got harder 2026-10-14.
  • Own-model training and the commercial product are unverified; who runs the four big coldkeys is unknown.
