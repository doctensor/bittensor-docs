What it is

UMI pays miners for models that turn American Sign Language (ASL) video into English text. Team-run evaluators score each model on held-out clips in numbered cohorts; models near or above a public baseline share the miner pool by score band.

How it works

Miners

Miners submit a model bundle: one offline Python entry point that turns video into English text, on CPU, with a permitted license. Since cohort 6 only models are paid; a model must reach the public baseline (within 1% from cohort 7), and the best one becomes the next baseline.

Validators

A team coordinator issues the test clips and two team-run evaluators score them; results are published. Permit holders re-derive those rows and set weights. Seven hold permits and five have weight rows; three set weights in the last epoch and four have not for over five epochs.

Incentive

Emission splits 30.75% owner, 15.5% validators, 53.75% miners (it was 18 / 41 / 41 until 2026-10-27). Scores fall into 2.5-point bands; each lower band or rank earns 0.85 times the one above. Identical models count once; unallocated share is burned.

Bear case

  • Team-run scoring, re-done once; 21 slots earn, one holds 30%.
  • No verified customers or revenue.

Getting started

  1. Register a hotkey on subnet 78 (0.375 TAO burn on 2026-10-31) and keep it registered.
  2. Read 'What miners should run now' in the repo; since cohort 6 only models are paid.
  3. Install the model client (Python 3.10 to 3.14, FFmpeg, uv) and rerun the installer for each new cohort.
  4. Download the current baseline (the cohort 6 winner) from the results API, then build an offline bundle: one entry point, declared training data, matching license.
  5. Sign and upload before the intake cutoff, then query admission: the first receipt only says pending.

Caveats

  • Evaluation is team-run (two evaluators in one control group, one coordinator); cohort 6 was re-scored once after a training-data complaint.
  • Rewards are concentrated: one slot holds 30% of incentive. Miners say near-copies of the baseline earn; counting identical models once only partly answers that.
  • Unverified: buyback funding, paid translators, any customers or revenue. Training hardware is not documented.
