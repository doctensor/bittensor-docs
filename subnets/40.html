What it is

Ralph is a subnet where miners compress one open model, Qwen3-8B, into tiny GGUF files in four bit-width tiers to run on a phone. Validators score how closely each file matches the original, and the best per tier is crowned. Round 9 (2026-11-13) was the last scored round; since 2026-11-25 all weight goes to one hotkey the team calls owner-held, while it builds the next mechanism.

How it works

Miners

Miners compress the pinned model into a tier with a bit-per-weight cap (binary 1.15, ternary 1.75, sub2 2.3, sub4 4.0) and serve a GGUF at a fixed Hugging Face revision, its hash committed on chain before the round's random value exists.

Validators

The scoring validator rents a GPU box and measures 'observer KL' retention: judge models compare how the miner's and the parent's steps move the judge (1 = identical). Exam items come from a block hash drawn after commitments close; records are signed and anchored on chain.

Incentive

Team rules: each tier has a fixed share (see table); the king takes 80%, the best improving challenger 20%. Chain, 2026-11-28: split 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. Since 2026-11-25 all seven weight-setters, owner uid 0 among them, put all weight on uid 13, so the miner share (about 2,944 alpha a day) goes to that one hotkey instead of being burned. Dividends: uid 0 64%, uid 6 22%, uid 149 11%.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Compresses the pinned Qwen3-8B into one bit tier and serves it as a GGUF with a committed hash.

  • Only GGUF passes the tiers; TQ1_0 and TQ2_0 are rejected (no mainline llama.cpp Metal kernels).
  • Chain, 2026-11-28: no independent miner earns; all incentive goes to uid 13, which the team says the owner holds.
  • No round has been scored since 2026-11-13; 28 entries wait for scoring (team status feed).

Validator Medium barrier

The team's validator scores on rented GPUs; others set weights from a round they verified.

  • Dividends follow stake: uid 6 holds 21.7% (28.6% on 2026-11-23). Uid 13 has a permit but has never set weights.

Auditor Low barrier

Anyone can recheck a signed round: arithmetic on a CPU, model effects on matching GPUs.

  • L0 recomputes the arithmetic; L1 re-digests the exam pool.
  • Burn and reward-allocation records are audited as a signed one-uid payout with no scores; a 'burn' aimed at another hotkey is rejected.

Getting started

  1. Read the README in RalphLabsAI/ralph: the pinned parent (Qwen3-8B), the four tiers and their bit caps.
  2. Compress the model into one tier as a GGUF; check it with the repo's bit-rate tool.
  3. Commit the file's hash before the round nonce exists, reveal after the round opens (miner/QUICKSTART.md).
  4. Check the team's status feed first.

Caveats

  • Until the burn, one unidentified uid (234) earned all incentive without setting weights; its 2,509-alpha stake was unstaked in one event on 2026-11-03.
  • The team's gap list (2026-09-13) and dashboard (2026-11-28) say no v2 allocation is applied on chain: true of scored rounds. Uid 149 applied one for 17 hours; since 2026-11-25 all weight goes to uid 13.
  • 'Held by the subnet owner' is the team's statement. The chain links uid 13 to another coldkey that also holds uids 73, 124, 125, 126 and 155; this page cannot confirm who controls it.
  • Members have alleged self-mining since July; the owner denies it. This page takes no side.
  • Retention measures fidelity to the parent, not capability; the figures and the iPhone test are the team's own.
