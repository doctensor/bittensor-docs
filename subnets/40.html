What it is

Ralph is a subnet where miners compress one open model, Qwen3-8B, into tiny GGUF files in four bit-width tiers to run on a phone. Validators score how closely each file matches the original, and the best per tier is crowned. It scored Round 9 on 2026-11-13 after a pause; the burn continues.

How it works

Miners

Miners compress the pinned model into a tier with a bit-per-weight cap (binary 1.15, ternary 1.75, sub2 2.3, sub4 4.0) and serve a GGUF at a fixed Hugging Face revision, its hash committed on chain before the round's random value exists.

Validators

The scoring validator rents a GPU box and measures 'observer KL' retention: judge models compare how the miner's and the parent's steps move the judge (1 = identical). Exam items come from a block hash drawn after commitments close; records are signed and anchored on chain.

Incentive

Team rules: each tier has a fixed share (see table); the king takes 80%, the best improving challenger 20%. Chain, 2026-11-21: split 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. All seven weight-setters, owner uid 0 among them, put all weight on the owner uid, so the miner share is withheld. Dividends: uid 0 59%, uid 6 27%, uid 149 11%.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Compresses the pinned Qwen3-8B into one bit tier and serves it as a GGUF with a committed hash.

  • Only GGUF passes the tiers; TQ1_0 and TQ2_0 are rejected (no mainline llama.cpp Metal kernels).
  • Chain, 2026-11-21: no miner earns yet; the four Round 9 crown uids hold no incentive or permit.

Validator Medium barrier

The team's validator scores on rented GPUs; others set weights from a round they verified.

  • Dividends follow stake: uid 6 went from 22.7% to 27.2% as alpha arrived on its hotkey (2026-11-16).

Auditor Low barrier

Anyone can recheck a signed round: arithmetic on a CPU, model effects on matching GPUs.

  • L0 recomputes the arithmetic; L1 re-digests the exam pool.
  • A burn record is audited as a signed one-uid payout with no scores.

Getting started

  1. Read the README in RalphLabsAI/ralph: the pinned parent (Qwen3-8B), the four tiers and their bit caps.
  2. Compress the model into one tier as a GGUF; check it with the repo's bit-rate tool.
  3. Commit the file's hash before the round nonce exists, reveal after the round opens (miner/QUICKSTART.md).
  4. Check the team's status feed first: Round 9 is scored; no miner is paid.

Caveats

  • Until the burn, one unidentified uid (234) earned all incentive without setting weights; its 2,509-alpha stake was unstaked in one event on 2026-11-03.
  • The team's gap list (2026-09-13) and dashboard (2026-11-21) say no v2 allocation is applied on chain; uid 149 applied one for 17 hours.
  • Members have alleged self-mining since July; the owner denies it. This page takes no side.
  • Retention measures fidelity to the parent, not capability; the figures and the iPhone test are the team's own.
