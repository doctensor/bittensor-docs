What it is

Ormas, run by Heron Cove LLC, sells verified coding outcomes. A client supplies a GitHub repo, a task and an automated test; miners quote a price, deliver a branch and are paid only if the test passes. Miners keep their models private.

How it works

Miners

A miner runs the open ormas-subnet software against the Ormas gateway. For each queued task it offers a firm price, a limit (estimate plus hard ceiling) or declines; the gateway awards the lowest expected total cost. The winner clones the repo, makes the change with its own tools, runs the tests and publishes the branch.

Validators

A checker re-runs the client's test on the branch and signs accept or reject. The reference checker is public, but the docs, README and the team's own feed say the team runs all acceptance today. Six hotkeys hold validator permits; all six put about 99% of their weight on the owner's uid.

Incentive

An accepted delivery freezes an emission target of 2x its settled USD price (since 2026-10-05); unearned emission goes to the owner's uid. On chain as of 2026-10-30 the owner's uid holds 99.2% of incentive and 3 of 256 uids earn. The stored split is 18% owner, 49% validators, 33% miners.

Getting started

  1. Check eligibility: the beta is US-only and the operator approves each miner.
  2. Sign in at ormas.ai and mint a runner key (30-day expiry).
  3. Install the miner (Python 3.10+) or re-clone the repo, then run ormas-miner login and register a miner name.
  4. Ask the operator for slot approval before paying for registration (0.0005 TAO on 2026-10-30, all 256 slots taken).
  5. Finish the proof task, bind your hotkey with ormas-miner register-hotkey, then take offers.

Caveats

  • Almost no emission reaches independent miners: the owner's uid holds 99.2% of incentive (2026-10-30). What stands behind the 2 other earning uids is not public.
  • The team runs acceptance and a miner of its own; whether the other 5 permit holders are independent of it is unknown.
  • Usage is the team's word: public network figures say 'insufficient data' and the case study is its own backlog.
  • Docs say independent Protected-tier mining is not open, yet a 2026-10-18 team post said a slot was approved; the treasury top-up is only planned.
