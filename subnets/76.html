What it is

Ormas, run by Heron Cove LLC, sells verified coding outcomes. A client supplies a GitHub repo, a task and a test; miners quote a price, deliver a branch and are paid only if the test passes.

How it works

Miners

A miner runs the open ormas-subnet software against the Ormas gateway. For each queued task it offers a firm price, a limit (estimate plus hard ceiling) or declines; the lowest expected total cost wins. The winner makes the change, runs the tests and publishes the branch.

Validators

A checker re-runs the client's test on the branch and signs accept or reject. The reference checker is public, but the team runs all acceptance today. The README says a weight service relays a gateway-computed vector.

Incentive

An accepted delivery freezes an emission target of 2x its settled USD price (since 2026-10-05); unearned emission goes to the owner's uid. On 2026-11-27 that uid held 72.5% of emission; the five other validators shared 27.5% as dividends and independent miners nothing.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Bids on coding tasks and is paid only if the client's tests pass on the delivered branch.

  • After approval and a proof task the claim cap rises from 0 to 50 (docs).
  • Pay is 2x the settled USD price as an emission target, in alpha; cost above your limit is your loss. SDK 0.0.4: you name your own USD price (--ask-usd).
  • Independent uids earned nothing at the latest read (2026-11-27).

Validator High barrier

Re-runs the client's test on a delivery and signs accept or reject; the team does this today.

  • 6 permit holders on 6 coldkeys, only uid 0 owner-linked; all rank the owner's uid first (2026-11-27).
  • Dividends: owner's uid 45.0%, uid 1 32.9%, uid 99 19.1%, the other three 3.0%.

Client Low barrier

Posts a GitHub repo, a task and a test; pays only for a delivery that passes.

  • An unclaimed job ends after 60 minutes at $0 (API docs). The client installer, paused earlier, is offered again (docs, 2026-11-27).

Getting started

  1. Check eligibility: the beta is US-only and the operator approves each miner.
  2. Sign in at ormas.ai and mint a runner key (no expiry).
  3. Install the miner (Python 3.10+), run ormas-miner login and register a miner name.
  4. Ask the operator for slot approval before paying for registration (0.0005 TAO, all 256 slots taken).
  5. Finish the proof task, bind your hotkey with ormas-miner register-hotkey, then take offers at your own USD price (--ask-usd).

Caveats

  • No steady emission reaches independent miners: the owner's uid held at least 98.5% of incentive in every read since 2026-10-26; who runs uids 13–16 is not public.
  • The team runs its own miner.
  • Usage rests on the team's feed: network acceptance 100% but the operator-run provider 70%, and no independent uid earns incentive now.
  • Miners report non-selection with no reason shown; no team chat post since 2026-10-18, and chat after 10-30 was unreadable on 11-27.
  • Docs say independent Protected-tier mining is not open, yet a 2026-10-18 team post said a slot was approved.
