What it is

engy sells LLM inference on subnet 53. Buyers call one OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible API with prepaid billing; GPU miners serve open models such as Kimi K3, GLM and DeepSeek. The team's master validator scores the work and signs the weights others copy.

How it works

Miners

Miners boot the team's Docker image on a GPU host with a gateway key, declare a model and SSH port, and the team verifies and activates the box. The gateway sends buyer requests round-robin.

Validators

The master validator is the owner hotkey: it scores miners from the gateway log, samples requests to verify (per the team) and signs one result per 7-day epoch. Outside validators check the signature and resubmit it.

Incentive

A miner's score is the dollar value of its billed work in a 7-day epoch; a model row serving under 95% of requests scores zero. Split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners, none burned.

Who controls scoring

  • Chain: the result equals the master validator's signed vector; outside validators cannot change it (2026-11-07).
  • Owner cut 18% plus about 16.9% as master-slot dividends: up to about 35% of emission.
  • Chain: five permit holders never set weights; all are miners earning 32.2% of incentive (uids 73 and 7 lost their permits). Uids 86, 149 and 182, none under the owner's keys, take 50.2%; their operators are unpublished.

Getting started

  1. Register a miner hotkey on SN53 (hardware: one H200 or 8 x H100, by model), log in at provider.engy.ai and claim a gateway key.
  2. Boot ghcr.io/hanlinai/engy-golden-serve:prod with only that key as ENGY_MINER_KEY.
  3. Declare the model and an SSH port the team can reach; wait for activation.
  4. Leave the box untouched. To validate instead, run the repo's CPU-only light validator with a permit hotkey.

Caveats

  • The master scorer is not public, and its published formula does not reproduce the signed scores.
  • Verification is unseen (no audit verdicts in epochs 17 to 20); which hotkeys are the team's is unpublished.
  • Usage and revenue are team data; dashboard and signed counts differ (36 miners vs 33 hotkeys). No licence file.
