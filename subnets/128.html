What it is

ByteLeap says it is a decentralized GPU cloud: miners earn alpha only while their GPUs are rented. Validators relay lease scores from the team's closed MeshHub service. Since 2026-11-12 six miner uids share the incentive; no public data shows rented compute.

How it works

Miners

A miner is a Python node that gathers Go worker agents running on bare-metal GPU hosts. Only leased GPUs earn; answering validator challenges earns nothing. Access: team allowlist (2026-08-18), then own GPUs (2026-09-20).

Validators

A validator links to the team's MeshHub service, stores the per-worker lease scores MeshHub sends, sums them per miner every 420 seconds and submits the shares as weights. No heartbeat for 30 minutes scores 0.

Incentive

A miner's weight is its share of all lease scores; nobody outside the team can recompute them. Configured split: 41% validators, 41% miners, 18% owner; RecycleOrBurn is Burn.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Connects bare-metal GPU hosts through a Python miner and Go worker; paid in alpha only while its GPUs are rented.

  • Worker needs Ubuntu 22.04+ on bare metal (no Docker or VMs); installed from a team-hosted script or zip.

Validator High barrier

Relays the team's MeshHub lease scores into on-chain weights; measures no GPU itself.

  • The example config ships a pre-filled MeshHub endpoint and token in a public repo; it may be revoked.
  • 23 hold a permit (2026-11-21); 11 set weights, all on the same six uids; 12 do not.
  • MeshHub can override its scoring and weight settings; it updates itself from GitHub releases unless run with --no-auto-update.

Getting started

  1. Check your hardware against the miner requirements above.
  2. Ask in the subnet channel whether your machine can join.
  3. Install the worker, bind the GPUs to VFIO, reboot, then run miner and worker with a registered hotkey.

Caveats

  • Lease scores come from a closed, team-run MeshHub; nobody outside the team can check that rented compute exists.
  • Chat shows what the team said; its revenue, full-rental, partner and buyback claims were not checked.
  • The earning uids' operators are unknown; no owner link on chain does not show independence or rentals.
  • Not read: the 2026-08-12 economic report, the team's Discord, chat after 2026-10-26, most of the weight tool.
