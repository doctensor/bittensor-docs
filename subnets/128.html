What it is

ByteLeap says it is a decentralized GPU cloud: miners earn alpha only while their GPUs are rented. Validators do not test the GPUs; they relay lease scores from the team's closed MeshHub service. Since 2026-11-12 six miner uids share the incentive (one earned it all before), and no public data shows rented compute.

How it works

Miners

A miner is a Python node that gathers Go worker agents running on bare-metal GPU hosts. Only leased GPUs earn; answering validator challenges earns nothing. Access: a partner compute-center allowlist (2026-08-18), then 'connect your own GPU' (2026-09-20).

Validators

A validator links to the team's MeshHub service, stores the per-worker lease scores MeshHub sends, sums them per miner every 420 seconds and submits the shares as weights. No heartbeat for 30 minutes scores 0.

Incentive

A miner's weight is its share of all lease scores; nobody outside the team can recompute them. Configured split: 41% validators, 41% miners, 18% owner; RecycleOrBurn is Burn.

Getting started

  1. Check fit against the miner requirements above and the team's GPU list.
  2. Ask in the subnet channel whether your machine can join.
  3. Install the worker, bind the GPUs to VFIO, reboot, then run miner and worker with a registered hotkey.

Caveats

  • Lease scores come from a closed, team-run MeshHub; nobody outside the team can check that rented compute exists.
  • Chat shows what the team said; its revenue, full-rental, partner and buyback claims were not checked.
  • The earning uids' operators are unknown; no owner link on chain does not show independence or rentals.
  • Not read: the 2026-08-12 economic report, the team's Discord, chat after 2026-10-26, most of the weight tool.
