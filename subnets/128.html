What it is

ByteLeap says it is a decentralized GPU cloud: miners earn alpha only while their GPUs are rented. Validators do not test the GPUs; they relay lease scores from the team's closed MeshHub service. By 2026-10-30 one miner uid earned all miner incentive, and no public data shows rented compute.

How it works

Miners

A miner is a Python node that gathers Go worker agents running on bare-metal GPU hosts. Only leased GPUs earn; answering validator challenges earns nothing. Access is limited: an allowlist of partner compute centers (2026-08-18), then 'connect your own GPU' (2026-09-20).

Validators

A validator links to the team's MeshHub service, stores the per-worker lease scores MeshHub sends, sums them per miner every 420 seconds and submits the shares as weights. No heartbeat for 30 minutes scores 0.

Incentive

A miner's weight is its share of all lease scores; nobody outside the team can recompute them. Configured split: 41% validators, 41% miners, 18% owner; RecycleOrBurn is Burn. Owner uid 0 earned dividends only: 14.8% of emission on 2026-10-30, falling to under 0.01% on 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-07.

Getting started

  1. Check fit against the miner requirements above and the team's GPU list.
  2. Ask in the subnet channel whether your machine can join (allowlist 2026-08-18; self-connect 2026-09-20).
  3. Install the worker, bind the GPUs to VFIO, reboot, then run miner and worker with a registered hotkey.

Caveats

  • Lease scores come from a closed, team-run MeshHub; nobody outside the team can check that rented compute exists.
  • Chat is what the team said; its claims on revenue, full rental, partner compute centers and buybacks were not checked.
  • The earning uid's operator is unknown; no owner link on chain does not show it is independent or renting GPUs.
