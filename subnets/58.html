What it is

Attune (Robotensor) pays for Vector, where miners submit robot-model weights that validators duel in simulation (30%, to four champions), and Horizon, where phone users control simulated robots or record tasks (70%, one vault hotkey).

How it works

Miners

A miner trains weights for one fixed network, uploads only model.safetensors to a gated Hugging Face repo and commits its hash on chain. One submission per registration.

Validators

Validators duel each entry, oldest first, against the king on 240 units (one demonstration in one scene, the task in another); a challenger needs +3.0 points. They give the Horizon vault 70% of their weights.

Incentive

Of miner emissions, 30% goes to Vector's four newest champions (40, 30, 20, 10%) and 70% to one Horizon vault hotkey; unclaimed weight goes to the owner's hotkey and burns.

Getting started

  1. Train weights for the fixed vector_v1.1 network under Level 4 variation (Level 3 plus clutter); the tensor layout is pinned.
  2. Install robotensor-attune 0.3.0, set a Hugging Face token, run attune miner check, upload (private, gated) and commit the digest.
  3. Keep the repo online until your duel: newer code drops an entry after 3 failed downloads; a retry needs a new registration.
  4. Validators: only one named Hugging Face account's token starts the code; plus a GPU host and the vault in config.
  5. Horizon needs no hotkey: sign in at horizon.robotensor.ai, install the app, link a wallet.

Caveats

  • Weights are validator opinions, not payouts: the chain shows who gets weight, not that duels run.
  • Vault payouts began 2026-11-12: its uid's stake fell from 14,259 to 207 alpha, then daily batches of 12, 16, 19 and 23 transfers left its coldkey (15,503 alpha now). The chain links the vault to neither owner key.
  • Scores are noisy: the king's last 10 duels scored 83.8% to 89.2%; the best challenger led by 2.5 points (3.0 needed).
  • PyPI 0.3.0 (2026-11-09) predates the repo head (11-13). Team chat after 2026-10-31 was unreadable.
