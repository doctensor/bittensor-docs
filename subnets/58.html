What it is

Attune (Robotensor) pays for Vector, where miners submit robot-model weights that validators duel in simulation (30%, to four champions), and Horizon, where phone users control simulated robots or record tasks (70%, one vault hotkey).

How it works

Miners

A miner trains weights for one fixed network, uploads only model.safetensors to a gated Hugging Face repo and commits its hash on chain. One submission per hotkey.

Validators

Validators queue commitments oldest first and duel each entry against the king on 240 units: one demonstration in one scene, the task in another. A challenger needs +3.0 points. They also give the Horizon vault 70% of their weights.

Incentive

Of miner emissions, 30% goes to Vector's four newest champions (40, 30, 20, 10%) and 70% to one Horizon vault hotkey; unclaimed weight goes to the owner's hotkey and burns.

Getting started

  1. Train weights for the fixed vector_v1.1 network under Level 4 variation (Level 3 plus clutter); the tensor layout is pinned.
  2. Install robotensor-attune 0.3.0, set a Hugging Face token, run attune miner check, upload (private, gated) and commit the digest.
  3. Keep the repo online until your duel: newer code drops an entry after 3 failed downloads; a retry needs a new hotkey.
  4. Validators: only one named Hugging Face account's token starts the code; plus a GPU host and the vault in config.
  5. Horizon needs no hotkey: sign in at horizon.robotensor.ai, install the phone app, try practice mode.

Caveats

  • Weights are validator opinions, not payouts: the chain shows who gets weight, not that duels run.
  • Vault payouts are off chain; the chain links the vault to neither owner key. Its stake rose 7,742 to 11,158 alpha in 1.6 days: nothing left it.
  • Scores are noisy: the king's duel scores ranged 77.9% to 82.1% over 9 defenses; it took the crown by 4.2 points (3.0 needed).
  • PyPI 0.3.0 (2026-11-09) predates the repo head (11-10). Team chat after 2026-10-31 was unreadable.
  • Horizon's FAQ dropped its 10% cap per person on 11-08; the rewards page says none; the top contributor holds 9.3%.
