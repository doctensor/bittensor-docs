What it is

Attune (Robotensor) pays for Vector, where miners submit robot-model weights that validators duel in simulation (30%, to four champions), and Horizon, where phone users control simulated robots or record tasks (70%, one vault hotkey).

How it works

Miners

A miner trains weights for one fixed network, uploads only model.safetensors to a Hugging Face repo and commits its hash on chain. One submission per hotkey.

Validators

Validators queue commitments oldest first and duel each entry against the king on 240 units (16 tasks x 15 runs): one demonstration in one scene, the task in another. A challenger needs +3.0 points. They also give the Horizon vault 70% of their weights.

Incentive

Of miner emissions, 30% goes to Vector's four newest champions (40, 30, 20, 10%) and 70% to one Horizon vault hotkey; unclaimed weight goes to the owner's hotkey and burns.

Getting started

  1. Read the live spec (spec.json in vector-orchestrator): level, runs and units changed within a month.
  2. Train weights for the fixed vector_v1.1 network under Level 3 variation; the tensor layout is pinned.
  3. Install robotensor-attune, set a Hugging Face token, run attune miner check, then submit for netuid 58.
  4. Keep the repo online until your duel: newer code drops an entry after 3 failed downloads; a retry needs a new hotkey.
  5. Validators: an NVIDIA GPU host, three Python environments or Docker, a Hugging Face token, the vault named in config.
  6. Horizon needs no hotkey: sign in at horizon.robotensor.ai, install the phone app, try practice mode.

Caveats

  • Weights are validator opinions, not payouts: the chain shows who gets weight, not that duels run.
  • Vault payouts are off chain, paid by Robotensor; none yet. The team says Robotensor owns the vault; the chain links it to neither owner key.
  • Pay versus cost is disputed: the team says 30% covers it, miners say a strong model costs more.
  • Scores are noisy: the king's averages ranged 81.7% to 88.3% over 20 defenses; its crowning win was 9 units net, so an equal model passes by luck about 1 time in 12 (estimate).
  • The newest repo code is not in the PyPI release (0.2.2). The slot earlier held Greevils; the team says Attune launched 2026-09-18. Team chat after 2026-10-31 was unreadable.
  • Horizon's FAQ still says a 10% cap per person; its rewards page says none, and one contributor holds 70.5% of this week's points.
