What it is

Attune (Robotensor) pays Vector (robot models, 30%) and Horizon (phone contributors, 70%). One owner validator sets all weights. Vector was rebuilt; the owner's 30% now goes to a new uid, 443.

How it works

Miners

Vector miners commit one expert script (a docker image) per hotkey and serve it to Vector's simulator. Horizon contributors register a hotkey in the app and earn points by robot control and recording.

Validators

Code: vector-validator sets Vector's signed weights; the attune validator weights Horizon hotkeys by score. Chain: only the owner hotkey may validate.

Incentive

Docs: a third of Vector's share to ranks 1 to 3, two thirds to contributors by points. Chain now: 70% Horizon miners by score, 30% uid 443.

Getting started

  1. Vector: read its new docs; one submission per hotkey, ever, after a 0.1 TAO fee; no rank is paid yet.
  2. Install vector-miner, write the expert, test it, push to ghcr.io, commit once, serve phase 1.
  3. Horizon: sign in at horizon.robotensor.ai, install the app and register in it with a new, empty wallet.
  4. Validators: closed on chain (one slot, the owner's).

Caveats

  • Weights are validator opinions; with one validator they are the owner's alone and do not show that scoring runs.
  • Code reads Horizon's scores from a configured URL; we could not see that feed.
  • Who holds uid 443 and why it gets Vector's 30% is not stated; no round is decided, so Vector's ranks are untested.
  • The vault is retired: week 45 went out in 7 daily transfers (14,044 alpha); its coldkey holds 15,067 alpha, linked to neither owner key.
  • Horizon docs say weeks close Thursday 12:00 UTC, its dashboard Friday 14:00. Team chat after 10-31 was unreadable.
