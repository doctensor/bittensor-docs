What it is

Attune, from Robotensor, builds adaptive robot intelligence: a model sees one demonstration and must repeat the task in a different simulated scene with frozen weights. Miners train one fixed 781M-parameter network; validators duel entries against the king. The four newest champions are paid, the rest is burned.

How it works

Miners

A miner trains weights for one fixed network and uploads only model.safetensors to a public Hugging Face repo; a commitment with its hash goes on chain. One submission per hotkey; a repo missing at its turn drops the entry.

Validators

Validators queue commitments oldest first and duel each entry against the king on 240 units (16 tasks x 15 runs): one demonstration in one scene, the task in another. A challenger needs +3.0 points. A duel takes about 90 minutes and every record is public.

Incentive

Vector gets 30% of miner emissions, split among the four newest champions 40, 30, 20 and 10%. The rest goes to the owner's hotkey and is burned until more competitions open. On chain (2026-10-30) the owner's uid holds 70.2% of incentive.

Bear case

  • 70% of incentive goes to the owner's uid and is burned.
  • Miners report thin pay, spam and 9 to 14 hour waits; Horizon is not open.

What would change this view: More emission reaching miners, spam and queue fixes, or named users would help; outflow or a quiet repo would hurt.

Getting started

  1. Read the live spec (spec.json in vector-orchestrator): level, runs per task and unit count changed within a month.
  2. Train weights for the fixed vector_v1.1 network under Level 3 variation; the tensor layout is pinned.
  3. Install robotensor-attune, set a Hugging Face token, run attune miner check, then attune miner submit for netuid 58.
  4. Keep the repo public; a retry needs a new hotkey (0.3 TAO).
  5. Validators: one NVIDIA GPU host, three Python environments or the Docker image, a Hugging Face token.

Caveats

  • Weights are validator opinions, not payouts: the chain matches the code's 70% burn and 12/9/6/3% split, but whether validators run duels or mirror the owner's row is unknown.
  • Pay versus cost is disputed: four uids share about 9.7 TAO a day (gross); miners say a strong model costs $1,000+, the team says 30% covers it.
  • Scores are noisy: one king scored 76.7% to 80.0% in three duels against a 3.0-point margin; spam and near-copies are disputed.
  • Horizon has no published rules, round or code.
  • The slot earlier held Greevils; the team says it launched on 2026-09-18, so older history may belong to others.
