What it is

Subnet 2 launched on 2024-04-10 as Omron (later DSperse), where miners returned zero-knowledge proofs. On 2026-10-16 it passed to DaniLabs, which is building an AI inference network for speed and low cost. Its validators weight only the owner's uid, so miner emission is burned; paid mining has no date.

How it works

Miners

No miner is paid today. The miner guide asks for an H100 80 GB (GPT-OSS-20B); uids 6, 126 and 146 serve GLM-5.3-Flash, which the scorer lacks.

Validators

DaniLabs runs one validator (uid 14), live since 2026-10-26; it paused 11-16 to 11-18 and weights again. All six permit holders weight that uid.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validator, 41% miner; the miner share is burned (chain, 2026-11-26). The team hoped to cut the burn by early November (chat, 2026-10-23); the docs are now silent.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Serve model requests through DaniLabs' signed kit. Pay is burned until the team lifts it.

  • No DaniLabs account or seed upload needed; Python 3.10-3.12 and OpenSSL 3+ (docs).
  • Some miners qualify by invitation (guide). The miner source is private; a signed release (0.2.2) is public.

Validator High barrier

Set weights from a permit; the new installer only verifies and scores. Only the team's burn weight is live.

  • Six of 64 permit slots are held. Owner uid 14 gets 72% of dividends, uid 124 19%, uid 5 7%, uid 64 1%. The other five hold about 1% of the subnet's alpha (probably root stake, inference).
  • On 2026-11-26 all six had set weights within one tempo (about 72 minutes).

How scoring is set up

  • Signed reports (72 minutes each) began 2026-10-26. Miner uid 58 handled about 1,400 requests, the last on 11-11. Uid 6 completed 335 on GLM-5.3-Flash on 11-19 and pinged until 11-21 01:08 UTC; the 59 reports after it are silent. Pings resume in the window ending 11-24 02:24 UTC: since then uid 6 completed 315, uid 126 181 and uid 146 23 (3 failed), all on GLM-5.3-Flash. Four windows were never published.
  • Miners are scored on decode speed 50%, first-token time 35%, reliability 10% and ping 5%, but only for GPT-OSS-20B; other models, such as GLM-5.3-Flash, are skipped (code, 11-23). Weights are only logged, and the repo's only signer is the burn signer (source read 2026-11-08).

Getting started

  1. Mining: the miner guide covers an installer for H100 80 GB hosts (some by invitation); no miner is paid. Follow #danilabs.
  2. Validators: the installer in the validator guide (CPU host, no key) verifies and scores reports but sets no weights; a permit needs stake.
  3. Distrust old DSperse instructions and 'Dsperse wallet' sites: chat warns of wallet-phrase scams.

Caveats

  • All weight on the owner uid is one configuration read (2026-11-26); the burn's end date is the team's hope.
  • Measurements come from DaniLabs' own gateway; a signature proves origin, not correct output. Unexplained: the 11-21 to 11-24 silence, who runs the miner uids, the 4 missing reports, why uid 14 paused.
  • No revenue is visible and the docs no longer mention payments; they give no zero-data-retention guarantee.
  • Not known: who runs the other five permit holders, whether old DSperse software runs, the earlier project disputes.
  • Team chat after 2026-11-01 was not read (archive unreachable 2026-11-05 to 11-26); newer statements may exist.
