What it is

Subnet 2 launched on 2024-04-10 as Omron (later DSperse), where miners returned zero-knowledge proofs of model predictions. On 2026-10-16 it passed to DaniLabs, which is building an AI inference network for speed and low cost. Its validators weight only the owner's uid, so miner emission is burned; paid mining has no date.

How it works

Miners

No miner is paid today. The docs describe a GPU kit (H100 is the pilot profile) and one approved model, openai/gpt-oss-20b.

Validators

DaniLabs runs one validator (uid 14), live since 2026-10-26; the owner hotkey changed the same day. All six permit holders weight that uid.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validator, 41% miner; the miner share is burned (chain, 2026-11-14). The team hoped to cut the burn by early November (chat, 2026-10-23); the docs are now silent.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Serve model requests through DaniLabs' signed kit. Pay is burned until the team lifts it.

  • Needs Python 3.10-3.12, OpenSSL and an NVIDIA driver; no DaniLabs account or seed upload (docs).
  • Minimum VRAM and disk are not stated; the miner source is private, a signed release (0.2.2) is public.

Validator High barrier

Set weights from a permit; the new installer only verifies and scores. Only the team's burn weight is live.

  • Six of 64 permit slots are held. Uid 14 gets 67% of dividends, uid 124 (registered 2026-10-22) 23%, uid 5 8%; the other five hold about 1% of the subnet's alpha (probably root stake, inference).
  • On 2026-11-14 all six had set weights within one tempo (23 to 335 blocks before the read).
  • The repo's one signer can only weight the owner uid; the installed worker holds no key (code read, not run).

How scoring is set up

  • Signed reports (72 minutes each) ran 2026-10-26 to 10-29, stopped for about five days and resumed on 11-03. One miner (uid 58) appears: 1,301 requests in five windows, 10 failed. None of the 110 reports from 11-05 04:42 UTC to 11-10 16:43 UTC lists a miner. From 17:55 UTC on 11-10 to 17:55 UTC on 11-11 uids 58 and 116 (registered 11-11) show pings; uid 58 completed 84 requests, none failed. The 50 reports since 19:07 UTC on 11-11 show none. Two windows were never published (11-06 01:06, 11-11 08:19 UTC).
  • Miners are scored on decode speed 50%, first-token time 35%, reliability 10% and ping 5%; weights are only logged, and the repo's only signer is the burn signer (source read 2026-11-08; two outside fixes to the quality module merged 11-10). A dry-run module can add independent token and correctness checks (code).

Getting started

  1. Mining: the docs describe an installer for H100 hosts, but no miner is paid and no date is given. Follow #danilabs.
  2. Validators: the installer in the validator guide (CPU host, no key) verifies and scores reports but sets no weights; a permit needs stake.
  3. Distrust old DSperse instructions and 'Dsperse wallet' sites: chat warns of wallet-phrase scams.

Caveats

  • All weight on the owner uid is one configuration read (2026-11-14); the burn's end date is the team's hope.
  • Measurements come from DaniLabs' own gateway; a signature proves origin, not correct output. Unexplained: the feed gap, who runs miner uids 58, 20 and 116, the missing report.
  • Payments are deferred, so no revenue is visible; the pilot docs give no zero-data-retention guarantee.
  • Not known: who runs the other five permit holders, whether old DSperse software runs, the earlier project disputes.
  • Team chat after 2026-11-01 was not read (archive unreachable 2026-11-05 to 11-14); newer statements may exist.
