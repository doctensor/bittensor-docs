What it is

Subnet 2 launched on 2024-04-10 as Omron (later DSperse), where miners returned zero-knowledge proofs. On 2026-10-16 it passed to DaniLabs, which is building an AI inference network for speed and low cost. Its validators weight only the owner's uid, so miner emission is burned; paid mining has no date.

How it works

Miners

No miner is paid today. The miner guide asks for an H100 80 GB (GPT-OSS-20B); the reports show uid 6 serving GLM-5.3-Flash.

Validators

DaniLabs runs one validator (uid 14), live since 2026-10-26; it paused 11-16 to 11-18 and weights again. All six permit holders weight that uid.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validator, 41% miner; the miner share is burned (chain, 2026-11-21). The team hoped to cut the burn by early November (chat, 2026-10-23); the docs are now silent.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Serve model requests through DaniLabs' signed kit. Pay is burned until the team lifts it.

  • No DaniLabs account or seed upload needed; Python 3.10-3.12 and OpenSSL 3+ (docs).
  • Some miners qualify by invitation (guide). The miner source is private; a signed release (0.2.2) is public.

Validator High barrier

Set weights from a permit; the new installer only verifies and scores. Only the team's burn weight is live.

  • Six of 64 permit slots are held. Owner uid 14 gets 66% of dividends, uid 124 24%, uid 5 8%, uid 64 1%. The other five hold about 1% of the subnet's alpha (probably root stake, inference).
  • On 2026-11-21 all six had set weights within the last tempo (about 72 minutes).

How scoring is set up

  • Signed reports (72 minutes each) began 2026-10-26. Miner uid 58 handled about 1,400 requests (10 failed), the last on 11-11. After 160 reports with no activity, uid 6 (registered 11-06) completed 335 requests on GLM-5.3-Flash on 11-19, none failed, and pinged until 11-21 01:08 UTC; the 10 reports since are silent. Three windows were never published (11-06, 11-11, 11-18).
  • Miners are scored on decode speed 50%, first-token time 35%, reliability 10% and ping 5%; weights are only logged, and the repo's only signer is the burn signer (source read 2026-11-08). A dry-run module can add independent token and correctness checks (code).

Caveats

  • All weight on the owner uid is one configuration read (2026-11-21); the burn's end date is the team's hope.
  • Measurements come from DaniLabs' own gateway; a signature proves origin, not correct output. Unexplained: the feed gap, who runs the seven miner uids, the 3 missing reports, why uid 14 paused.
  • No revenue is visible and the docs no longer mention payments; they give no zero-data-retention guarantee.
  • Not known: who runs the other five permit holders, whether old DSperse software runs, the earlier project disputes.
  • Team chat after 2026-11-01 was not read (archive unreachable 2026-11-05 to 11-21); newer statements may exist.
