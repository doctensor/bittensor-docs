What it is

Subnet 2 ran for years as DSperse, where miners returned zero-knowledge proofs of model predictions. On 2026-10-16 it passed to DaniLabs, which says it is building an AI inference network around no data retention, speed and low cost. Its validator is live on Finney but weights only the owner's uid, so the miner side of emission is burned. The team says mining opens 'soon' and gave no date.

How it works

Miners

No miner is paid today. The miner kit is private and the team gives no date: mining opens 'soon' and will need GPUs at the start (chat, 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-29). The scoring code targets one model, openai/gpt-oss-20b, at 300 tokens per second and 500 ms to the first token. One miner uid registered around 2026-10-27 and shows no requests in the public reports.

Validators

DaniLabs runs one validator (uid 14), announced live on 2026-10-26; the owner hotkey on chain changed the same day. All six permit holders put their weight on that uid. The repo's validator scores signed reports from DaniLabs' own gateway, but the results are only logged. The signer that writes weights sends 100% to the owner uid.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validator, 41% miner, and the withheld miner share is burned (chain, 2026-10-30). The team hopes the burn is off or decreasing by the end of the first week of November (chat, 2026-10-23): a hope, not a commitment. Paying miners needs a separate signer review and independent checks of token counts and correctness (repo docs).

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Serve model requests for DaniLabs' gateway. Not open yet: no software, no date, no pay.

  • Only openai/gpt-oss-20b has a target so far; other models need a policy update (repo docs).
  • Token counts are miner-reported and execution is unattested (repo docs).

Validator High barrier

Hold a permit and set weights. Only the team's burn weight is in use today.

  • Six of 64 permit slots are held; the team has not said who runs the other five (chain, 2026-10-30).
  • One permit holder has not set weights for about 3.5 days (chain, 2026-10-30).

How scoring is set up

  • A signed report per epoch (about 72 minutes) is public: 50 exist, 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-29, none newer at the 2026-10-30 check.
  • Each miner and model is scored on decode speed 50%, first-token time 35%, reliability 10% and ping 5%, all multiplied by reliability (policy v3). The weights are logged, never sent.

Getting started

  1. Mining is not open: the team says 'soon' (2026-10-29), gives no date, and the miner kit is private. Follow #danilabs and the X account.
  2. Validators: the repo runs an observer and a scorer that only log; weight submission is off. Onboarding is in a private chat (team, 2026-10-22).
  3. Distrust old DSperse instructions and 'Dsperse wallet' sites: chat warns of wallet-phrase scams.

Caveats

  • All weight on the owner uid is one configuration read (2026-10-30); the burn's end date is the team's hope.
  • Measurements come from DaniLabs' own gateway; a signature proves origin, not correct output. The newest report ended 2026-10-29 11:52 UTC, about 19 hours before the check; the reason is unknown.
  • Who runs the other five permit holders, and whether old DSperse software still runs, is unknown.
  • Disputes about the new lead's earlier project, and Inference Labs' proof counts, were not investigated.
  • Service, platform and miner-kit repos are private; the site's Docs and Sign in pages and the model API need an account, so prices and usage are not visible.
