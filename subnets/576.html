What it is

Microtensor pays miners for small language models fine-tuned for one narrow task (session compaction, page parsing, SQL), with a router and an escalation path, and tests them on hidden items. It lost its mainnet slot in September and now runs a redesigned version as testnet netuid 576, seeking a new slot.

How it works

Miners

Each arena fixes one base model. A miner fine-tunes it and adds a harness, a router and an escalation path (repairs doubtful answers via a frontier model). In the evaluation window it runs the hidden items on its own GPU and uploads a signed transcript of every call (up to 3 uploads per epoch).

Validators

Validators replay each transcript on CPU (every recorded call must match), grade it once labels are out, zero duplicated models and routers and set weights; the published score is the stake-weighted median of their reports. The owner's coordinator re-runs sampled calls on a GPU (40 for compaction) and needs an exact token match; a flag zeroes the miner for three epochs unless an appeal succeeds.

Incentive

Miner emission is split across five pools (model, harness, router, escalation, whole system); the top five in a pool get 40, 25, 15, 12 and 8 percent, and an empty pool is burned. While a new round is live, the earlier result is burned to the owner's hotkey until it settles. The README says 20% per pool; the serving docs set up to 10% aside first (18% per pool).

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Fine-tunes the arena's base model, builds harness, router and escalation, and runs the evaluation on its own GPU.

  • Two Hugging Face repositories per arena: artifact (harness, router, escalation) and model weights.
  • min_compute.yml still names netuid 92 and an RTX 3090 minimum (pre-move). Licence: proprietary, participation only.

Validator High barrier

Replays miner transcripts on CPU, grades them and sets weights; needs relay access granted by the team.

  • Validators run no spot checks; they wait up to 5 hours for the owner's GPU coordinator report.

Inference miner Medium barrier

Serves certified models on its own GPUs through the team's gateway; paid per card from signed receipts.

  • Pay: 90% of metered billing once billing opens (95% until 11-08), plus a serving pool (10% of arena emission under 5,000 USD monthly billing, 5% under 50,000, 2% above), paid per card by success and probe pass rate; one paid hotkey per coldkey, team operators earn nothing.

Getting started

  1. Testnet only: use a testnet wallet and test TAO.
  2. Miners: start from the miner template, pick an arena (ctx_compress is live on testnet), fine-tune its base model and check it with mt check-model.
  3. Commit the digest (72 h window), reveal before Freeze 2, run mt miner run in the evaluation window, upload before Freeze 2; test with mt miner replay first.
  4. Validators and inference miners: follow docs/validator_setup.md or docs/inference_miner.md; validators must ask the team for relay access.

Caveats

  • Quality and cost claims are the team's; no usage or revenue is visible. The slot loss comes from team posts and a moderator note, not the chain.
  • No channel discussion of the new mechanism: participant experience is undocumented; the client-rendered docs site was not read.
