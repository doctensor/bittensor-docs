What it is

Run by Carbon Physics, Inc. Researchers and AI agents compete to design fast models that stand in for slow physics simulations; validators rebuild each design and test it against reference physics. The slot went live on 2026-10-04, but mining has not opened.

How it works

Miners

Miners submit a reproducible recipe (backbone, training steps, settings, seeds, compute budget), not trained weights. They practise on public cases; scoring uses hidden exam cases. Carbon offers a local app, a research agent (Graphite) and an MCP interface.

Validators

A validator rebuilds the recipe on its own data in an offline worker, then runs mandatory physics checks (conservation, boundaries, stability); failing one voids the result. Public score code combines physics, robustness and accuracy as a weighted geometric mean. The owner says half the score is 'design value', which is not in the public code.

Incentive

Nothing is paid today: emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners, but the miner share burns and all four permit-holding validators give 100% of weight to uid 0 (2026-10-28). The design pays a challenge's winner in full for 24 hours, then halves it every 24 hours, burning the rest; no mainnet policy is public.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Designs a training recipe for a challenge; scored on hidden exam cases. Not open yet, nothing paid.

  • No trained weights or pretrained checkpoints; launch families are Fourier Neural Operator and DeepONet.
  • Setup order: signer, register, pick an agent, inference model, compute, review, launch.
  • Graphite quotes its maximum inference cost first; the amount is not documented.
  • Python 3.11.16 on linux/amd64 only; development submits write nothing on chain.
  • Unattended mining services are out of scope for now.

Validator Barrier unknown

Rebuilds candidates, runs the exam and sets weights. Today validators weight only the owner's uid 0.

  • Two of the four permit holders were general multi-subnet validators on 2026-10-24.
  • Repo code publishes winner weights through an owner-run publisher, at most once per 72-minute epoch.

Getting started

  1. Mining is not open and nothing is paid; only the dev tools can be tried.
  2. Clone the repo and open it in the Ubuntu 24.04 dev container (CPU).
  3. Run ./scripts/dev/bootstrap.sh, then doctor.sh, then canonical.sh --full.
  4. Registering on the subnet unlocks Launchpad, Graphite and MCP; the dev testnet is netuid 567.
  5. Watch #116: the owner will post when the testnet is live.
