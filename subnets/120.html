What it is

Affine pays miners for improving one shared language model that reasons through math-style tasks. Each epoch the team trains on one miner's uploaded batch, tests the result on private tasks, and pays that miner only if the model improved. A second mechanism, 10% of emission, runs a video-model contest.

How it works

Miners

There is no mining client any more: a hotkey uploads one JSON file of prompts, its own outputs and its own rewards (up to 125 prompts, 1,000 rollouts). Hotkeys queue by registration and get one turn, ever; the team trains one step on the file and tests it on private tasks.

Validators

Only the owner's validator sets weights (one permit, UID 0). A turn is green if the gain on private tasks is positive, its z-score (gain relative to noise) is above 1.00 and cut-off answers stay under 20%. The bar was z above 0 until 2026-10-30. Red pays nothing.

Incentive

Weight is split equally across the last up to 10 green hotkeys still registered; unearned weight burns to UID 0. On 2026-10-31 ten uids earned 10% each, 7 of them under the older, looser bar. The video contest gets 10% of emission, but the chain paid only UID 0 there.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Uploads one batch file; paid only if its single turn passes the held-out test.

  • Rewards are used as given and nothing is regraded; the held-out test is the only check (team).
  • 14 of 115 turns were green, 1 of 48 since the z > 1 bar; the 7 turns on files so far were all red.
  • A missing or invalid file is skipped, not charged, and keeps your place; 9 of 12 listed files were valid on 2026-10-31.

Getting started

  1. Read the current guide and miner quickstart; the archived older guide's client steps no longer apply.
  2. Check the queue: about 544 hotkeys had not had their one turn on 2026-10-31.
  3. Register a hotkey with the 0.5 TAO burn (proof of work is off), then activate it with the repo's command.
  4. Build one batch file (prompts, your outputs, your rewards) in the published format.
  5. Check it with the helper script, upload it, and confirm 'valid' in the status file.

Caveats

  • Payee and green counts are snapshots: rules changed almost daily and 14 greens in 115 turns is a small sample.
  • The weight writer and the gate are not in the public repo; the pay rule is the team's statement, and the score file and chain match it.
  • The file format is hours old: 7 turns on files so far, all red; old-format batches count until 2026-10-31 09:00 UTC.
  • Model-gain figures (0.875 on 32 held-out tasks, 2026-10-25) are team numbers on a small sample; miner complaints are allegations.
