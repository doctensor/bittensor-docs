What it is

Affine pays miners for improving one shared math model. Mechanism 0: training files; each round crowns the best, and the next round must confirm it before it is paid. Mechanism 1, the Generator (10% of emission): problems the model solves only sometimes; its weight is spread over 261 uids again.

How it works

Miners

Training files: one JSON file (up to 2,000 prompts); one registration buys one turn; 86 seats wait. Generator: sign and upload up to 3 problem IDs from a pool of 811,609 before each 4-hour UTC deadline.

Validators

Only the owner's validator sets weights (UID 0), hourly, for both mechanisms. Each round trains one LoRA per file and scores it on 2,000 public tasks; the best gain wins if z is above 1.5. The next round re-scores the new head against its parent and pays the crown only on a gain with a fresh z above 0.5.

Incentive

Mechanism 0 (90%): up to 10 confirmed winners split the weight equally while their hotkey holds the UID; each held round drops the oldest, down to one (one uid now: 870). Mechanism 1 (10%): an equal share per accepted problem over 24 hours (261 uids); a payout over six hours old burns to UID 0.

Roles and requirements

Miner (training file) Medium barrier

Uploads one file; paid only if it wins a round, then while among the last 10 winners.

  • Per-file settings (learning rate, steps, LoRA rank) are published only if you win.
  • File check 04:27 UTC on 11-28: 3 of 86 waiting seats valid.

Miner (Generator) Low barrier

Signs problem IDs per 4-hour epoch; each accepted problem earns an equal share for 24 hours.

  • Accepted when the frozen head passes 25% to 75% of 8 to 32 samples; a problem pays once, the first upload wins.
  • 00:00 UTC epoch on 11-28: 80 of 1,413 tasks accepted; payout file 02:32 UTC: 362 claims, 264 UIDs; epochs 11-25 to 11-27 12:00 UTC were void.

Validator High barrier

Sets the weights; in practice only the owner's validator does.

  • Judge, payout and weight writers are public in the repo (v2 merged into main on 11-25).
