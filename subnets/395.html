What it is

Entangle Protocol is a cross-chain message relay network on Bittensor. Scanner miners watch source chains for message events, relay miners compete to deliver them on the destination chain, and validators check and score both. The team pitches it as the messaging layer for apps such as buying subnet alpha from other chains. Everything here comes from team documents; no code is public.

How it works

Miners

Two miner types share the subnet. Scanners watch chains for cross-chain events and report them fast; they need private RPC endpoints. Relay miners win sealed-bid auctions and deliver the message from their own funded wallet on the destination chain. Validators check miners about every 10 minutes and skip any that do not answer within 60 seconds.

Validators

Validators ask scanners for events and time their arrival, spot-check about 20 percent of reports, keep a shared gas-price estimate, run the relay auctions, confirm each delivery on the destination chain and set weights every epoch.

Incentive

Scanners are scored by how early their report arrives. Relay score is delivery success 50 percent, speed 25, proof correctness 15, fee efficiency 5 and reliability 5; the auction winner's score adds a bid-quality part. Emission is split 30 percent scanners and 70 percent relays (an owner-set value); the testnet holds two mechanisms split 30 / 70.

Roles and requirements

Miner (scanner) Medium barrier

Watches source chains and reports cross-chain message events to validators as early as possible.

  • Docs: a scanner on a shared public RPC will systematically lose to private, low-latency infrastructure.

Miner (relay) Medium barrier

Wins sealed-bid auctions and delivers messages on the destination chain from its own wallet.

  • Defaults: at most $10 of gas per relay, 12 relays in parallel, tasks with under 20 seconds left are refused.

Validator Medium barrier

Queries scanners, runs auctions, verifies deliveries, signs verified messages and sets weights.

  • Eight neurons hold validator permits out of 64 slots; none has set weights on either mechanism in about 97 days (2026-10-31).
  • Docs: messages execute only after validators holding over 60 percent of registered stake attest.

Getting started

  1. Read the Running a Miner page in the docs: it lists the environment settings for scanners and relay miners.
  2. Register on testnet netuid 395 (btcli with --network test); the subnet does not exist on mainnet.
  3. The miner and validator code is in a GitHub repository that returns 404. Ask in the channel where it is before investing time.

Caveats

  • Testnet only: nothing here describes mainnet emissions or economics. Testnet token and pool numbers are not real value and are left out.
  • No code was read: the linked repository returns 404 (private or removed). Mechanism details come from team docs; the whitepaper was not read and the demo site (test.entangleprotocol.com) does not respond.
  • The testnet looks idle: no incentive, no weight update for about 97 days on either mechanism. Two uids take about 54% and 35% of last-epoch emission; who holds them was not traced. Days assume 12 s per block.
  • Revenue, message-volume and cost figures in the docs (including the running costs quoted above) are team projections, not observed numbers.
