What it is

Quantum Compute (SN48), run by qBitTensor Labs, is the job side of Open Quantum, a platform where users run circuits on real quantum processors (IonQ, Rigetti, IQM, AQT) and pay in dollars. Miners submit the jobs and earn alpha. Usage and savings figures are the team's own.

How it works

Miners

The 2.0 code routes each circuit through a provider adapter (only Open Quantum exists), paid per job from the miner's organization account; nothing checks QPU access, and the README says none is needed. A miner needs 5,000 alpha (more stake, more jobs), a public address and credits.

Validators

A validator pulls jobs from the Open Quantum API, sends each to an eligible miner, skips a miner for 24 hours when its lease stalls or a proof fails, and sets weights from a frozen platform snapshot, not from chain data.

Incentive

The snapshot gives each miner's job cost; a miner is owed cost times a markup (code fallback 1.2). If the total fits the miner emission, weights follow cost share, so miners get the whole emission, not only what is owed; otherwise rank order pays until it runs out.

Getting started

  1. Ask the team first: the README says anyone can mine, the site wants a quantum computer, and most pay goes to one coldkey.
  2. Miner (2.1.2 code): Python 3.12, SDK v11, a hotkey with 5,000 alpha, a public axon, an Open Quantum key, credits above 50.
  3. Validator: ask the team to onboard the hotkey, then run neurons/validator.py with the axon off.

Caveats

  • Who controls the miners' and owner's coldkeys is not shown; the team says it runs the miners (unverified).
  • The weight snapshot sits behind a closed API: costs and markup cannot be rechecked.
  • Weights are opinions, not payouts; who owns uid 141's stake and its parent hotkeys is unread.
  • Open mining is only partly confirmed: uid 2 earns 12%, uid 218 lost 11% within hours, uid 155 earns 0; their owners are unread (team or outside).
  • Usage, traffic share and buybacks are team statements; the whitepaper link was 404 on every check from 11-03 to 11-14; payout design unread.
