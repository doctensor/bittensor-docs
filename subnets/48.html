What it is

Quantum Compute (SN48), run by qBitTensor Labs, is the job side of Open Quantum, a platform where users run circuits on real quantum processors (IonQ, Rigetti, IQM, AQT) and pay in dollars. Miners submit the jobs and earn alpha. Usage and savings figures are the team's own.

How it works

Miners

Version 1 miners had to own a quantum processor. The 2.0 code routes each circuit through a provider adapter (only Open Quantum exists), paid per job from the miner's organization account; nothing checks QPU access, and the README of 2026-11-02 says none is needed. A miner needs 5,000 alpha, a public address and credits.

Validators

A validator pulls jobs from the Open Quantum API, sends each to an eligible miner, bans miners whose lease expires or proofs fail, and sets weights from a frozen platform snapshot, not from chain data.

Incentive

The snapshot gives each miner's job cost; a miner is owed cost times a markup (code fallback 1.2). If the total fits the miner emission, weights follow cost share, so miners get the whole emission, not only what is owed; otherwise rank order pays until it runs out.

Where pay and alpha sit

  • Miner pay (2026-11-07): all incentive on 3 uids of one non-owner coldkey: 91%, 6% and 3%. On 11-06 it was four uids (39%, 27%, 18%, 17%); the coldkey's total, 2,944 alpha a day, did not change.
  • Alpha: the owner's coldkey holds 28% of what was read, the miners' coldkey 24%. Pay does not follow stake: uid 146 holds about 22,700 alpha and takes 91%; uid 206 holds 52,900 and gets 3%.
  • Validators: uid 141 takes 85% of dividends on 51% of the alpha read; 10 of 11 top-weight uid 146.

Getting started

  1. Ask the team first: the README promises onboarding within a week, the guide says coming soon, the site says mining needs a quantum computer.
  2. Miner (2.0 code): Python 3.12, SDK v11, a hotkey on netuid 48 with 5,000 alpha, a public axon, an Open Quantum organization id and SDK key, credits above 50.
  3. Validator: ask the team to onboard the hotkey, then run neurons/validator.py with the axon off.

Caveats

  • Who controls the miners' coldkey and the owner's is not shown; the team says it runs the miners (unverified).
  • Weights come from a snapshot behind a closed API, so costs and the markup cannot be rechecked.
  • Weights are opinions, not payouts; who stakes to uid 141 is unread.
  • Open mining is unconfirmed: the earning uids are one coldkey's; the last full check (2026-10-26) found no new uid since about 2026-10-09.
  • Usage, traffic share and buybacks are team statements; the whitepaper link returned 404 on 2026-11-03, so the payout design is unread.
