What it is

Vanta (formerly the Proprietary Trading Network, by Taoshi) works like an open prop firm. The team sells evaluation accounts on top.

How it works

Miners

A miner picks one asset class, locks alpha as collateral (500 USD of capacity per alpha, 300 to 1,000 alpha) and sends LONG, SHORT or FLAT orders. A hotkey registered after 11-14 00:00 UTC opens a standard account sized once from collateral and trades all markets.

Validators

Validators price orders from paid data feeds, score each miner's profit, drawdown and penalties, and set weights every 5 minutes.

Incentive

Pay is debt-based since December 2025: each week ends Sunday 00:00 UTC, a miner is owed its cumulative profit times penalties minus the emissions already received, and weights follow what is still owed. Weight owed to no one goes to UID 229, the owner's hotkey, and is burned. Since 11-11 a standard account's weight stops at what it is owed: no surplus share, no dust.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Trades on collateral-backed capital; paid weekly on profit.

  • Challenge: drawdown under 5%, return above 10% (8% forex); since 11-11 no minimum days, rank or time limit; dust weight until then.
  • Later a 5% intraday or 8% end-of-day drawdown eliminates; collateral is slashed on losses and burned.
  • Account sizes are fixed from 11-14. A standard account ($5,000 to $100,000, collateral 0.7 to 1.5% of size) auto-promotes to a pro size paid at 1.3x to 2.0x.

Validator Medium barrier

Prices orders, tracks profit and drawdown, sets weights every 5 minutes.

  • Plagiarism scores come from a team-run service and only the team's designated validator slashes collateral.

Entity miner High barrier

One hotkey hosting many trader subaccounts, each with its own size, fees and challenge.

  • Anyone with 1,000 alpha of collateral can register while fewer than 10 entities exist.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/miner.md; pick one asset class (none on a standard account).
  2. Install the repo (it includes the vanta CLI; remove the old vanta-cli package first).
  3. Register a hotkey on netuid 8 with btcli; never reuse an eliminated one.
  4. Stake into alpha with btcli, deposit 300 to 1,000 alpha with the vanta CLI, then (standard account) `vanta account open --tier N`.
  5. Send signals within the drawdown limits or join an entity miner; validators: docs/validator.md.

Caveats

  • Revenue, buyback, burn, collateral and trader counts are team-published, unaudited. Vanta Trading sells simulated evaluation accounts (demo capital; fees 33 to 1,299 USD).
  • The top uid holds a validator permit and has never set weights itself.
  • Critics said in September that burns are miners paying to earn; the team cites evaluation fees.
  • Per a 10-31 report, fills may use lagging prices; the team disputes it. Whether the best bid/offer feed on main since 11-06 answers it is not stated.
  • Whether validators run the new standard-account track, or any account has opened, is not visible.
  • Chat after 10-31 was unreadable; a later team reply is unchecked.
