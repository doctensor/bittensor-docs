What it is

Vanta (formerly the Proprietary Trading Network, built by Taoshi) is a trading subnet that works like an open prop firm. Miners trade crypto, forex, equities or commodities with accounts sized by the alpha they lock as collateral, validators track and price every order, and miners are paid in emissions in proportion to the profit they made. Team-run products on top include Vanta Trading, a prop-firm service that runs as one miner, and Hyperscaled. Whether the subnet earns enough outside revenue to justify its emissions is argued openly in chat and was not resolved there.

How it works

Miners

A miner registers a hotkey, picks one asset class, locks collateral in the subnet's alpha (theta in the docs) and sends LONG, SHORT or FLAT orders. Each theta locked unlocks 500 USD of trading capacity, with a minimum of 300 and a maximum of 1,000 theta. New miners start in a challenge period of up to 90 days and must show at least 61 trading days, no drawdown over 5%, returns above 10% (8% for forex) and a score at or above the 25th miner of their asset class. Passed miners are eliminated at a 5% intraday or 8% end-of-day drawdown. Entity miners are a variant: one hotkey hosts many subaccounts, each with its own account size, challenge and fees, so a single slot can serve many traders.

Validators

Validators receive miners' orders, price them from paid market-data feeds, and keep each miner's positions, PnL, drawdown and penalties in ledgers. The docs set a validator stake of 1,000 alpha and weight setting every 5 minutes. Validators that rebuild their state restore from a checkpoint provided by the team.

Incentive

Since December 2025 the code uses debt-based scoring. Each week ends at 00:00 UTC on Sunday; a miner's needed payout is its cumulative PnL in USD times penalties up to the end of the previous week, the actual payout is the emissions it has received (in USD), and weights are proportional to the remaining difference. Every miner also gets a small floor weight that depends on its stage (3x for main competition, 2x for probation, 1x for challenge). Any weight not owed to a miner goes to a burn address (UID 229 on mainnet). Average daily PnL carries 100% of the score; other metrics are tracked at 0%. On chain on 2026-10-21 the emission split between owner, validators and miners was 0%, 50% and 50%, matching the team's 2026-10-20 post, and the subnet's alpha emission was 1 alpha per block.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/miner.md in the vanta-network repo, choose one asset class, and plan around its leverage caps and trading hours.
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 8 (the chain showed a 0.0005 TAO registration burn on 2026-10-21). Never reuse a hotkey that was eliminated or deregistered: the network blacklists it permanently.
  3. Deposit collateral with the Vanta CLI: 300 to 1,000 theta, worth 500 USD of capacity each. Collateral can be slashed on losses.
  4. Send signals and stay inside the drawdown limits. To leave the challenge period you need at least 61 trading days, returns above 10% (8% forex) and a top-25 score in your asset class.
  5. Alternatively join an entity miner as a subaccount (Vanta Trading is one) or register your own; see docs/entity_miner.md for fees and cross-margin rules.
  6. To validate, follow docs/validator.md: 1,000 alpha stake, paid price-data feeds, and docs/regenerating_validator_state.md to restore state.

Caveats

  • Chain state was read as aggregates: one UID holds 77.6% of incentive, but its owner was not identified and validator stakes were not read. The team says Vanta Trading is an entity miner; the chain does not confirm which UID that is. Weights are validator opinions, not payouts.
  • Revenue, buyback, burn, collateral and trader-count figures come from the team (chat and its own sites). The dashboard and tokenomics pages are client-rendered and could not be read from the research machine, so no burn or buyback totals are published here.
  • Whether the subnet creates enough outside revenue is disputed in chat: critics call burns internal token flows, the team points to eval fees, on-chain payouts and copy-trading. Neither side posted audited figures in the channel.
  • Emissions were cut for the subnet in early September 2026 according to chat; the date they resumed, and the reasons beyond the team's account, were not found.
  • Docs and README lag code in places: the README still describes signals generically, while the code and chat describe a prop-firm model. The 2025 pinned posts (for example 175 USD of capacity per alpha) are superseded by the current 500 USD.
  • Null Consensus, permissionless Vanta Trading with USDC rails and the agentic trading platform were announced in chat only; the chain still showed 256 maximum UIDs on 2026-10-21.
  • A community report of a changed burn and reserve split (70% and 30%) on 2026-10-16 is not confirmed by code or chain.
