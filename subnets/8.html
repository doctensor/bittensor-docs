What it is

Vanta (formerly the Proprietary Trading Network, by Taoshi) works like an open prop firm: miners trade on accounts sized by the alpha they lock and are paid in emissions in proportion to profit. The team sells evaluation accounts on top (Vanta Trading).

How it works

Miners

A miner picks one asset class, locks alpha as collateral (500 USD of capacity per alpha, 300 to 1,000 alpha) and sends LONG, SHORT or FLAT orders. Entity miners are a variant: one hotkey hosts many trader subaccounts.

Validators

Validators price every order from paid data feeds, track each miner's profit, drawdown and penalties, and set weights every 5 minutes.

Incentive

Pay is debt-based since December 2025: each week ends Sunday 00:00 UTC, a miner is owed its cumulative profit times penalties minus the emissions already received, and weights follow what is still owed. Weight not owed to anyone goes to UID 229, the owner's own hotkey, so it is burned. Scoring code unchanged since 2026-10-15.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Trades one asset class on collateral-backed capital; paid weekly in proportion to profit.

  • Challenge period up to 90 days: 61+ trading days, drawdown under 5%, return above 10% (8% forex), top-25 score; dust weight until then.
  • Later a 5% intraday or 8% end-of-day drawdown eliminates; collateral is slashed on losses and burned.

Validator Medium barrier

Prices orders from paid data feeds, tracks profit and drawdown, sets weights every 5 minutes.

  • Chain, 2026-10-30: 18 permit holders, 14 with weights set. Plagiarism scores come from a team-run service and only the team's designated validator slashes collateral.

Entity miner High barrier

One hotkey hosting many trader subaccounts, each with its own size, fees and challenge.

  • Vanta Trading is the team's own entity miner; the team says anyone will be able to run one later (see the table).

Where the pay goes

Chain, 2026-10-30.

  • The miner half of emissions is about 114 TAO a day before costs; one uid takes 91.8% of incentive and five take all of it.
  • The chain does not say who runs the top uid; the team says Vanta Trading is one entity miner. 12 of 14 validators put their top weight on it, which a deterministic scorer produces anyway.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/miner.md and pick one asset class.
  2. Install the repo (the vanta CLI comes with it; remove the old vanta-cli package first).
  3. Register a hotkey on netuid 8 (0.0005 TAO burn on 2026-10-30); never reuse an eliminated one.
  4. Stake into alpha and deposit 300 to 1,000 alpha as collateral with the CLI.
  5. Send signals within the drawdown limits, or join an entity miner; to validate, see docs/validator.md.

Caveats

  • Revenue, buyback, burn, collateral and trader counts are team claims with no audited figures. Vanta Trading sells simulated evaluation accounts (demo capital, fees about 33 to 1,299 USD).
  • The top uid is not identified. It holds a validator permit and has never set weights itself.
  • Critics said in September 2026 that burns are miners paying to earn; the team points to evaluation fees and payouts. Unresolved.
