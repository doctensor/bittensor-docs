What it is

Netuid 47 changed hands on 2026-11-13: GPUForge was removed and a new owner registered the number one block later. The chain identity names it Optia (tryoptia.com), now a live paid AI brand-visibility service. No repo or docs say what miners or validators do; the owner's uid takes all incentive.

How it works

Miners

Not described: no task, repo or docs exist (2026-11-21). 217 of 256 uids are registered, 216 before the registration cost rose on 2026-11-18 and one since. Only the owner's uid 0 earns incentive, and that share is burned; the other 216 earn nothing.

Validators

Not described; no validator software is published. Two uids hold permits, the owner's uid 0 and uid 5 (not owner-linked, registered 2026-11-14); both put 100% of their weight on uid 0.

Incentive

About 5,888 alpha a day (about 59 TAO) is shared after an 18% owner cut. Half is the miner side: uid 0 takes all of it and it is burned (about 29 TAO a day). The other half goes to validators: uid 0 about 77%, uid 5 about 23% (about 7 TAO a day).
