What it is

Score pays miners for computer-vision models. The team publishes a list of tasks: seven public object-detection tasks and two private ones (football events, cricket deliveries). Its own validator scores miners against ground truth, and the best miner per task takes that task's share.

How it works

Miners

Public tasks: train an open-source model, publish it on Hugging Face, host it on Chutes and commit it on chain. Private tasks: run a Docker image that answers challenges on port 8000 and commit its registry address.

Validators

The team's validator sends challenges, scores answers against ground truth from Score and its data partners, and sets weights. Public tasks score 0.6 x box accuracy plus 0.4 x a false-positive score, averaged over 3 days. Private images are spot-checked; mismatches are blacklisted.

Incentive

The pool splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. A public task's share goes to its best miner; if scores are within the task's margin (0.05; 0.045 room dirtiness; 0.039 fallen person), the earlier on-chain commit wins. Private tasks pay the top three 80/15/5 with no tie-break.

  • A public winner earns about 4.6 TAO per day (gross, 2026-10-31).

Miner (private tasks) Medium barrier

Serve a Docker image that answers football or cricket challenges within 30 seconds.

  • The commit must carry the exact task id, or the miner is skipped.
  • Varied solutions, OCR of cricket speed or a model left out of the image get hotkeys blacklisted (team).

Validator High barrier

Fetch challenges, score miners, set weights; in practice the team's validator decides payouts.

  • Private miners only answer the team's validator, so others cannot score private tasks.

Who is paid

  • 13 uids earn miner incentive (2026-10-31): seven public winners at about 5% each, the football podium (36 / 7 / 2%) and the cricket podium (15 / 3 / 1%).
  • The football uids share one coldkey, the cricket uids another: 65% of miner pay sits on two coldkeys. The chain does not say who holds them; the football uids show identical published scores.

Getting started

  1. Install the sv tool, create a wallet and register a hotkey on subnet 44 (burn about 0.063 TAO).
  2. Public: get a developer-enabled Chutes account and Hugging Face token, meet the 100 ms limit on 2 vCPUs, run sv deploy-os-miner.
  3. Private: build the /challenge Docker image, push it to a private registry, give Score read access, set your axon, run sv deploy-pt-miner.
  4. Read the pinned rules post for your task; tasks and shares change.
  5. Commit once and keep your hotkey registered: a new commit resets your score.

Caveats

  • Shares come from a team-published list that can change without a code change (read 2026-10-29).
  • Payouts rest on one team-run validator and team ground truth; chain weights are validator opinions, not payouts.
  • Miners dispute the tie-break and labels almost daily; the team removes only clear annotation mistakes.
  • The team denies self-mining; the owner's wallet earns nothing, but the chain cannot say who holds the paid coldkeys.
  • Docs lag code: the README names a root MINER.md that does not exist; the card-grading task is not live.
