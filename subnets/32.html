What it is

It's AI detects whether a text was written by a person or an AI model. Miners run classifiers and score batches that validators build. The company behind it, ITSAI Technologies, sells the detector as a website, API and browser extension; its accuracy claims are its own.

How it works

Miners

A miner runs its own text classifier and returns, for each text in a batch, the probability that an AI wrote it. The shipped baseline would not survive on the live network (FAQ), so miners bring their own model. Since v3.15.0 answers can be encrypted.

Validators

A validator builds each round: human texts from the Pile and AI texts from 30+ open models via Ollama, plus a Common Crawl set as out-of-domain test. It queries a random sample of miners, re-asks a few texts to catch inconsistent answers, scores them and sets weights on chain.

Incentive

Reward is the average of F1, a false-positive score and average precision on in-domain texts. It is zero if the running out-of-domain F1 is under 0.9, answers leave 0 to 1, or re-asked answers differ. A softmax across sampled miners follows. Pay then depends on stake-weighted validator agreement.

When validators break

Pay follows validators' weights combined by stake, so a validator holding over half the stake can zero every miner (a member's explanation, 2026-10-30; the chain matches).

  • 2026-10-28: at 09:00 UTC the only weight-setting validator pointed at the owner's UID, so all incentive went there (the setting is Burn). Normal again by 2026-10-29.
  • 2026-10-30: incentive fell to zero between 12:00 and 15:00 UTC and was still zero at 21:00. UID 222 had one weight target, itself.
  • The team replied to earlier outages within a day but gave no cause; no reply on this one by 17:47 UTC.

Getting started

  1. Get a 24 GB GPU (RTX 4090 advised) and your own detector; the baseline fails the quality bar.
  2. Clone the repo, pip install -e ., download the DeBERTa weights named in docs/mining.md.
  3. Register with btcli s register --netuid 32 (0.0301 TAO on 2026-10-30; slots are full). No alpha stake needed.
  4. Run neurons/miner.py under pm2 with an open port; test with detection/data_generator.py. Testnet 87 is idle.
  5. Check the channel first: payouts were zero on 2026-10-30. Validators: docs/validating.md.

Caveats

  • Payouts hang on few validators; no cause or operator is known for the 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-30 failures.
  • Accuracy, benchmark, press and revenue figures are the team's own and unverified; members doubt the detector is current (2026-10-10).
  • A minimum alpha rule exists in code but defaults off; whether any validator sets it is unknown.
  • A CPU miner and registration margins are member claims only.
