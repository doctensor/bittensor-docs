What it is

Graphite is a financial knowledge graph: miners extract entities and facts from SEC filings. The team sells access through a portal and an MCP server. Most emission goes to UIDs under the owner's key, and the subnet is for sale.

How it works

Miners

Miners watch each validator's feed of new SEC filings, extract entities and facts, and post signed submissions with source URL, hash and quote. Any stack works; the repo has regex and LLM reference miners.

Validators

Validators fetch the sources themselves, check hash and quote, and score eight measures. Scores of 0.3 or more are merged into the graph on a team-run server; a verified fact that contradicts an unverified one halves that miner's multiplier.

Incentive

Code: weight is the sum of a miner's scores times its slash multiplier, scaled to 20%; the other 80% goes to one burn UID, rotating among owner-coldkey UIDs 20, 74, 102, 155 and 177.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Extract facts from SEC filings; post signed, sourced submissions.

  • SEC requires a real contact email.
  • The default client connects to validators with 10,000+ stake plus the owner; the guide's example says 1,000.

Validator High barrier

Verify sources, score submissions, merge to the graph, set weights hourly.

  • The owner can revoke the items a validator merged.
  • Two outside UIDs share 0.18% of dividends, about 0.02 TAO a day.
  • Two of 10 permit holders set weights this tempo; the outside one also validates on at least seven other subnets.

Where emission goes

On chain, 2026-11-06, gross:

  • Miner and validator pool: about 5,888 alpha (23 TAO) a day, half each.
  • Owner-key UIDs take 89.9%: 79.9% of the miner side sits on one rotating owner UID (UID 20 now), which also holds 99.8% of validator dividends, shared with its stakers.
  • Five outside miners share about 591 alpha (2.34 TAO) a day; two outside validators about 5 alpha (0.02 TAO).

Getting started

  1. Ask in the subnet channel first: the subnet is for sale and the 1,000 alpha stake is unconfirmed.
  2. Read docs/miner_readme.md for wire format and signing; its weight formula is outdated.
  3. Register a hotkey on subnet 43 (burn 0.1 TAO on 2026-11-06) and set a real SEC contact email.
  4. Start from a reference miner (regex or LLM); connect to the validators in the metagraph.
  5. Post signed submissions with source URL, sha256 and quote; split or repeated reports get slashed.

Caveats

  • The subnet is for sale: ownership, rules and emission could change quickly; the emission split is owner-settable (unchanged here).
  • Live validators may run unpublished code; later rules exist only in chat.
  • UID 20's hotkey holds 87% of staked alpha (owner coldkey 14%, two others 42% and 31%); who they are and the hotkey's cut were not read.
  • Graph size and contributions come from one team API answer on 2026-11-03 (silent since); usage and revenue are unverified.
  • Miners accuse the owner of self-mining; the team denies it. The API lists no owner-key contributor, but other keys are not ruled out.
  • Chat after 2026-11-01 is unread; a later sale update would not show here.
