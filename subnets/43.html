What it is

Graphite is a financial knowledge graph: miners extract entities and facts from SEC filings, each with a source URL, hash and quote. The team sells access through a portal and an MCP server. Most emission goes to UIDs under the owner's key, and the subnet is for sale.

How it works

Miners

Miners watch each validator's feed of new SEC filings, extract entities and facts, and post signed submissions with source URL, document hash and quote. Any stack works; the repo has a regex and an LLM reference miner.

Validators

Validators fetch the sources themselves, check hash and quote, and score eight measures, from accuracy to latency. Scores of 0.3 or more are merged into the graph on a team-run server; a verified fact that contradicts an unverified one halves that miner's multiplier.

Incentive

Code: weight is the sum of a miner's scores times its slash multiplier, scaled to 20%; the other 80% goes to one burn UID rotating among UIDs 74, 102, 155 and 177. Those and UID 20, the owner's hotkey, sit under the owner's coldkey.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Extract facts from SEC filings; post signed, sourced submissions.

  • SEC requires a real contact email.
  • The default client connects to validators with 10,000+ stake plus the owner; the guide's example says 1,000.
  • Some miners were blacklisted for splitting documents (2026-09-06).

Validator High barrier

Verify sources, score submissions, merge to the graph, set weights hourly.

  • Merges go to the team's graph server by signed request; the owner can revoke a validator's items.
  • UID 20 holds 98.2% of dividends; three others share 1.8%, about 0.2 TAO a day.
  • Four permit holders set weights this tempo; one permit is under the owner's key.

Where emission goes

On chain, 2026-10-30, gross:

  • Miner and validator pool: about 5,888 alpha (23 TAO) a day, half each.
  • Owner-key UIDs take 88.6%: the chain withholds 78.9% of the miner side, and UID 20 holds 98.2% of validator dividends.
  • Five outside miners share about 620 alpha (2.45 TAO) a day; three outside validators about 52 alpha (0.2 TAO).

Getting started

  1. Ask in the subnet channel first: the subnet is for sale and the 1,000 alpha stake is unconfirmed.
  2. Read docs/miner_readme.md for wire format and signing; its weight formula is outdated.
  3. Register a hotkey on subnet 43 (burn 0.1 TAO on 2026-10-30) and set a real SEC contact email.
  4. Start from a reference miner (regex or LLM) and connect to the validators in the metagraph.
  5. Post signed submissions with source URL, sha256 and quote; the team slashes split or repeated reports.

Caveats

  • The subnet is for sale: ownership, rules and emission could change quickly.
  • Live validators may run unpublished code; later rules exist only in chat.
  • UID 20 holds 98.2% of validator dividends; whether they are paid out or burned was not traced.
  • Graph size and contribution counts are team claims: the API host did not answer on 2026-10-26 or 2026-10-30.
  • Miners accuse the owner of self-mining and the team denies it; dollar figures in chat are unverified. Who runs the other validators is not shown.
