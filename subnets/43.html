What it is

Graphite started as a graph-optimization subnet (routing and portfolio problems) and in April 2026 relaunched as a Financial Knowledge Graph: miners extract entities, relationships and facts from financial documents, starting with SEC filings, and attach a source URL, hash and quote to every claim. The team plans to sell access to the resulting graph through APIs. Only a fifth of the weight is set on miners; the rest is put on burn UIDs registered with the owner's key. Miners and the team have argued since May over whether anyone outside the team can earn, and on 2026-10-15 the team said the subnet is for sale.

How it works

Miners

Miners run their own extraction process on any stack, subscribe to each validator's event feed (new SEC filings) or submit their own discoveries, and post signed knowledge deltas to every validator. Each claim carries the source URL, a sha256 of the document and a quoted passage. Costs are the miner's own (compute, and an LLM API if used). The team said on 2026-09-06 that miners must hold 1,000 alpha staked to be scored, but that rule is not in the public repo.

Validators

The validator fetches whitelisted source documents itself (sec.gov by default, plus official sites of twelve countries added on 2026-06-03), hashes them and checks the miner's quotes. It scores each submission on eight weighted measures (accuracy, provenance, reproducibility, novelty, latency, depth, freshness, coverage) with minimum accuracy, provenance and novelty floors for discoveries. A verified fact that contradicts an unverified one halves the earlier miner's multiplier, and the penalty compounds. On 2026-07-22 the team said it runs only the owner's validator.

Incentive

Weights are set hourly. In the repo, a miner's weight is the sum of its scores times its slash multiplier (the README says mean times the square root of the submission count, but the code and a 2026-05-29 team post use the sum). The miner weights are scaled to 20% of the total and the other 80% goes to one burn UID chosen by rotation. On chain on 2026-10-21 UID 20, which shares a coldkey with the subnet owner and holds a validator permit, had 79.1% of incentive; four other UIDs shared about 21%. The team says owner-key UIDs are burned; this was not traced.

Getting started

  1. Read the repo's miner guide for the wire format, signing and scoring rules, and note that the README's weight formula is older than the code.
  2. Register a hotkey on subnet 43 (registration burn was 0.1 TAO on 2026-10-21).
  3. Write or fork an extraction process; two reference miners are in the repo, a regex one and one that uses an LLM API you pay for.
  4. Connect to the validators found in the metagraph, take new filings from their event feed, and post signed submissions with source URL, hash and quote.
  5. Ask in the subnet channel whether the 1,000 alpha miner stake announced on 2026-09-06 is in force and whether the subnet has changed hands, because both are unconfirmed and the team has said it is for sale.

Caveats

  • The team listed the subnet for sale on 2026-10-15; ownership, rules and emissions could change at short notice.
  • The repo's last commit is 2026-06-03. The 1,000 alpha stake, fact-based counting and other later rules exist only in chat.
  • README and guides describe weight as mean score times the square root of the submission count; the code and the team's 2026-05-29 post use a sum.
  • Burn UIDs in the repo defaults (74, 102, 155, 177) differ from the owner-key UID that held most incentive on 2026-10-21 (UID 20), so the live validator is configured differently from the repo defaults or runs newer code.
  • Miners accuse the owner of self-mining and of blocking others; the team denies it. Dollar-per-day figures quoted in chat were not verified.
  • The metagraph dashboard showed no data when read on 2026-10-21, so every contribution or graph size figure in chat is a team claim.
  • Who runs the four other weight-setting validators and the four non-owner incentive UIDs is not shown by chat or chain.
  • The chain identity still points at the old Graphite-Subnet repository, which has not changed since 2025-10-12.
