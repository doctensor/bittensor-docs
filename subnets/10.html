What it is

Pareton (SN10) pays miners to speed up open-source AI model serving. The team pins a model, GPU setup and engine; miners submit patches, scored in rounds, and the leader holds until beaten by 1 percent. A new campaign opened on 2026-11-12; no paying customer is shown.

How it works

Miners

Write a git patch against the campaign's pinned commit, upload it and record its hash on chain with the repo's CLI, which pays the fee. Patches stay private unless a campaign opts into a delayed reveal.

Validators

The team's watcher builds each patch in a pinned container, benchmarks it against the leader on one rented pod and checks answer quality. Score: speedup per prompt-length tier minus a failed-prompt penalty; a challenger must win by 1 percent.

Incentive

Emission splits 18 percent owner, 41 validators, 41 miners. The team's weights pay an open campaign's seated leader 40 percent of the weight, falling to 0 over about 28 days from the winning round (a new win restarts it), and burn the rest through the owner's UID (now all of it: the open campaign has no leader). Between campaigns all miner reward burns; validators still earn dividends.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Writes a patch that speeds up the campaign's pinned engine and commits it on chain with a fee.

  • A campaign is open on 2026-11-13: Muse Spark Open, 8x H200, 0.2 TAO fee; no round has run yet.
  • Pull the public baseline image by digest; a local rebuild scores differently. Use --dry-run; avoid --timelock, which never reveals.
  • You keep the copyright, but a commit lets Pareton run, change, ship and publish the patch; no royalty.

Validator Medium barrier

Sets weights: copies the team's vector with a script, or runs its own full benchmark stack.

  • The team runs the validator that decides leaders (UID 201, the owner's UID).
  • 15 hotkeys hold permits; 14 set weights, all on UID 201, the team's 100% burn vector (2026-11-13).

Getting started

  1. Read the open campaign's manifest in the API: pinned commit, allowed paths, model, GPUs, scoring rule, fee.
  2. Benchmark your patch on matching GPUs.
  3. Submit with the miner CLI; builds take hours.
