What it is

Pareton (SN10) pays miners to speed up open-source AI model serving. The team pins a model, GPU setup and engine; miners submit patches, scored in rounds, and the leader holds until beaten by 1 percent. One campaign is open; no paying customer is shown.

How it works

Miners

Write a git patch against the campaign's pinned commit, upload it and record its hash on chain with the repo's CLI, which pays the fee. Patches stay private unless a campaign opts into a delayed reveal.

Validators

The team's watcher builds each patch in a pinned container, benchmarks it against the leader on one rented pod and checks answer quality. Score: speedup per prompt-length tier minus a failed-prompt penalty; a challenger must win by 1 percent. Other validators copy the team's weights.

Incentive

Emission splits 18 percent owner, 41 validators, 41 miners. The team's weights pay the seated leader of an open campaign 40 percent of the weight, falling to 0 over about 28 days from the round the leader won (a new win restarts it), and burn the rest through the owner's UID (now 39.7 leader, 60.3 burned). With no open campaign all miner reward burns; validators still earn dividends.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Writes a patch that speeds up the campaign's pinned engine and commits it on chain with a fee.

  • Pull the public baseline image by digest; a local rebuild scores differently. Use --dry-run; avoid --timelock, which never reveals.
  • You keep the copyright, but a commit lets Pareton run, change, ship and publish the patch; no royalty.

Validator Medium barrier

Sets weights: copies the team's vector with a script, or runs its own full benchmark stack.

  • The team runs the validator that decides leaders (UID 201, the owner's UID).
  • 15 hotkeys hold permits and 14 set weights: 13 follow the team's 60 / 40 split, one sends all to UID 201 (2026-11-07).

Getting started

  1. Read the open campaign's manifest in the API: pinned commit, allowed paths, model, GPUs, scoring rule, fee.
  2. Pull the public baseline image by digest; benchmark your patch on matching GPUs.
  3. Run the miner CLI with --dry-run, then submit; builds take hours.

Caveats

  • The team sets pay: all 14 weight setters rank the owner's UID first (2026-11-07).
  • Idle gaps burn all miner reward (2026-10-19 to 10-21, 10-28 to 11-04).
