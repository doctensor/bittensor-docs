What it is

Beam moves data between cloud storage services and between software agents. Clients start transfers with an SDK, CLI or Studio; the team's BeamCore service splits them into chunks for miners' orchestrators, whose workers move the bytes. Miners earn by verified megabytes delivered.

How it works

Miners

Miners register a hotkey, sign up with BeamCore (free) and run a Go orchestrator plus worker servers. BeamCore sends chunk jobs to orchestrators; workers move the byte ranges and report back. Room work (live agent-to-agent traffic) is optional.

Validators

Validators check no transfers and compute no scores: they fetch BeamCore's weight vector, submit it to the chain and post a proof. Thirteen uids hold permits (2026-11-06); seven set weights, all ranking one uid first; six have been silent for over five epochs.

Incentive

Routing uses PRISM, a 24-hour head-to-head score between neighbouring orchestrators: reliability first, then speed. Since 2026-10-29 it is also multiplied by the share of the miner's tasks verified successful. Pay follows verified MiB uploaded in that window, ranked into five tiers (table below), then adjusted by bonuses, sale limits and penalties. Split: owner 18%, validators 41%, miners 41%. On 2026-11-06, 216 of 256 uids earned incentive; the top uid held 2.1% and the top ten 19% (54% on 11-04). One coldkey holds ranks 1, 3 and 9 (5.9% together); another holds six uids (8.3%).

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Runs an orchestrator that takes transfer jobs from BeamCore and farms them out to its workers.

  • Needs Go 1.24+, a registered hotkey, a public gateway URL and a TLS certificate. Workers need only a hotkey; worker pay is not documented.
  • Qualifying takes 120 verified standard tasks (40 for Rooms).
  • Failed or cut tasks cost routing; a failed recovery task costs 3 times the first-wave stake.
  • Selling alpha costs weight: about 80% of base emission may be sold, ideally weekly.

Validator High barrier

Fetches BeamCore's weight vector and submits it to the chain; does no scoring of its own.

  • Submits at most every 100 blocks (about 20 minutes); no approval step is documented.

Client Low barrier

Pays to move files or run live Rooms through the SDK, CLI or Studio builder.

Getting started

  1. Build the orchestrator and worker from the beam repo (Go 1.24+).
  2. Register a hotkey on subnet 105 with btcli.
  3. Sign up with BeamCore by signing '<hotkey>:<fee percentage>'; keep the returned orchestrator id and API key.
  4. Set the BeamCore, NATS, gateway and TLS settings from the orchestrator guide; run under a supervisor.
  5. Register workers with the capabilities you want; for Rooms keep their ports reachable and set the coordinator URL.
  6. Complete calibration work until each workload qualifies. To validate instead, run actors/validator.

Caveats

  • Rules changed on 2026-10-19, 10-29 and 10-30 and keep moving; older chat is outdated.
  • The sale multiplier and eligibility reviews are not in the public core repo. Keyless audit routes show only your own scores; telemetry lists need a customer key.
  • Weights are validator opinions, not payouts. Whose stake earns uid 0's 91% of dividends is not shown.
  • Client volume is unknown; Zapier, EVM receipts and failure claims are unverified. Relay receipts are Beam-reported, not DON-attested.
  • Chat after 2026-11-01 was not read; why the top uid keeps changing, and who runs the multi-uid coldkeys, is unknown.
