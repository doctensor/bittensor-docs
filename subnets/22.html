What it is

The subnet behind the Desearch search API. Miners no longer answer searches: since late September 2026 they crawl web pages and embed page text on GPUs, and the team builds its search index from the work that passes validators' checks.

How it works

Miners

Crawl tasks: up to 1,000 URLs fetched through the miner's own proxies, uploaded as extracted text within 3 minutes. Embed tasks: up to 1,000 page texts turned into vectors by the team's model on a GPU within 5 minutes. A task budget (start 1, cap 100) grows with well-paid tasks and halves on failures; strikes mean lockouts of 1, 12, then 48 hours.

Validators

Validators re-fetch sampled pages from a drawn share of uploads (every upload of a new hotkey until 10 passes) and compare the text; for embeddings they re-embed 48 texts and need cosine similarity of 0.999. Each keeps its own ledger from the team's public task log and sets weights itself.

Incentive

Emission splits 30% burn (the burn UID is the owner hotkey), 20% crawl, 50% embed; a pool nobody earned burns too. A miner's share is its credited work over 24 hours, minus what failed checks take back. The burn was 80% (09-30), 70% (10-05), 50% (10-14), 30% since 10-23.

Roles and requirements

Miner (crawl) Low barrier

Fetches up to 1,000 URLs per task through its own proxies; uploads text in 3 minutes.

  • Pages a validator can load but you could not go unpaid; 236 of 246 crawl miners held budget 100+ on 2026-10-30.

Miner (embed) Medium barrier

Embeds up to 1,000 page texts per task on its own GPU; uploads in 5 minutes.

  • Other precisions or model revisions fail the check; vectors must match within cosine 0.999.

Validator High barrier

Re-checks sampled uploads; sets weights from its own ledger.

  • 10 UIDs hold permits and 9 set weights, but only 2 (UIDs 58 and 113) report to the task API.

Task supply and concentration

  • Per-UID shares are flat (none above 1.4% of crawl work), but 246 miner UIDs sit under 19 coldkeys, and three coldkeys hold 40 UIDs each: about 59% of crawl and 60% of embed work (task API, 2026-10-30).
  • The team said on 10-21 that extra UIDs claiming scarce tasks is a flaw with no clear fix. It sets task volume: crawl tasks fell from 466,897 to 301,541 a day since 10-23 while embed tasks rose from 22,563 to 62,443.

Getting started

  1. Choose crawl (proxies, fast uploads) or embed (NVIDIA GPU, 16 GB or more).
  2. Test locally with docs/test-your-miner.md before registering on netuid 22 (burn about 0.012 TAO).
  3. Copy neurons/miners/.env.template to .env, set proxies or the model server, and start with PM2.
  4. Validators need a permit, 10,000 total stake, 20 alpha and a ScrapingDog key (docs/validator.md).

Caveats

  • The task API is team-run and its live code cannot be audited; a critic's claim of favoured keys (10-21) is unproven.
  • Only 2 of 9 weight-setting validators report to the task API; UID 58 shares the owner's coldkey and holds about 75% of dividends.
  • Concentration figures come from the team's task API; chain-side coldkey grouping was not repeated.
  • Search-API usage and revenue are team claims, not visible on chain or in the repo.
  • Reliability reports (outages, 404s, timeouts) are mostly miners' own; whether keys struck on 10-22 were reinstated was not answered.
