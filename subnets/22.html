What it is

The subnet behind the Desearch search API. Since late September 2026 miners no longer answer searches; they crawl pages and embed text on GPUs, and the team builds its search index from the work that passes checks.

How it works

Miners

Crawl tasks: up to 1,000 URLs fetched through the miner's own proxies, uploaded as text within 3 minutes. Embed tasks: up to 1,000 page texts embedded on a GPU within 5 minutes. A task budget (cap 100) grows with well-paid tasks and halves on failures; strikes lock a key out for 1, 12, then 48 hours.

Validators

Validators re-fetch sampled pages and compare the text; for embeddings they re-embed 48 texts and need cosine similarity of 0.999. About 2% of crawl and 11% of embed uploads are checked in a day; the rest are paid at the miner's 72-hour check rate.

Incentive

Emission splits 30% burn (the burn UID is the owner hotkey), 20% crawl, 50% embed; a pool nobody earned burns too. A miner's share is its credited work over 24 hours minus what failed checks take back.

Task supply and concentration

  • Concentration rose: UID 219 (a 2-UID coldkey) holds 3.3% of crawl share, 3x the next UID (0.82% was the largest on 11-10). Coldkeys fell from 73 to 46; the top three hold 40, 35 and 30 UIDs: 52.2% of crawl and 48.5% of embed work.
  • Team, 10-21: extra UIDs claiming scarce tasks are a flaw with no clear fix. Queues ran empty 54,447 (crawl) and 294,248 (embed) times in 24 h.
  • No gap: every full hour in 24 h had tasks (crawl 19,400-20,900; embed 1,400-2,600); median check verdict 127-154 s.
  • Slots keep turning over: in 55 h 73 of 247 crawl and 72 of 246 embed UIDs got a new hotkey. The six coldkeys that took slots on 11-08 and 11-09 hold 27 UIDs again (4 on 11-10); replaced UIDs earned about a third of the average share (inference: pruning).

Getting started

  1. Choose crawl (proxies, fast uploads) or embed (NVIDIA GPU, 16 GB or more).
  2. Test locally with docs/test-your-miner.md before registering on netuid 22 (burn about 0.07 TAO, it moves with demand).
  3. Copy neurons/miners/.env.template to .env, set proxies or the model server, and start with PM2.

Caveats

  • The task API is team-run and its live code cannot be audited; a critic's claim of favoured keys (10-21) is unproven.
  • Search-API usage and revenue are team claims, not visible on chain or in the repo.
  • Reliability reports are mostly miners' own; no team post after 10-23 and chat unreadable after 11-02, so the 11-07 task gap and 11-08 verdict slowdown stay unexplained.
