What it is

IOTA, run by Macrocosmos, pretrains language models by splitting one model into layers across many unreliable machines. Most small participants use the Train at Home app (Apple Silicon Macs only).

How it works

Miners

The orchestrator gives each miner one layer of a training run; the miner computes forward and backward passes, uploads weights at set points and downloads merged weights. Slow miners can be dropped.

Validators

A thin client: it downloads scoring code from the orchestrator, runs the tasks it is given and sets the orchestrator's miner scores as chain weights. If the orchestrator does not answer, it copies the stake-weighted weights of the other validators.

Incentive

A miner's weight is its share of its run's score, scaled by the run's burn factor; the docs say unearned emission is burned to the owner. Chain split (2026-11-03): 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. The orchestrator, not the validators, decides which UIDs get weight. The team says pay flows through a payout wallet; the chain cannot confirm this.

Getting started

  1. Apple Silicon Mac, 16 GB RAM minimum, 10 to 30 GB free disk, fast stable internet.
  2. Download Train at Home from the IOTA website and press Start; no account needed.
  3. Paste your public coldkey address into the app to receive rewards; it never asks for private keys.
  4. Expect a queue; keep the Mac plugged in and update the app.

Caveats

  • The chain shows where weight points, not who is paid. The second incentive UID changed on 2026-11-01.
  • Runs stall often; users report queue resets, peer connection failures and, in September, missing payouts (32 open user issues).
  • Team performance claims and the three pilots are unverified; the dashboard's loss and throughput feeds omit the nine live runs.
  • The chat archive failed on five days since 2026-11-02 (502 or hung): nothing after 2026-10-31 was read.
