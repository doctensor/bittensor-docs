What it is

IOTA, run by Macrocosmos, pretrains language models by splitting one model into layers across many ordinary, unreliable machines. Miners earn emissions for verified work. Most small participants join through the Train at Home app (Apple Silicon Macs only); the team's orchestrator controls assignments and scoring.

How it works

Miners

The orchestrator gives each miner one layer of a training run. The miner computes forward and backward passes, uploads its weights at set points and downloads the merged weights. Runs come in Iron, Bronze, Silver and Gold tiers; the slowest or lowest-scoring miners can be dropped.

Validators

A thin client: it downloads scoring code from the orchestrator, runs the tasks it is given and sets the orchestrator's miner scores as chain weights. Per the docs, scores check forward-pass activations over about a day.

Incentive

A miner's weight is its share of its run's score, scaled by the run's burn factor; unearned emission is burned. Chain split (2026-10-30): 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. The team says Train at Home pay flows through a payout wallet in proportion to work done; the chain cannot confirm this.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Runs one layer of a training run on a Mac via Train at Home; paid through a payout wallet.

  • Docs: Linux planned, Windows unsupported; keep the Mac plugged in, reconnects may drop rewards.
  • Community helper (2026-10-23, not in the docs): 24 GB RAM recommended, 200 Mbit/s upload minimum; the slowest uploaders are dropped each epoch.
  • Team post (2026-06-23): pay needs a successful weight upload and is forfeited if a run's loss breaks.
  • Users report queue resets and an M4 crash (GitHub issues 224, 226, 227).

Validator High barrier

Runs the team's scoring code and sets the orchestrator's miner scores as weights.

  • Scoring functions come from the orchestrator and are not in the public repo; the repo guide gives no hardware or stake figures.
  • Whether the orchestrator accepts outside validators is not documented.

Getting started

  1. Check your Mac: Apple Silicon, 16 GB RAM minimum, 10 to 30 GB free disk, fast stable internet.
  2. Download Train at Home from the IOTA website and press Start; no account needed.
  3. Paste your public coldkey address into the app to receive rewards; it never asks for private keys.
  4. Expect a queue; keep the Mac plugged in and update the app when asked.

Caveats

  • Scoring code is private, so per-miner scoring comes from the docs, not code.
  • The chain shows where weight points, not who is paid; the top incentive UID changed at least five times from June to October 2026.
  • Runs stall often; users report queue resets and, in September, missing payouts. Token economics are undocumented.
  • Team performance claims (100B model, 5x faster merging) and the three pilots are unverified.
