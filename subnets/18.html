What it is

Zeus, built by Ørpheus AI, is a weather-forecasting subnet. Miners submit hourly forecasts, validators score them against ERA5 reanalysis data once real values exist, and most emission goes to the miners that win the most head-to-head comparisons.

How it works

Miners

Miners run their own models and forecast four variables (2 m temperature, 100 m wind, surface solar radiation) on a 0.25 degree global grid at 48 hours and 15 days. Before the real values are known the miner commits a hash of its forecast on chain, so it cannot copy a rival; the validator later checks the forecast against it.

Validators

Validators wait for the real ERA5 values, then score each miner's error (mean of RMSE and MAE) weighted by area and by a per-cell map the team added on 2026-08-25 (wind and solar capacity, population for temperature). About 80% of each map's weight sits on 1% of cells (36% in Germany, 44% in the rest of Europe). Bad shapes, non-finite or negative-solar values and near-copies are penalised.

Incentive

Eight challenges (four variables times two horizons) carry fixed weights. In each, the miner with the best win rate over its last 4 (48 h) or 8 (15 d) scored challenges takes 95% of the weight; the rest falls on a sliding scale. Until 2026-10-16 average rank decided. Until 2026-11-20 validators also sent a team-set burn share to owner uid 84; uid 200 held 43% on 11-28.

Getting started

  1. Read the mining guide in the repo: wallet, registration on subnet 18, commit-then-reveal.
  2. Start from the default miner; it already commits a hash on chain and reveals later.
  3. Train on public ERA5 data; a GPU speeds training, inference runs on about 4 CPU cores and 16 GB RAM.
  4. Match the required forecast shape and keep the exact bytes you committed; a hash mismatch is penalised.
  5. Watch the dashboard and Discord: scoring rules changed several times since August.

Caveats

  • Burn: a team API outsiders cannot read set the share (owner slot 2-67% to 11-20, then 0%); team-labelled uid 200 earns 43%. Uid 150 (no weights set) earned 14% on 11-23, then 0.1%: unexplained.
  • Benchmark, user and revenue claims are the team's own; how the dataset picks its named miner is unstated.
  • The forecast dataset paused 10-28 to 11-06 and has had no upload since 11-24; files were not hash-checked.
  • Miners questioned fairness in chat (self-mining, anonymous top miners); not audited.
