What it is

Zeus, built by Ørpheus AI, is a weather-forecasting subnet. Miners submit hourly forecasts, validators score them against ERA5 reanalysis data once real values exist, and most emission goes to the miners that win the most head-to-head comparisons.

How it works

Miners

Miners run their own models, usually trained on public ERA5 history, and forecast four variables (2 m temperature, 100 m wind, surface solar radiation) on a 0.25 degree global grid at 48 hours and 15 days. Before the real values are known the miner commits a hash of its forecast on chain, so it cannot copy a rival; the validator later checks the full forecast against the hash.

Validators

Validators wait for the real ERA5 values, then score each miner's error (mean of RMSE and MAE) weighted by area and by a per-cell map the team added on 2026-08-25 (wind and solar capacity, population for temperature). About 80% of each map's weight sits on 1% of cells; 36% is in the Germany box, 44% in the rest of Europe. Bad shapes, non-finite values, negative solar radiation and near-copies of another miner (not checked for solar radiation) are penalised.

Incentive

Eight challenges (four variables times two horizons) carry fixed weights. In each, the miner with the best win rate over its last 4 (48 h) or 8 (15 d) scored challenges takes 95% of the weight; the rest falls on a sliding scale. Until 2026-10-16 average rank decided. Validators also send a burn share, set by a team service, to owner uid 84.

Getting started

  1. Read the mining guide in the repo: wallet, registration on subnet 18, commit-then-reveal.
  2. Start from the default miner; it already commits a hash on chain and reveals later.
  3. Train on public ERA5 data; a GPU speeds training, inference runs on about 4 CPU cores and 16 GB RAM.
  4. Match the required forecast shape and keep the exact compressed bytes you committed; a hash mismatch is a penalty.
  5. Watch the dashboard (delivery and scores per miner) and Discord: scoring rules changed several times since August 2026.

Caveats

  • Burn: a team API outsiders cannot read sets the share; the real level shows only on chain (table above).
  • Benchmark, user and revenue claims are the team's own. The public dataset names a miner and rank per forecast; how they are chosen is unstated.
  • The forecast dataset resumed on 2026-11-06 after a pause from 10-28 and updated on 11-07; files were not hash-checked.
  • Miners questioned fairness in chat (self-mining, anonymous top miners); not audited.
