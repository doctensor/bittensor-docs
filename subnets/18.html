What it is

Zeus, built by Ørpheus AI, is a weather-forecasting subnet. Miners submit hourly forecasts, validators score them against ERA5 reanalysis data once real values exist, and most emission goes to the miners that win the most head-to-head comparisons.

How it works

Miners

Miners run their own models, usually trained on public ERA5 history, and forecast four variables (2 m temperature, 100 m wind, surface solar radiation) on a 0.25 degree global grid at 48 hours and 15 days. Before the real values are known the miner commits a hash of its forecast on chain, so it cannot copy a rival; the validator later checks the full forecast against the hash.

Validators

Validators wait for the real ERA5 values, score each miner's error (mean of RMSE and MAE, weighted by latitude and geography) and rank miners per variable and horizon. Since 2026-08-25 Germany carries 40% of the weight, the rest of Europe 40% and the world 20%. Bad shapes, non-finite values, negative solar radiation and near-copies are penalised.

Incentive

Eight challenges (four variables times two horizons) carry fixed weights. In each, the miner with the best win rate over its last 4 (48 h) or 8 (15 d) scored challenges takes 95% of the weight; the rest falls on a sliding scale. Until 2026-10-16 average rank decided. Validators also put a team-set burn share on owner uid 84.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Trains a weather model and answers forecast challenges, committing a hash first.

  • Winning needs a model that beats the best rival per challenge: the top 5 uids take 92.7% of incentive (2026-10-30).
  • Gross pay: about 9.8 TAO per day over 25 uids, 0.39 on average.

Validator Medium barrier

Sends challenges, scores forecasts against ERA5 and sets weights on chain.

  • 9 hotkeys hold permits out of 64 allowed slots and all 9 set weights within one tempo (2026-10-30).
  • The owner's uid 84 holds 56.8% of dividends, so the team validator earns most validator emission.

Getting started

  1. Read the mining guide in the repo: wallet, registration on subnet 18, commit-then-reveal.
  2. Start from the default miner; it already commits a hash on chain and reveals later.
  3. Train on public ERA5 data; a GPU speeds training, inference runs on about 4 CPU cores and 16 GB RAM.
  4. Match the required forecast shape and keep the exact compressed bytes you committed; a hash mismatch is a penalty.
  5. Watch the dashboard and Discord: scoring rules changed several times since August 2026.

Caveats

  • Burn: the share comes from a team API; validators put 9.5% of incentive on burn uid 84 on 2026-10-30, against the team's May figure of about 50%.
  • The README and both guides still describe rank averages; the code has used win rate since 2026-10-15, and old geography constants remain in it.
  • Benchmark claims against ECMWF and revenue figures are the team's own; how sold forecasts relate to miner output is not stated.
  • Miners questioned fairness in chat (self-mining, anonymous top miners); the team points to on-chain hashes, not audited here.
