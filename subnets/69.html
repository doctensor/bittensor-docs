What it is

Herald is a subnet for earned media. PR firms upload an article's text before publishing, get it placed in a listed news outlet and submit the link; validators run a code-only check and weight each contributor by the verified value. No model, no GPU. The owner is DSV Fund (team-stated), which also sells placements itself.

How it works

Miners

PR firms and professionals pick a brief, upload and sign the article text before publishing, publish in a listed outlet and add the link. The old miner program was removed on 2026-11-13.

Validators

Each day validators read new submissions from the team's backend and check signature, brief, outlet (team-signed registry of 215), live page and date, 60% text match, editorial not paid, and topic.

Incentive

An article is worth 500 x tier (1.0, 0.6 or 0.2) x search (1.0 if indexed, 0.5 if not), paid over 30 days while it stays live. A contributor's weight is its payable value divided by the larger of all payable value and that day's miner emission in dollars; the rest goes to UID 0 and is burned.

Getting started

  1. Contributors: sign in to the Herald console with Google and request to become a miner; the team approves by hand.
  2. After approval, connect a wallet and register your own miner key (burn about 0.0005 TAO, not refunded).
  3. Open a brief, upload and sign your draft before publishing, publish in a listed outlet, then add the link.
  4. Validators: follow docs/validator.md; you need the team's credential, fetch and search keys, a permit and stake.

Caveats

  • Payouts are unproven: the team says none paid, the ledger shows 0 alpha and the chain shows no incentive.
  • Briefs, approvals, the ledger and signing keys sit with the operator; the registry's signature verifies and matches its on-chain anchor (consistency, not neutrality).
  • Not shown: who the contributors and the 16 other sealed-commitment keys are, why articles expired, who runs the validators or the bittensor 11 build, who unstaked UID 186.
  • The team also sells placements itself (Green Compute, July 2026); fund backing and the SN26 and Lium campaigns are team statements.
