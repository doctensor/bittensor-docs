What it is

ChipForge pays miners to design AI-accelerator chips; validators score them with open tools. Run by the Tatsu team: subnet 84 until 2026-05-01, subnet 108 since 2026-09-20. Quest 001 closed on 2026-10-15; Quest 2 opened on 2026-11-02.

How it works

Miners

Miners download the public Quest 2 package, build an NPU with a compiler and C runtime, test it in Docker, and submit a signed zip (one per hotkey). Winning designs are shown to registered hotkeys after 18 hours.

Validators

Validators run the team's evaluation server with its signed evaluator. A team-run challenge server decides records and sends the payout schedule they follow. A record needs two validators to agree within 0.1%.

Incentive

A new best design is a 'record': from the second on it must win by 20%, wait 24 hours, then is paid for 48 hours; up to three share. Only a fifth of validator weight is for miners; the rest goes to UID 0.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Builds the chip, compiler and runtime, and submits the signed package.

  • 1 submission per hotkey, 60 minutes apart; a queued design can be replaced (2026-11-04). A full local run took about 1.5 hours on 32 vCPUs.

Validator High barrier

Runs the team's evaluation server, scores designs and sets weights.

  • 5 hold permits and set weights within 1 tempo; 3 run the team's software, evaluator V-0.5 ready (2026-11-07). Old evaluator versions filled one validator's disk (215 GB); the server now deletes them.

Getting started

  1. Register a hotkey on subnet 108 (0.05 TAO on 2026-11-07) and clone ChipForge_SN108; the README covers setup.
  2. Download the Quest 2 package (no login) and read npu_v2.0.md; copy the starter template (CPU only, passes the quick check).
  3. Run the local checker in Docker: the quick check takes about 10 minutes, a full score hours.
  4. Submit a signed zip (up to 50 MB) before 2026-11-16 10:06 UTC; the team has paused submissions, so check the rules feed first.

Caveats

  • No record has been set or paid yet; the 16 Quest 2 designs are queued again and the earlier 'voided' / 'exhausted' statuses are unexplained.
  • UID 0 is the owner's own hotkey; what its validator dividends (about 60%) do was not traced.
  • Winners, payouts, submission counts and leaderboard figures come from the team's own server.
  • 2 of 5 permit holders (uids 1 and 2) show no sign of running the team's software; whether they follow payouts is unknown.
  • Open-source designs, fabricated chips and revenue are team statements; none was found. Quest 2 scoring may change (3 W cap, energy scoring announced).
