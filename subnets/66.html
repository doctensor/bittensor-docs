What it is

Conjectures.io pays alpha bounties for the first Lean proof or counterexample of an open maths conjecture that the Lean kernel accepts and a human approves. A second track, Tensor kernels (live since 2026-11-10, replacing TorchLean), pays the fastest proved Lean tensor kernels.

How it works

Miners

Pick an open problem (269 have live bounties), write a Lean proof or counterexample, check it free, then pay one credit and submit with your AI chat logs. Tensor kernels miners submit one proved Kernel.lean.

Validators

The team runs the check: a sandbox builds each submission with the Lean kernel, then a human reviews the chat logs. All 10 permit holders set weights, all topping one treasury neuron.

Incentive

Bounties come from a team treasury (93,721 alpha on 2026-11-12) filled by about 80% of validator weight; the other 20% goes to Tensor kernels champions.

Roles and requirements

Miner (maths board) Medium barrier

Finds a Lean proof or counterexample, pays a fee per attempt, submits with chat logs.

  • No hotkey: use the website or CLI; rewards go to a payout wallet you set.
  • Use CLI 0.2.4 or newer (chat-log terms v6).

Miner (Tensor kernels contest) Medium barrier

Submits one proved Lean file of 19 kernels; the team times it.

  • Timed on the required CPU environments; limits 960 s build, 1200 s benchmark, 3600 s per run.
  • Champions register a new hotkey per entry. The seven earners sit under three coldkeys (98, 49 and 8 of 256 uids); overall champion 9.9% of incentive, each board 1.6%.

Validator High barrier

Permits exist, but the team runs the check and weights follow its treasury.

  • Their other weight goes to Tensor kernels champions.

How bounties are priced

  • A new problem starts at 2.5% of the free treasury and rises to 12.5% over 15 days, locked on submission: about 2,343 alpha on day one and 11,715 at 15 days.
  • A proof with non-compliant chat logs waits for compliant ones before paying (team, 2026-10-28).
  • Proofs approved before chat-log intake opened (2026-10-13) can earn a log bonus: alpha worth USD 500 (Erdős) or 250.

How Tensor kernels is scored

  • One Kernel.lean holds 19 kernels. Seven boards (overall plus six model families) each time your entry against their champion. To win: 0.97x its time or better, a solid gain, no cell over 10% slower (contest repo defaults).
  • The champion takes the board’s share of the 20% contest share: overall 50%, each model board 8.3%; the API pays only the current champion.
  • Each timing pass runs in a fresh process; a baseline kernel is timed for reference only.

Getting started

  1. Create an account on conjectures.io or install the miner CLI, then run conjectures tasks sync and conjectures tasks challenge <task>.
  2. Write the proof in Main.lean, then run conjectures build and the free local checks.
  3. Keep your AI chat logs, buy a credit (0.25 TAO), upload the logs, submit, and set a payout wallet.
  4. Tensor kernels: register a hotkey on SN66 (one submission each), clone the contest repo, follow MANUAL.md, run miner/submit.py.

Caveats

  • Payouts depend on a team treasury and manual review; its balance matches the chain, but payouts were not traced.
  • No revenue yet (owner, 2026-10-13); the plan is to sell AI chat logs, which the team keeps private.
  • Solve counts come from the team's own API; the chain identity still lists the old Ninja repo. Beware of support DMs.
  • A coldkey is not one person; none of the champion coldkeys is linked to the owner.
