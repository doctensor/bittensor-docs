What it is

Conjectures.io pays alpha bounties for the first Lean proof or counterexample of an open maths conjecture that the Lean kernel accepts and a human approves. Side contests pay the fastest proved kernels: Tensor kernels (2026-11-10 to 2026-11-19) and Exact integer kernels (from 2026-11-19).

How it works

Miners

Pick an open problem (278 have live bounties), write a Lean proof or counterexample, check it free, then pay one credit and submit with your AI chat logs. Kernel-contest miners submit one file.

Validators

The team runs the check: a sandbox builds each submission with the Lean kernel, then a human reviews the chat logs.

Incentive

Bounties come from a team treasury (106,298 alpha on 2026-11-27) filled by validator weight (80%, briefly 100%); the rest goes to kernel-contest champions.

Roles and requirements

Miner (maths board) Medium barrier

Finds a Lean proof or counterexample, pays a fee per attempt, submits with chat logs.

  • No hotkey: use the website or CLI; rewards go to a payout wallet you set.
  • Use CLI 0.2.4 or newer (chat-log terms v6).

Miner (kernel contests) Medium barrier

Submits one file to a team-run kernel contest; only Exact integer kernels is open.

  • Exact integer kernels: one entry.c up to 262,144 bytes, scored by instruction counts.
  • Champions register a new hotkey per entry (61 promotions by 2026-11-26). Now uids 44 and 84 (one coldkey of 11 uids) and uid 216 (35 uids).

Validator High barrier

Permits exist, but the team runs the check and weights follow its treasury.

  • Nine of ten give the treasury 80% across four targets; one gives it 100%.

How bounties are priced

  • A new problem starts at 2.5% of the free treasury and rises to 12.5% over 15 days, locked on submission: about 2,657 alpha on day one and 13,287 at 15 days.
  • A proof with non-compliant chat logs waits for compliant ones before paying (team, 2026-10-28).
  • Team repo, 2026-11-25: eight targets retired: the Riemann hypothesis, Erdős 462 and 985 after independent checks, Erdős 143 (ii) and 535 (two) solved elsewhere, Erdős 373 and 393 withdrawn.

How the kernel contests are scored

  • Exact integer kernels: one C file of three correctly rounded float functions in integer arithmetic, scored by instructions on emulated rv32im and thumbv6m chips, checked on all 2^32 inputs.
  • Boards: overall 50%, rv32im 25%, thumbv6m 25% of the 20% contest share; only the current champion is paid.

Getting started

  1. Create an account on conjectures.io or install the miner CLI, then run conjectures tasks sync and conjectures tasks challenge <task>.
  2. Write the proof in Main.lean, then run conjectures build and the free local checks.
  3. Keep your AI chat logs, buy a credit (0.25 TAO), upload the logs, submit, and set a payout wallet.
  4. Exact integer kernels: register a hotkey on SN66 (one submission each), then send entry.c with the miner kit’s miner/submit.py (team docs).

Caveats

  • Payouts depend on a team treasury and manual review; its balance matches the chain, but payouts were not traced.
  • No revenue yet (owner, 2026-10-13); the plan is to sell AI chat logs, which the team keeps private.
  • Solve counts come from the team's own API; the chain identity still lists the old Ninja repo. Beware of support DMs.
  • A coldkey is not one person; none of the champion coldkeys is linked to the owner.
