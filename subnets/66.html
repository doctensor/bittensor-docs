What it is

Conjectures.io pays alpha bounties for the first Lean proof or counterexample of an open maths conjecture that the Lean kernel accepts and a human approves; most statements come from DeepMind's Formal Conjectures repository. A second track, TorchLean (live since 2026-10-28), pays the fastest proved Lean kernels.

How it works

Miners

Pick an open problem (272 have live bounties), write a Lean proof or counterexample, check it free, then pay one credit and submit with your AI chat logs. TorchLean miners submit one proved Kernel.lean.

Validators

The team runs the check: a sandbox builds each submission with the Lean kernel, then a human reviews the chat logs. Ten of 11 permit holders set weights, all topping one treasury neuron.

Incentive

Bounties come from a team treasury (90,262 alpha on 2026-11-07) filled by about 80% of validator weight; the other 20% goes to TorchLean champions, one winner per board.

Roles and requirements

Miner (maths board) Medium barrier

Finds a Lean proof or counterexample, pays a fee per attempt, submits with chat logs.

  • No hotkey: use the website or CLI; rewards go to a payout wallet you set.

Miner (TorchLean contest) Medium barrier

Submits one proved Lean kernel file; the team times it.

  • Entries are timed on amd64 and arm64; limits 960 s to build, 1200 s to benchmark.
  • Champions register a new hotkey per entry; the four current ones share one coldkey that holds 23 of 256 uids.

Validator High barrier

Permits exist, but the team runs the check and weights follow its treasury.

  • Their other weight goes to TorchLean champions. The top dividend earner (uid 227) uses the same keys on seven other subnets.

How bounties are priced

  • A new problem starts at 2.5% of the free treasury and rises to 12.5% over 15 days, locked on submission: about 2,257 alpha on day one and 11,283 at 15 days.
  • A proof with non-compliant chat logs waits for compliant ones before paying (team, 2026-10-28).
  • Proofs approved before chat-log intake opened (2026-10-13) can earn a one-off bonus for accepted logs: alpha worth USD 500 (Erdős) or 250 (others).

How TorchLean is scored

  • Each board times your whole entry against that board's current champion on two CPU types. To win: 0.97x its time or better, a statistically solid gain, and no cell more than 10% slower.
  • The champion takes the board's share (overall 50%, each model board 10% of the 20%). One entry can hold several boards; an unheld board pays the treasury. DEFLATE and FloatLib no longer pay.
  • Contest code of 2026-11-04 hardens timing: a fresh process per sample, a new input per inference, the median of three runs.

Getting started

  1. Create an account on conjectures.io or install the miner CLI, then run conjectures tasks sync and conjectures tasks challenge <task>.
  2. Write the proof in Main.lean, then run conjectures build and the free local checks.
  3. Keep your AI chat logs, buy a credit (0.25 TAO), upload the logs, submit, and set a payout wallet.
  4. TorchLean: register a hotkey on SN66, clone the contest repo, follow MANUAL.md, then run miner/submit.py.

Caveats

  • Payouts depend on a team treasury and manual review; its balance matches the chain, but payouts were not traced.
  • No revenue yet (owner, 2026-10-13); the plan is to sell AI chat logs, which the team keeps private.
  • Solve counts come from the team's own API; the chain identity still lists the old Ninja repo. Beware of support DMs.
  • A coldkey is not one person; none of the champion coldkeys is linked to the owner.
