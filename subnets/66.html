What it is

Conjectures.io pays alpha bounties for the first Lean proof or counterexample of an open maths conjecture that the Lean kernel accepts and a human approves; most statements come from DeepMind's Formal Conjectures repository. A second track, TorchLean (live since 2026-10-28), pays the fastest proved Lean kernels.

How it works

Miners

Pick an open problem (256 have live bounties), write a Lean proof or counterexample, check it free, then pay one credit and submit it with your AI chat logs. TorchLean miners submit one proved Kernel.lean instead.

Validators

The team runs the check: a sandbox builds each submission with the Lean kernel, then a human reviews the chat logs. 10 of the 11 permit holders set weights, all with their top weight on one treasury neuron.

Incentive

Bounties come from a team treasury (84,431 alpha on 2026-10-31) filled by about 80% of validator weight; the other 20% goes to TorchLean champions, winner takes all per board. Emission split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners.

Roles and requirements

Miner (maths board) Medium barrier

Finds a Lean proof or counterexample, pays a fee per attempt, submits with chat logs.

  • No hotkey: use the website or CLI; rewards go to a payout wallet you set.
  • Chat logs of every AI session are required (up to 256 MiB) and may be sold or licensed.

Miner (TorchLean contest) Medium barrier

Submits one proved Lean kernel file; the team times it.

  • To take a board: 0.97x the champion's time or better, no cell over 1.10x slower.
  • Entries are timed on amd64 and arm64; build limit 960 s, benchmark limit 1200 s.

Validator High barrier

Permits exist, but the team runs the check and weights follow its treasury.

  • All 10 that set weights put their top weight on one neuron holding about 81% of incentive.
  • Verification, review and payouts run on the team's own API and database.

How bounties are priced

  • A new problem starts at 2.5% of the free treasury and rises to 12.5% over 15 days; the amount locks on submission. At 84,431 alpha that is about 2,110 alpha on day one and 10,550 at 15 days.
  • A proof with non-compliant chat logs waits for compliant ones before it pays (team, 2026-10-28).
  • Proofs submitted before chat-log intake opened (2026-10-13) and approved in full can earn a one-off bonus for accepted logs, claimable within a year: alpha worth USD 500 (Erdős) or 250 (others).

Getting started

  1. Create an account on conjectures.io or install the miner CLI.
  2. Run conjectures tasks sync, then conjectures tasks challenge <task> to save the exact Lean statement.
  3. Write the proof in Main.lean, then run conjectures build and the free local checks.
  4. Keep your AI chat logs, buy a credit (0.25 TAO), upload the logs, submit, and set a payout wallet.
  5. TorchLean: register a hotkey on SN66, clone the contest repo, follow MANUAL.md, then run miner/submit.py.

Caveats

  • Payouts depend on a team treasury and a manual review; payout flows were not checked on chain.
  • No revenue yet (owner, 2026-10-13); selling AI chat logs is the plan.
  • Solve counts come from the team's own API; the chain identity still lists the old Ninja repo. Beware of support DMs.
