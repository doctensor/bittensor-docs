What it is

Allways lets a user swap one native asset for another with one transfer from their own wallet; a miner delivers the other asset against posted collateral. Validators verify and slash failures; a Solana program settles swaps and Bittensor handles registration, scoring and emission.

How it works

Miners

A miner posts a rate per direction backed by collateral (SOL on Solana, or a TAO bond in a Bittensor vault). It must deliver within 10 minutes by default; a miss slashes collateral to the user and adds a lifetime strike.

Validators

Validators are whitelisted by the contract admin; they run the reservation draw and vote each outcome on Solana.

Incentive

A miner's reward per direction is its eligibility (at most 2 lifetime timeouts, a fill in the last 12 hours) times the direction's pool times a blend of 65% crown time and 35% share of fills. Since 2026-10-21 each pool is scaled by min(1, 2 x 24-hour swap fees / miners' 24-hour emission); the rest goes to the owner slot (UID 53).

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Post a rate and collateral per swap direction, then deliver the destination asset on time.

  • A locked TAO bond cannot be withdrawn on demand.
  • A missed deadline slashes 110% of the swap value.

Validator High barrier

Verify both legs of each swap, vote the outcome on Solana and set Bittensor weights.

  • The admin whitelists each validator's Solana key (up to 16 in code); unlisted validators do not influence outcomes.
  • 13 permit holders on chain (2026-11-13); 12 set weights, all with their top weight on the owner slot.

Getting started

  1. Check recent swap volume in the team's public API first: no miner outside the owner slot earned on 2026-11-13.
  2. Prepare a Bittensor wallet, a funded Solana keypair, a free Solana RPC key and a signing key per asset you deliver.
  3. Run alw miner init, then alw doctor; follow the on-chain order the guide gives (deposit, bind, register, bond, start, post quotes).
  4. Validators: hold a permit, fund a Solana keypair, buy a paid Solana RPC and ask the contract admin to whitelist your key.
