What it is

Allways lets a user swap one native asset for another, for example TAO for SOL or BTC, with one transfer from their own wallet; a miner delivers the other asset straight to the user. Miners post rates backed by collateral and validators check both transfers and slash a miner that fails. A Solana program settles swaps; Bittensor handles registration, scoring and emission.

How it works

Miners

A miner posts a rate per swap direction plus collateral (SOL on Solana, or a TAO bond in a Bittensor vault). When a user reserves the quote, the miner must deliver the destination asset within 10 minutes by default; a miss slashes collateral to the user and adds a lifetime strike.

Validators

Validators are whitelisted by the contract admin. They run the reservation draw, check both legs of each swap, vote the outcome on Solana and set Bittensor weights.

Incentive

A miner's reward per direction is its eligibility (at most 2 lifetime timeouts, a fill in the last 12 hours) times the direction's pool times a blend of 65% crown time and 35% share of fills. Since 2026-10-21 each pool is scaled by min(1, 2 x 24-hour swap fees / miners' 24-hour emission); the rest goes to the owner slot (UID 53).

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Post a rate and collateral per swap direction, then deliver the destination asset on time.

  • Collateral sits on Solana or as a TAO bond locked in a Bittensor vault; a locked bond cannot be withdrawn on demand.
  • A missed deadline slashes 110% of the swap value; at most 2 lifetime timeouts stay eligible.

Validator High barrier

Verify both legs of each swap, vote the outcome on Solana and set Bittensor weights.

  • The admin whitelists each validator's Solana key (up to 16 in code); unlisted validators do not influence outcomes.
  • 13 permit holders on chain (2026-10-30); 12 set weights, all with their top weight on the owner slot.

Getting started

  1. Check recent swap volume in the team's public API first: independent miners shared about 0.03 TAO a day on 2026-10-30.
  2. Prepare a Bittensor wallet, a funded Solana keypair, a free keyed Solana RPC and a signing key per asset you deliver, on a hardened Docker host.
  3. Run alw miner init, then alw doctor; follow the on-chain order the guide gives (deposit, bind, register, bond, start, post quotes).
  4. Validators: hold a permit, fund a Solana keypair, buy a paid Solana RPC and ask the contract admin to whitelist your key.

Caveats

  • Incident causes (2026-10-14 to 10-20) are the team's account; commits fit but do not prove which fix answered which exploit.
  • Usage numbers come from the team's own API (newest 200 swaps plus counters); the July figures are team posts. Neither was independently checked.
  • Low miner pay is arithmetic on sourced numbers and matches the chain in size; no validator's own computed share was read.
  • Docs lag code: the incentive page omits the fee-scaled pool; the vote threshold and stake split were not read.
