What it is

SN89 pays miners who show skill at calling price direction. Since 2026-11-09 most main-mechanism emission lands on one team-run entity UID that pays accounts off chain; 17 own-UID miners earn the rest.

How it works

Miners

LF miners seal a call (pair and direction) under a 15-minute timelock and post its hash on chain, up to 3 a day. Since 2026-11-09 they earn through the team's entity accounts, or on their own UID with a bond (500 alpha from 11-11, 1,000 before).

Validators

Validators open the commitments after the timelock and grade each call on public prices: take-profit first wins, stop-loss first loses.

Incentive

Mechanism 0 gets 80% of emission, mechanism 1 (referrers) 20%; mechanism 0 pays LF 37.5% and agent books 62.5%. A miner qualifies with 8+ decisive calls beating a coin flip at about 90% confidence; weight is qualified wins times a tier (1.0x at 55% to 2.0x at 70%), fading over 30 days.

Getting started

  1. Use Python 3.10, clone the repo and install requirements.txt.
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 89 with btcli (burn 0.025 TAO on 11-14); an own-UID LF miner also needs a 500-alpha bond (code).
  3. Send /token to the team's Telegram signals bot and export it as SN89_FEED_TOKEN.
  4. Run neurons/miner.py for LF calls. Expect 8 days at dust weight; call at least every 3 days.
  5. Hosted: the team's Telegram bot (infinitequant.app/how-to-mine) runs a miner for you; payout terms are undocumented.
