What it is

SN89 pays miners who show skill at calling price direction; validators grade the calls on public prices. The team says it trades the signals and sells them by API and challenges. Half of miner emission waits on its hosted Agent contest.

How it works

Miners

LF miners seal a call (pair and direction) under a 15-minute timelock and post its hash on chain, up to 3 a day. HF ended 2026-11-05; Agent accounts are hosted by the team.

Validators

Validators open the commitments after the timelock and grade each call on public prices: take-profit first wins, stop-loss first loses.

Incentive

Mechanism 0 gets 80% of emission, mechanism 1 (referrers) 20%; mechanism 0 pays LF 37.5% and Agent 62.5%. A miner qualifies with 8+ decisive calls beating a coin flip at about 90% confidence; weight is qualified wins times a tier (1.0x at 55% to 2.0x at 70%), fading over 30 days. Copying is penalised.

Getting started

  1. Use Python 3.10, clone the repo and install requirements.txt.
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 89 with btcli (burn 0.025 TAO on 11-07).
  3. Send /token to the team's Telegram signals bot and export it as SN89_FEED_TOKEN.
  4. Run neurons/miner.py for LF calls. Expect 8 days at dust weight; call at least every 3 days.
  5. Hosted routes: the team's Telegram bot (infinitequant.app/how-to-mine) runs a miner for you; IQ Agent payout terms are undocumented.
