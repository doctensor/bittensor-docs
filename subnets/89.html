What it is

SN89 pays miners who show statistical skill at calling price direction; validators grade the calls against public prices. The team (IQ / Delta Compute) says it trades the signals and sells them by API. Its Up/Down prediction markets show no payout to miners on chain.

How it works

Miners

LF miners lock a call (pair and direction) under a 15-minute timelock and post its hash on chain, up to 3 a day. HF miners sent up to 30 signed calls a day until 2026-11-05. Hosted Agent accounts are graded like LF. Referrers form a separate pool.

Validators

Validators open the commitments after the timelock and grade each call on a public price series: take-profit first wins, stop-loss first loses.

Incentive

Mechanism 0 gets 80% of emission, mechanism 1 (referrers) 20%. A miner qualifies with 8+ decisive calls and a hit rate that beats a coin flip at about 90% confidence. Weight is qualified wins times a tier (1.0x from 55%, 1.2x from 60%, 2.0x from 70%), fading over 30 days. Copying is penalised.

Getting started

  1. Use Python 3.10, clone the repo and install requirements.txt.
  2. Register a hotkey on netuid 89 with btcli (burn 0.025 TAO on 2026-10-30; the README says about 0.05).
  3. Send /token to the team's Telegram signals bot and export it as SN89_FEED_TOKEN.
  4. Run neurons/miner.py for LF calls; HF stops taking calls on 2026-11-05. Expect 8 days at dust weight and call at least every 3 days.
  5. The hosted route is IQ Agent (infinitequant.app/agent); payout terms are undocumented.

Caveats

  • Chain figures are validator outputs on 2026-10-30, not payouts.
  • HF weight depends on a closed integrity score (51 of 59 entries at or above 0.80); it ends with HF.
  • Usage, API sales and the team's own trading are team claims; some chat users call the subnet a scam without evidence.
  • Rules change almost daily and the README lags: check the commit log before relying on a number.
