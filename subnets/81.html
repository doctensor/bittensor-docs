What it is

Reliquary (SN81) trains language models. A 158-step RL run, graded by the team, made the released 9B Teutonic-I-Reliquary. Now miners serve batch, evaluation and agentic orders, paid per verified token by a price auction. 96% of incentive burns now. It rebuilt the former Grail subnet (owner since 2026-08-14).

How it works

Miners

Run reliquary corpus mine (corpus mine-agentic for SWE and terminal orders): it follows the order list, takes an open order, leases samples and generates them on the order's model at your offer (alpha per million tokens). Samples are audited against a forced draw; a confirmed failure bars the hotkey from new leases.

Validators

The team runs the only trainer and grader (the owner's slot): leases, audits, training, signed checkpoints. Other validators are weight-only and replay its pay archives.

Incentive

Pay is verified tokens times a price, capped per 72-minute period (2% of the miner pool on the open 9B orders, 0.6% on the 27B evaluation order). Supply nobody earns goes to the owner slot, which burns it: 68-96% of miner-side incentive since 11-20.

What the team ships now

  • Model (11-04): Teutonic-I-Reliquary, 9B, Apache-2.0. In the team's own tests it beats Qwen3.5-9B on 5 of 8 benchmarks (SWE-bench Verified 43.6 vs 35.1) and loses on TMax, GPQA Diamond and MMLU-Pro.
  • Orders (11-25 list): seven, six on Qwen3.5-9B and one 27B evaluation order not started. The open generation order (36,621 prompts x 4 samples) has 2 miners at 0.0034 alpha per million tokens (ceiling 0.76); one open evaluation order has 1 miner at 2.63; four orders are draining. The 27B generation order is gone; agentic orders are in code, none listed.
  • Token-price auction (design 11-03, rules in code 11-10 to 11-19): miners offer alpha per million tokens, each lease is paid the price of its moment; since 11-18 the 27B orders give leases to the cheapest miners first (design 11-17). RL training orders priced by the same auction are in code (11-17 to 11-19); none is listed.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/mining-orders.md; docs/mining.md is the ended RL run's guide.
  2. Check the live order list before renting GPUs: model, ceiling price and open prompts.
  3. Register a hotkey on netuid 81 (burn 0.0005 TAO or more) and run reliquary corpus mine with an offer near your real cost.

Caveats

  • Figures are single readings (11-25); 0.6 TAO a day is gross.
  • The benchmarks are the team's own and unchecked. Why the RL run ended and when the next starts is not stated; chat after 11-02 was unreadable.
  • The pay board keeps only the newest periods and ended orders leave the list; the causes of the burned share's swings are inferred.
  • The list names models, not customers; the 69 other earners are unidentified (ck:4f300d, 36 UIDs, gets 22% of the team validator's other weight).
