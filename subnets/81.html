What it is

Reliquary (SN81) trains language models. GPU miners write answers for reinforcement learning (RL); the team's validator grades them and its trainer improves a 9B model. The run that ended 11-02 produced the released Teutonic-I-Reliquary. With no run active, 96% of miner-side incentive burns. It rebuilt the former Grail subnet (owner since 2026-08-14).

How it works

Miners

Run mine-async: lease prompts from the team validator, write 16 answers per prompt on the latest checkpoint and submit every group. The validator recomputes each token; matches of 0.90 or better are paid. Its pay and checkpoint routes return HTTP 503 (11-07).

Validators

The team runs the only trainer and grader (the owner's slot): leases, audits, training, signed checkpoints. Other validators are weight-only and replay its pay archives.

Incentive

Pay is matched tokens times a per-environment price, capped per 72-minute period. Supply the trainer cannot use goes to the owner slot, which burns it: 54-98% of miner-side incentive through 11-03, then 94% (11-04), 97% (11-06) and 96% (11-07).

What the team ships now

  • Model (11-04): Teutonic-I-Reliquary, 9B, Apache-2.0. In the team's own tests it beats Qwen3.5-9B on 5 of 8 benchmarks (SWE-bench Verified 43.6 vs 35.1) and loses on TMax, GPQA Diamond and MMLU-Pro.
  • Orders: one object for a customer batch job or an evaluation, every sample audited; since 11-05 also signed-sandbox episodes (SWE, terminal), paid once graded. No customer or price is named.
  • Token-price auction (design note, 11-03): miners ask a price, the cheapest are served at one price per round under a spend ceiling; coded for orders, not RL. RL pricing oscillated and pinned every environment at its ceiling; RL returns with the next run, no date.

Getting started

  1. Skip the README and docs/mining.md (earlier 4B task); use the launch post and the draft async design doc.
  2. Check the run monitor before renting GPUs: no run is listed (11-07).
  3. Register a hotkey on netuid 81 (burn 0.0005 TAO or more) and run mine-async from the latest main with a context length that fits your card; lower precision risks the 0.90 match floor.

Caveats

  • Figures are single readings (11-07); the burned share moved between 54% and 98% since 11-02; 0.69 TAO a day is gross.
  • The benchmarks are the team's own and unchecked. Why the run ended and when the next starts is not stated; chat after 11-02 was unreadable.
  • The 71 other earners (4% of incentive) are mostly unidentified; the top ones are inactive miners, and one coldkey holds 31 UIDs, 7 of the 10 that UID 55 weights most.
