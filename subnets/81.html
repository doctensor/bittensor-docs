What it is

Reliquary (SN81) trains language models. A 158-step RL run, graded by the team, made the released 9B Teutonic-I-Reliquary. Now miners serve batch and evaluation orders, paid per verified token by a price auction (5 orders listed on 11-13). 88% of miner-side incentive still burns. It rebuilt the former Grail subnet (owner since 2026-08-14).

How it works

Miners

Run reliquary corpus mine: it follows the order list, takes an open order, leases samples and generates them on the order's model at your offer (alpha per million tokens). Samples are audited against a forced draw; a confirmed failure bars the hotkey from new leases.

Validators

The team runs the only trainer and grader (the owner's slot): leases, audits, training, signed checkpoints. Other validators are weight-only and replay its pay archives.

Incentive

Pay is verified tokens times a price, capped per 72-minute period (a share of the miner pool: 25% on generation orders, 4% on evaluations). Supply nobody earns goes to the owner slot, which burns it: 54-98% of miner-side incentive through 11-03, 94-97% on 11-04 to 11-08, 77% to 94% in single reads since (88% on 11-13).

What the team ships now

  • Model (11-04): Teutonic-I-Reliquary, 9B, Apache-2.0. In the team's own tests it beats Qwen3.5-9B on 5 of 8 benchmarks (SWE-bench Verified 43.6 vs 35.1) and loses on TMax, GPQA Diamond and MMLU-Pro.
  • Orders (11-13 list): 3 open, 1 draining, 1 starting: generation on MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B (16,160 prompts), two evaluations on Qwen3.5-9B, two 27B orders. Signed-sandbox episodes (SWE, terminal) since 11-05.
  • Token-price auction (design 11-03, rules in code 11-10 to 11-13): miners offer alpha per million tokens, the cheapest are served first, each lease is paid the price of its moment. RL returns with the next run, no date.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/mining-orders.md; docs/mining.md is the ended RL run's guide.
  2. Check the live order list before renting GPUs: model, ceiling price and open prompts.
  3. Register a hotkey on netuid 81 (burn 0.0005 TAO or more) and run reliquary corpus mine with an offer near your real cost.

Caveats

  • Figures are single readings (11-13): the burned share swung between 54% and 98% since 11-02; 1.75 TAO a day is gross.
  • The benchmarks are the team's own and unchecked. Why the RL run ended and when the next starts is not stated; chat after 11-02 was unreadable.
  • Order pay is a projection: no settled period is listed, so the price per token (auction 0.11 or ceiling 0.64 alpha per Mtok) is unconfirmed.
  • The list names models, not customers; the 77 other earners are unidentified (one coldkey holds 31 UIDs).
