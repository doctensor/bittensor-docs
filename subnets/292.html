What it is

Polaris was a decentralized compute subnet (mainnet SN49) where miners rented out GPU and CPU machines. It was deregistered on 2025-10-31 after months of emission disputes. The chat channel moved to testnet in March 2026, but testnet slot 292 shows another project and no activity. The team now sells confidential-compute sandboxes for AI agents; they appear on mainnet SN94 as Cathedral.

How it works

Miners

Nothing is documented for testnet 292. On the old mainnet SN49, miners registered GPU or CPU machines on the Polaris Cloud dashboard, passed checks and a manual review, and ran a heartbeat service. For the team's current plans see SN94, where mining was not open yet on 2026-10-22.

Validators

No testnet guide exists. The last public validator repository (polarisvalidator, last push 2025-10-29) scored SN49 machines on uptime and container use; its last commit set a 90% burn.

Incentive

None on testnet 292: no uid earns incentive or dividends (2026-10-31). Uid 0 takes 56.6% of the last epoch's alpha emission and the pool (109.98 TAO) is unchanged since 2026-10-21. The team said on 2026-07-01 it now builds confidential-compute products, not a miner-reward design.

Caveats

  • Netuid 292 here is a testnet netuid. Nothing on this page describes mainnet emissions or economics.
  • The link between this channel and testnet slot 292 rests only on the archive's channel name and a 2026-03-05 moderator post; the slot's own name says titan-subnet.
  • The link to SN94 rests on public traces, not an announcement: a shared team chat account, the name Cathedral, a licence line in its sandbox repo and a model host called Polaris Inference.
  • polaris.computer and its docs never mention miners, a subnet or testnet 292. Cathedral figures (agents, puzzles, signed receipts) are the team's own claims, not verified here.
  • Mechanism details come from 2025 mainnet-era repositories and chat; the reasons for the 2025 deregistration were disputed in chat and are unverified.
  • Testnet reads cover parameters and aggregate counts only; ownership and per-validator weights of testnet 292 were not examined, and block dates are estimates.
