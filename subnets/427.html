What it is

Dinobets presents itself as a decentralized casino on Bittensor where every game round is meant to be provably fair and auditable by anyone. Per the team's January 2026 launch post, miners produce a cryptographic hash for each round, validators check it, and operators run ready-made games. None of this can be checked: the repository is not public and the websites are offline.

How it works

Miners

Per the launch post, miners generate a cryptographic hash for each game round of the operator games. The task format, hash scheme and hardware needs are not documented anywhere readable.

Validators

Per the launch post, validators verify the miners' hashes in real time, and the history can be audited by anyone. How that verification becomes a score is not documented.

Incentive

Not found: no reward formula was published and the code is not readable. Nothing is read from mainnet, where the netuid is not registered; on the testnet slot no uid has incentive or dividends (2026-10-31).

Caveats

  • Testnet only: netuid 427 is not on mainnet (2026-10-31), so no emission or economic claims are made, and testnet TAO has no market value.
  • Testnet slot 427 (7 neurons) was registered 2026-02-21, after the launch post, and its chain identity holds only the name oneoneone. Whether it is Dinobets's is unconfirmed; it may be recycled.
  • On that slot, 3 of 7 neurons hold validator permits and none has set weights for 140+ days (newest about 2026-06-11, two in February 2026); no uid earns incentive. Dates are estimates (12 s per block).
  • The fairness claims, and how hashes are made, verified and rewarded, come only from the launch post and could not be tested: no code, no live site.
  • No team post after 2026-01-23, so whether development continues is unknown.
  • The product is a gambling service. The sources say nothing about licensing, jurisdiction or player protection, and this page makes no claims about them.
