What it is

Leadpoet (SN71) ran a daily contest in which miners improved a public sales-research agent. The team retired it on 2026-11-24. Miners now fund simulated sales jobs on their own OpenRouter keys. The pilot pays no emissions; paid jobs are planned at 125% of measured cost, with no date. Until then the miner side of emission is burned. The team sells a sales product; its website never mentions Bittensor.

How it works

Miners

A miner registers a hotkey and enrolls an OpenRouter key with a daily cap. The subnet's worker runs one assigned model through a simulated sales task on that key; the miner's statement lists every charge.

Validators

Permit holders fetch the team's signed weights and set them on chain; all 8 put 100% on the owner uid.

Incentive

Nothing reaches miners today: the signed weights send the whole vector to the owner's burn uid. Once paid collection starts, each verified job earns a fixed quote.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Funds simulated sales jobs on your own OpenRouter key; the pilot pays no emissions.

  • Default is one new assignment per UTC day, up to six on request; a failed job still counts toward the day.
  • Pilot quote is zero. Revoking stops new calls, but calls already sent can still be billed; use a dedicated key with prompt logging off.

Validator High barrier

Fetches the team's signed weights and sets them on chain; no scoring jobs remain.

  • A permit alone gets the signed weights: the 24-hour recent-work gate was removed, and the 75,000 effective-stake minimum applied only to scoring jobs, which ended.

Reviewer No barrier

Rates simulated sales workflows blind; no wallet needed; the team decides who is accepted.

No further detail found in the sources.

Collection pilot

  • Six open models share each scenario pack: Kimi K3.1 Pro, DeepSeek V4 Pro, Mistral Large 4, Qwen3.8-27B, Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash; each runs on a pinned zero-retention endpoint and the team pays the judges.
  • Run traces can become training data once reviewed, under each funder's signed terms; read the text for your scope before signing.

Getting started

  1. Clone leadpoet/leadpoet (Python 3.11) and register a hotkey on subnet 71 (burn about 0.0005 TAO).
  2. Create a dedicated funded OpenRouter key plus a read-only management key for the same workspace, with prompt logging off.
  3. Run leadpoet collection-terms for your scope, read it, then leadpoet collection-register --mode pilot with a daily cap and the terms digest.
  4. Check charges with collection-statement; stop new calls any time with collection-revoke. Reviewing needs no wallet.

Caveats

  • The model changed fast: 212 commits in four days turned a daily contest into a pilot, so older pages and docs are stale.
  • The team runs the gateway; scenarios, graders and judge prompts are private, and no enrollment or job count is public.
  • Validators copy the team's signed weights, so chain weights show configuration, not independent judgment.
  • Miners fund the pilot with their own money and may never be paid: paid mode needs a qualified pilot and a signed price approval.
