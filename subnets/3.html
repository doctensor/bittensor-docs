What it is

Teutonic is a competition to train one shared language model, Teutonic-II-110B-A7B (a mixture-of-experts, about 110B parameters). Miners submit full checkpoints; a challenger that beats the current king on next-token loss by a set margin is crowned and earns emission. Seven competitions run: MAIN and six data mixes.

How it works

Miners

Miners submit a full checkpoint with the repo's miner tool. The hotkey must be Ed25519 (upload credentials are encrypted to it). Each hotkey gets one irreversible submission, shared across all competitions; the miner picks MAIN or a MIX.

Validators

The team's validator verifies each checkpoint against its on-chain commitment; a remote GPU evaluator then compares challenger and king on 20,000 text windows of 2,048 tokens plus about 30 million tokens of whole documents. A challenger wins if the lower confidence bound of its loss gain exceeds 0.0015, the same in all seven competitions.

Incentive

Rewards are paid in alpha: 25% of miner rewards to the MAIN king, 12.5% to each MIX king, switching at once when a king changes. Weights are published about every 20 minutes; commit-reveal delays when they count by hours.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Trains and submits a full checkpoint of the shared model.

  • The model is about 220 GB in bf16; the evaluator runs four king and four challenger copies on 8 GPUs.
  • Training data and the kings' models are public; upload credentials are free.
  • Registration cost moves with demand (minimum 1 TAO); all 256 slots are filled.

Validator High barrier

Checks submissions and sets weights; in practice the team runs it.

  • On 2026-10-28 three validators set weights with the same top target; one is the owner's hotkey, the other two are unidentified.
  • A fourth permit holder has not set weights for about 6.8 days.

Who gets paid

  • Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; the seven kings share the miner part.
  • On 2026-10-28 the MAIN king earned about 31 alpha an hour (about 0.7 TAO), a MIX king about half.
  • One wallet holds three king slots (MAIN, MIX01, MIX02): half the reward weight. Two other wallets hold a quarter each.
  • The owner's hotkey is the main validator: 98.6% of validator dividends, about 49% of last-epoch emission, roughly what all seven kings get together. Part may go to its stakers (not checked).

Getting started

  1. Check hardware: a 220 GB model needs a multi-GPU node (8x H200/B200-class or better).
  2. Clone the repo, make a Python 3.11+ environment and install the miner extras.
  3. Create an Ed25519 hotkey with btcli and save its wallet path with the miner configure command.
  4. Run the register command (it can spend TAO; check-only mode never pays).
  5. Pick a competition from the competitions JSON, train on the public datasets, then upload the checkpoint and mark it ready. One submission per hotkey, irreversible.

Caveats

  • Rules move fast (rebuilt on 2026-10-21, tuned on 10-22); the README intro and the guide's live-chain section are out of date. Trust the dashboard and competitions JSON.
  • Evaluation, queue order and crowning run on team servers; challenger models stay private until crowned.
  • Chain weights lag the dashboard by hours (commit-reveal). One weight publication failed on 2026-10-28; the next attempt, about 20 minutes later, went through.
  • The owner key pair changed once around 2026-08-14 to 08-15; who holds it now is not verifiable. The Dendrite team, which also runs SN97, operates it per chat.
  • A wallet is not a person: the concentration figures count wallets only.
