What it is

Teutonic is a competition to train one shared language model, Teutonic-II-110B-A7B. Miners submit full checkpoints; a challenger that beats the current king on next-token loss by a set margin is crowned and earns emission.

How it works

Miners

Miners submit a full checkpoint with the repo's miner tool. Each hotkey gets one irreversible submission, shared across all competitions; the miner picks MAIN or a MIX.

Validators

The team's validator verifies each checkpoint against its on-chain commitment; a remote GPU evaluator then compares challenger and king on whole documents only (70 million tokens, up to 16,384 each). A challenger wins if the lower confidence bound of its loss gain exceeds 0.0015.

Incentive

Rewards are paid in alpha: 25% of miner rewards to the MAIN king, 12.5% to each MIX king, switching at once when a king changes.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Trains and submits a full checkpoint of the shared model.

  • The model is about 220 GB in bf16; the evaluator runs four king and four challenger copies on 8 GPUs. A checkpoint must support 16,384 positions.
  • Training data and the kings' models are public; upload credentials are free.
  • A new attempt needs a new hotkey (1 TAO). Of the six kings crowned on 11-06, four registered 6 to 9 days earlier, two 2 to 4 hours earlier.

Validator High barrier

Checks submissions and sets weights; in practice the team runs it.

  • Evaluation, queue order and crowning run in the team's services; no outside-validator guide exists.
  • On 2026-11-07 all three permit holders put 25% on the MAIN king (uid 84); the other two hold 1.0% of dividends.

Who gets paid

  • Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; the seven kings share the miner part.
  • The seven paid slots sit on four wallets: one with 41 uids holds four (62.4% of reward weight), three hold 12.5% each; MIX07's newest king (a fifth wallet) is not paid yet.
  • Since 10-21 two wallets took 34 of 52 accepted challenges; the larger (41 uids) lost its last slot on 11-05 and took four back on 11-06. The team does not say who runs the wallets.
  • The owner's hotkey is the main validator: 99.0% of dividends and about 49% of emission, as much as all seven kings.
  • King hotkeys hold 0 to 130 alpha, against 360 to 738 alpha earned a day.

Getting started

  1. Check hardware: a 220 GB model needs an 8x H200/B200-class node.
  2. Clone the repo, make a Python 3.11+ environment, install the miner extras and create an Ed25519 hotkey with btcli (save its wallet path with the miner configure command).
  3. Run the register command (it can spend TAO; check-only mode never pays).
  4. Pick a competition from the competitions JSON, train on the public datasets, then upload and mark ready. One submission per hotkey, irreversible.

Caveats

  • Rules move fast (rebuilt 2026-10-21; whole-document tests and MIX07 on 11-04); the guide lags the repo. Trust the dashboard and competitions JSON.
  • Weights count hours after a crowning (commit-reveal; the 11-05 MIX07 king was paid within 9.6 hours). Dashboards showed publication 'failed' on 10-31, 11-03, 11-04 and again from 11-06 21:40Z.
  • The owner key pair changed around 2026-08-14 to 08-15; who holds it now is not verifiable. Per chat, the Dendrite team (also SN97) operates it.
  • Chat after 2026-10-29 was not read (archive down).
