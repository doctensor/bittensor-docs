What it is

Netuid 70 has no documented purpose today. The chain name is 'Consted 2.0', and the identity text is a critical statement about the central party that decides subnet emission, not a description of work for miners. The channel is named after an older placeholder ('pending'). Everything said about NexisGen and Vericore in the archive belongs to earlier occupants of the netuid, not to this registration.

How it works

Miners

Nothing is published: no miner task, software, guide or API. Chat has no instructions either; members who asked on 2026-09-15, 2026-10-07 and later how to mine or when mining starts got no answer in the archive. On chain, exactly one uid (12) has incentive, and it is not the owner's. Who runs it and whether it does any work is not shown anywhere.

Validators

Three uids hold validator permits: uid 0, which matches the subnet owner's hotkey and coldkey, and two others that do not. Read on 2026-10-21 (mainnet block 9334694), each of the three sets weights on a single target, uid 12, at 100 percent, and none gives anything to uid 0. Weights are committed and revealed (commit-reveal on, one epoch); two of the three had updated within about 1 minute and the third about 15 hours earlier. No validator code or scoring rule is public.

Incentive

Only chain facts are known. Incentive is 100 percent on uid 12 (registered about 2026-09-15); dividends go to uids 0, 1 and 2. The emission split is 0 burn, 0 recycle, about 33 percent owner, 33 percent validators, 34 percent miners. Emission is enabled and about 241 alpha were emitted in the last epoch before block 9334589 (uid 0 received about 26 percent of it). Registration cost fell from about 0.5 TAO to 1111 rao (roughly zero) on 2026-10-19. These are set values, not proof of any real work.

Getting started

  1. No miner or validator onboarding exists: nothing public says what to run or how to be scored.
  2. To check the current state yourself, look at the subnet on Taostats (link above): owner identity, incentive per uid and validator weights.
  3. Registration is nearly free (1111 rao on 2026-10-21), but with no software or reward rule published, registering earns nothing documented and the burn setting has changed many times.
  4. Do not stake or register on the strength of chat posts; the moderators' own pin on 2026-08-12 advised not to stake until the subnet is verified and its operators are known.
