What it is

TrajectoryRL is a continuous contest. Miners submit a pack: a SKILL.md plus a fusion policy that picks which of eight open models answers each agent request. The team's validator tests packs in sandboxed tasks. One miner at a time holds the seat and gets all miner emission. The team plans to serve the winning policy on its Engy platform.

How it works

Miners

A miner writes a pack: a SKILL.md and a fusion policy (JSON, Python or an OpenAI-style server). The miner tool burns the fee on chain, then sends the pack; one pack per hotkey can wait. A pack that earns any reward passes its copyright to trajrl.com.

Validators

An integrity judge rejects packs with hints matching many scenarios. A first-in-first-out queue feeds one challenger per epoch (about 1 hour). Only the team's validator (UID 74) evaluates, in a sandbox with eight listed models and a 0.30 USD cap per scenario; other validators copy its winner.

Incentive

Score is tests passed over tests total, summed over 20 scenarios (maximum 20; the seat holder has 19.94). A challenger must beat the seat by about 3% at low scores, less near the top. A first win is provisional: two re-runs of both packs must confirm it.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner and fusion policy guides, install the miner tool with pip, and write a SKILL.md (keep it general or the integrity check rejects it) and a policy from the repo's examples.
  2. Test with scripts/eval_pack.py (Docker, your own Engy key).
  3. Check the queue first: 199 packs wait, about 13 days, and the seat holds 19.94 of 20 (2026-10-31).
  4. Register a hotkey (0.02 TAO burn on 2026-10-31), hold 50+ alpha, then submit with the web-submit command.

Caveats

  • One party runs all evaluation, and scores cannot be checked on chain.
  • The confirmation rule has no public text (chat 2026-10-22 and site data only).
  • Usage and revenue are unverified: the website's totals are frozen since 2026-10-21 and Engy lists no auto mode.
  • Why the owner validator's dividend share rose from 74.6% to 87.4% since 2026-10-28 is not known.
