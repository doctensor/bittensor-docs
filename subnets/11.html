What it is

TrajectoryRL is a continuous contest. Miners submit a pack: a SKILL.md plus a fusion policy that picks which of eight open models answers each agent request. The team's validator tests packs in sandboxed tasks. One miner at a time holds the seat and gets all miner emission. The team plans to serve the winning policy on its Engy platform.

How it works

Miners

A miner writes a pack: a SKILL.md and a fusion policy (JSON, Python or an OpenAI-style server) and sends it with the miner tool. A pack that earns any reward passes its copyright to trajrl.com.

Validators

An integrity judge rejects packs with hints matching many scenarios. A queue feeds one challenger per epoch (about 1 hour). The team whitelists evaluators; only its validator (UID 74) reports scores now, in a sandbox with a 0.30 USD cap per scenario. Other validators copy its winner.

Incentive

Score is tests passed over tests total, summed over 20 scenarios (maximum 20; the seat holder has 18.76). A challenger must beat the seat by about 3% at low scores, less near the top. A first win is provisional: two re-runs of both packs must confirm it.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner and fusion policy guides, install the miner tool with pip, and write a general SKILL.md and a policy from the repo's examples.
  2. Test with scripts/eval_pack.py (Docker, your own Engy key).
  3. Check whether public submissions have reopened; the repo guide says paused, the website has not caught up.
  4. When open: register a hotkey (0.02 TAO burn on 2026-11-06) and submit with the web-submit command.

Caveats

  • One party runs all evaluation, and scores cannot be checked on chain.
  • The confirmation rule has no public text.
  • Usage and revenue are unverified; the site's totals are self-reported and flat since 2026-11-01.
  • Dividends do not follow stake: UID 48 holds 267k alpha and earns 0.2%, the owner's UID 74 holds 437k and earns 88%; why is unknown.
  • Chat after 2026-11-01 is unread (archive down): the ban and pause rest on the API and repo docs.
