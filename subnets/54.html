What it is

Yanez pays miners for synthetic identity data: face variations (including fake screen replays meant to fool the team's liveness detector) and cloned voices. The team says the data helped it ship its Passive Liveness v3 check; it also sells an ID app and an approval service for AI agents.

How it works

Miners

Miners run the repository's miner on a GPU. Validators send a seed face and ask for six synthetic variations: two backgrounds, three combined lighting, expression and pose edits, and a synthetic screen replay showing a named device. Since 2026-10-19 miners also clone a reference voice reading a set prompt.

Validators

Validators send the challenges, pass submissions to a grading service the team runs, and set weights. Nine hold permits and all rank the partner hotkey first. Manual review, similarity checks and the final reputation score are the team's; validators mostly relay.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 49% validators, 33% miners (18/41/41 before 2026-10-27). The miner side goes about half to the partner hotkey (UID 14) and half to data miners, with nothing burned (it was 35/35/30). Of the data-miner half, about 10% is live image grading and 90% a reputation score.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Generates face variations, a screen replay and a cloned voice each round.

  • Base model FLUX.2 Klein 4B (FLUX.1 Kontext optional); accept the licences.
  • The screen replay counts only if it breaks the team's liveness check.

Validator High barrier

Relays challenges and team scores and sets weights; in practice team-controlled.

  • Only 9 hotkeys hold permits; the owner hotkey holds 83% of dividends and 37% of staked alpha.

Partner / referrer Medium barrier

Brings real verified ID-app users and earns referral alpha through UID 14.

  • Pay: 1 alpha per verified user, 1 when the user registers an agent, 10 at 20 Pulse transactions (team, 2026-10-22).

How scoring works

  • Face: five variations (up to 30 points) and a screen replay (up to 6); full marks need identity kept, the prompt followed and the liveness check broken. Voice: up to 5 points, right words and speaker similarity 0.6+.
  • Face and voice count half each in the reputation score; duplicate checks can cut it.

Who actually earns

  • 17 of 309 listed hotkeys score above zero (59 on 2026-10-23); the top one holds 24% of the score, the top ten 94%.
  • In Phase 4 Cycle 5 the team penalised 172 of 199 scored hotkeys for similar images; several hotkeys lost more.
  • By arithmetic, each of the four miner slots after the partner earns about 75 alpha (about 0.35 TAO) a day.

Getting started

  1. Check the economics first: most listed miners earn nothing, pay arrives weeks later, and scores decay 0.05 per allocation.
  2. Prepare a GPU (8 GB+ VRAM), 16 GB RAM, 80 GB disk, a free Hugging Face token with licences accepted, and open port 8091.
  3. Run scripts/miner/setup.sh, set HF_TOKEN and FLUX_DEVICE, then start neurons/miner.py with --netuid 54 under pm2.
  4. Use one hotkey (similar data across hotkeys is penalised) and test screen replays against the team's liveness check, one call per hotkey every 5 minutes.
  5. For voice, implement generate_voice_clone in MIID/miner/voice_generator.py and follow the 18-token prompt in the code, not the 15 in docs/miner.md.

Caveats

  • Miner pay figures conflict: the team says miners keep about 1,033 alpha a day, while docs/scoring.md raises their share of total emission from 14.35% to 16.5%.
  • Scoring, review and the liveness check run on team servers; chain weights are validator opinions, not payouts.
  • Who controls UID 14 and how much of its share reaches partners is not shown; the team denies self-mining. The owner hotkey holds 83% of dividends; who benefits is unread.
  • docs/miner.md still lists a 15-token voice prompt (code: 18); the date of the split change on chain is not pinned.
