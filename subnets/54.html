What it is

Yanez pays miners for synthetic identity data: face variations, fake screen replays and cloned voices. The team says the data helped it ship its Passive Liveness v3 check; it also offers an ID app and an approval service for AI agents.

How it works

Miners

Miners run the repository's miner on a GPU. For each seed face they return six synthetic variations (backgrounds, lighting, expression and pose edits, one screen replay) and, since 2026-10-19, a cloned reference voice.

Validators

Validators send challenges, pass submissions to the team's grading service and set weights. Nine hold permits; the eight with weights all rank the partner hotkey first.

Incentive

Stored emission split: 18% owner, 49% validators, 33% miners. The miner side goes half to the partner hotkey (UID 14), half to data miners; the 30% burn ended.

How scoring works

  • Five face variations score out of 25 (since 2026-10-29 no detector break is needed); a screen replay out of 6, full marks only if it breaks the team's liveness check; a voice clip out of 5.
  • Voice counts half, face variations and screen replay a quarter each (the team's dashboard predictions have fit this mix since 11-04). Live grading samples one face slot a round; the rest is reviewed after the cycle, and near-identical images across miners are penalised.

Who actually earns

  • The finalised leaderboard (last cycle's scores) lists 3 of 309 hotkeys with a positive score (5 on 11-04); the top one holds 74%.
  • The team's face dashboard predicts this cycle, before similarity penalties: 196 of 334 hotkeys positive, behind at least 54 coldkeys (the largest covers 14).
  • From the chain's incentive shares, the four slots after the partner earn about 330 alpha (1.8 TAO) a day each on average, slots 6-10 about 2, the other 136 under 1.

Getting started

  1. Check the economics first: few miners earn, pay arrives weeks later (this cycle ends about 2026-11-19) and scores decay 0.05 per allocation.
  2. Prepare a GPU (8 GB+ VRAM), 16 GB RAM, 80 GB disk and a Hugging Face token; run scripts/miner/setup.sh, open port 8091, start neurons/miner.py --netuid 54 under pm2.
  3. Vary models, prompts, accessories and backgrounds: near-identical images are penalised.
  4. For voice, implement generate_voice_clone (MIID/miner/voice_generator.py).

Caveats

  • Scoring, review and the liveness check run on team servers; chain weights are not payouts.
  • Who controls UID 14 and whether its share reaches partners is not shown (the team denies self-mining); the owner hotkey's 80% of dividends is unexplained.
  • Revenue and client figures are second-hand from the owner's 10-30 update, not tied to the subnet.
