What it is

Yanez (repository name MIID-subnet) pays miners to generate synthetic identity data: identity-preserving face image variations, including synthetic screen replays meant to fool the team's passive-liveness detector, and, since Phase 5 Cycle 1, cloned voices. The subnet's on-chain description says it generates synthetic identities to challenge Yanez's humanhood, presence and uniqueness detection models, and the team says miner submissions helped it ship Passive Liveness v3 on 2026-10-17. The same team sells a face-verified identity app (YID) and a human-approval service for AI agents (Pulse). On chain, 35% of the miner-side incentive goes to one partner-referral hotkey (UID 14) tied to those products and 30% is burned, so data-generation miners share about 35%.

How it works

Miners

A miner runs the repository's miner on a GPU (README: 8 GB VRAM or more, 16 GB RAM, 80 GB disk, a Hugging Face token, open port 8091). Validators send a seed face image and variation instructions; the base miner uses diffusion models such as FLUX.2 Klein or PuLID. Per the team's 2026-10-07 description, a round asks for six synthetic images (an indoor background, an outdoor background, three combined edits of lighting, expression and pose, and one synthetic screen replay that names a device and shows at least two screen cues). Since Phase 5 Cycle 1 it also asks for one cloned voice: the validator supplies a reference clip of about 30 seconds in English or Spanish and an 18-token prompt (the first and last 4 characters of the miner's own hotkey around 3 digit words and 7 pool words, the format in the code since 2026-10-16); the voice must say the prompt while sounding like the reference speaker, and the team's suggested self-check is a speaker-similarity score above 0.6. Results are encrypted, uploaded to S3 and returned as signed references. Physical screen replays stopped being accepted on 2026-10-07; synthetic ones count only if they pass as live in the team's /is_live check, which has been Passive Liveness v3 since 2026-10-17 and which registered miners may call through the miner API at most once every 5 minutes per hotkey. Registering is cheap: on 2026-10-23 the registration burn sat at its minimum (0.0005 TAO) with all 256 UIDs in use.

Validators

Validators send the challenges, then pass each submission set to a grading API run by the team (plain HTTP on a bare IP in the code), which returns per-image validation and identity-preservation scores. The top 50 miners with average identity preservation of at least 0.6 get an exponentially decaying rank-based reward; this is the live part, called KAV. Validators also upload each round's results to a team-run server and receive a reputation snapshot that drives the larger part, UAV. The team says each validator sends about one request an hour and sets its KAV weights after each one. On chain on 2026-10-23, 10 UIDs held validator permits and 9 had set weights (commit-reveal is on, with a 5-epoch reveal period); all nine gave about 35% of their weight to UID 14 as their top target, so every weight-setting validator runs the partner allocation from the code. The team's stats feed shows score rows from 6 validators, so not every validator queries each round. Manual review of submissions, similarity and duplicate checks, and the final UAV scores are done by the team, not by validators.

Incentive

The code splits the miner side of emissions 35% to data-generation miners, 35% to a commercial-partner hotkey and 30% burned to UID 59, and the chain matches. At block 9343854 (2026-10-23, about 04:30 UTC) UID 14, the partner hotkey in the code, held 35.1% of incentive, UID 59, the owner's own hotkey (the subnet burns incentive sent to owner hotkeys), held 30.1%, and the other 158 miner UIDs shared about 34.8%. That split applies only to the miner half of emission: the chain's emission weights are 18% owner, 41% validators and 41% miners, which on the 7,200 alpha a day the team cites is about 2,950 alpha a day for the miner side, roughly 1,040 to UID 14, 890 burned and 1,030 to data miners, in line with the team's July plan. Within the miner share, 10% is KAV (live image grading) and 90% is UAV (reputation from manual validation). A miner's UAV value follows its current reputation score on a flattened curve (about 0.5 at a score of 0.1, 1.0 at 5, 1.5 at 30, 1.8 at 50, 2.0 at 150 and 2.5 at 250), multiplied by a tier factor from 0.90 (Watch) to 1.35 (Black), so a score-158 Platinum miner counts only about 2.5 times a score-8 Bronze one. The score itself comes from the team's manual review: face variations and screen replays are scored separately, weighted 50/50, and multiplied by a bonus for how many past cycles the miner took part in. Each validator allocation lowers every miner's score by 0.05 until it reaches zero, and new hotkeys earn no UAV until they have validated reputation. Between 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-23 the team's leaderboard went from 77 hotkeys with a positive score to 59, and the top score fell from about 168.7 to 157.9. Cycles run as a Sandbox of about two weeks (only KAV is paid, the previous cycle is reviewed) and an Execution of about a month in which the previous cycle's finalized UAV is paid gradually: Phase 5 Cycle 1 Execution is 2026-10-19 to 11-19, and work done in it is paid from Phase 5 Cycle 2 Execution. On chain the leaderboard matches payouts: the 58 hotkeys with a positive leaderboard score held 92.5% of the miner-side incentive, with rank correlation 0.98. Pay is spread widely: the top miner UID held about 2.4% of the miner side, the top 50 about 83%, and 45 coldkeys held incentive, some through many UIDs. The partner share is the team's referral program: its July document describes paying vetted commercial partners per verified unique person (a daily rate, about 0.1 to 10 alpha), first to public partner wallets and later through on-chain weights, with an emission-funded buffer wallet called the Hold and about 20% of partner revenue bought back into the subnet. On 2026-09-30 the team opened referrals through UID 14 to any miner or Bittensor user, and on 2026-10-22 said rewards come in three steps (1 alpha per new verified user, 1 alpha when the user registers an agent, 10 alpha at 20 Pulse transactions). The team says it prefers one strong hotkey and set the decay to favor that.

Recent activity

The top miner UID earned roughly 24 alpha a day, about 0.12 TAO at the pool price that day. The team's leaderboard feed listed 59 hotkeys with a positive UAV score, down from 77 on 2026-10-21, and the repository had no new commit since 2026-10-19.
  • 2026-10-22 (#54-yanez) The team answered payout complaints. It described UID 14 as its dual-incentive mechanism, funded from part of what would otherwise be burned rather than from miner share, and said referral rewards now come in three steps: 1 alpha per new verified user, 1 alpha when that user registers an agent, and 10 alpha when that user reaches 20 Pulse transactions (the repository PDF still lists two steps). It published model projections of cumulative UAV pay for the top ten miners (about 3,670 alpha for first down to about 276 for tenth, not guaranteed), said the daily UAV pool is about 930 alpha split among miners with score left, and predicted top miners' daily pay would rise as weaker miners decay out. Miners replied that the top miner earned about 0.1 TAO a day, too little for GPU and API costs, and asked for UID 14 to be removed; some called it self-mining, which the team denies.
  • 2026-10-22 (#54-yanez) The team lead restated the rules: no UAV score accumulates in the sandbox, final scoring includes post-validation with face variations and screen replays weighted equally, duplicate and similarity checks run on the full dataset and can change the face score, and the 0.05 decay is meant to discourage spreading work over many hotkeys (five hotkeys lose 0.25 combined per allocation, one loses 0.05). It added that popular models with similar prompts produce near-duplicate data and asked miners for more distinctive generation workflows.
  • 2026-10-21 (#54-yanez) The team explained that the live dashboard showed only the face prediction, that screen replays were reviewed by hand, and that the final score is the average of the two. It argued that rewards exceed costs (about 21,600 images per 30-day window, API cost roughly 324 to 367 dollars in the worst case); several miners disputed this and called the wait of about two months between a cycle's work and its pay too harsh. The team said a voice dashboard was still being built.
  • 2026-10-20 (#54-yanez) Miners disputed the final Phase 4 Cycle 5 ranking: one who had been 4th on the live prediction fell to about 15th because the manually graded screen-replay score, weighted equally with the face score, had not been shown live. Miners also challenged the UAV decay of 0.05 per allocation, which they said was 0.02 before. The team defended the decay as a push toward one strong hotkey; one miner reported about 80 hotkeys running from one machine, and the team said it recommends one hotkey, runs similarity checks and cannot force it.
  • 2026-10-19 (#54-yanez, GitHub) Phase 5 Cycle 1 Execution began and the team started releasing finalized UAV rewards for Phase 4 Cycle 5, saying payouts arrive gradually as validators update. The repository docs and PDFs were updated the same day: the cycle's last report counted 204 active hotkeys, about 1.59 million submissions and more than 12,000 real screen-replay images, which the team says produced Passive Liveness v3.
  • 2026-10-17 (#54-yanez, GitHub) The voice target text changed to 3 numbers, 7 words and the first and last 4 characters of the miner's hotkey, and Passive Liveness v3 went live for testing through /is_live; screen replays count only if they break v3. The repository code matches the new text format (commit of 2026-10-16).

Getting started

  1. Check the economics before buying hardware or API credits: on 2026-10-23 the top miner UID earned about 0.12 TAO a day, UAV pay arrives weeks after the work, and UAV scores fall 0.05 per allocation.
  2. Check hardware and accounts: a GPU with 8 GB or more VRAM (per the README), 16 GB RAM, 80 GB free disk, a Hugging Face token, a funded wallet to register on netuid 54, and port 8091 open.
  3. Run bash scripts/miner/setup.sh, activate miner_env, set HF_TOKEN and FLUX_DEVICE (cuda or mps), then start neurons/miner.py with --netuid 54 under pm2, as the README shows.
  4. Plan for one hotkey: the team recommends concentrating on a single strong hotkey, the UAV decay works against many weak ones, and its similarity checks penalize near-duplicate data across hotkeys.
  5. For the screen-replay image, iterate against the team's /is_live check (Passive Liveness v3) before submitting; the team says only replays that break it count.
  6. To join the voice task, implement generate_voice_clone in MIID/miner/voice_generator.py and follow the 18-token prompt in the code and the Phase 5 PDF, because docs/miner.md still shows the older 15-token text.
  7. Track KAV and UAV scores on the team dashboard (a voice view was still being added on 2026-10-21) and read the cycle timeline in docs/miner.md.

Caveats

  • Chain reads on 2026-10-23 (block about 9343854) confirm the split but not the intent: weights are validator opinions, not payouts. Stake behind the validators was not read. UID 59, the owner's hotkey, holds about 83% of the dividend vector, and where those dividends end up was not determined; only its incentive share is burned. Who controls UID 14 was not shown: its coldkey differs from the owner's, and the team calls it the partner mechanism and denies self-mining.
  • Most scoring runs on team-run hosts: the grading API, the reputation server and the liveness check are plain-HTTP, bare-IP services in the code, UAV is finalized by the team's manual review, and validators mostly relay those results. Public code does not let anyone recompute a miner's UAV, although on 2026-10-23 chain incentive tracked the team's leaderboard closely.
  • About a third of miner-side emission (35%, more in sandbox periods) goes to the partner hotkey. The team's own July document says it is paid out to vetted partners per verified unique user, through a buffer wallet and later on-chain weights, but the Hold wallet and the partner wallets were not found in the documents read, so this page cannot show how much of UID 14's emission reaches partners. The team also says that since June 2026 a subnet that burns miner emission gives up chain emission for it; that was not checked. What the generated data is used or sold for beyond the team's statements about its own detection models is not documented in the sources read.
  • Payout timing has slipped repeatedly: the Phase 4 Cycle 5 UAV payout was promised for about 10-05, then 10-14, and began on 10-19, with payment spread over time. Work done now is paid from Phase 5 Cycle 2 Execution, and UAV scores decay with each allocation.
  • The team projects much larger daily pay later in the cycle (a daily pool of about 930 alpha, top-ten cumulative projections of about 276 to 3,670 alpha, marked as not guaranteed) because weaker scores decay out, while the chain showed about 0.12 TAO a day for the top miner on 2026-10-23. Decay is linear and the same for every hotkey, so the outcome depends on how fast the highest scores run down; this was not tested.
  • The live dashboard score during a cycle left out the manually graded screen-replay part, which carried half of the Phase 4 Cycle 5 final score. The team explained this on 2026-10-21; miners still call the prediction a poor guide and ask for per-miner explanations, which the team handles by direct message.
  • Docs and code disagree in places: docs/miner.md still lists the old 15-token voice text, while the code and the Phase 5 PDF give 18; the docs and PDF timelines say burn ratio 35% where the chain shows 30% burn plus 35% partner; the PDF's tier ranges (for example Gold from 10) differ from the code's cutoffs (Gold from 30); the repository PDF lists two referral steps where the team described three on 2026-10-22; a code comment says decay 0.02 while the constant is 0.05 (the team said on 2026-10-22 the increase is meant to hit multi-hotkey farming); KAV and UAV are expanded as Address Variation in some files and Attack Vector in another.
  • Running many hotkeys is still allowed and cheap: 19 coldkeys held 5 or more registered UIDs on 2026-10-23 (up to 14), six coldkeys each had exactly 10 incentive-earning UIDs for reasons not found in the code or docs, the team recommends one hotkey and checks for similar data but cannot force it, and a miner reported about 80 hotkeys on one machine on 2026-10-20. A 2026-07-31 exploit involving withheld hotkeys led to a temporary UAV pause, later replaced by universal decay.
  • Figures from the team's feeds on 2026-10-23 are snapshots: the leaderboard listed 309 hotkeys, 59 with a non-zero score (77 on 2026-10-21); the stats feed listed 245 miners, 159 with a non-zero reward, and the top 10 held about 14% of its reward field. The team's only own miner, a public base miner announced 2026-05-13, was deregistered after its cycle according to the team and is no longer on the chain.
