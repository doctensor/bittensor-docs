What it is

Yanez pays miners for synthetic identity data: face variations, fake screen replays and cloned voices. The team says the data helped it ship its Passive Liveness v3 check; it also offers an ID app and an approval service for AI agents.

How it works

Miners

Miners run the repository's miner on a GPU. For each seed face they return six synthetic variations (one a screen replay) and, since 2026-10-19, a cloned reference voice.

Validators

Validators send challenges, pass submissions to the team's grading service and set weights. Nine hold permits (one is the partner hotkey, which sets none); the other eight all rank it first.

Incentive

Stored emission split: 18% owner, 49% validators, 33% miners. By code the miner side is 50% partner hotkey (UID 14), 45% manual-review reputation, 5% live grading; unused reputation share goes to the partner.

How scoring works

  • Five face variations score out of 25 (no detector break needed since 10-29); a screen replay out of 6, full marks only if it breaks the team's liveness check; a voice clip out of 5.
  • Voice counts half, face variations and screen replay a quarter each. Live grading samples one face slot a round; the rest is reviewed after the cycle, and near-identical images across miners are penalised.

Getting started

  1. Check the economics first: one hotkey takes 95%, pay arrives weeks later and scores decay 0.05 per allocation.
  2. Prepare the GPU and a Hugging Face token; run scripts/miner/setup.sh, open port 8091, start neurons/miner.py --netuid 54 under pm2.
  3. Vary models, prompts, accessories and backgrounds: near-identical images are penalised.
  4. For voice, implement generate_voice_clone (MIID/miner/voice_generator.py); the passcode is 20 tokens, 8 from your hotkey.

Caveats

  • Scoring, review and the liveness check run on team servers; chain weights are not payouts.
  • UID 14 and UID 186 have unknown controllers: UID 14 is swept daily near 14:00 UTC, sets no weights and has a permit only above about 1,100 alpha; the team denies self-mining. The owner's 86% of dividends is unexplained.
  • Revenue and client figures are second-hand from the owner's 10-30 update, not tied to the subnet.
