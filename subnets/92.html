What it is

REAX is run by a German company that also sells a decision-model API. A decision model is a small AI model that answers one narrow, typed question fast. Miners serve pinned models; validators re-check the answers. Half of miner pay goes to two contests that the team says pause after the 2026-10-30 slots; inference pay is meant to follow customer demand.

How it works

Miners

Inference miners serve one pinned model behind a signed endpoint (pools s1-fast, s1-pro, s1-lite). Pay rises with verified throughput up to a per-hotkey cap. Contest miners submit an engine image or a fine-tuned model of up to 36B.

Validators

The team validator sends each miner 8 items about every two minutes and recomputes some on its own model copy. Wrong answers or swapped models score zero; speed is a fifth of the score. Three more permit holders set weights.

Incentive

Chain split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; nothing burned. The miner part was 50% inference, 20% engine contest, 30% model contest. From 2026-10-30 inference gets 40% and unpaid shares are burned (team, FAQ 3.4).

Inference miner Medium barrier

Serves a pinned model on its own hardware; paid for verified speed and volume.

  • Only exact pinned weights count. Needs 4 cores, 32 GB RAM and a public IP.
  • 48 GB cards are allowed for mammoth-1 once your self-check passes (team).
  • s1-cpu (CPU only, capped at 4 decisions/s) is in the code but no longer a listed pool.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner FAQ (version 3.4) and docs/MINER.md; the README is older than the code.
  2. Check the live registration fee (0.5 TAO on 2026-10-30) and pick the pool that fits your GPU.
  3. Run the pinned model server and miner and pass the self-check (mammoth-1: use the 2026-10-29 kit).
  4. Expect lower, changing pay after 2026-10-30.

Caveats

  • Control is concentrated: the team validator (also the owner hotkey) earns about 96% of dividends; contest items and award approvals are the team's. The owner coldkey holder is not named.
  • Rules change weekly and some rulings were reversed after protests; the team calls shares and pauses its decisions, not promises.
  • Demand is team-stated only; no traffic or revenue figure is public.
