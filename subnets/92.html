What it is

REAX is run by a German company that also sells a decision-model API. A decision model is a small AI model that answers one narrow, typed question fast. Miners serve pinned models; validators re-check the answers.

How it works

Miners

Inference miners serve one pinned model behind a signed endpoint (pools s1-fast, s1-pro, s1-lite). Pay rises with verified throughput up to a per-hotkey cap.

Validators

The team validator sends each miner 8 items about every two minutes and recomputes some itself. Wrong answers or swapped models score zero; speed is a fifth of the score. Three more permit holders set weights.

Incentive

Chain split: 26% owner (18% until 2026-10-30), 41% validators, 33% miners. Team rule: of the miner part inference gets 30% (40% until 2 Nov), the engine contest 20%, the generalist contest 30%; unpaid shares are burned.

Roles and requirements

Inference miner Medium barrier

Serves a pinned model on its own hardware; paid for verified speed and volume.

  • Only exact pinned weights count. Needs 4 cores, 32 GB RAM and a public IP.
  • Validators probe around the clock, so a shared GPU loses pay; capacity above the demand target goes unpaid (team).
  • Gross pay per paid miner on 13 Nov, median (mean) TAO a day: s1-pro 0.14 (0.22), s1-fast 0.09 (0.15), s1-lite 0.03 (0.03).

Contest entrant High barrier

Submitted an engine image or a fine-tuned model; both tracks paused, no restart date.

  • A champion's pay runs in full 3 days from its award approval, then falls to zero by day 7 (team).
  • A 300-item dev set with answers is public (eval-v5/devset-v1).

Contest pause and the burned share

  • Both contests paused since 2026-10-30, not closed. The owner uid, which gets the burned part, rose from 17% of incentive on 5 Nov 00:07Z to 55% on 6 Nov and 62% on 7 to 10 and 13 Nov.
  • Uids 83, 114, 240 and 18, which held 10-12% each on 5 Nov, sit at a floor of 0.02-0.18%. Cause unconfirmed; it fits the FAQ run-out (zero by day 7).
  • Chain: owner share 26% (was 18%), miners 33% (was 41%) since 2026-10-30; the team says the extra 8 points are locked (one 230.55 alpha lock on chain).

Getting started

  1. Read the miner FAQ (version 3.8) and docs/MINER.md; the README is older than the code.
  2. Check the live registration fee (0.50 TAO on 2026-11-13, the floor) and pick the pool that fits your GPU.
  3. Run the pinned model server and miner and pass the self-check.

Caveats

  • Control is concentrated: the team validator (owner hotkey) earns about 96% of dividends.
  • Rules change weekly and docs lag chat; chat after 2 Nov 09:00 UTC was not read (archive down).
  • Burn and lock logic is not in the public repo.
  • Miner pay is gross and an average: the median paid miner earns 0.03 TAO a day; the per-uid chain vector would give about 12% more.
