What it is

REAX is run by a German company that also sells a decision-model API. A decision model is a small AI model that answers one narrow, typed question fast. Miners serve pinned models; validators re-check the answers.

How it works

Miners

Inference miners serve one pinned model behind a signed endpoint in pools s1-fast, s1-pro, s1-lite or s1-cpu; pay rises with verified throughput up to a cap.

Validators

The team validator sends each miner 8 items about every two minutes and recomputes some itself. Wrong answers or swapped models score zero; speed is a fifth of the score. Four more permit holders set weights.

Incentive

Chain split: 26% owner (18% until 2026-10-30), 41% validators, 33% miners. Team rule: of the miner part inference gets 30% (45% from the pot start), the engine contest 20%, the generalist contest 30%; unpaid shares are burned.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner FAQ (version 4.10), the mining v2 page and docs/MINER.md; the README is older than the code.
  2. Check the live registration fee (0.50 TAO on 2026-11-26) and pick the pool that fits your GPU.
  3. Run the pinned model server and miner and pass the self-check.

Caveats

  • Control is concentrated: the team validator (owner hotkey) earns about 82% of dividends (96% on 17 Nov).
  • Rules change weekly and docs lag chat; chat after 2 Nov 09:00 UTC was not read (archive down).
  • Code: the owner uid takes the residual weight (miner_slot.py); the 8-point lock was not read.
  • Pay rules change by 1 Dec: per-model pots; TEE may be required from 3 Dec (team).
