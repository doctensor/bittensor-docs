What it is

Subnet 77 pays miners to hold Uniswap V3 liquidity on Ethereum, mostly in wTAO pools, so TAO is easier to trade outside Bittensor. Alpha holders vote on which pools earn. A team server turns the votes and the miners' live positions into one weight vector, and validators copy it onto the chain.

How it works

Miners

Miners bridge TAO to wTAO, open Uniswap V3 positions in voted pools and link a hotkey to the Ethereum address with a signed claim. On 2026-11-06, 19 of 111 linked hotkeys held positions (about 0.53 million USD, server figures).

Validators

Validators do not measure liquidity. The reference validator downloads the team server's weight vector every 5 minutes, smooths it and sets weights every 101 blocks.

Incentive

Each voting alpha holder splits 10,000 points across pools, weighted by their share of voting alpha. Inside a pool only in-range positions score: a bell curve of the distance to the range's edges and midpoint, times liquidity. Emission split 0 / 0 / 18% / 41% / 41% (burn / recycle / owner / validators / miners), unchanged since 2026-10-21.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Hold Uniswap V3 liquidity on Ethereum in voted wTAO pools and link the hotkey to that address.

  • No minimum position is documented; the 19 miners with positions held under 1 USD to about 88,500 USD (2026-11-06).

Validator Medium barrier

Download the team server's weight vector and set it on chain; no own measurement.

  • On 2026-11-06, 12 of 19 permit holders had set weights within a tempo; 7 had set none for over 5 tempos.

Pool voter Low barrier

An alpha holder who splits 10,000 vote points across pools; the vote sets how miner emission is divided.

  • Vote cooldown 72 minutes, doubling on frequent changes up to 8 hours (server code, 2025-09-02).

What the scoring rewards

  • Weight follows Uniswap liquidity, not dollars: narrow ranges near the price earn more per dollar.
  • Of 23 positions on 2026-11-06, nine were 200 ticks wide with 62% of value and 76% of weight; a 2,200-tick one held 16% of value, 0.6% of weight.

Getting started

  1. Have wTAO (bridged from TAO), WETH or USDC, and ETH for gas to fund Uniswap V3 positions.
  2. Register a hotkey on subnet 77 (burn 0.0005 TAO on 2026-11-06) and hold some alpha.
  3. Open a position in a voted pool (see /pools) in a narrow range near the price; out-of-range positions score zero.
  4. Link the hotkey to the Ethereum address with the repo's register script. Never paste keys into chat; DMs offering help are scams.
  5. Check /positions and /weights or run `just verify-weights`; holders vote with `just vote`, validators run `just validate`.

Caveats

  • Scoring runs on the team's server, so an outage stops weight updates; this happened on 2026-04-24, 2026-07-28, 2026-09-20 and 2026-10-15.
  • The server's weights match the public formula on its own position data, not checked against Ethereum; its public code dates from 2025-09-02.
  • Emission was off from 2026-06-22 to 2026-08-14; another pause is possible.
  • Chat claims about miner earnings conflict; the site's liquidity figure (about 530,000 USD) is server-reported.
  • Who runs the 17 earning uids is not shown; uid 4 (20.4% of incentive) is not under the owner's keys.
