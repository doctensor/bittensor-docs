What it is

Subnet 72 launched in March 2025 as StreetVision by NATIX: miners trained models that spot roadwork in street photos. NATIX stopped paying miners in May 2026, and another team took over the slot in August. The on-chain name and links still say NATIX; the new owner has published no repository, site or plan.

How it works

Miners

None is paid. Before the halt, miners submitted roadwork classifiers that validators tested on real and synthetic images (design kept in NATIX's repository). Chain incentive now sits on the owner's uid only.

Validators

The old validator scored miners on recent accuracy and correlation, cubed so only strong miners earned much. The current NATIX code skips scoring and gives one full-weight vote to the owner uid.

Incentive

Since 2026-10-26 the stored split is 33% owner, 30% validators, 37% miners (before: 18/41/41); 33% is the chain's limit for the owner cut. The owner uid holds 100% of incentive, about 2,400 alpha a day (about 5 TAO), which the settings say is burned. Its hotkey stake grew about 2,415 alpha a day since 2026-11-11, at or a little above its whole emission and above the 33% owner cut (about 1,590); other stake moves also change it.
