What it is

Subnet 72 launched in March 2025 as StreetVision by NATIX, where miners ran image classifiers that decide whether a street photo shows roadwork. NATIX stopped paying miners in May 2026 while it shifted to Physical AI, and the slot was then taken over through the chain’s conviction-based ownership process. The on-chain name and identity links still say StreetVision by NATIX, while the new owner’s channel uses the name chillyshort. The new owner has published no repository, site or roadmap.

How it works

Miners

None are being paid. Before the halt, miners trained binary roadwork classifiers and published them to Hugging Face for validators to test. That design is documented in the former occupant’s repository, but the chain shows incentive on a single UID, which is the owner’s own.

Validators

The NATIX validator code in the repository (last commit 2026-05-07) skips the challenge round and sets all weight on the subnet owner’s UID. On 2026-10-21, 9 of the 10 validators that have weights on chain pointed fully at one UID, and that UID is registered under the owner’s keys. One validator points at UID 0 instead.

Incentive

Nothing is distributed to miners by design. The repository comment says alpha routed to the owner UID this way is burned by the protocol, and NATIX called the measure a 100% miner-emission burn. I did not confirm on chain where that alpha ends up. Validator weights were read; payouts and the owner emission split were not.

Recent activity

  • 2026-10-13 (#72 chillyshort) Asked whether NATIX still owns the slot and whether it is for sale, the owner replied with a terse yes/no pair that reads as: NATIX no longer owns it and it may be for sale. The following day the same account confirmed it owns the slot.
  • 2026-09-29 (#72 chillyshort) Asked what the subnet is for, a reply said there is currently no plan for it. On 2026-10-03 the owner-role account answered a question about a rug with “not planned”.
  • 2026-08-28 (#72 chillyshort) A bot screening post rated the subnet a low-quality OUT, noting 9 validators and no miners in the activity window. This is a bot assessment, not a team statement.
  • 2026-08-07 (#72 chillyshort) The new owner side said it bought the slot at a significant loss. Others in the channel said the subnet was burning.
  • 2026-08-06 (old StreetVision channel (archived)) Ownership of the slot changed. A channel moderator said the next day that it had been acquired through the conviction mechanism by the team behind SN29 and one other community member, and archived the old channel.
  • 2026-05-07 (old StreetVision channel (archived), NATIX blog and GitHub) NATIX announced that 100% of miner emissions would be burned until a new roadmap, expected in 6-8 weeks, as it moved toward Physical AI. The repository changed the same day so validators send all weight to the owner UID.

Caveats

  • Nothing here is a live mining opportunity: chain data on 2026-10-21 showed all incentive on the owner’s own UID.
  • The README and miner guide in the NATIX repository describe the design from before May 2026, not what runs now.
  • The new owner has published no repository, docs or site, so the plan can only be judged from short chat answers.
  • The 2026-10-13 ‘for sale’ reading is interpretive because the reply was two words.
  • It was not confirmed on chain whether the alpha sent to the owner UID is burned or kept; that rests on a code comment in the NATIX repo.
  • The new channel’s name chillyshort is the owner’s joke name; the chain still shows StreetVision by NATIX as the subnet name.
