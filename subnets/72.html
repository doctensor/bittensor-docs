What it is

Subnet 72 launched in March 2025 as StreetVision by NATIX: miners trained models that spot roadwork in street photos. NATIX stopped paying miners in May 2026, and another team took over the slot in August (owner keys changed 2026-08-06). The on-chain name and links still say NATIX; the new owner has published no repository, site or plan.

How it works

Miners

None is paid. Before the halt, miners submitted roadwork classifiers (hosted on Hugging Face) that validators tested with real and synthetic images. That design survives in NATIX's repository; the chain now shows incentive on the owner's uid only, which has never set weights.

Validators

The old validator scored miners on their last 100 answers (correlation) and last 10 (accuracy), cubed so only strong miners earned much. The current NATIX code skips that and gives one full-weight vote to the owner uid. On 2026-10-30, 10 of 11 permit holders had set weights, all for the owner uid.

Incentive

Since 2026-10-26 the stored split is 33% owner, 30% validators, 37% miners (before: 18/41/41); 33% is the chain's limit for the owner cut (announced 2026-10-19). The owner uid holds 100% of incentive, about 2,400 alpha a day (about 5 TAO), which the settings say is burned; no burn event was seen.
