What it is

EveryFrame (SN117) serves EveryFrame Studio, an AI video, image and audio studio that resells models such as Seedance and Veo. Miners run no models: they use their own provider keys in an approved worker inside a confidential VM on Phala Cloud. The team runs the coordinator and accounting.

How it works

Miners

Miners deploy the approved worker with everycli, using their own Phala Cloud and provider accounts, and post a discount per model; the coordinator assigns jobs from those offers. The worker calls the provider and signs a receipt; an operator approves each job.

Validators

The owner side publishes a signed accounting report per 361-block window and an assignment naming the report to score and the unused-share target. Validators run the public image, verify signatures and submit weights, not checking customer payment.

Incentive

Pool in dollars = alpha emitted x miner share (33%) x alpha price. A miner's weight is its approved work (plus surcharge) over the larger of the pool and total work. The unused rest goes to one uid per epoch: an owner uid until 11-03, then one of two treasury-wallet uids picked by a public seed.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Serves AI jobs with your own provider keys in a cloud worker.

  • Jobs need operator approval; treasury uids are not miners (docs). Miner Terms (v1.0.0): you secure provider resale rights and pay hosting and provider bills. A spent OpenRouter key budget blocks those routes (repo docs).
  • Pay on 2026-11-14: the 34 other earning uids share about 8.1 TAO a day before costs (chain); it swings by epoch.

Validator High barrier

Checks the owner's signed accounting and submits weights.

  • Weights follow the owner's signed assignment per chain epoch; the treasury pick needs v0.8.0 or newer (repo).
  • 11 of 64 slots are held; 5 have not set weights for over 5 tempos, incl. both treasury uids (chain).

Getting started

  1. Check demand first in the dashboard and signed epoch feed.
  2. Register a hotkey (0.05 TAO burn), open a Phala Cloud account with a workspace token, fund a provider account.
  3. Install everycli (v1.2.2), run setup with an hourly ceiling, deploy, post a discount per model; after releases run everycli update, then worker-update.

Caveats

  • Weights are validator opinions, not payouts: the treasury's 15,142 alpha lists no payout.
  • The operator runs accounting and job approval; the coordinator is private, the TEE shows which image ran, not that the provider did the work.
  • Usage and revenue come only from the team, which says buybacks will follow, with no amount or date.
  • Not shown: why the price swings, who holds the treasury coldkey, why owner uid 0 paused 11-05 to 11-09, whether uid 7 is the team's validator.
  • Team chat after 2026-11-01 was unreadable (archive down); newer posts may change this page.
