What it is

ComputeHorde offers validators of other Bittensor subnets cheap, trusted GPU capacity: miners register executors, validators send them synthetic and organic jobs and check results on a trusted GPU. Minos (SN107) added an optional SDK runner that keeps jobs on the validator’s machine, but no paying customer is confirmed, and the live burn setting sends about 99% of miner weight to a team-chosen burn list.

How it works

Miners

Miners run executors (several GPUs behind one miner hotkey) that take jobs from validators. Only the A6000 class is documented as supported, with A100 next. They can post collateral with validators for organic jobs and run an optional DDoS shield.

Validators

Validators run synthetic jobs and relay organic jobs from a facilitator, checking results on a separate trusted GPU. Each block mints compute allowance per miner and validator pair, scaled by validator stake; a job spends allowance equal to its runtime. Validators then apply the burn described below before setting weights.

Incentive

Paid runtime per miner is summed by coldkey and split across that coldkey’s hotkeys. Validators send a share of weight to burn hotkeys; the share and list come from a config in the team’s public repository, so they change without a release. It read burn rate 0.9935 and partition 0.975 on 2026-11-27: each two-epoch cycle one burn hotkey gets 97.5% of the burn (about 96.9% of all weight), the other five split the rest. The chain agrees: owner uids hold 99.4% of incentive. The team says it avoids drawing in miners before demand exists.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Runs executors with GPUs that take jobs from validators; pay is nearly all burned.

  • Non-owner uids hold about 0.6% of mechanism 0 incentive; the miner side is burned (chain, 2026-11-27).
  • The chain has 2 mechanisms; mechanism 1 gets 0% of emission and nobody sets weights on it (chain, 2026-11-27); its purpose is undocumented.

Validator High barrier

Sends synthetic and organic jobs, checks results on a trusted GPU and sets weights.

  • 10 permits held and 8 sets of weights; 3 permit holders have not set weights in over 5 tempos (2026-11-27).
  • Outside validators must follow the team's burn config.

Getting started

  1. Read the live burn settings in the dynamic config repository first: about 0.6% of incentive reaches outside miners.
  2. Read the README and the miner and validator READMEs; the install scripts are in the repo root.
  3. Test a miner on testnet netuid 174 before registering on mainnet 12.
  4. A6000 is the only documented hardware class.

Caveats

  • The burn is team-set and can change without a release.
  • No traffic, revenue or paying customer is confirmed; SN107’s optional runner runs locally and the image preload shows no payment.
  • Chat after 2026-10-29 was not read (archive timed out 2026-11-27); chat claims about owner alpha sales were not checked.
  • What the alpha-transfer switch blocks was not tested; the 2026-10-22 security commit was not audited.
