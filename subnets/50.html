What it is

Synth (SN50) pays miners to forecast the whole range of an asset’s future price, not a single number, and validators score each forecast against what the market did. Three classic competitions cover crypto, stocks and commodities over 1-hour and 24-hour horizons; a fourth, Synth Ultra, asks for 10-second forecasts.

How it works

Miners

Classic: answer validator prompts with 1,000 simulated price paths (1-minute steps over one hour, or 5-minute steps over 24 hours). Crypto covers BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and HYPE; the commodities and equities contest covers eight assets, including gold and the S&P 500. Ultra miners submit a Docker model instead.

Validators

Each answer is scored with CRPS, which measures how well the forecast range fits the realised price. Scores are made relative to the best miner and averaged over 5 days (1h) or 10 days (24h); a missing answer is charged the 95th percentile of the field. Synth’s own scorer handles Ultra.

Incentive

Each competition turns scores into weights with its own softmax (lower CRPS pays more). Validator code gives the four competitions a quarter each and splits Ultra’s quarter among the assets that validator rewards (default BTC only; the team says all four have paid since 2026-10-20), then reserves 22% of its weights for the owner’s uid.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Answers prompts with simulated price paths.

  • By default a miner only answers validators holding at least 65,000 stake.
  • The field is flat: 246 of 256 uids earned incentive, and the four best non-owner miners hold about 6% together.

Ultra miner High barrier

Submits a Docker model predicting 10-second price percentiles for four crypto assets.

  • The real latency limit is one second per call; the 5 ms target was not enforced as of 2026-10-14.
  • On 2026-10-28, 29 miners were paid on BTC and 28 on each other asset; the top miner holds about 5.5-6.7%.

Validator High barrier

Sends prompts, scores answers and sets weights on chain.

  • 12 uids hold a permit and 7 set weights; 5 have not set weights for over 5 tempos (about 6 hours).
  • Only 7 uids earn dividends: the top 5 take 98.9%, and the largest sits under the owner’s coldkey.

Owner share and burn

  • Since 2026-10-19 validator code sends a fixed 22% of weights to the owner uid. The team calls it a burn and said the rate stays this week.
  • On chain, that uid’s miner-side share is withheld and burned: about 9% of all subnet emission, beside the standard 18% owner cut.
  • The top validator (about 62% of dividends) belongs to the owner’s coldkey; dividends are shared with its stakers, so it is not all the owner’s.
  • History: about 50% to the owner uid from 2025-10-17, none from 2026-07-22, 22% from 2026-10-19.

Getting started

  1. Register a hotkey on subnet 50 (burn 0.8 TAO on 2026-10-28); classic mining and Ultra both need a registered uid.
  2. Classic: edit simulations.py as the miner tutorial shows, test with the synth-lib backtester, then run it with PM2.
  3. Ultra: read ONBOARDING.md and FAQ.md in synth-ultra, test on the sample payloads and apply with client/submit.py. After approval, push a linux/amd64 image and submit its digest.

Caveats

  • Ultra’s real reward share is not visible: which validators run its score feed and which assets they reward (BTC only by default) is unknown.
  • The weekly burn reset and the promised open-source reference models appear only in chat.
  • The README and Ultra’s specification are out of date: three equal competitions, a 90th-percentile rule, submissions closed.
  • Revenue, buyback and top-10 claims are unverified and the promised Q3 report has not appeared.
  • The team says duplicates are removed, yet miners alleged 20-slot farms on 2026-10-22.
