What it is

Synth (SN50) pays miners to forecast the whole range of an asset’s future price, not one number, and validators score each forecast against what the market did. Three competitions (since 2026-06-23) cover crypto, stocks and commodities over 1-hour and 24-hour horizons; a fourth, Synth Ultra (since 2026-08-20), asks for 10-second forecasts from a Docker model.

How it works

Miners

Classic miners answer validator prompts with 1,000 simulated price paths (1-minute steps over one hour, or 5-minute steps over 24 hours) for crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, HYPE) or eight commodity and equity assets. Ultra miners submit a Docker model that predicts 10-second price percentiles for BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP.

Validators

Each answer is scored with CRPS, which measures how well the forecast range fits the realised price, relative to the best miner and averaged over 5 days (1h) or 10 days (24h); a missing answer is charged the field’s 95th percentile. Synth’s own scorer handles Ultra (rolling 24 hours, no decay) and, from 2026-11-02, counts response time.

Incentive

Each competition turns scores into weights with its own softmax. The four get a quarter each; Ultra’s quarter is split among the assets a validator rewards (BTC only by default in code; the team says all four have paid since 2026-10-20; on chain, incentive fits all four better than BTC alone).

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Answers prompts with simulated price paths.

  • A new uid needs about 10 days of scores but is immune for 9.1 days; first pay seen at 1.8 days (0.0002 TAO a day); two uids earned nothing at 2.2 and 4.9 days.

Ultra miner High barrier

Submits a Docker model predicting 10-second price percentiles.

  • A call over 10 ms is a miss; a 24-hour mean above 5 ms earns nothing (team, from 2026-11-02).
  • Paid on 2026-11-14: 30-31 miners per asset, the top one 4.9-5.4%; mean latency tops out at 4.5 ms; 4 miners had over 1% of calls above 5 ms (one 31% on BTC, one 8-11% on every asset).
  • 2 pinned cores on a shared host in a gVisor sandbox; file and memory calls are slow (FAQ).

Validator High barrier

Sends prompts, scores answers and sets weights on chain.

  • 9 uids hold a permit and 6 set weights; the other three (the owner uid and two Ultra miners) have not for over 5 tempos.
  • Only 6 uids earn dividends: the top 5 take 99.3%, the largest (69%) under the owner’s coldkey.
  • Stake read on 11-14 (82% of staked alpha): the owner’s coldkey holds 24.6%, nearly all behind permit hotkeys; top 10 coldkeys 53%.

Owner share and burn

  • Each validator sends a fixed 22% of its weights to the owner uid (a code constant since 2026-10-19); on chain it is burned, beside the standard 18% owner cut. Before: about 50% from 2025-10-17, none from 2026-07-22.

Getting started

  1. Register a hotkey on subnet 50 (burn 0.83 TAO on 2026-11-14); to rehearse first, use testnet netuid 247.
  2. Classic: edit simulations.py as the miner tutorial shows, test with the synth-lib backtester, then run it with PM2.
  3. Ultra: read ONBOARDING.md and FAQ.md in synth-ultra, apply with client/submit.py, and after approval push a linux/amd64 image and submit its digest.

Caveats

  • Ultra’s reward share is not visible: which validators run its score feed is unknown.
  • Unchecked team claims: the AutoQuant-linked burn, open-source reference models and a Q3 report have not appeared; the docs still price an API the team calls closed.
  • Ultra docs disagree: README says 5 ms is strictly enforced, the FAQ says not yet, the specification says submissions are closed.
  • Chat after 2026-11-02 is unread (archive down); later replies are unchecked.
