What it is

SN16 changed hands on 2026-10-29: the earlier project (Fast Thinker, later Zero Hour and Kenju) was dissolved and a new owner registered the number within one block. The subnet was started on 2026-11-03 at 14:53 UTC, after 4.6 idle days. The owner has set no name, repo, site or docs. One uid, registered ten minutes after the start and holding no stake, earns all incentive; both validators put all their weight on it.

How it works

Miners

Not described: no task, repo or docs exist (checked 2026-11-14). Of 83 registered slots only uid 82, new on 2026-11-03, earns incentive.

Validators

Not described. Two uids hold validator permits, the owner's uid 0 and uid 1; both put all weight on uid 82. Uid 1's coldkey is not the owner's and validates on at least seven other subnets.

Incentive

Incentive, the miner share, is 100% on uid 82, which has no permit, no stake and no weights set; it gets about 2,944 alpha (4.9 TAO) a day, gross. Validators take the other half: uid 0 (the owner's) 76%, uid 1 24%. Stored split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; unspent incentive is set to Burn.
