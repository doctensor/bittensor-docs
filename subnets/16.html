What it is

SN16 changed hands on 2026-10-29: the old project was dissolved and a new owner registered the number within one block. It was started on 2026-11-03 after 4.6 idle days; the owner has set no name, repo or docs. One uid, registered ten minutes after the start, earns all incentive and both validators weight only it; it sold its pay until about 11-18, kept it since, and on 11-23 moved its stake to uid 1's hotkey.

How it works

Miners

Not described: no task, repo or docs exist (checked 2026-11-24). Of 83 registered slots only uid 82, new on 2026-11-03, earns incentive.

Validators

Not described. Three uids hold validator permits: the owner's uid 0, uid 1 and, new since 11-18, uid 82; uids 0 and 1 put all weight on uid 82, which sets none. Uid 1's coldkey is not the owner's and validates on at least seven other subnets.

Incentive

Incentive, the miner share, is 100% on uid 82, which sets no weights; it gets about 2,944 alpha (3.6 TAO) a day, gross. It sold its pay until about 11-18, then kept it; on 11-23 its whole stake (15,646 alpha) was moved to uid 1's hotkey, not sold. Validators take the other half: uid 0 (the owner's) 75%, uid 1 25%. Stored split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; unspent incentive is set to Burn.
