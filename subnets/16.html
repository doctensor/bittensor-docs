What it is

How it works

Miners

Not described: no task, repo or docs exist for this registration (checked 2026-10-31). 13 of 256 slots are registered and only one uid, the owner's, earns incentive.

Validators

Not described. Two uids hold validator permits and both put all their weight on uid 0, the owner's own. Weights are committed hidden and revealed one epoch (about 72 minutes) later.

Incentive

Incentive, the miner share of rewards, is 100% on uid 0, the owner's hotkey, which also takes 77% of validator dividends. The last epoch's emission was 0 alpha, so nothing has been paid yet. The stored split is 18% owner, 41% validators and 41% miners (the chain default), and unspent incentive is set to Burn.
