What it is

SN16 changed hands on 2026-10-29: the earlier project was dissolved and a new owner registered the number within one block. It was started on 2026-11-03 after 4.6 idle days; the owner has set no name, repo, site or docs. One uid, registered ten minutes after the start, earns all incentive and both validators weight only it; it sold its pay until about 11-18 and has kept every epoch since.

How it works

Miners

Not described: no task, repo or docs exist (checked 2026-11-21). Of 83 registered slots only uid 82, new on 2026-11-03, earns incentive.

Validators

Not described. Three uids hold validator permits: the owner's uid 0, uid 1 and, new since 11-18, uid 82; uids 0 and 1 put all weight on uid 82, which sets none. Uid 1's coldkey is not the owner's and validates on at least seven other subnets.

Incentive

Incentive, the miner share, is 100% on uid 82, which sets no weights; it gets about 2,944 alpha (3.6 TAO) a day, gross. On 11-16 one epoch's pay was sold in full 31 blocks later; since about 11-18 it is kept (10,775 alpha). Validators take the other half: uid 0 (the owner's) 76%, uid 1 24%. Stored split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; unspent incentive is set to Burn.
