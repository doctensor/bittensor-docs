What it is

Claims (SN111) turns scientific papers into claims tied to quoted evidence, judged by AI. Until 2026-11-08 miners extracted claims daily; since 11-09 they enter weekly contests with packaged pipelines. Mainnet since 2026-08-28.

How it works

Miners

Until 11-08 miners extracted claims from daily paper batches. Since 11-09 they upload a packaged pipeline before a weekly deadline; the validator runs every package on the same 320 new papers and scores it against a baseline pipeline.

Validators

Two AI judges check each claim against the source and a third breaks ties. A package must pass copy, code and security audits; its score is the average gain over the baseline on the same paper.

Incentive

The best pipeline gets 70% of the miner pool, the next two 20% and 10%; an empty slot burns. Since 11-12 the validator sends 87.6% of its weight to UID 0 (burned), so miners share 12.4%; the operator wallet (UID 39) gets nothing.

Validator High barrier

Runs the AI judges that accept or reject claims and sets weights.

  • The team's backend runs the contest and signs the weights; 3 of 4 permit holders set the same row.
  • 4 of 64 permit slots held; the owner hotkey has 87.4% of validator dividends (2026-11-13).

Weekly mainnet contest (from 9 Nov)

  • Round 1: 31 miners, 32 packages, 29 cleared, no disputes; result published 11-12 after the rules were amended to revision 3.
  • Scoring: one 320-paper stage (9,600 jobs); no gold cases or miner votes were used.
  • Limits: $0.10 and 1,000,000 tokens per paper; DeepSeek v4-flash and Qwen3-32B only.
  • Pay: UID 222 gets 8.70% of weight, UID 149 2.48%, UID 96 1.24%; the rest, and any empty slot, burns.
  • Round 2 closes 11-16 12:00 UTC (17 miners, 24 packages); round 1's winner goes live 11-16, round 2's on 11-23.

Getting started

  1. Read the README, docs/submission.md and the round rules; start from the sample or reference package.
  2. Build a package and manifest; models are DeepSeek v4-flash and Qwen3-32B.
  3. Register a hotkey (about 0.05 TAO burn); check the entry policy (2 mainnet consensus rounds in 7 days).
  4. Upload with a signed request and publish the on-chain commitment before the Monday 12:00 UTC deadline.

Caveats

  • The weight policy moved from a 90% operator share to a burn within days (11-11 to 11-12); the team's validator sets it.
  • Usage figures are the team's own and disagree (119 a day in a post, about 200 in the API); the paper feed has been at zero since 11-09.
  • Round 1's top gains lie within one standard error. How entrants meet the consensus-round entry rule is not documented.
  • UID 0 earns 87.4% of validator dividends, about 10 TAO a day gross, shared with its stakers.
  • Miners report flood attacks and selection bias; none verified. Chat after 2026-11-01 unread.
