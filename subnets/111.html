What it is

Claims (SN111) turns scientific papers into claims tied to quoted evidence. Miners extract them, validators use AI judges, and since 2026-09-27 miners also review each other's hardest cases. Mainnet since 2026-08-28.

How it works

Miners

Miners get batches of about 50 papers (about 4 a day, not for all), extract claims with quoted evidence and upload them. A review task arrives about every 3 hours, due in 30 minutes; a last-three average of 0.75 keeps extraction access.

Validators

Two AI judges check each claim against the source, a third breaks ties. A miner's score is the share of the accepted set its claims cover, split equally when several submit the same claim; hidden known-answer cases score reviewers.

Incentive

First place gets 70% of the miner pool, then 16, 8, 4 and 2%. Since about 2026-10-10 the team routes 90% of miner incentive to UID 39, its stated operator recipient, to pay for compute. Five other miners share the remaining 10%.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Extracts claims with quoted evidence from paper batches and answers review tasks.

  • Miners pay model costs today; the team says the contest ends that (chat, 2026-10-29).
  • A batch goes to 10 to 13 miners; a zero score bars a miner about a day.
  • Testnet contest (SN530): no server, API key or prior reviews needed; nothing is paid (emission off).

Validator High barrier

Runs the AI judges that accept or reject claims and sets weights.

  • Production profile: 50 papers across about 15 miners per round.
  • 4 of 64 permit slots held; the owner hotkey has 84.4% of validator dividends (chain, 2026-11-07).

What changes on 9 November

Team plan; testnet rules are public.

  • Round 4 (25 packages from 23 miners) closes 11-07 12:00 UTC. Plan: first mainnet round 11-09, results 11-12.
  • Entry: none on testnet (unresolved funding links waived); mainnet rule (two qualifying reviews in 7 days) unpublished.
  • Testnet limits: $0.10 and 1,000,000 tokens per paper; pipelines run on open-weight models only.
  • Pay: miner emission stays 10%, split 70/20/10 among the top three; the team pays compute.
  • Old daily rounds end 9 November; their last weights stay until 12 November 18:00 UTC.

Getting started

  1. Read the README and agent miner README; use the reference miner or your own.
  2. Install Python 3.12 and Bittensor SDK 10.5.0; add a hosted-model key (OpenRouter advised).
  3. Register a hotkey (0.05 TAO burn) and open a public TCP port.
  4. Answer every review task within 30 minutes and keep your last-three average at 0.75 or above.
  5. Try the contest on testnet (subnet 530), no prior reviews needed; first mainnet deadline planned 2026-11-09 12:00 UTC.

Caveats

  • The 90% operator share is seen on chain; what the team does with it is its statement.
  • Usage figures are the team's own and disagree (119 papers a day in a post, about 200 in the API).
  • Mainnet round rules are not published; the weight process ran only on testnet; licence terms are a manifest flag only.
  • Miners report flood attacks, selection bias and funding-link errors; the team disputed two; none verified. Chat after 2026-11-01 unread.
