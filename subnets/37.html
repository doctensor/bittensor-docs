What it is

Miners train sparse autoencoders (SAEs), tools that read a model's internal activations, for one pinned model, OLMo-2 7B layer 16. An evaluator scores how much accuracy each SAE keeps; the champion takes the reward.

How it works

Miners

Miners train an SAE (32,768 features) on their own GPUs and run the miner service on a Linux machine with a public IPv4 address. Each evaluation costs 10 work tokens (WT), bought with alpha at 1 WT per alpha.

Validators

The chain allows one validator permit (cut from 64 on 2026-10-10). Its evaluator splices each SAE into the model and measures the loss recovered; its code is not public.

Incentive

Config v37 (unchanged on 11-27) burns 70% and gives this competition the other 30%. The champion's share of its 30% scales with its SN37 alpha (about 16% at 100 alpha, 95% at 10,000); the rest is burned. A challenger must beat the champion by 0.003, or a quarter of the gap if larger. Payout needs a paid result under 12 hours old.

Getting started

  1. Read INCENTIVES.md and lanes/lane1.md; the miner asks you to confirm them.
  2. On a Linux server with a public IPv4 address, register a hotkey on subnet 37 (burn 0.0005 TAO) and hold alpha.
  3. Install per the README, make a hotkey-only wallet copy, fill in .env and run aurelius-miner serve.
  4. Buy WT with aurelius-miner fund (preview, then --execute); check the runtime pin first.
  5. Train an SAE with SAE-Lens 6.46.0, run aurelius-sae check, then upload it and track it with aurelius-sae status.

Caveats

  • The docs call the rules an open beta; the signed config is authoritative and rewards may be adjusted retroactively.
  • Chain weights are one validator's opinions, not payouts. The champion's identity and score are not public.
  • Not public: why the champion's weight was zeroed on 11-16 and restored on 11-22, who sold on 11-25 and 11-27, or who now holds UID 243 (coldkey swapped 11-22).
  • A stake-retention mechanism is built but disabled and unpublished.
  • Chat after 2026-10-31 was unreadable on 2026-11-27; a team reply is unchecked.
