What it is

AdTAO is a prediction contest for Google Ads. Miners submit containerised models that forecast how real advertising changes will move cost, conversions and efficiency over 7, 14 and 28 days. The team runs every model daily on real account changes, scores it when outcomes settle and pays the 20 most accurate.

How it works

Miners

Miners ship a model, not daily predictions: a container that reads one account-change episode per line and returns P10/P50/P90 ranges for cost, conversions and efficiency at 7, 14 and 28 days. The team runs it daily, offline, and scores it when outcomes settle. New models first pass an admission test.

Validators

The team's pipeline builds each daily basket, collects settled outcomes and scores each prediction once (interval accuracy 50%, width 10%, direction 15%, goal metric 15%). Validators fetch the team's weight vector with an API key and set it on chain.

Incentive

Models are ranked by recent score against the field on the same changes; the top 20 are paid on a fixed curve (50%, 25%, 10%, then 80% of the previous rank). Copies pay only the earliest. On chain, 19 UIDs hold incentive in line with the curve; nothing sits on UID 0.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Builds a forecasting container; the team runs and scores it daily.

  • Sandbox: no network, 1 GB RAM, 15 CPU-minutes per basket; the spec says about 250 episodes a day, the latest held 2,276.
  • Paid: top 20, 7 scored days, 50 scored predictions, earliest of identical models only.
  • Gross pay: about 13 TAO a day over 19 miners, 0.70 each on average, 0.18 outside the top 5.

Validator High barrier

Sets the team's daily weight vector on chain; scoring stays with the team.

  • 51 UIDs hold permits; 8 set weights, 43 none in over 5 epochs; 7 of the 8 top UID 107.

Rule change from 2026-10-30

Announced 2026-10-23; scored results do not change.

  • Episodes carry change descriptions and account context.
  • Results compare the 28 days before a change with the days after; zero-conversion cases get no efficiency result.
  • A waiting or rejected new model leaves the last admitted one running.

Operator control and self-mining

  • The team supplies baskets and outcomes and runs and scores the models; receipts are public, inputs are not.
  • Team-owned miners are UIDs 1, 154 and 221. Members allege more self-mining; the team asked for evidence and none was settled in chat.

Getting started

  1. Register a hotkey on netuid 21 (mainnet) and set up Docker and the repo tools.
  2. Create the one-time ed25519 key and bind it on chain (quickstart steps 3 and 4).
  3. Start from the reference model and train on the published slices (training v5).
  4. Return 7, 14 and 28 day blocks per episode, repeatably, offline, within 1 GB and 15 CPU-minutes.
  5. Push the image by digest to a public registry and commit sn21-model:v1:<repo>@sha256:<digest> on chain.
  6. Hold 1,000 alpha; check admission on the miner-status page. Validators: ask the operator team for an API key.

Caveats

  • The team's pipeline supplies baskets, outcomes and scoring; receipts are public, but no score was recomputed for this page.
  • Usage, customers and revenue are team claims; nothing on chain or in code shows them.
  • The 2026-10-30 rules show in no receipt yet; some switched-off rules have no start date.
  • Reports of hotkeys shown as not registered (2026-10-22) got no team answer in the channel.
