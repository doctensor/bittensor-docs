What it is

Enigma (SN63), run by qBitTensor Labs, is a prize subnet. It posts hard problems: factor ever larger RSA numbers (with Terra Quantum) or find the hidden peak of an obfuscated quantum circuit (with BlueQubit). The first valid solution wins the prize and is released as open source; miner emission funds the prize pool, not per-miner pay.

How it works

Miners

Build a Docker solver, test it with the repo's workbench, then submit the zip with the mine-enigma CLI or the web form; the CLI also pays the TAO fee on chain. The solver runs on the validator's machine (4 hour limit).

Validators

Validators fetch submissions, check the fee proof and run each solution in Docker (no network) on a 96 GB GPU box. A winner must succeed in 3 runs (5 for level 4). Validators also vote on prize payouts; 10 hold permits and 9 set weights (2026-10-30).

Incentive

Validators point weight at one of 5 treasury sink hotkeys, named by the team platform each tempo; on 2026-10-30 100% of incentive sat on one. The miner share (41%, about 5,890 alpha a day) piles up in the vault and pays prizes. Split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Writes a solver for an open milestone; only the first solution to pass validation wins.

  • Submit with the mine-enigma CLI or the web form (the 560-bit winner used the web wallet). The 0.1 TAO fee was about $23 on 2026-10-30; the solver gets up to 14,400 s on the validator box.

Validator High barrier

Runs submitted solvers, sets weights on the sinks and votes on payouts; the team whitelists voters.

  • Voting needs the treasury admin to whitelist your EVM address; miners offer work only to 3 hard-coded validator hotkeys unless overridden (code, head 9913c93).
  • One hotkey holds 94.2% of dividends and funds every payout (chain, 2026-10-30).

Milestones

  • Breaking RSA: 7 of 8 solved (340-bit 2026-06-05 to 560-bit 2026-10-27), $100,000 in prizes; the 1024-bit milestone ($250,000, open since 2026-09-01) has 0 submissions.
  • Hardening Quantum Proof: levels 1 to 4 solved, $57,500; the next level waits on BlueQubit. 262 submissions in total.

Prize payouts

  • A prize is fixed in SN63 alpha (560-bit: 14,340 alpha for $20k); paying it needs a validator vote (60% approval, 50% quorum, about 3 days, 1 day timelock).
  • Twelve payouts ran by 2026-10-23, the nine matched to milestones 4 to 7 days after the win; the 560-bit prize had no proposal on 2026-10-30. The 480-bit vote failed on 2026-07-06 and the team paid from its own wallet.

Terms before you submit

  • Every submission assigns the author's own components to qBitTensor Labs; winners are released under AGPL-3.0 and the lab may license them commercially. Entrants must be 18+; unclaimed prizes are forfeited after 90 days.

Getting started

  1. Check the team site for open milestones and prizes; the repo README lags it.
  2. Clone qbittensor-labs/enigma, run pip install -e . and read the workbench README; past winners' solvers are public.
  3. Build a Docker solver and test it with enigma-workbench test <challenge> --solution <dir>, which mirrors the validator; it is the only free test.
  4. Register a hotkey on netuid 63 (burn 0.0005 TAO), run the miner and submit with mine-enigma; the 0.1 TAO fee is paid after the upload.
  5. Validators: follow VALIDATORS.md and ask the treasury admin to whitelist you.

Caveats

  • Chain figures are one block (2026-10-30); the sink that gets the emission rotates by tempo, so they show where weight sits, not intent.
  • That the pool cap is off rests on the burn sink getting no weight on 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-30; validators' settings are not public.
  • Dollar figures on the team site are team-reported; vault and committed-wallet alpha were last read on 2026-10-23.
  • The licensing thesis is unproven: no licence revenue on 2026-10-12, and a first deal was only being explored on 2026-10-01.
