What it is

Enigma (SN63), run by qBitTensor Labs, is a prize subnet. It posts hard problems: factor large RSA numbers (with Terra Quantum) or find the hidden peak of an obfuscated quantum circuit (with BlueQubit). The first valid solution wins the prize and is released as open source; miner emission funds the prize pool, not per-miner pay.

How it works

Miners

Build a Docker solver, test it with the repo's workbench, then submit the zip with the mine-enigma CLI or the web form; the CLI also pays the TAO fee on chain. The solver runs on the validator's machine (4 hour limit). The first solution to pass all validation runs wins; validators take one submission at a time (team, 2026-10-30).

Validators

Validators fetch submissions, check the fee proof and run each solution in Docker (no network) on a 96 GB GPU box. A winner must succeed in 3 runs (5 for level 4). Validators also vote on prize payouts; 11 hold permits and 9 set weights (2026-11-07).

Incentive

Validators point weight at one of 5 treasury sink hotkeys, named by the team platform; on 2026-11-07 100% of incentive sat on one (uid 28). The miner share (41% of emission, about 2,940 alpha or 12 TAO a day) piles up in the vault and pays prizes; the owner takes 18% and validators 41%.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Writes a solver for an open milestone; only the first solution to pass validation wins.

  • Submit with the mine-enigma CLI or the web form (the 560-bit winner used the web wallet). The 0.1 TAO fee was about $23 on 2026-11-07.

Validator High barrier

Runs submitted solvers, sets weights on the sinks and votes on payouts; the team whitelists voters.

  • Voting needs the treasury admin to whitelist your EVM address; miners offer work only to 3 hard-coded validator hotkeys unless overridden (code).
  • One hotkey holds 66% of dividends (chain, 2026-11-07; 92% on 11-06); it sent the 560-bit payout on 2026-11-02 (governor page). A second holds 17%.

Milestones

  • Breaking RSA: 7 of 9 solved, $100,000 in prizes (latest: 560-bit, 2026-10-27). Open, with 0 submissions each: 580-bit ($10,000, live since 2026-10-30) and 1024-bit ($250,000, since 2026-09-01).
  • Hardening Quantum Proof: levels 1 to 4 solved, $57,500; the next level waits on BlueQubit. 262 submissions in total.

Prize payouts

  • A prize is fixed in SN63 alpha (560-bit: 14,340 alpha for $20k, about 61 TAO at today's price); paying it needs a validator vote (60% approval, 50% quorum, about 3 days, 1 day timelock).
  • Thirteen payouts have executed. Nine matched milestones 4 to 7 days after the win (by 2026-10-23); the 560-bit prize (won 2026-10-27) was paid on 2026-11-02. The 480-bit vote failed on 2026-07-06; the team paid from its own wallet.

Terms before you submit

  • Every submission assigns the author's own components to qBitTensor Labs; winners are released under AGPL-3.0 and the lab may license them commercially. Unclaimed prizes are forfeited after 90 days.

Getting started

  1. Check the team site for open milestones (580-bit and 1024-bit) and prizes; the repo README lags it.
  2. Clone qbittensor-labs/enigma, run pip install -e . and read the workbench README; past winners' solvers are public.
  3. Build a Docker solver and test it with enigma-workbench test <challenge> --solution <dir>; it mirrors the validator and is the only free test.
  4. Register a hotkey on netuid 63 (burn 0.0005 TAO), run the miner and submit with mine-enigma; the 0.1 TAO fee is paid after the upload.
  5. Validators: follow VALIDATORS.md and ask the treasury admin to whitelist you.
