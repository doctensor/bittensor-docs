What it is

Enigma (SN63) is qBitTensor Labs' prize subnet. It posts hard problems: factor large RSA numbers (with Terra Quantum) or find the hidden peak of an obfuscated quantum circuit (with BlueQubit). The first valid solution wins the prize and is released as open source; miner emission funds the prize pool, not per-miner pay.

How it works

Miners

Build a Docker solver, then submit the zip with the mine-enigma CLI or the web form; the CLI also pays the TAO fee on chain. The solver runs on the validator's machine (4 hour limit). The first solution to pass all validation runs wins; validators take one submission at a time (team, 2026-10-30).

Validators

Validators fetch submissions, check the fee proof and run each solution in Docker (no network) on a 96 GB GPU box. A winner must succeed in 3 runs (5 for level 4). 9 hold permits and 8 set weights; the silent one is the owner hotkey (2026-11-27).

Incentive

Validators point weight at the 5 treasury sink hotkeys, named by the team platform. Incentive follows one sink at a time and changed 44 times in 60 epochs to 2026-11-22; on 2026-11-27 it sat on uid 79. The miner share (41% of emission, about 2,940 alpha or 10 TAO a day) piles up in the vault and pays prizes; the owner takes 18%, validators 41%.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Writes a solver for an open milestone; only the first solution to pass validation wins.

  • The 560-bit winner used the web wallet. The 0.1 TAO fee was about $19 on 2026-11-27.

Validator High barrier

Runs submitted solvers, sets weights on the sinks, votes on payouts; the team whitelists voters.

  • Miners offer work only to 3 hard-coded validator hotkeys unless overridden (code).
  • Uid 147, which sent the 560-bit payout (governor page, 11-02), held 92% of dividends on 11-06, 1% on 11-09 and 91% on 11-17, 11-20, 11-22 and 11-27. Uid 12 holds 4%, uid 141 3% (chain, 11-27).

Milestones

  • Breaking RSA: 8 of 9 solved, $120,000 in prizes (580-bit solved 2026-11-21 after 6 submissions). Open: 1024-bit ($250,000, since 2026-09-01, 0 submissions).
  • Hardening Quantum Proof: levels 1 to 4 solved, $57,500; the next level waits on BlueQubit. 268 submissions in total.
  • Two anonymous keys won $100,000 of the $177,500 solved.

Prize payouts

  • A prize is fixed in SN63 alpha (560-bit: 14,340 alpha for $20k, about 48 TAO at today's price); paying it needs a validator vote (60% approval, 50% quorum, about 3 days, 1 day timelock).
  • Thirteen payouts have executed: nine 4 to 7 days after the win and the 560-bit prize (won 10-27) on 11-02. The 480-bit vote failed on 2026-07-06; the team paid from its own wallet. The team API lists both payout proposals for the 580-bit prize (about 114 TAO) as executed; dates unknown.

Terms before you submit

  • Every submission assigns the author's own components to qBitTensor Labs; winners are released under AGPL-3.0 and the lab may license them commercially. Unclaimed prizes are forfeited after 90 days.

Getting started

  1. Check the team site for open milestones (1024-bit) and prizes; the repo README lags it.
  2. Clone qbittensor-labs/enigma, run pip install -e . and read the workbench README; past winners' solvers are public.
  3. Build a Docker solver and test it with enigma-workbench test <challenge> --solution <dir>; it mirrors the validator and is the only free test.
  4. Register a hotkey on netuid 63, run the miner and submit with mine-enigma; the 0.1 TAO fee is paid after the upload.
  5. Validators: follow VALIDATORS.md and ask the treasury admin to whitelist you.

Caveats

  • Chain figures are a snapshot (2026-11-27); sinks rotate between 5 hotkeys, so weight rows and incentive can differ.
  • The pool cap looks off only because the burn sink got no weight; validators' settings are not public.
  • Team-site dollar figures are team-reported; vault and committed-wallet alpha last read 2026-10-23.
  • API payout states are bare numbers, read as OpenZeppelin Governor states (7 executed, 5 queued, 3 defeated): an inference.
