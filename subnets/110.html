What it is

Green Compute (SN110) rents RTX 4090 GPUs by the hour and serves two open models through an OpenAI-compatible API, on hardware it says runs on renewable power at UK farms. Providers need team approval; sales and buybacks are team claims.

How it works

Miners

Providers apply with a renewable-power certificate and owned hardware. A node runs one Docker daemon that serves vLLM models and hosts SSH GPU pods rented by the hour.

Validators

The team runs the gateway and validator: canary prompts about every 5 minutes, scoring each epoch, then weights and a signed report that an open-source auditor rechecks.

Incentive

Score = capacity x reliability^1.3 x performance^1.1 x fraud penalty x utilization^0.8 x rental bonus (up to 10%); green power is checked at onboarding, not in the score. Only probes of the last 7 days count (the 11-06 rise, the 11-07 and 11-12 falls fit it), so a failed stretch holds the score low. Only whitelisted hotkeys get weight, so one miner gets 100% at any score. Chain split (2026-11-14): 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners.

Roles and requirements

Miner (provider) High barrier

Hosts approved 8-GPU RTX nodes for rentals and inference; the team whitelists each hotkey.

  • Accepted proof: REGO, GoO, REC or I-TRACK certificate in the business name, under 12 months old, on-site; hardware owned.
  • Per-provider wallets are off by default; the team says one central wallet pays out.
  • Only RTX 4090 is a supported node; RTX 5090 rentals are paused (2026-11-14).

Validator High barrier

The team validator scores and sets weights; others replay its signed reports with an audit tool.

  • 15 hotkeys hold permits; 13 set weights; scores come from the team's probes.

Renter (customer) Low barrier

Rents RTX 4090 pods by the hour or calls two models through an API.

  • 14 RTX 4090 free, up to 8 per deployment (2026-11-14); MCP server, events and webhooks documented.

Getting started

  1. Meet the bar: renewable-power certificate, owned 8x RTX 4090 or 5090 nodes; apply on the website and wait for whitelisting.
  2. Prepare Linux with NVIDIA driver 545+, Docker and the NVIDIA Container Toolkit.
  3. Clone the node repo, copy .env.example to .env, set hotkey, validator address and hardware, then run docker compose up -d.
  4. Open port 8007 and 30000-31000; check http://localhost:8007/readyz.

Caveats

  • One uid (registered 2026-04-24, not owner-linked) earns all incentive; its coldkey is the top alpha holder (12% of the read) and kept its pay. Who runs it is not visible.
  • Revenue and bought-alpha handling are team claims; no ledger was found.
  • Live use looks small: two models, one serving miner each, about 0.1 requests a minute. Why probes failed is not public.
  • The chain identity has no GitHub repo; the 7-day scoring window is inferred from the score history.
