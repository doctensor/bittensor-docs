What it is

Green Compute (SN110) rents RTX 4090 GPUs by the hour and serves two open models through an OpenAI-compatible API, on hardware it says sits at UK farms with certified renewable power. Providers need team approval, and one miner uid earns all incentive. The team says it sells to enterprise customers and buys back alpha with part of the revenue; that cannot be checked.

How it works

Miners

Providers apply with a renewable-power certificate and owned hardware; the team whitelists approved hotkeys. A node runs one Docker daemon that registers with the team's control plane, serves vLLM models and hosts SSH GPU pods rented by the hour.

Validators

The team runs the gateway, control plane and validator. It sends canary prompts about every 5 minutes, scores miners each epoch (about 72 minutes), sets weights and publishes a signed report anchored on chain. An open-source auditor rechecks its arithmetic.

Incentive

Score = capacity x reliability^1.3 x performance^1.1 x fraud penalty x utilization^0.8 x rental bonus (up to 10%); green power is checked at onboarding, not in the score. Only whitelisted hotkeys get weight, so one scored miner gets 100% at any score. Chain split (2026-10-31): 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners.

Roles and requirements

Miner (provider) High barrier

Hosts approved 8-GPU RTX nodes for rentals and inference; the team whitelists each hotkey.

  • Accepted proof: REGO, GoO, REC or I-TRACK certificate in the business name, under 12 months old, on-site generation; hardware must be owned.
  • Per-provider wallets exist in code but are off by default; the team says payouts go through one central wallet.

Validator High barrier

The team validator scores and sets weights; others replay its signed reports with an audit tool.

  • 15 hotkeys hold permits and 13 set weights, all 100% on the one paid uid (2026-10-31); scores come from the team's own probes.

Rents RTX 4090 pods by the hour or calls two models through an API.

Getting started

  1. Meet the bar: renewable-power certificate, owned hardware, 8x RTX 4090 or 5090 nodes; apply on the website and wait for whitelisting.
  2. Prepare Linux with NVIDIA driver 545+, Docker and the NVIDIA Container Toolkit.
  3. Clone the node repo, copy .env.example to .env, set hotkey, validator address and hardware, then run docker compose up -d.
  4. Open port 8007 and 30000-31000; check http://localhost:8007/readyz.

Caveats

  • One uid (registered 2026-04-24, not owner-linked) earns all incentive; the chain cannot show who runs it, though the site claims dozens of UK farms.
  • Revenue and bought-alpha handling are team claims; no ledger was found.
  • Live use looks small: two models with one serving miner each at 0.1-0.2 requests a minute. The cause of the score dips and report gap is not public.
  • The chain identity has no GitHub repo; 2026 emission-switch dates were not read.
