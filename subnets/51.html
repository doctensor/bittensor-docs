What it is

Lium rents NVIDIA GPUs by the hour. Providers list hosts. Emission is a second income, but one team validator decides who gets it and most miner emission goes to a team-controlled key.

How it works

Miners

Providers run a node agent on each GPU host, list it through Lium's provider server (default) or their own, and set an hourly price.

Validators

The team runs the only validator: it checks each node every 15 minutes and lists those that pass.

Incentive

Rental fees are the main income. Emission is second: the team validator splits the miners' 50% into a rented pool, idle pay and a remainder it calls burn.

Roles and requirements

Miner (provider) Medium barrier

Lists NVIDIA GPU hosts for rent and sets prices; earns 95% of rental fees plus emission.

  • Ports 8080, 2200 and three renting ports; SSH must arrive directly, not via a relay; HGX H100 or H200 need Fabric Manager.
  • Rental fees arrive as alpha or USDT; emission is alpha on a hotkey, or USDT for an e-mail or Google account (docs).
  • Idle pay needs disk of 1.5 times GPU memory, RAM of half the GPU memory (since 11-24) and a price at or below 1.5 times market p90; V100 gets none.
  • Secure: a rental ending on an inactive node costs 48 hours of its price.
  • Spot idle pay is at most 80% of Secure; idle Spot nodes of one GPU model together get at most 90% of Default Jobs' earnings. GB200 / GB300 trays need GPU splitting.
  • Cards below the reported model, or a foreign GPU workload on an idle node, earn nothing (code, 11-13).
  • The central miner needs public node addresses; a self-hosted miner may use a private IP (code, 11-20).

Validator High barrier

The team runs the only validator; outsiders can only run an auditor that copies its public results.

  • 9 of 12 permit holders set weights in the last tempo; 3 are silent, the oldest for about 648 days; uid 210 (uid 47's coldkey) never set weights.
  • Executor, miner and watchtower take the trusted validator from the owner's signed message; no release to switch it (code, 11-23).

Renter Low barrier

Rents GPUs by the hour on lium.io, the CLI, SDK or API.

  • On demand only; if a provider's node drops, billing pauses and processes must restart.

How pay works

  • Emission: 9% owner, 41% validators, 50% miners (18 / 41 / 41 until 11-12); the miners' share goes to rented nodes (7.5% of it), idle nodes under a soft limit and burn.
  • Burn goes to one uid that a code comment calls a team payout key; it is not shown to be destroyed. It held 82.5% of miner incentive on 11-25 (79.5% on 11-22); others share about 8.7% of total emission, 1.1% of it uid 210 (same coldkey). Its hotkey alpha fell 63% in 12.9 days (35,185 to 12,952) despite about 33,400 earned.
  • Team says: rental-income buy-backs and burns ran through the owner key (chat, July and August); unchecked here.

Getting started

  1. Sign up in the provider portal with an e-mail or Google account, or register your own hotkey on subnet 51 (0.5 TAO burn).
  2. Connect Discord, join the Lium server and confirm an e-mail; idle pay needs them.
  3. Prepare the host, then choose Add Node and run the install command.
  4. Set a price at or below the soft limit, pick Secure or Spot and check the node is listed.

Caveats

  • Where the burner key's alpha goes is not visible on chain; 'burn' here means paid to a team key.
  • Usage, revenue and fleet figures are the team's own.
  • The rented share fell from 87% to 67% after outages, then ranged 65% to 80% (11-25: 74%); causes unknown.
  • Docs say e-mail or Google accounts get emission in USDT (11-12); the earning hotkey is undocumented.
  • Team chat after 2026-11-01 was not read.
