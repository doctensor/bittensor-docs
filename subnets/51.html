What it is

Lium rents NVIDIA GPUs by the hour. Providers list hosts; renters rent them by website, CLI, SDK or API, and Lium keeps 5%. Emission is a second income, but one team validator decides who gets it and most miner emission goes to a team-controlled key.

How it works

Miners

Providers run a node agent on each GPU host, list it through Lium's provider server (default) or their own, and set an hourly price.

Validators

The team runs the only validator: it checks each node every 15 minutes and lists those that pass.

Incentive

Rental fees are the main income. Emission is second: the team validator splits the miners' 41% into a rented pool, idle pay and a remainder it calls burn.

Roles and requirements

Miner (provider) Medium barrier

Lists NVIDIA GPU hosts for rent and sets prices; earns 95% of rental fees plus emission.

  • Ports 8080, 2200 and three or more renting ports; one GPU model per node; HGX H100 or H200 need Fabric Manager.
  • Emission needs Discord connected in the portal and joined to the Lium server, plus a confirmed e-mail; rental fees do not.
  • Idle pay needs disk of at least 1.5 times GPU memory and a price at or below the soft limit; the team can switch it off per hotkey (code, 2026-10-30).
  • Secure: a rental ending while the node is inactive costs 48 hours of its price. Spot: no penalty; idle pay only while Lium's filler jobs run.

Validator High barrier

The team runs the only validator; outsiders can only run an auditor that copies its public results.

  • The auditor sets weights from Lium's public results and does not check nodes.
  • 9 of 11 permit holders set weights, all naming the burner uid first; the other 2 have not for about 620 days.

Renter Low barrier

Rents GPUs by the hour on lium.io, the CLI, SDK or API.

  • On demand only. If a provider's node drops, billing pauses and the pod keeps its disk; running processes must restart.

How pay works

  • Emission: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; the miners' share goes to rented nodes, idle nodes under a soft limit (every GPU model since 2026-10-26) and burn.
  • Burn goes to one uid that a code comment calls a team payout key; it is not shown to be destroyed. That uid held 80.9% of miner incentive on 2026-10-31 (77% on 10-23, 84% on 10-28); all other paid miners share about 7.8% of total emission.

Getting started

  1. Register a hotkey on subnet 51 (0.5 TAO burn, 2026-10-31) from your own machine, not the GPU host.
  2. In the provider portal pick Lium's provider server, connect Discord, join the Lium server and confirm an e-mail.
  3. Prepare the host (Ubuntu 24.04 or 22.04 HWE, NVIDIA driver, Docker; ports 8080, 2200 and three or more renting ports), then choose Add Node and run the install command.
  4. Set a price at or below the soft limit, pick Secure or Spot and check the node is listed.

Caveats

  • What the burner key does with its alpha is not visible on chain; 'burn' here means paid to a team key.
  • Usage, revenue and fleet figures are the team's own; a holder's claim of six owner-wallet buy-and-burns is unchecked; chat answers often come from a support assistant.
  • The rented share is a point-in-time count taken after the 10-28 and 10-29 outages; why it fell is not visible.
