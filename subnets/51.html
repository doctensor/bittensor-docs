What it is

Lium rents NVIDIA GPUs by the hour. Providers list hosts; renters rent them by website, CLI, SDK or API, and Lium keeps 5%. Emission is a second income, but one team validator decides who gets it and most miner emission goes to a team-controlled key.

How it works

Miners

Providers run a node agent on each GPU host, list it through Lium's provider server (default) or their own, and set an hourly price.

Validators

The team runs the only validator: it checks each node every 15 minutes and lists those that pass.

Incentive

Rental fees are the main income. Emission is second: the team validator splits the miners' 41% into a rented pool, idle pay and a remainder it calls burn.

Roles and requirements

Miner (provider) Medium barrier

Lists NVIDIA GPU hosts for rent and sets prices; earns 95% of rental fees plus emission.

  • Ports 8080, 2200 and three renting ports; HGX H100 or H200 need Fabric Manager.
  • Rental fees arrive as alpha or USDT (5% fee); emission is alpha on a hotkey.
  • Idle pay needs disk of 1.5 times GPU memory and a price at or below 1.5 times market p90 (H100 80GB ceiling $3.77 to $5.06 on 11-07); V100 gets none (code).
  • Secure: a rental ending on an inactive node costs 48 hours of its price.
  • H100 SXM without NVLink is paid the fixed H100 PCIe idle rate (code).
  • Partly rented split node: free GPUs need 3 free ports per split and 50 GB free disk for idle pay (docs).
  • Docs list 100 Mbit/s down; code also needs 10 up.

Validator High barrier

The team runs the only validator; outsiders can only run an auditor that copies its public results.

  • 8 of 11 permit holders set weights in the last tempo; 3 are silent for over 6 hours, the oldest for about 629 days.

Renter Low barrier

Rents GPUs by the hour on lium.io, the CLI, SDK or API.

  • On demand only; if a provider's node drops, billing pauses and processes must restart.

How pay works

  • Emission: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; the miners' share goes to rented nodes, idle nodes under a soft limit and burn.
  • Burn goes to one uid that a code comment calls a team payout key; it is not shown to be destroyed. It held 84.5% of miner incentive on 11-07 (78.1% on 11-02); other paid miners share about 6% of total emission.
  • Team says: rental-income buy-backs and burns ran through the owner key (chat, July and August); no policy is published; unchecked here.

Getting started

  1. Sign up in the provider portal with an e-mail or Google account, or register your own hotkey on subnet 51 (0.5 TAO burn).
  2. Connect Discord, join the Lium server and confirm an e-mail; idle pay needs them.
  3. Prepare the host (Ubuntu 24.04 or 26.04, NVIDIA driver, Docker, open ports), then choose Add Node and run the install command.
  4. Set a price at or below the soft limit, pick Secure or Spot and check the node is listed.

Caveats

  • The burner key's use of its alpha is not visible on chain; 'burn' here means paid to a team key.
  • Usage, revenue and fleet figures are the team's own.
  • The rented share fell from 87% to 67% after outages, then 80% and 76% as 194 more GPUs were listed; why it first fell is unknown.
  • Docs do not say how an account without its own hotkey receives idle pay.
  • Team chat after 2026-11-01 was not read.
