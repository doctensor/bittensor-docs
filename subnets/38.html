What it is

ChronoLLM pays miners to train language models that know only what was public up to a chosen year, so finance and research teams can backtest without hindsight. Each weekly round covers one year, walking back toward 1999 (2009 now). CrunchDAO has run the subnet since June 2026.

How it works

Miners

Each week miners train a model for the round's year and register it on chain; everything resets each round. Models may be up to 2.2B parameters.

Validators

A secure enclave fetches a private dataset and rejects copies, earliest entry first. A leak test checks that a model knows its own period but not later ones; the best 30 then duel on about 650 fresh prompts judged by an OpenAI model.

Incentive

Score is 30% leak test plus 70% duel win rate; failing any check scores zero. The top 10 share miner pay on a steep curve. Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners; about 85% of validator pay goes to the owner's slot.

Roles and requirements

Miner Medium barrier

Trains a model for the round's year, publishes it on Hugging Face and registers it on chain by Monday 12:00 UTC.

  • Any Hugging Face causal-LM architecture; custom ones need a reviewed pull request.
  • Optional self-test: pass or fail on a sample dataset, 12 calls per day, CPU only.

Validator High barrier

Runs the weekly enclave evaluation and sets weights; in practice the owner's slot does this.

  • The private dataset comes from the team's backend, which refuses unapproved enclave images (allowed 2026-10-28: dstack-0.5.12).

Product and customers

  • The site sells models back to 1999; how its 72B demo relates to the 2.2B subnet models is not documented.
  • The site promises free model downloads; the project's Hugging Face page lists no models.

Getting started

  1. Read docs/miner.md and check the live round page for the vintage year.
  2. Train a model of at most 2.2B parameters on pre-cutoff data; upload it to Hugging Face as safetensors.
  3. Optionally run the self-test, then register a hotkey on subnet 38.
  4. Pin the model to a commit SHA, submit before Monday 12:00 UTC and make the repository public within an hour.

Caveats

  • The evaluation data, backend and config are team-run with no change log, and the owner's slot posted the results of rounds 13 to 16; a zero score cannot say why.
  • Copy checks: the reshuffled-vocabulary gap closed on 2026-11-02; an edited file keeps its earlier timestamp (confirmed bug), and the duplicate-weights check is skipped if the backend errors.
  • Fairness and self-mining accusations (2026-10-12, 2026-10-29) are unresolved; the team gave no reason for the price falls. Customer claims are chat statements. Chat after 2026-11-02 was not read (archive down).
  • History: the slot (registered 2026-01-09) was Colosseum's; CrunchDAO took over on 2026-06-10.
