What it is

Actual is a subnet operated by Actual Computer Inc., a company building software that runs open AI models on computers people own, including several machines combined into a cluster. The company's product lets users reach their own machines through a private API and a confidential relay. The subnet is meant to reward miners who contribute inference capacity, but the mining side has not been shown publicly: the owner's last description of it dates from 2025 and the documentation only mentions mining controls as available where enabled. On chain on 2026-10-21 emission is on, but the miner share is directed entirely at the owner's own slot and burned, so no miner is rewarded yet.

How it works

Miners

Not documented. In 2025 the owner said miners would bring clusters of machines running the company's inference software, starting with one head node connected directly to the others over at least 25 Gb networking, and that qualifying clusters could have their inference resold. The current docs mention mining controls in account settings, to be enabled after connecting GitHub and a Bittensor wallet where available, but give no mining steps. On chain on 2026-10-21 exactly one UID, the owner's own slot (uid 0), has any incentive, so no miner is being rewarded.

Validators

Not documented. The owner promised public validator code in 2025 (on 2025-09-23: 'sometime next week') and it was not found. On chain on 2026-10-21, 13 UIDs hold validator permits and 12 of them have set weights, on 11 different coldkeys (one coldkey runs two), none of them the owner's. Each of those 12 puts 100% of its weight on uid 0, the owner's own slot; the thirteenth permit holder is the owner's slot itself, which has no weights set. Weights are committed and revealed (commit-reveal is on). The software these validators run is unknown.

Incentive

No published rules. The owner said on 2025-10-23 that scoring would combine miner speed in tokens per second, platform and location. The subnet has been in a 'burn' state since at least 2025-09-23, when the owner said the intent was to get off it as soon as possible; chain data on 2026-10-21 shows it still is. Emission is on and splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. All of the miner 41% goes to the owner's slot, and the chain's setting for such emission is 'burn' (destroy), so it is not paid out. Members have asked since 2026-05-01 when the burn ends; no team answer was seen.

Getting started

  1. No mining or validator onboarding is documented. Ask the team through its official channels, which are listed in the pinned post of the subnet's Discord channel.
  2. To try the product, create an account at actual.inc, install Actual on a computer with the command shown there, authorize the device and load a model. The docs describe this flow, but it is not mining.
  3. Optionally connect a Bittensor wallet in account settings. This only proves control of the address by a signed message and sends no transaction.

Caveats

  • No public subnet repository was found. The subnet's chain identity names github.com/actual-computer/actual-subnet-95 (chat also named it in 2026-06 and 2026-08); it returned not found on 2026-10-21, and the organisation lists only a llama.cpp fork and toks.
  • The chain was read once, on 2026-10-21 (blocks 9334694 to 9334714). Weights and the burn setting can change at any block, and weights are validators' opinions, not payouts. The chain does not show who runs the validators or what software they run; a validator UID holding about 53% of staked alpha is not on the owner's coldkey, and nothing more is known about who controls it.
  • The owner's descriptions of mining are from 2025 and the last owner post in the channel is 2026-02-28. On 2025-09-23 the owner said the burn would end as soon as possible and public validator code would follow within about a week; neither is visible 13 months later, and no dated plan has been given since.
  • Two impersonation incidents on 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-05 caused real losses according to moderators. Verify any announcement, account or link against the pinned post and the company site, and ignore direct messages offering help.
  • Moderators ask that investment and price talk stay out of the channel. Nothing here is investment guidance.
  • Claims about partners and investors, including NVIDIA, Hermes and a venture fund, come from chat or from the project's own materials and were not verified. The toks and gigatoken lineage question is an unverified chat claim.
