What it is

Actual is a subnet run by Actual Computer Inc., whose software runs open AI models on computers people own, alone or in a cluster. The subnet is meant to pay miners for inference capacity, but the mining side has never been shown publicly. Today the miner share of emission goes to the owner's own slot and is burned.

How it works

Miners

Not documented. In 2025 the owner said miners would bring clusters of machines running the company's software. The docs only mention 'mining controls' in account settings.

Validators

Not documented. On 2026-10-31, 13 of the 14 permit holders had stored weights, each putting 100% on the owner's slot (uid 0).

Incentive

No published rules. The owner said in 2025 that scoring would cover miner speed, platform and location. The miner share of emission (41%; validators 41%, owner 18%) goes to the owner's slot and the chain setting for it is 'burn'.

Scorecard

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Would supply inference capacity (clusters of machines). Closed in practice: no client exists and no miner is paid.

  • 2025 owner plan (chat): a head node linked directly to the other machines at 25 GbE or better, about 5-7 machines, an M2 Ultra as the example; qualifying clusters could have inference resold.
  • The product runs on Apple Silicon Macs, Linux and Windows x86-64 with NVIDIA or AMD GPUs, and Linux ARM64 including DGX Spark. One account does not by itself make a cluster.

Validator Barrier unknown

Sets weights on chain. Every validator with stored weights gives 100% to the owner's slot, so there is nothing to score.

  • 14 of 256 slots hold a validator permit (64 allowed). Commit-reveal is on: weights stay hidden for one epoch.
  • The 14th permit is the owner's own slot, which has never set weights since registration (2025-04-22). Only uid 0 sits under the owner's coldkey; on 2026-10-21 the weight-setters used 11 coldkeys.

Bull case

  • The company is real and shipping: a live product and a tokenizer library under Apache-2.0.
  • The pool has stayed between 23,100 and 26,000 TAO at every weekly check since 2026-10-01.

Bear case

  • No miner is paid: the whole 41% miner share goes to the owner's slot and is burned.
  • No public subnet code 13 months after it was promised; owner silent in the channel since 2026-02-28.
  • Users net sold about 2,240 TAO in the 14 days to 2026-10-31; the owner's coldkey holds at least 27.8% of staked alpha.

What would change this view: Turns bullish if the burn ends and independent miners earn incentive, or public miner and validator code with a dated plan appears. More outflow like the last two weeks would deepen the bearish view.

Getting started

  1. Mining and validating are not documented. Ask through the official channels listed in the pinned post of the subnet's Discord channel.
  2. To try the product: create an account at actual.inc, install Actual on a supported computer with the command shown there, authorize it and load a model. This is not mining.

Caveats

  • Weights are validators' opinions, not payouts, and can change at any block; the chain does not show who runs the validators.
  • The 2026-10-31 stake read reached 72% of staked alpha: the owner's 27.8% is a floor, other shares are of what was read.
  • Check announcements against the pinned post and the company site; ignore messages offering recovery help.
  • Partner and investor claims (NVIDIA, Hermes, a venture fund) are unverified.
