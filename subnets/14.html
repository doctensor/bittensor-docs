What it is

Cacheon runs speed contests for AI inference. Miners submit code that speeds up one part of a serving engine (SGLang); the team's evaluator checks that it is faster with no loss of quality. One arena is open now (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash).

How it works

Miners

Build a bundle that replaces one part of the SGLang baseline, host it, pay the fee and commit it on chain; model weights may not change.

Validators

The team's evaluator replays a sealed workload against baseline and candidate and audits speed and quality. One signed weight offer goes out; follower validators republish it.

Incentive

Credit needs one audited pass that beats the arena's best earlier pass by 1.5%, and grows with the log of the speedup. Chain split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Builds and tests a faster bundle for the open arena; pays 0.5 TAO per entry.

  • Score: output tokens per second against stock; gate: 150 tokens/s per user at the decode median.
  • A paid entry must beat the winner by 1.5% (about 1.039x stock now); a qualification takes 40 to 90 minutes.

Validator High barrier

Nine permit holders, seven publish weights, all the team's one offer; the team does the scoring.

  • On 2026-10-31 all seven weight-setters put the same miner first at 90% and one claimant at 10%; who they are is not known.
  • If the gateway fails, followers repeat the last offer for up to 12 hours, then send 100% to a burn hotkey.

Challenge claimant Medium barrier

Competes in Meta's kernel challenge, then claims the code here for a share of 10% of weights.

  • A claim counts while its job ranks top three on a board; claims split 10% equally (2 claims, 1 paid).

Getting started

  1. Read the miner guide and examples/arena_inputs/dsv41pd on main, then the newest evaluator rules on the linked branch.
  2. Check the dashboard queue first; then install the package (Python 3.11+) and run scan and verify on an example.
  3. Register a hotkey on subnet 14, build on the arena's SGLang baseline, then chain-submit (0.5 TAO, not refunded if an arena pauses).
  4. Kernel-challenge route: submit there, register your username on chain, then claim without a fee.

Caveats

  • Scoring and weight publication run on team servers; the chain shows what is paid, not that scoring is fair (one crown was revoked by operator ruling).
  • Pay moved from two Muse winners to one DeepSeek miner, to zero and back within three days; a second winner's share of the 90% is not published.
  • The code behind the newest rules and the payment bar is on a branch and was not read; who runs the seven followers and gets the 18% owner cut is unknown.
  • Four paid Muse entries (101 to 136 hours old) were never answered; arena shares, decay and fees are unpublished operator settings.
