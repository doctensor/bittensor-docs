What it is

Cathedral sells sealed sandboxes: small Linux machines for training and testing AI agents, run on confidential hardware (AMD SEV-SNP now, Intel TDX next) with a signed receipt for every command. Miners are meant to supply those machines and validators to verify them, but no outside miner is paid yet.

How it works

Miners

A miner runs a worker inside a confidential VM and announces its public address as the hotkey's endpoint. Several machines can share a hotkey, but each must verify alone. The team admits founding miners by application and said on 2026-10-24 that it will seed the first nodes itself.

Validators

A validator checks each machine's fresh hardware evidence and TLS key, zeroes duplicates, sends one SAT puzzle per machine and weights the verified work. A prober that checks claimed CPU and memory is described in the docs, but the validator code only records its receipts.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. The only uid with incentive is the owner's (uid 0), 12 of 13 validators weight it, and its miner share is burned. How customer payments would reach miners is not documented.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

Runs a sealed confidential-compute machine that validators verify and test; none is paid yet.

  • Duplicate hardware, endpoint or TLS key scores zero for every claimant, including two guests on one host.
  • Founding miners apply by form (answer within 48 hours); registering alone does not admit a machine to the customer pool.

Validator Medium barrier

Verifies miner machines, tests them and writes weights from the verified work.

  • All 13 permit holders set weights within the last tempo; the hotkey only is used, never a coldkey.
  • By default a TDX machine counts on a genuine fresh quote alone; the operator's policy decides which AMD machines count.

Product and customers

  • Team says: sealed sandboxes at $0.035 an hour for 2 vCPU and 4 GiB; one-shot runs with a signed receipt cost $0.20.
  • Cathedral leases its AMD hosts; several customers share a machine, and attestation is not re-checked per room yet.
  • No customer names, usage counts or revenue were found. The escape room is open (host-only); prize money is not live, and the validator can pay breakers up to 10% of weights (5% planned, off by default).

Getting started

  1. Miners: the team said on 2026-10-22 that mining is not open; apply through the founding-miner form in the mining guide.
  2. Run the guide's local rehearsal three times before you rent hardware or register a hotkey.
  3. Validators: get a permit on SN94 (1,000 TAO stake threshold) and follow the operator runbook; timelocked commit-reveal is the default.
  4. To use the sandboxes, sign in at cathedral.computer, add prepaid credit and install the ctcli client.

Caveats

  • Usage, customers and revenue are team claims. The 4,000-agent run was the team's own test, and no published build is linked to the sealed measurement.
  • Uid 0 may stand for team-seeded machines or a placeholder; the chain read cannot say.
  • AMD SEV-SNP admission on SN94 is unverified in the miner guide, and a TDX quote proves the hardware, not the image after boot.
  • The price table against E2B and Daytona is the team's own; sealed-room billing did not charge the list price on 2026-10-22.
  • Repo image names still say SN39; the move to SN94 was never explained.
