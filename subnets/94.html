What it is

Cathedral sells confidential sandboxes: small Linux machines for AI agent work, run on AMD SEV-SNP hardware with a signed receipt per command, now $0.01 an hour. Validators verify the miners' machines; since 2026-11-12 only approved miners are paid, and since 2026-11-20 outside AMD servers may apply.

How it works

Miners

A miner runs a worker inside a confidential VM and announces its public address as the hotkey's endpoint. Since 11-20 AMD SEV-SNP servers may apply: hand review, then 24 hours of test traffic. Only the owner's approved list earns, today its own three (uids 19 to 21).

Validators

A validator checks each machine's fresh hardware evidence and TLS key and sends one SAT puzzle per machine. Since 2026-11-12 it gives approved miners 10% by verified machines (scaled by uptime since 11-25), proven escapes 10% (on by default since 11-20), and burns the rest.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. All 8 validators that set weights put 90% on uid 0, so 90% of the miner side is burned; uids 19 and 21 get 5% each (about 1.06 TAO a day). Customer payments to miners are undocumented.

Getting started

  1. Miners: with an AMD SEV-SNP bare-metal server, run the host check at cathedral.computer/bring-your-compute and apply; none admitted yet.
  2. To try the escape contest, register a hotkey on SN94; no hardware needed.
  3. Validators: get a permit on SN94 (1,000 TAO stake threshold) and follow the README; timelocked commit-reveal is the default.
  4. To use the sandboxes, sign in at cathedral.computer, add prepaid credit and install the ctcli client.

Caveats

  • Usage, customers and revenue are team claims; the 4,000- and 1,000-agent runs were its own tests; the 6 Nov card has no verify command.
  • Uids 19 to 21 are the owner's own per the validator repo. Unlinked coldkeys hold 58 uids (85 to 147), 28 and two blocks of 20; who runs them is undocumented.
  • A hotkey swap after the validator's one-block check keeps the weight; its doc says to close this before outsiders are listed.
