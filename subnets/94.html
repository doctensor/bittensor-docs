What it is

Cathedral sells sealed sandboxes: small Linux machines for training and testing AI agents, run on confidential hardware (AMD SEV-SNP now, Intel TDX next) with a signed receipt for every command. Miners are meant to supply those machines and validators to verify them, but no outside miner is paid yet.

How it works

Miners

A miner runs a worker inside a confidential VM and announces its public address as the hotkey's endpoint. Founding miners apply by form; the team said it will seed the first nodes itself. The team's list of 2026-11-07 shows 10 Intel TDX machines on 8 uids, all registered on 2026-11-04 by one coldkey not linked to the owner, plus 3 AMD ones.

Validators

A validator checks each machine's fresh hardware evidence and TLS key, sends one SAT puzzle per machine and weights each uid by its number of verified machines; if none verifies it writes nothing.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. 12 of 14 validators weight only the owner's uid 0, so it alone has incentive and its miner share is burned; two weight the 13 verified machines, which earn nothing. Customer payments to miners are undocumented.

Product and customers

  • Team says: one-shot runs with a signed receipt cost $0.20; since 2026-10-02 only sealed sandboxes are sold.
  • Cathedral leases its AMD hosts; several customers share a machine, and attestation is not re-checked per room yet.
  • No customer, usage or revenue figures were found; the 6 Nov run of 1,000 small-model agents (424 solved and sealed) is the team's own test.
  • The escape room (host only, no cash prize) has no proven break yet; 5% of the team's votes are reserved for breaks (about 148 alpha a day), paid only if stake-heavy validators follow them.

Getting started

  1. Miners: mining is not open (team, 2026-10-22); wait for the launch notice or apply through the founding-miner form in the mining guide.
  2. Run the guide's local rehearsal three times before you rent hardware or register a hotkey.
  3. Validators: get a permit on SN94 (1,000 TAO stake threshold) and follow the README; timelocked commit-reveal is the default.
  4. To use the sandboxes, sign in at cathedral.computer, add prepaid credit and install the ctcli client.

Caveats

  • Usage, customers and revenue are team claims; the 4,000- and 1,000-agent runs were its own tests, and the 6 Nov card uses a throwaway key with no verify command yet.
  • One coldkey the chain does not link to the owner holds 63 uids (the 8 behind the Intel machines hold no stake); another holds uids 240-242 and 173. Who runs them, and why 12 validators weight only uid 0, is undocumented.
  • The miner guide calls SEV-SNP admission on SN94 unverified; a TDX quote proves the hardware, not the booted image.
