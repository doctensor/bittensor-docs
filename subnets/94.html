What it is

Cathedral sells sealed sandboxes: small Linux machines for training and testing AI agents, run on confidential hardware (AMD SEV-SNP today; Intel TDX one-shot runs use a separate path) with a signed receipt for every command. Miners are meant to supply those machines and validators to verify them; since 2026-11-12 only approved miners are paid.

How it works

Miners

A miner runs a worker inside a confidential VM and announces its public address as the hotkey's endpoint. Since 2026-11-12 only the owner's approved list earns; it holds the owner's own three (uids 19 to 21). The team's list of 11-13 shows 2 verified AMD machines and no Intel TDX one (10 on 11-11).

Validators

A validator checks each machine's fresh hardware evidence and TLS key and sends one SAT puzzle per machine. Since the 2026-11-12 release it gives approved miners 10% by verified machines, proven escapes 10%, and burns the rest.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. All 6 validators put 90% on uid 0, so 90% of the miner side is burned; uids 19 and 21 get 5% each (about 1.04 TAO a day). Customer payments to miners are undocumented.

Product and customers

  • Team says: one-shot runs with a signed receipt cost $0.20; since 2026-10-02 only sealed sandboxes are sold.
  • Cathedral leases its AMD hosts; customers share one. Its pricing says its API sees commands unless you seal them; a 7 Nov gate covers every sealed host.
  • Code: 60 commits since 10 Nov add encrypted persistent workspaces (docs: not on any live host), sealed env after create, allowlisted start commands and a benchmark runner.
  • No customer, usage or revenue figures were found. The 6 Nov run of 1,000 agents solved 424; 557 failed and 18 hit errors on Cathedral's side.
  • The escape room (host only, no cash prize) takes 20 rooms at once (was 1), now one VM on a shared server; no proven break. Payout is 10% of the team's weight (about 295 alpha a day) if validators follow.

Getting started

  1. Miners: compute mining is closed and has no application (team, 2026-11-12); wait for the announcement.
  2. To try the escape contest, register a hotkey on SN94; no hardware needed, no cash prize.
  3. Validators: get a permit on SN94 (1,000 TAO stake threshold) and follow the README; timelocked commit-reveal is the default.
  4. To use the sandboxes, sign in at cathedral.computer, add prepaid credit and install the ctcli client.

Caveats

  • Usage, customers and revenue are team claims; the 4,000- and 1,000-agent runs were its own tests, and the 6 Nov card has no verify command yet.
  • Uids 19 to 21 are the owner's own per the validator repo. One unlinked coldkey holds 63 uids (79 to 147), another 20; who runs them is undocumented.
  • A hotkey swap after the validator's one-block check keeps the weight; its doc says to close this before outsiders are listed.
