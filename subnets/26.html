What it is

Netuid 26 changed hands on 2026-11-15: the Perturb project was removed and a new owner registered the number one block later. The owner started the subnet on 2026-11-16 and has published no name, repo, site or docs. One uid, the owner's own, takes all incentive, the miner share burns, and two outside validators earn the dividends.

How it works

Miners

Not described: no task, repo or docs exist. 216 of 256 slots are filled; only the owner's uid 0 earns incentive.

Validators

Three uids hold a permit: the owner's uid 0 and two outside uids (116, 176); all three put 100% of weight on uid 0.

Incentive

Incentive is 100% on uid 0; the MinerBurned setting withholds it (41% of emission), so no outside miner is paid. Last epoch: uid 0 50% (withheld), uid 176 44%, uid 116 6% as dividends.
