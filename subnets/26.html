What it is

Perturb is an adversarial-robustness subnet: miners fool a fixed image classifier with changes too small to see, or fine-tune it to resist them. The team plans paid robustness scans. Since 2026-10-22 no outside miner is paid and the team has not said why.

How it works

Miners

Scanning: every 2 minutes a team API posts an image; miners have 40 seconds to alter it so the classifier changes its answer. Training: miners fine-tune the classifier and commit it on chain. The task feed has been frozen since 2026-10-22.

Validators

Validators score each submission locally, share reports through the team API and set weights from the stake-weighted mean rank; daily they test each committed model. The two that set weights lately put everything on uid 0.

Incentive

Per the code, miner pay is 80% scanning (top 3 get 75, 20 and 5%) and 20% to one daily model winner; a burn rate from the team API sends a share to uid 0, the owner's.

Getting started

  1. Check first whether anyone is paid (only the owner's uid since 2026-10-22), then register a hotkey on subnet 26 (burn 0.1 TAO on 2026-10-30).
  2. Miner: set wallet, NETUID 26 and storage keys in scripts/miner.env, then run scripts/run_miner.sh. Training track: follow training/README.md.
  3. Validator: ask the team for an API key, then fill scripts/validator.env and run scripts/run_validator.sh.

Caveats

  • Unexplained: why the incentive moved, why the owner swapped its hotkey on 10-22 and why the team API stopped. A deliberate burn, a fault and hand-set weights look alike.
  • 'Burned' rests on the RecycleOrBurn setting: the incentive lands on the owner's uid. Owner holdings are a floor.
  • Tasks, reports and the burn rate come from team-run services; past burn rates are not archived.
  • The scan service (Basic 99 USD per scan) and the 100T and 500T alpha buybacks are unverified. Members suspect near-identical top scores mean self-mining; nothing is concluded.
