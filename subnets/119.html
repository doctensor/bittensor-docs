What it is

SN119 is now promoted as CapabilityForge Next. The team describes an incentive network for discovering training-data improvements that make a learning-oriented, youth-friendly language model more accurate, clear and safe, starting from the Gemma 4 12B model with tasks in math, language and basic science. It is not live, and how miners or validators will work is not documented anywhere that was found.

How it works

Miners

Not documented. The team said on 2026-09-27 that the miner repo, specification and participation details were still being prepared. Team posts speak of contributors whose contributions are evaluated for real, reproducible capability gains, but what a miner would run or submit is not stated.

Validators

Not documented. No validator code or guide was found. Team posts say model updates are driven by evaluation results rather than training scale alone, and that the first tasks are in math, language and basic science.

Incentive

Not published. The team describes a loop of contribution, evaluation and model improvement, rewarding gains that can be measured and reproduced. How weights are set or how emission is split was not found, and chain weights were not read.

Caveats

  • Nothing about the current project could be checked in code. Every description above comes from the team's own posts relayed in chat, and no site, repository, miner or validator guide, or scoring rules were found. Chain weights, emission status and the on-chain identity were not read.
  • A different project, also called Satori (a Japan-based AI companion product), previously held this netuid. Its GitHub link in the older pinned posts returns a not-found error as of 2026-10-21, and those pinned posts (2025-11-24, 2026-01-15) belong to that earlier team, not to CapabilityForge Next.
  • Statements about continuity conflict. On 2026-09-02 a member of the earlier pinned team introduced the community manager who now posts the CapabilityForge Next updates, while on 2026-10-01 that community manager said the project and team are completely different from the earlier Satori. Both are on record in the channel and the relationship could not be settled from it.
  • A GitHub account named capabilityforge has one repository, cf-subnet (a single commit on 2026-05-21). It describes a subnet for agent capability packs, uses netuid 472 in its examples and does not mention SN119, learning or youth AI. The team never linked it in the channel, so it is unverified whether it is related.
  • The team said on 2026-09-09 that the official Git repository had been synchronized but gave no link, so a repository may exist that was not found.
  • Most channel content is relayed social posts and greetings. Marketing claims such as being the world's first youth-friendly language model were not checked.
