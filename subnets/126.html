What it is

Attelier is a generative-video company with apps and a paid API; its subnet is meant to be the GPU layer behind them. Outside pay is small and unsteady: it stopped 2026-11-10 13:55Z and returned 11-11 about 17:30Z. Two outside uids get 4.9% of incentive; owner uid 0 holds the rest and burns it.

How it works

Miners

Providers apply on the team's dashboard with a wallet and hotkey and list HTTPS worker endpoints; the team activates each worker by hand (six listed). Workers serve approved video profiles (5 to 15 s, 480p to 1080p).

Validators

A light CPU process fetches the gateway's signed hourly feed, checks it against the chain registry and publishes the weights. It cannot confirm a provider ran the agreed model, so validators follow the team's feed.

Incentive

Each hourly UTC window, up to 5% of the miner pool is shared by eligible workers by work units (cut by their bid; organic jobs count double). The rest burns on uid 0.

Getting started

  1. Provider: create an Attelier account at providers.attelier.ai/manage and sign a message with your provider wallet and your registered SN126 hotkey.
  2. Choose New application (provider name, concurrent jobs), add workers with HTTPS endpoints and capacity; the team reviews and activates them.
  3. Validator: install the repo (Python 3.12, OpenSSL 3), run attelier-validator --dry-run, then with your wallet and hotkey; a validator permit is required.

Caveats

  • Outside pay is capped at 5% of the miner pool (about 0.6 of 12.0 TAO a day) and unsteady: gaps of about 33 hours (11-02), 15+ (11-04), 35 (11-06) and 28 (11-10); uid 242 lost it 11-09, uid 240 on 11-10; no reason given.
  • Jobs are almost all the team's own test traffic: 473 in the day to 11-13 09:00Z, none organic; 4 organic in the week, sender unknown.
  • Trust sits with the team: it admits providers, runs the gateway and signs the feed; apps, customers and revenue are its statements.
  • No windows were sealed from 11-10 12:00Z to 11-11 13:00Z; no reason given. The gateway is not public; chat after 2026-10-31 is unread (archive down).
