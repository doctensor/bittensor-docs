What it is

Attelier is a generative-video company with apps and a paid API. Its subnet is meant to be the GPU layer behind them: approved providers serve one video profile and a CPU validator turns the team's accounting into weights. No outside provider is paid yet.

How it works

Miners

Providers apply on the team's dashboard, sign with a wallet and a hotkey and list HTTPS worker endpoints; the team activates each worker by hand. Workers serve one profile (5 s, 480p video) for jobs from the team's gateway, which decides success. The worker package is unpublished.

Validators

A light CPU process fetches the gateway's signed hourly feed, checks it against the chain registry and publishes the weights. It cannot confirm that a provider ran the agreed model, so validators follow the team's feed by construction.

Incentive

Each hourly UTC window, 5% of the miner pool is shared by eligible workers by completed video seconds; the other 95% burns on uid 0, as does a window with no eligible work. Chain 2026-10-30: all incentive is on uid 0.

Roles and requirements

Miner (GPU provider) High barrier

Serves the video model on its own GPU behind the team's gateway; paid per eligible completed video second.

  • The team reviews hotkey, worker binding, profile and expiry; a health check alone admits nobody. One worker per application and hotkey.
  • Eligible completed video seconds share 5% of the miner pool per hour: about 0.64 TAO a day for all workers together, before costs (chain, 2026-10-30).
  • Status 2026-10-30: one worker ready; it served 3 videos in the 10:00-11:00Z window, all succeeded; hardware listed as unknown.

Validator Medium barrier

Runs the light validator: reads the team's signed hourly feed, checks it against the chain and publishes the weights.

  • It publishes by default with the named wallet; --dry-run simulates. Work finished in window W sets the vector published in W+1.
  • Chain 2026-10-30: 10 permits, 8 validators with weights averaging 99.4% on uid 0, 3 permit holders silent for over 5 tempos.

Bull case

  • The rollout is moving: applications open, one worker ready, first eligible work counted 2026-10-30.
  • The unpaid miner share burns on chain instead of being sold.
  • Apps and a paid API are live.

Bear case

  • No outside provider is paid; workers get at most 5% of the miner pool.
  • Owner uids hold 70.65% of emission (a floor); the team runs admission, gateway and feed.
  • Plans changed twice in six weeks; the 48-hour weight start promised on 2026-10-26 is not on chain.

What would change this view: Bullish: more windows with eligible work, weights reaching outside workers, customer demand. Bearish: an idle gateway or no date from the team.

Getting started

  1. Provider: create an Attelier account and open providers.attelier.ai/manage.
  2. Sign a message with your provider wallet and with your registered SN126 hotkey.
  3. Choose New application, enter a provider name and concurrent jobs, and submit it for review.
  4. Add workers with HTTPS endpoints and capacity; the team reviews and activates them.
  5. Validator: install the repo (Python 3.12, OpenSSL 3), run attelier-validator --dry-run, then with your wallet and hotkey; a validator permit is required.

Caveats

  • Nothing is paid on chain yet: 3 eligible videos were counted in one window (2026-10-30), but no weights were published. All workers together can earn about 0.64 TAO a day at best.
  • Trust sits with the team: it admits providers, runs the gateway and signs the feed validators follow.
  • Provider terms are open: no minimum hardware, the worker package is unpublished, and any extra licence is 'confirmed by the operator'.
  • The repo has no licence or release and the gateway is not public; apps, customers and revenue are team statements.
