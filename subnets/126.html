What it is

Attelier is a generative-video company with apps and a paid API; its subnet is meant to be the GPU layer behind them. Outside providers shared about 0.5 TAO a day until 2026-11-06; since then the gateway shows no jobs and the miner pool burns on uid 0.

How it works

Miners

Providers apply on the team's dashboard with a wallet and hotkey and list HTTPS worker endpoints; the team activates each worker by hand (six listed). Workers serve approved video profiles (5 to 15 s, 480p to 1080p) for the team's gateway.

Validators

A light CPU process fetches the gateway's signed hourly feed, checks it against the chain registry and publishes the weights. It cannot confirm a provider ran the agreed model, so validators follow the team's feed.

Incentive

Each hourly UTC window, up to 5% of the miner pool is shared by eligible workers by work units, cut by each worker's frozen bid; organic jobs count double. The unused part, the other 95% and empty windows burn on uid 0.

Getting started

  1. Provider: create an Attelier account at providers.attelier.ai/manage and sign a message with your provider wallet and your registered SN126 hotkey.
  2. Choose New application (provider name, concurrent jobs), add workers with HTTPS endpoints and capacity; the team reviews and activates them.
  3. Validator: install the repo (Python 3.12, OpenSSL 3), run attelier-validator --dry-run, then with your wallet and hotkey; a validator permit is required.

Caveats

  • Pay is capped at 5% of the miner pool (about 0.6 of 12.3 TAO a day) and is unsteady: gaps of about 33 hours (11-02) and 15+ hours (11-04), and none since 11-06; no reason given.
  • Trust sits with the team: it admits providers, runs the gateway and signs the feed; apps, customers and revenue are its statements.
  • The gateway is not public. Chat after 2026-10-31 could not be read (archive down).
