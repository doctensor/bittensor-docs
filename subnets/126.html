What it is

Attelier is a generative-video project that plans to run a Bittensor subnet where miners compete to make open video models better, faster and cheaper, feed an inference API, and power creative apps (Create, Studio with an editing agent called Atte, Templates, Pipelines). The same owner previously ran Poker44 on this netuid. The apps and a third-party-model inference API are described as live in an invite-only private beta, while the Bittensor optimization layer is listed as roadmap in the project docs.

How it works

Miners

Not yet defined publicly. The team says miners will compete to optimize open video models on quality, speed, cost and reliability, starting with Wan2.1 T2V 1.3B (a small model chosen to test the incentive design) and later larger models such as MiniMax H3 (team statements 2026-09-16 and 2026-10-06). No miner guide, dashboard or repository has been published, and miners asking in chat when mining opens had no team answer as of 2026-10-21.

Validators

No public description of validator scoring. The team names four evaluation metrics (quality, speed, cost, reliability) but has not published a benchmark or code. On chain, 8 of 11 validator-permit holders have set weights, and most put the bulk of their weight on uid 0.

Incentive

Nothing is documented. On chain, all incentive has been on a single uid, uid 0, since about 2026-09-07 (still true on 2026-10-21), and uid 0 is the subnet owner's own registered hotkey. Earlier, around 2026-09-01, incentive sat on two uids with 95% on one miner uid, which fits the Poker44 period. Weights are validator opinions rather than payouts, and the team has not said whether the owner slot holding incentive is deliberate.

Recent activity

  • 2026-10-21 (attelier.ai, docs.attelier.ai, #attelier) The project docs describe the apps as a private beta open to invited accounts only, with public sign-up closed, and list optimizing open models on Bittensor as roadmap. The website roadmap marks the API and apps live and the project's own GPUs and models as still being built. No new team announcement had appeared in chat since 2026-10-06 apart from greetings.
  • 2026-10-06 (#attelier) The team said the subnet code is ready and being tested on testnet, starting with Wan2.1 T2V 1.3B, with a full miner dashboard in progress and no timeline. It also announced a new landing page and said it is hiring for content creation.
  • 2026-09-30 (#attelier) After attending the Exploit event in Montreal, the team reported conversations with other subnets and with infrastructure and data-center providers for the inference layer.
  • 2026-09-21 (#attelier) The team said it was working on three tracks in parallel: talking to investors, validating the incentive mechanism with a lighter model, and building the first apps. It added that proprietary video models still beat open ones for many uses, so the apps will integrate the best available models rather than only its own.
  • 2026-09-16 (former SN126 channel (archived), #attelier) The owner posted the Attelier announcement in the old Poker44 channel: a stack of model optimization, decentralized inference and video apps. Moderators archived that channel on 2026-09-17 because the subnet's goal was changing and opened a new channel.
  • 2026-09-07 (chain reads (chain_neurons uids at several blocks)) On-chain reads show all incentive on uid 0 (the owner's own hotkey) by this date, down from two incentive-holding uids around 2026-09-01. The reads at 2026-09-07, 2026-09-28 and 2026-10-21 all show the same single-uid pattern.

Caveats

  • No public code: the subnet repository named in the on-chain identity returned not-found on 2026-10-21 and the GitHub organization has no public repositories, so miner and validator behavior, scoring and benchmarks cannot be verified.
  • Miners and validators cannot currently follow a documented path: no mining guide, dashboard or opening date has been published, and the team has not answered members' chat questions about when mining starts.
  • Chain data shows incentive concentrated on the owner's own uid. It proves what is set on chain, not the team's intent; the team has not explained it.
  • Docs and site describe the apps and inference API (which route to third-party providers' models) as private beta; the Bittensor optimization layer is roadmap. Do not read the apps' availability as the subnet being operational.
  • The project whitepaper (PDF dated 2026-09-12) was not read.
  • Plans, schedules and target models come from team statements in chat and may change. No market, emission or price data is included on this page.
