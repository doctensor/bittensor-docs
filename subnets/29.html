What it is

Hotfloat is the current identity of subnet 29, which began in July 2024 as COLDINT, a fork of subnet 9 for collaborative model training. In June 2026 the owners announced a reboot as a smaller-scale version of the SN14 inference-speed competition, where miners would submit optimized LLM serving setups on affordable hardware. As of 2026-10-21 no mining competition has launched, the team has given no launch date, and the linked repository still documents SN14.

How it works

Miners

No mining mechanism is live. The team described a plan (in chat, 2026-06-20 and 2026-06-21) in which miners would submit Docker images with an optimized inference setup, starting with the Qwen model family on rented single-GPU hardware, with smaller and quantized helper models provided by the validator. None of this appears in code.

Validators

Planned: validators would run each submission and score it on two separate tracks, time to first token and tokens per second, with a 50:50 split and a winner rule that lets a slightly better entry win only after a margin decays. This design comes from chat only. The repository linked from the chain identity is a copy of the Cacheon (SN14) code, whose validator compares miner servers against a vLLM baseline on 8-GPU pods.

Incentive

On 2026-10-21 the chain showed one UID (19) with 100% of incentive, and all 11 weight-setting validators put their top weight on it. That UID did not share a coldkey or hotkey with the subnet owner in the chain read. Who operates it is not stated anywhere found. The team said on 2026-05-06 that alpha was being collected, and on 2026-06-22 that miner-side alpha still has value.

Recent activity

  • 2026-10-17 (#hotfloat) A post in the channel said the subnet had emission on and asked the owner to run an idea for paying for a mechanism. The date emission was switched back on was not found.
  • 2026-10-15 (#hotfloat) The team said the subnet is not a scam, just not active.
  • 2026-09-26 (#hotfloat) Asked what is being built, the team answered that nothing is being built at the moment. A question about a new challenge on 2026-09-29 was answered with 'don't know' on 2026-10-02.
  • 2026-09-08 (#hotfloat) Holders who could not sell in apps were told alpha transfers are not enabled on this subnet and that a plain unstake works. This was not checked on chain.
  • 2026-08-20 (#hotfloat) The team said it was still developing and might launch on SN29 or on SN72, which it said it had acquired. The SN72 claim was not checked.
  • 2026-07-10 (#hotfloat) After weeks of launch questions, the team cited other commitments for the delay. The thread included accusations about the owners' token sales; these are claims in chat and were not verified.
  • 2026-06-22 (#announce, #hotfloat) A chain-side announcement stopped emissions to subnets with no miner distribution or no code, and SN29 was on the list. The team said emission would have been zero anyway and that miner-side alpha was unaffected.
  • 2026-06-21 (#hotfloat) The team described design details: Qwen models, Docker-based submissions, a 50:50 split between time to first token and tokens per second, one submission per hotkey registration and no extra fee at first.
  • 2026-06-18 (#hotfloat) Pinned pre-announcement: COLDINT becomes HOTFLOAT, a testbed for inference optimization on smaller models and cheaper hardware, with launch time unknown and deregistration still possible.
  • 2026-05-06 (#hotfloat) The team said there was no mining yet, that alpha was being collected to steer the slot toward the deregistration price, and invited prospective subnet owners to run a sub-subnet on the slot.

Getting started

  1. No SN29-specific miner or validator guide exists as of 2026-10-21, and the team said mining has not started.
  2. Follow the pinned post and recent messages in the SN29 channel for any launch announcement before spending anything on registration.
  3. To see the intended design, read the Cacheon (SN14) docs, since the team said Hotfloat would follow that concept with smaller models.

Caveats

  • The repository linked on the chain identity is a copy of Cacheon (SN14): its README and default netuid refer to 14, and no reference to Hotfloat, COLDINT or subnet 29 was found in its code. The smaller-model design exists only in chat.
  • The website hotfloat.io and coldint.io resolved but refused connections from the research machine on 2026-10-21, so their content was not read. A third-party automated review in chat on 2026-08-28 described the site; that was not verified.
  • Chain weights are validator opinions, not payouts. Who operates UID 19 is unknown, and a member's 2026-06-16 question about whether it is the owner's UID went unanswered in the data read.
  • Emission is flagged on at 2026-10-21 but chat says it was stopped on 2026-06-22. The date and reason it came back were not found. A 2026-09-10 chat claim that emission was toggled for SN29 was not matched to any announcement.
  • The SN72 acquisition and any move of the project there are team statements only.
  • Chat includes strong accusations about the owners. None were checked, and this page does not repeat them as fact.
  • Chain data was read as aggregates only; no addresses are published.
