What it is

Subnet 29 began in July 2024 as COLDINT, a model-training subnet. A June 2026 relaunch as an inference-speed contest never launched. The miner share of emission goes to one uid (19), which the team says it uses to collect the reward.

How it works

Miners

No mining task runs; the team said so on 2026-01-28 and 2026-09-26. The one uid with incentive, 19, never sets weights. A chat-only relaunch design (2026-06-20) has miners submit Docker images tuned for speed; none is in code.

Validators

The legacy validator loads a hall-of-fame file from the team's config repo and gives each listed hotkey weight; with no contest configured, its one entry, uid 19, gets all. On 2026-11-14, 10 of 12 permit holders had weights, all topping uid 19.

Incentive

Incentive: 100% on uid 19. Last epoch uid 19 took 50% of emission and uid 144, same coldkey and 84% of dividends, 42%; owner-linked uids 0%. Split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. The team said it routes the miner reward to uid 19 to lower the subnet's equilibrium price and will unstake 'eventually' (2026-01-28, 2026-01-29).

Who holds the stake

Read 2026-11-14, 99.4% of the staked total reached; a coldkey is not proof of who controls it.

  • The coldkey behind uids 19 and 144 holds 43.4% of the 2.51 M staked alpha and the owner's coldkey 40.3% (43.0% on 2026-11-11); the next three hold 3.1%, 2.3% and 0.9%.
  • 91.5% of the stake sits on validator-permit hotkeys and 88.7% on uid 144's alone; the owner's own hotkeys hold 0.2% (2.2% on 2026-11-11).
  • Alpha transfers are switched off on chain since at least 2025-10-01 (only SN12 shares this), as the team said on 2026-09-08.
  • Since 2026-11-11 the owner coldkey's stake fell about 107,200 alpha, uid 19's hotkey 122,600 and the owner's own hotkeys 53,200; the pool took in 114,400 alpha in one hour.
