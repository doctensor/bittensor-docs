What it is

Subnet 29 began in July 2024 as COLDINT, a model-training subnet. A June 2026 relaunch as an inference-speed contest never launched. The miner share of emission goes to one uid (19), which the team says it uses to collect the reward.

How it works

Miners

No mining task runs; the team said so on 2026-01-28 and 2026-09-26. The one uid with incentive, 19, never sets weights. A relaunch design in chat (2026-06-20) had miners submitting Docker images tuned for inference speed; none of it is in code.

Validators

The legacy validator loads a hall-of-fame file from the team's config repo and gives each listed hotkey weight; with no contest configured, its one entry, uid 19, gets all. On 2026-10-30, 10 of 12 permit holders had weights, all topping uid 19 (inferred to run this code).

Incentive

Incentive: 100% on uid 19. Last epoch uid 19 took 50% of emission and uid 144, same coldkey and 84% of dividends, 42%; owner-linked uids 0%. Split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. The team said it routes the miner reward to uid 19 to lower the subnet's equilibrium price and will unstake 'eventually' (2026-01-28, 2026-01-29).

Getting started

  1. There is nothing to mine today; watch the SN29 channel for a launch notice (none through 2026-10-30) before paying a registration burn.
  2. To see how weight reaches uid 19, read neurons/validator.py in coldint_validator and hall_of_fame.json in coldint/sn29.
  3. To see the intended design, read the Cacheon (SN14) docs, which the team said the relaunch would follow.

Caveats

  • The repo linked on chain is a copy of SN14's Cacheon, last commit 2026-06-15; the smaller-model design exists only in chat.
  • That validators weight uid 19 because of the hall-of-fame file is inferred; no running validator was inspected.
  • Owner-linked uids hold 0%, yet the team says it collects the reward on uid 19 under another coldkey: key-linking is a floor.
  • Why emission was switched back on (2026-10-15) was not found; chat accusations about the owners were not checked and are not repeated.
