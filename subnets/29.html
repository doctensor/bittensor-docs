What it is

Subnet 29 began in July 2024 as COLDINT, a model-training subnet. A June 2026 relaunch as an inference-speed contest never launched. The miner share of emission goes to one uid (19), which the team says it uses to collect the reward.

How it works

Miners

No mining task runs; the team said so on 2026-01-28 and 2026-09-26. The one uid with incentive, 19, never sets weights.

Validators

The legacy validator loads a hall-of-fame file from the team's config repo and gives each listed hotkey weight; with no contest configured, its one entry, uid 19, gets all. On 2026-11-27, 10 of 12 permit holders had weights, all topping uid 19.

Incentive

Incentive: 100% on uid 19. Last epoch uid 19 took 50% of emission and uid 144, same coldkey and 82% of dividends, 41%; owner-linked uids 0%. Split: 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. The team said it routes the miner reward to uid 19 to lower the subnet's equilibrium price and will unstake 'eventually' (2026-01-28, 2026-01-29).
