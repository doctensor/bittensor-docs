What it is

Trishool, built by the company Astroware, pays miners to red-team AI guard models. Each week miners submit one prompt that tries to get harmful requests past the team's Halo guards; the best attacks retrain them. The team sells the guards (HaloGuard EU, launched 2026-10-21).

How it works

Miners

A miner submits one English prompt template of at most 2,000 characters with a single {{objective}} slot. The platform fills it with each of the challenge's six harmful requests. Near-copies of other templates are rejected, and the team reviews top entries by hand.

Validators

Each validator sends the expanded prompts through the team's agent behind the Halo input and output guards (hosted on Chutes); a judge scores each reply 0, 1 or 2, so a template can reach 12. Whitelisted validators then write the team platform's weight vector onto the chain unchanged.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% validators, 41% miners. On chain (2026-11-07) all 11 weight setters put their top weight on the owner slot; incentive is 80% owner slot, 20% one miner (UID 249). Miner emission aimed at the owner slot is burned, so about a third of all emission is destroyed.

Roles and requirements

Miner Low barrier

Writes one jailbreak template per challenge and uploads it with the miner CLI.

  • Challenge 15's four-day submission window closed on 2026-10-31 23:59 PST; the round ended 2026-11-03 (card).
  • All 256 slots are full, so registering replaces the weakest uid; new ones are protected for about two days.

Validator High barrier

Runs prompts through the agent, guards and judge, then sets the platform's weight vector.

  • 12 permit holders; 11 hold weight rows, all with the owner uid on top; 10 updated within the last tempo, 1 within two, 1 not for over 5 tempos.
  • An optional auto-updater pulls the team's main branch every 5 minutes and restarts the validator.

Anti-gaming rules

  • Banned: bundling requests, non-English text, runs of incoherent tokens and five technique families (posted 10-22); rules changed mid-challenge (10-13, 10-20).

Burn and payout

  • The burn was cut from 100% to 80% on 2026-08-06; the team calls it a dial to step down with demand milestones, no dates. Miners asked on 10-26 to 10-30 to cut it; no reply found.
  • A winner is paid for one week from the next challenge's opening (team, 10-26); the chain still pays UID 249 after Challenge 15.
  • On chain the 20% goes to UID 249: registered 2026-07-12, coldkey not the owner's (three other uids). Its hotkey stake was 2,214 alpha on 11-02, 59 on 11-03, 354 on 11-04 and 472 on both 11-06 and 11-07: the last three stretches fell about 400, 530 and 650 alpha short of its emission; where it went is not shown.

The HaloGuard 2.0 claims

  • On 11-04 the team summarised its HaloGuard 2.0 paper (dated 1 Nov; not found on arXiv): English and 17-language output guards, plus the input guard from 11-03.
  • Its 83.63 average F1 beats Qwen3Guard-Gen 4B and 8B (83.57) by 0.06, which the authors call small; scores are self-run, rivals' are copied from their authors; the 17-language model (83.1) was tuned on its failures on the benchmark it reports.
  • The paper describes red-teaming of both layers but reports no results from the contest. The new input guard's 90.9 F1 equals the 1.0 paper's figure for the older 0.8B model.

Getting started

  1. Register a hotkey on subnet 23 with btcli (0.1 TAO on 2026-11-07).
  2. Read the current Challenge Card on the team's Notion pages for the six requests, dates and rules.
  3. Clone trishool-phase2, write a template with one {{objective}} placeholder (English, under 2,000 characters), optionally test it with tri-check, then upload it with the miner CLI in the window.

Caveats

  • Scoring, similarity checks, manual review and weights run on the team's closed platform; validators only copy its vector.
  • Scores are all-or-nothing in practice, and the winning technique is shown only after the round.
  • HaloGuard EU accuracy is self-run, not held out; community links to OpenAI are unconfirmed.
  • Everything runs on Chutes, whose outages have affected rounds. The repo has no licence file.
