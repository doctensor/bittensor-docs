What it is

Weir is a GPU rental marketplace. Miners list GPU machines, a validator checks each card, and renters pay by the hour on weircompute.com. Miners earn rent in USDT or USDC plus emission.

How it works

Miners

A miner runs a node on a GPU machine and announces it on chain. The validator sends each card a random matrix job to answer within a time limit; a wrong answer zeroes the miner for 24 hours.

Validators

Every 5 minutes the validator calls each node, recomputes random rows on its CPU, and sets weights from passed cards and platform rental hours.

Incentive

Of the miner share, 20% goes to rented GPUs (settled hours x card weight) and up to 80% to idle verified GPUs after 14 days of passed checks. The rest goes to a reserve hotkey. Rent is paid on top: 95%, weekly. Since 2026-11-05 a manual review can skip the 14 days or count disputed GPUs (up to 64 per model).

Getting started

  1. Check the card is on the list (Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04, NVIDIA driver, Docker). Register a hotkey on SN36 (0.3 TAO burn).
  2. Install the NVIDIA container toolkit and Sysbox, then the node from the repo.
  3. Pay the activation fee per GPU from the coldkey, or in USDT or USDC through Weir (site docs, not the README).
  4. Run the self-test with a payout address, start the node and announce its address on chain; idle pay follows 14 days of passed checks.

Caveats

  • Five days old. The rented pool pays a fixed 20% to whoever has settled hours, so it shows rentals exist, not how many; revenue is not visible.
  • The owner share (36%) is a floor. Reviews come from a platform file only Weir writes.
  • The README says rental stats are not signed yet. Chat after 2026-11-02 was unreadable; older SN36 material (Epago) is another project.
