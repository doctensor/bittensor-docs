What it is

UR (SN25) pays people who share their home internet connection as relay points in a VPN-style privacy network. The first operator is BringYour, which runs the ur.io apps and says it has 100,000+ providers to move over.

How it works

Miners

A provider runs the provider program, signs in with an operator account (BringYour by default) and registers a wallet. Providers need no slot or burn; they claim proof-backed shares from the operator's slot.

Validators

Validators walk server-assigned chains of providers to test that they carry traffic. A pool's weight is its operator's reported usage times a quality score of 0.75 to 1.0. Only the owner's validator measures.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% miners, 41% validators, no burn. Launch policy gives providers 10% of the miner share and sends 90% to a 2-of-3 multisig, the Network Capacity Reserve (team: funds capacity ahead of demand). v2 pays by completed usage in 7-day epochs; from epoch 8 (team plan) that tenth splits 4/10 provider pools, 1/10 capture the flag, 5/10 Top 200 miners, and extenders get a pool of their own.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner guide (updated 2026-11-18); BringYour is the only listed operator.
  2. Install the newest provider release (latest 2026-11-25); service miners must update by hand once from the first release after 11-11.
  3. Run 'provider setup' (sign in, register the device, sign a payout-address message with your coldkey) as a service.
  4. After an epoch's payout list finalizes, claim with 'provider claim --epoch N' via an EVM relayer key.
  5. Validators: use the repo's outside-validator guide; site docs say to ask the team.
