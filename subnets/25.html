What it is

UR (SN25) pays people who share their home internet connection as relay points in a VPN-style privacy network. The first operator is BringYour, which runs the ur.io apps and says it has 100,000+ providers to move over. The slot was Mainframe's before 2026-08.

How it works

Miners

A provider runs the provider program, signs in with an operator account (BringYour by default) and registers a wallet. Providers need no slot or burn: the operator holds one shared slot, and providers claim proof-backed shares.

Validators

Validators walk server-assigned chains of providers to test that they carry traffic. A pool's weight is its operator's reported usage times a quality score of 0.75 to 1.0. Only the owner's validator measures.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% miners, 41% validators, no burn. Launch policy gives providers 10% of the miner share and sends 90% to a 2-of-3 multisig, the Network Capacity Reserve, which the team says funds capacity ahead of demand. In 7-day epochs v1 pays only providers the validator measured; v2 pays by completed usage.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner guide (updated 2026-10-22); BringYour is the only listed operator.
  2. Install the newest provider release for your OS (v2026.10.30); the old miner is deprioritised from 2026-11-11.
  3. Run 'provider setup' (sign in, register the device, sign a payout-address message with your coldkey) as a service.
  4. After an epoch's payout list finalizes, claim with 'provider claim --epoch N' via an EVM relayer key; v2 claims start no earlier than 2026-11-08.
  5. Validators: see the repo's outside-validator guide (2026-10-31); site docs still say to ask the team.

Caveats

  • Dates keep moving: v2 slipped from epoch 2 to 3 to 4; claims opening about 11-08 are the next test.
  • The v1 vault holds 8,114 alpha (epochs 1 and 3 unpaid, 499 captured for epoch 4); the team says funds are safe.
  • One operator and one measuring validator, both UR's; no validator activation on chain.
  • Scale and privacy claims are the team's; its threat model says no independent audit exists yet.
  • Chat after 2026-10-30 was unreadable; later team posts are missing.
