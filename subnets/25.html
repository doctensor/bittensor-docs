What it is

UR (SN25) pays people who share their home internet connection as relay points in a VPN-style privacy network. The first operator is BringYour, which runs the ur.io apps and says it has 100,000+ providers to move over. The slot was Mainframe's before 2026-08.

How it works

Miners

A provider runs the provider program, signs in with an operator account (BringYour by default) and registers a wallet. Providers need no slot or burn: the operator holds one shared slot, and providers claim proof-backed shares.

Validators

Validators walk server-assigned chains of providers to test that they carry traffic. A pool's weight is its operator's reported usage times a quality score of 0.75 to 1.0. Only the owner's validator measures.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% miners, 41% validators, no burn. Launch policy gives providers 10% of the miner share and sends 90% to a 2-of-3 multisig, the Network Capacity Reserve, which the team says funds capacity ahead of demand. v2 pays by completed usage in 7-day epochs; from epoch 8 (Nov 27, team plan) that tenth splits 4/10 provider pools, 1/10 capture the flag, 5/10 top-level miners, and extenders get a pool of their own.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner guide (updated 2026-11-12); BringYour is the only listed operator.
  2. Install the newest provider release (none since 2026-10-31); service miners must update by hand once from the first release after 11-11.
  3. Run 'provider setup' (sign in, register the device, sign a payout-address message with your coldkey) as a service.
  4. After an epoch's payout list finalizes, claim with 'provider claim --epoch N' via an EVM relayer key; v2 claims opened 2026-11-08.
  5. Validators: use the repo's outside-validator guide; site docs say to ask the team.

Caveats

  • Dates kept moving (v2 slipped from epoch 2 to 3 to 4; the recovery missed epoch 5).
  • v1's 8,114 alpha is in the v2 vault; the timelocked sweep call (ready 11-09) had not run by 11-13 03:25 UTC.
  • One operator and one measuring validator, both UR's; no validator activation; uid 127 (pool coldkey) has a new permit.
  • Scale and privacy claims are the team's; its threat model says no independent audit exists yet.
  • Chat after 2026-10-30 was unreadable; later team posts are missing.
