What it is

UR (SN25) pays people who share their home internet connection as relay points in a VPN-style privacy network with layered encryption. The first operator is BringYour, which runs the ur.io apps and says it has 100,000+ providers to move over. The slot was Mainframe's before 2026-08.

How it works

Miners

A provider runs the provider program on a PC, server or Raspberry Pi with one stable public IP, signs in with an operator account (BringYour by default) and registers a Bittensor wallet. Most providers need no slot or burn: the operator holds one shared slot for all of them, and each provider claims a proof-backed share from a settlement contract.

Validators

Validators walk server-assigned chains of providers to test that they carry traffic, then set weights each tempo. A pool's weight is its operator's reported usage times a quality score of 0.75 to 1.0. Only the owner's validator measures at launch. Of 16 permit holders, 12 set weights and 11 rank the same reserve slot first; whether they are independent of the team is unknown.

Incentive

Emission splits 18% owner, 41% miners, 41% validators, no burn. Launch policy gives providers 10% of the miner share and sends 90% to a 2-of-3 multisig, the Network Capacity Reserve, which the team says will fund capacity ahead of demand. Payouts run in 7-day epochs: v1 pays only providers the validator measured (about 1,500 an epoch); v2 pays by completed usage.

Getting started

  1. Read the miner guide (updated 2026-10-22); BringYour is the only listed operator, so join its pool.
  2. Install the newest provider release for your OS (v2026.10.27); the old miner is deprioritised from 2026-11-11.
  3. Run 'provider setup': sign in, register the device and sign a payout-address message with your coldkey; run it as a service.
  4. After an epoch's payout list finalizes, claim with 'provider claim --epoch N' using an EVM relayer key; v2 claims start no earlier than 2026-11-08.
  5. Validators: not self-service; ask the team in the channel first.

Caveats

  • Dates keep moving: v2 slipped from epoch 2 to 3 to 4 and is confirmed only when epoch 4 begins; the 'modern provider' gate moved to 2026-11-11.
  • Why epoch 2's close was missed is unknown; epoch 3's close (about 2026-10-30 18:25 UTC) is the next check.
  • One operator and one measuring validator, both UR's; whether the other weight-setters are independent is unknown.
  • Scale and privacy claims are the team's (100,000+ providers); its threat model says no independent audit exists yet.
