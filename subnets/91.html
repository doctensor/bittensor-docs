What it is

Cascade makes training the competition for an open time-series forecasting model. Miners write data generators (lane A, closed 2026-11-25), domain-expert generators (lane B) or a market fine-tuning recipe (lane C); the owner's trainer runs each entry and signs the result.

How it works

Miners

Lane A (closed): a synthetic-data generator. Lane B: a generator that trains chosen expert rows of the king model on a rented leg. Lane C: a recipe that fine-tunes the 28M Timestral model on market bars. A win needs a gain bound of at least 1%.

Validators

The owner's trainer runs each entry and signs the record; validators replay it and set weights.

Incentive

Lane A 50% (the king's cut is 10%), lane B 35%, lane C 15% since 2026-11-25. A lane B slot pays until beaten, a lane C slot 5-20 days; an empty slot burns.

Roles and requirements

Miner High barrier

  • Lane B costs (docs): 8xH100 about $42 for 3 h, $168 for 12 h; 8xRTX4090 about $10 and $41. A failed leg is not refunded.

  • So far: lane C 15 legs started, 2 finished, none paid. Lane B econ_fin was requeued 24 times: its chosen GPU (4xA100) had no capacity and no fallback.

Validator High barrier

Replays the owner's signed training record and sets weights.

  • Satellite validators replay signed receipts and never re-score; a full evaluator needs a GPU.
  • On 2026-11-28 all 8 permit holders had set weights: 5 put 100% on uid 0, 3 put 95% and 5% on one other uid; the frozen king (uid 164) holds no permit.

Getting started

  1. Read the lane B (domain experts) or lane C (MarketCast) brief; lane A takes no entries.
  2. Clone the repo, pip install -e '.[hippius,chain,generation]', and commit only through the cascade CLI.
  3. Lane B: write a generator, run lane-b verify-repo and lane-b test, then deploy and fund. Lane C: build a recipe, run lane-c test and check, then upload, commit and fund.
  4. Register a hotkey on netuid 91 (0.1 TAO), set a GPU-rental key, and run cascade rules b, cascade rules c and cascade status.

Caveats

  • Lane B and C pay is unproven: no holder yet, and lane B pay waits on an owner decision on merging experts (docs).
  • The owner trains, signs and holds the private windows, and has used that power (a king removed 2026-09-28).
  • The first v2 king (uid 220) and the old king (uid 31) share one coldkey with 14 of 256 uids.
  • The operator account fell below its $50 rent reserve on 11-27 and again today; the cause of the 11-27 price drop is unknown.
  • Lane D is documented but not in the live feed. Chat after 2026-11-01 was not read.
